## Purpose

This issue is the **single box-checkable closure ledger for a complete GMI theory**.

It does not replace the specialist trackers. It integrates and gates them:

- #592 — 40-item master formal research audit

The target is stronger than representability or retrospective explanation.

A "complete GMI theory" at a registered scope should support four predictive maps:

```text
A. architecture/domain derivation
   (ecology, resources, verifier, history) -> morphology/domain

B. capability prediction
   (morphology/domain, ecology, resources, history) -> capability profile

C. development/evolvability prediction
   (current system, ecology, history) -> future morphology/capability/search burden

D. domain discovery
   failure of known-domain closure -> candidate new carrier/operator law ->
   neutral recovery -> parent reduction -> replicated new-domain residual
```

and must survive prospective tests, negative twins, parent subtraction, neutral search, real-scale transfer, and explicit falsification.
