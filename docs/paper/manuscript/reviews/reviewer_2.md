# Reviewer 2 (contribution, prior work, target-specific significance)

Written from scratch against manuscript draft V3 and the receipts; blind to the other reports. Lens: what does an informed reader learn that they did not know, how is the work positioned against its nearest parents, and does the contribution match the venues named in the plan. Recommendation posture: the scientific object is real but narrower than a whole-programme paper implies; reposition or split before a selective venue sees it.

## Overall assessment

The paper's discipline is its strongest feature: every mechanism names a parent, every comparison has a pre-registration hash, negative and parent-sufficient results are reported as results, and the authors refuse novelty, frontier, human-usefulness and benchmark claims they cannot support. That discipline is also what makes the contribution hard to state in one sentence. After the claim narrowing the paper itself performs, the surviving positive finding is: on a bounded, authored world, a persistent evidence-state runtime beats a retrieval-plus-template parent on protected conversations in every one of eight paired lifetimes, and matches the parent wherever no revision, identification, role typing or repair is needed. That is a clean, replicated methodological result about a comparison design, not a result about language, work or science in the wild.

## Major concerns

**R2-1 (must address, positioning). The nearest neighbours are not named as papers.** Section 1 lists parents (ATMS, provenance semirings, Kleene logic, rough sets, lattice information flow, personalised PageRank, version spaces, Gold limits) and Section 8 names theory parents. No section names the three to six closest current systems: truth-maintenance-based agents, provenance-tracking knowledge bases with revocation, continual-learning agents with explicit memory and audit, or governed self-modifying systems. The manuscript's assimilation-first stance is exactly right, but it must be executed against contemporary systems, not only against the 1976–1997 sources of the mathematics. Resolution test: add a short related-systems paragraph (in Section 1 or a new Section 1.1) naming the closest current systems and stating, for each, the one axis on which the matched-comparison methodology or the KnowledgeSpace semantics differs, without a superiority claim. If the repository's parent-subtraction tables already contain this, cite them and summarise.

**R2-2 (must address, contribution statement). The one-sentence contribution should be the methodology plus one result, not the programme.** The title and abstract present the whole build. The evidence supports (i) a matched-comparison discipline (pre-registration, matched information and matched acceptance discipline, exact tests with named scales, kill gates, hostile mutants, replication) that produced a mixed, honest terminal table across twelve milestones, and (ii) one replicated lifetime-level residual on one family. Resolution test: rewrite the last two sentences of the abstract so that the first says what was measured and how, and the second says the one inferential result and the ties; move the "programme" framing to the Introduction.

**R2-3 (major, target fit). Venue mismatch as drafted.** The plan's first candidate (Nature Machine Intelligence Article) admits a main text of about 3,500 words with six display items; this draft is about 8,500 prose words with ten tables. The material is a long-form methods-and-evaluation paper. Either (a) target a long-form venue, or (b) write a 3,500-word main text around Sections 4, 5.11 and 6 with everything else in Methods and Supplementary Information. This is a target decision, not a science defect. Resolution test: the readiness report names the chosen rung and the manuscript form for it.

**R2-4 (major, significance boundary). External validity is L2 and the paper should say so in one place.** Every environment is authored by the programme, every language stream is a substitution of one 55-fact world, and the external-benchmark coverage is 0. The manuscript states each fact in its own section. A reader at a selective venue will ask what field-level fact changes because of this paper. The honest answer is a methodological one: that a residual can be measured against a matched parent only when the parent is matched in discipline as well as information, and that when this is done most families come out parent-sufficient. Resolution test: state that answer as the paper's significance claim, in the Introduction's last paragraph or in Section 9, and do not let the abstract imply more.

**R2-5 (major). The theory loop as a contribution.** Section 8 reports 89 checked items in seven batches and 16 runtime defects found by them, which is a strong result about the practice of coupling theory to a build. As written it is a summary table plus a paragraph. Either give it the space of a result (one worked example of a theorem that found a runtime defect, from statement to fix) or move it to a companion paper as the venue contract suggests. Resolution test: one of the two, stated in the revision log.

## Minor comments

- The phrase "machine epistemics" is used as the programme's name; define it once as a term of art in Section 1 (the abstract already does) and avoid using it as if it were an established field.
- "Parent-sufficient" is the paper's own terminal vocabulary; the first use in Section 1 should gloss it as "the matched parent explains the result at the evaluated scale".
- The reference arm section is long for a descriptive, non-comparator result; a selective venue would move Table 9 to Supplementary Information and keep the four-class finding in one paragraph.
- Section 7 (the self-application ledger) is a methods contribution in its own right and reads as an appendix; if it stays, its first sentence should say what a reader gains from it (a record of how design and custody defects, not mechanism defects, changed what could be claimed).
- References: 44 entries, all verified by DOI, handle or bibliographic search in this round; two are repository documents ([1], [15]), which some venues will not accept as references and would need to become footnotes or Data-availability items.

## What would change my assessment

A protected re-evaluation of the self-repair cells under the corrected gate, and a second language world (or a natural-language suite with non-zero coverage) so that the primary result is not confined to one authored world. Neither can be produced by editing.
