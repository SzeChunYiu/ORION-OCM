# Machine epistemics: one persistent cognitive machine with explicit, revisable evidence state learns language, work, science and self-repair across a lifetime, and where a matched parent is sufficient

Manuscript draft V3.4 (2026-09-06, pipeline round 2 complete; V5 protected study on the corrected runtime added as the study of record for the lifetime residual).

## Abstract

Statistical learners acquire broad competence from text, but the evidence behind an answer is not an explicit object that can be inspected, revoked or re-derived. We report machine epistemics, a programme whose working hypothesis is that a machine should learn methods of doing with an explicit, revisable evidence state, absorb the strengths of statistical learners as governed operators rather than as authority, and be evaluated against the strongest matched parent buildable from known components with identical information. The runtime object is a KnowledgeSpace: atoms carry warrant intervals whose liveness composes exactly under conjunction and alternative by Kleene's strong three-valued logic; revocation reopens exactly the dependents whose liveness changed; authority composes by lattice meet, so nothing internal can promote itself; and every acquisition enters through a registered channel, with feedback never becoming warrant. One persistent instance was built along thirteen milestones, from a bounded microworld language (134 of 134 protected utterances interpreted exactly) and a conversational alpha (42 of 42 scripted steps, 0 integrity incidents) to work transfer, scientific reasoning and governed self-repair, and was evaluated across a heterogeneous lifetime without reset. Against the matched whole-system parent, a pre-registered study of eight paired lifetimes on fresh protected streams rejected on its primary family, conversations, in 8 of 8 lifetimes (one-sided exact sign test, p = 0.0039), replicated byte-identically on a second host; six secondary families also rejected at 8 of 8 but are flagged as one coin because their differences do not vary across streams. Work success, experiment selection, pre-registered analysis and proof checking are ties, and learned organisation is parent-sufficient (the matched parent explains the result) at the evaluated scale; we report these as results. The contribution is therefore a measured comparison discipline and one replicated residual, not a general capability claim. A local open-weight language model, run as a labelled reference rather than a comparator, asserted an answer to 237 of the 240 questions that the given facts do not settle, 199 of them true in the world, while acquiring the world's lessons unevenly (25 of 48). Residuals over frontier models, blinded human usefulness and external benchmarks remain undetermined by design of the environment; an engineering review after the receipts were frozen reopened the self-repair adoption cells for protected re-evaluation; no novelty, consciousness or human-equivalence claim is made.

## 1. Introduction

Three properties are wanted from a machine that reasons on behalf of people, and current statistical learners deliver at most one of them at a time. The first is competence acquired by learning rather than by hand-writing rules. The second is an explicit evidence state: for any answer or action one can ask which observations, lessons, demonstrations or checks warrant it, revoke one, and see exactly which later commitments must be reopened. The third is governance: the machine cannot promote its own conjectures to truth, adopt a change to itself without an external decision, or act beyond the authority it was given. Foundation models supply the first property at scale and are the strongest existing parent for language, but their evidence state is implicit, so the other two properties must be bolted on from outside.

Machine epistemics builds the second and third properties into the substrate and then learns the first on top of them. Its working thesis, recorded at the start of the build, is that the machine should learn methods of doing, not imitate a transformer; that the strengths of language models should be absorbed where they earn their keep, as operators whose outputs enter with an unknown warrant and a score outside the evidence lattice; and that whatever non-human form of intelligence results should evolve under an external constitution owning checking, authority, metering and commitment [1]. The programme is assimilation-first: every mechanism names the prior work that owns it and is reported as parent-owned unless a pre-registered test rejects that reading. The parents are old and mostly exact: assumption-based truth maintenance [2, 3], provenance semirings [4], Kleene's three-valued logic [5] with Belnap's information reading [6], rough sets [7], lattice information flow [8, 9], personalised PageRank [10], Kemeny–Snell lumpability [11], Kleene algebra with tests [12], version spaces [13] and Gold-style identification limits [14]. What the programme adds is their coupling into one executable object with exact checkers and planted mutants, and a discipline for measuring whether the coupling produces anything the parents do not.

This paper reports the whole build as one artifact and one evaluation: the runtime and the theorems that fix its semantics, the milestones that grew one persistent instance from a bounded language to self-repair, the matched-comparison methodology, the pre-registered terminals including the families where the parent is sufficient, the reference arm, the two loops beside the build (a self-application ledger and a theory loop that returned runtime defects), and limitations that are structural rather than incidental.

Three claims are not made anywhere below. We do not claim a residual over a frontier foundation-model agent; no such matched parent can be built without network access or model weights, and the reference arm is labelled as unbound. We do not claim human usefulness; the blinded rating protocol is frozen but was not run. We do not claim novelty of any mechanism; every registry row names a parent, and the general-novelty terminal of every receipt reads not established. What the paper does claim is methodological: a residual over a parent can be measured only when the parent is matched in discipline as well as information, and when that is done most families come out parent-sufficient; the parent-subtraction tables in the repository carry the per-mechanism comparison against the named parents, and contemporary learned agents enter only through the labelled reference arm.

## 2. The KnowledgeSpace and its runtime

### 2.1 Warrant intervals and three-valued liveness

A KnowledgeSpace is a finite typed hypergraph of atoms and relations over an evidence universe E. Each atom carries a warrant interval ⟦L, U⟧: L is the antichain of exhibited sufficient evidence sets, U the antichain of every evidence set that could still suffice, and L ≤ U in the semiring order of antichains [4]. A profile is certified when L = U, partial when U = {∅}. Under a revoked set R ⊆ E an atom is LIVE when some exhibited warrant avoids R, DEAD when no possible warrant avoids R, and UNKNOWN otherwise; ⊗ and ⊕ act coordinate-wise.

The central semantic fact is that liveness is an exact homomorphism from (⊕, ⊗) on intervals to Kleene's strong (∨, ∧) on {LIVE, UNKNOWN, DEAD} (obligation KS-T21) [15]. Certified intervals never read UNKNOWN, and refinement (a certificate that raises L or lowers U) can move UNKNOWN to LIVE or DEAD but never flip LIVE and DEAD. A single completeness bit does not compose; the counterexample that forced the interval object is row S1 of the self-application ledger (Section 7). The interval is Pawlak's lower and upper approximation [7] applied to the ATMS label [2] with Kleene's connectives [5]; nothing here is new mathematics. The checker enumerates every valid interval over three evidence ids (168 intervals) and every revocation, 225,792 homomorphism checks, and catches two planted mutants that read UNKNOWN as LIVE or DEAD.

Two consequences carry through the build. A FEEDBACK-admitted atom carries the certified-zero interval and is DEAD under every R, so reward or preference signals never enable anything (KS-T18), proof-carrying admission [16] with the empty ATMS label. A summary over exported constituents has warrant Λ_corr ⊗ ⨂ Λ(x), LIVE only when every export is LIVE; the majority aggregation is the planted mutant (KS-T23).

### 2.2 Composition, authority and scope

Composing components under a bridge or operator yields warrant ⊗, authority meet and scope intersection (KS-T20). Authority is a product lattice of named non-negative ranks with an undeclared coordinate read as zero, and meet is the coordinate-wise minimum, so composition never raises any coordinate (Denning's lattice flow and Biba's low-water-mark [8, 9]). Hence an atom produced by any internal operator has commit rank zero, no chain of internal operations produces commit authority, and only an external action receipt can (theory batch 1, T1). The same rule makes discourse safe: a record that a speaker said p has speaker authority only, ten speakers repeating p compose to the same bottom, and the machine's own claim about p becomes LIVE only through a bridge from a warranting channel (batch 2, B1). Scope is a context set with a half-open epoch; supersession is a revocation family indexed by time whose reopening is exactly the reopening report of the ended evidence (batch 2, B5).

### 2.3 Revocation and reopening

Revoking evidence changes liveness only for atoms whose intervals cite it. The impact cone is the least dependency-closed superset of the changed set (KS-T09). The reopening report partitions the cone into REOPEN (liveness changed), RECHECK (a dependent kept an alternative live warrant, so only derived content is re-evaluated) and UNAFFECTED (KS-T22); an irrelevant revocation is a no-op. This is the ATMS label update once the label is an antichain [2, 3], and the over-deletion set of incremental materialisation intersected with the changed-derivability set [17, 18]. Reinstatement restores the space exactly; relearning admits a new evidence id with a lineage link (batch 2, B6). Structural changes made by a governed Jump are stamped with the Jump certificate, and rollback is revocation plus quarantine, never deletion (batch 1, T4).

### 2.4 Navigation, extraction and the four-valued outcome

Navigation is a restart walk over the warranted matrix, a = α s + (1 − α) Pᵀ a, with denominators frozen on the registered structure so that revocation is exact-share pruning at the fixed point (KS-T04, KS-T04b, KS-T04c) [10]. A query's outcome is four-valued: FOUND, GAP (target absent or not reached within budget), OBSTRUCTION (only when the ungated ceiling walker also fails) and CANNOT_CHECK (zero budget or an unresolved UNKNOWN on the path) (KS-T19). Timeout alone is a gap, never an obstruction: the completeness-threshold discipline of bounded model checking [19, 20] with Bruns–Godefroid's third value [21]. The budget bracket that makes a FOUND within budget sound for the fixed point is theorem T2 of batch 1; its proof surfaced that the runtime iterated from the wrong start vector, the first theory-reported defect counted in Section 8.

### 2.5 Acquisition channels and the learner contract

Every admission names its channel: INSTRUCTION, DEMONSTRATION, INTERACTION, EXPERIMENTATION, FEEDBACK or EXACT_CHECKER. A target-absent gap is closed only through the learning channels, a learner proposal is admitted only when the version space agrees on the registered query family, and the admitted atom's warrant is the per-input antichain of minimal agreeing example sets (batches 2 and 3, B2, B3, C6) [13], so revoking one example reopens exactly the inputs whose every agreement set contained it. Per-channel acquisition bounds on the registered finite classes (teaching dimension, membership-query depth, exact identification from random aligned pairs) are tabulated and parent-owned [22, 23]; a channel that identifies below its bound has received outside information, the laundering detector of Section 4.

### 2.6 Constitution and self-model

The organisation of the space is data under an external constitution 𝔠 = (Check, Authority, Meter, Commit). The self-model is a fibre of observations about the machine's own runs with authority {self_model: 1} and scope self; a self-model tail may only have self-model heads, a proposal is ⟦0, U⟧ until an external commit receipt adopts it, and a self-model record cannot close an object-level claim (batch 5, E1). Adoption is a stamped double-pushout rewrite with hash-exact rollback (batch 5, E6) [24, 25]; proposals are metered so the meta-level loop cannot livelock (batch 1, T8; batch 5, E7) [26]. The Gödel-machine reading [27] is kept honest by construction: any proof about the machine is a certificate produced outside the self-model.

### 2.7 What the runtime is

The reference implementation is the ORION Cognitive Machine (OCM), a Python package with an event-sourced ledger of twenty-three event families, a single reducer as the only writer, replay-exact persistence and restart, and an obligation registry of 127 rows across thirteen registries (90 proved, proved on a finite fragment or proved with a clause, 25 finite calibrations, 3 parent-owned, 7 open, 2 undetermined), each with a checker, a planted mutant and a named parent. The eight controlled terminals of the predecessor repository replay under the unified runtime without any upgrade of their authority.

### 3.1 Language (M3 to M6)

The language stack is a construction grammar over a bounded microworld: a lexicon with morphology and live-exception overrides, phrase and clause constructions, a canonical meaning graph (exact canonical labelling for fragments of at most seven vertices, undetermined beyond, since Weisfeiler–Leman hashing is not canonical [28, 29]), and an interpretation whose candidates each carry their own interval, so that ambiguity is a set of live candidates and collapse is an evidence event, never a score (batch 1, T6). The dialogue workspace adds speaker-indexed commitment records, proposition-indexed correction and supersession, persistent reference resolution, clarification as an information action valued in expected cells on the registered query family (batch 3, C1) [30], and an external commitment gate that commits a surface only if its decoded meaning equals the plan's, every asserted proposition is LIVE with sufficient authority and every marker follows the interval (batch 3, C2) [31]. Continual acquisition runs under five disclosed regimes with every word, lesson, demonstration and annotation counted. The conversational alpha binds these to a 55-fact bounded world (52 verified, 3 reported from sources) and runs eleven demonstration points from a clean checkout, from answering and clarifying to teaching a word and watching the capability stop when its evidence is revoked.

### 3.2 Work (M9), science (M10) and self-repair (M11)

Work is represented as task contracts (allowed and forbidden actions, hidden checker state, budget, authority, rollback), operators with registered roles and lineage, skill capsules with an invariant skeleton and per-domain adapters, and transfer maps whose warrant is the source warrant ⊗ the correspondence warrant; a transfer is refused when the target operator's registered role differs from the source role, whatever the surface similarity. Three self-contained environments with exact oracle state (enterprise support, software operations, data-analysis preflight) each have a version 2 in which a stale binding fails the hidden checker. Science is represented as tasks with an estimand, costed and risked experiments and a hidden oracle; observations where corroboration counts distinct sources and replicates never corroborate; hypotheses in symbolic, statistical, causal, simulation, program and formal layers; conclusions carrying the identification assumptions a causal claim needs; and a proof kernel whose kernel warrant and formalisation-correspondence warrant are kept apart. The self-model diagnoses a failure as a distribution over layers D0 to D8 from counterfactual evidence, names the minimum sufficient layer, raises an architecture alarm only under a valid obstruction certificate, and proposes a change in classes C0 to C6, where C6 (constitutional) is a recommendation packet only. Governance runs the challenger in shadow on a frozen snapshot, requires the pre-outcome prediction to be realised on tasks the proposer never saw, adopts only through an external adopter (self-approval has no token), meters adoptions per window, and rolls back exactly by stamped-evidence revocation.

### 3.3 The lifetime (M12)

One persistent instance, one ledger root, shared by the chat session, the work skills, the science ledger and the self-model, was run through seven phases: A language and social (the protected conversation, factual, lesson and negative-transfer suites), B enterprise work, C software work, D data and science, E cross-domain transfer, F revision (revoke a fact's source, retract an observation, revoke a demonstration) and G self-repair (an undisclosed planted fault). Version 2 ran one lifetime per ordering under three orderings; version 3 ran eight paired lifetimes on per-lifetime protected streams generated by seeded substitution inside the bounded world; version 4 re-registered the analysis with one primary family and ran on eight fresh streams. The matched whole-system parent combines the language matched parent, the work skill library, the science parent procedures and the two repair parents, with identical manifest, lessons, demonstrations, plans and budgets.

## 4. Matched-comparison methodology

The evaluation discipline was fixed before the first protected outcome was read and then tightened by the theory loop.

**Strongest faithful matched parent.** For every family the comparator is the strongest parent buildable here from known components with identical information and budget. For language it is a retrieval memory over the same 55 facts, a dialogue-state memory with string-level supersession, an in-context lesson table and a template renderer, with no warrant intervals, reopening cone, version space or gate. For work it is a skill library with name-similarity transfer, a trajectory-replay memory and a fresh-start learner; for science, naive regression, entropy-driven experiment selection and the same pre-registered analysis plan; for repair, parameter search over the router and revocation settings and reflection-retry. A template floor (fact glosses without dialogue state) is reported beside them. Matched means matched discipline as well as matched information: when the first work study's fresh-start parent acquired each domain more cheaply, it had skipped the withheld acceptance test every other arm pays; the parent was matched and the run relabelled (ledger row S27).

**Pre-registration and freeze before outcome.** Every study has a pre-registration document whose hash is bound into the receipt before any protected outcome is read. Any code change after outcome access relabels the run as development calibration, which carries no claim, and requires a new versioned study on new protected content; this happened at M7, M9 and M12 (Section 7).

**Exact tests and their scale.** Paired families are decided by an exact McNemar-style test on discordant pairs with a pre-registered equivalence margin δ = 0.05 on the paired rate difference and two one-sided tests at α = 0.05; a residual requires the one-sided exact test to reject and the difference to exceed δ; equivalence requires both one-sided tests to reject. The minimum n for an inferential terminal is 40 items; below it the family is descriptive. Theory batch 4 (D1) proved the size of these rules exhaustively over small tables, computed the size inflation of three hostile practices, and showed that equivalence at margin 1/10 on the discordant scale needs at least 76 discordant pairs, so every equivalence verdict now names its scale [32, 33]; the predecessor terminal of zero discordant pairs in 540 became a rate-difference equivalence at margin 7/1000 (row S28).

**Unit of inference.** Within one lifetime, tasks are dependent through shared state. Batch 6 (F2) proved that pooling the three re-orderings of one frozen stream multiplies the discordant count without adding information (the p-value falls from 1/4 to 1/64 at three replicas) [34, 35], and gave the exact size of an item-level sign test under block dependence at n = 54: if the turns fall in blocks of six decided by one coin, a nominal α = 0.05 test has size 65/256. The V2 claims are therefore made on the principal ordering only, the conversations family carries an item-exchangeability caveat, and V3 makes the lifetime the unit: eight independent paired lifetimes with distinct stream seeds and an exact sign test over lifetime differences, which cannot reject below five pairs and has power 0.81 at eight pairs at a per-lifetime win probability of 0.9. ### 5.10 Toward open-vocabulary English (N1, first result)

The roadmap's next milestone asks whether the same acquisition path scales from the bounded world to web English. Its first receipt is a negative and is reported as one. From the Universal Dependencies English Web Treebank training split (12,544 sentences) the machine induced a lexicon of 13,413 lexemes (6,008 seen once) with 155 irregular past and 28 irregular present exceptions as demonstration-channel evidence, and a recursive phrase grammar whose single-order families count as learned. On the protected test split the lexicon covers 19,729 of 21,188 tokens but only 1,162 of 2,016 sentences in full. The bottom-up span matcher of Section 3.1 exhausted memory on open text (ledger row S39) and was replaced by a packed-forest chart parser with exact derivation counts and declared caps, which agrees with the matcher on the bounded world. Under the frozen protocol, with a 600-second budget per split and no other job on the host, the chart engine reached 1,760 test sentences and interpreted 0 uniquely; of the sentences reached, 65% stop at an unknown lexeme (1,141), 27% exceed the declared chart cap (469), 6% find no clause construction (112) and 2% are ambiguous (38, with the gold tree among the unpacked derivations in 4); a further 154 were cut by the time budget. The number of sentences reached inside the budget varies with host load, so the proportions, not the counts, are the reported result; the receipt of record is a load-free run. Admitting out-of-lexicon tokens as gaps (an underspecified entity with an UNKNOWN warrant, never LIVE) removes the lexical stop but not the outcome: with gaps the engine parsed 566 test sentences within its time budget and interpreted 0 of them uniquely, 496 at the chart cap and 11 ambiguous. One stage therefore remains: attachment ambiguity, a property of the learned grammar rather than of the parser, which needs evidence-licensed ranking of derivations rather than a unique parse.

## 7. The self-application ledger as method

The build was run as a problem the finished machine would solve: every obstruction met while building was written in the machine's own vocabulary (the stage at which the gated walk stopped, the four-valued outcome, the dispositions tried, the fix as a registered object with a checker and a planted mutant, the parent that owned it, the minimum sufficient level of change), and an obstruction above local repair must pass the Jump interface. The ledger has 38 rows (S1 to S38). One row is a representation change adopted at the core (S1, the warrant interval, forced by an exact counterexample to the completeness bit); the rest are local repairs, custody findings or evaluation-design findings. The rows that changed what the evaluation could claim are: a comparator that skipped the acceptance discipline every other arm paid (S27); equivalence verdicts that named no scale (S28); a preservation check that passed vacuously (S29); a harness restart that split the runtime identity unnoticed (S31); pooled orderings that tripled n from zero new information (S32); a mislabelled planted fault (S33); a pre-registration hash read after the file had been removed (S36); a decision rule that named neither its family count nor its sidedness (S37); and an "unlicensed" label never checked against the knowledge base (S38). None was a mechanism change. Two lessons recur: a regime is defined by its information channel and the receipt must record what was supplied (S17), and an absence claim needs a check whose scope is justified (S14, S31). The adoption-binding defect of Section 5.8, found after the last ledger row, is of the same kind: a gate that checked a name where it should have checked an identity. An underperformance backlog beside the ledger (one-stage attribution, a lever, the test that must move; never tuned to outcome) holds three open items: per-source normalisation of the surprise background, an unbounded exact extraction, and a directed random-walk comparator arm.

## 9. Limitations

**No matched frontier parent.** The strongest whole-system parent buildable here has no learned language model in it; a residual over a frontier foundation-model agent is undetermined by construction, and the reference arm's information channel is unbound, so its numbers are a reference and not a comparator. This limitation is structural to the environment (no network, no containers, no weights beyond a local 7B model) and heads the revival backlog.

**One bounded world.** Every language stream is a substitution of one bounded world with about 60 lexemes and 7 constructions; the V3 and V4 streams are not samples of natural language. Coverage of BLiMP and of the Universal Dependencies treebank under the frozen admissibility protocol is 0, and the BabyLM and CHILDES comparisons were not run because their data and terms were not pinned. The work, science and repair environments are authored by the programme with exact oracle state; five external agent benchmarks, three external science benchmarks, the miniF2F and Lean 4 route and any frontier target are undetermined.

**No human rating.** The blinded rating protocol (seven criteria, three arms plus a labelled external reference, a recorded randomisation seed) is frozen but was not run; usefulness to a person is not claimed.

**Small n and the one-coin collapse.** Within one lifetime the work, science, transfer and repair families have n between 4 and 12 and are descriptive. At eight paired lifetimes the sign test rejects only near unanimity, and in V4 all six secondary families are flagged as collapsed because the planted design does not vary with the language substitution. The lifetime-level inference therefore rests on one family, conversations, in both V3 and V4; making the secondary families vary needs per-lifetime variation of the planted faults, revision events and transfer cells, which with the generator's manifest-licence check (row S38) is the next study.

**Runtime revalidation after the receipts.** The receipts reported here are frozen and unchanged, but the runtime that produced them was reviewed after the freeze (2026-09-05) and corrected in several places: the adoption gate checked a component table keyed by the machine rather than the exact predecessor of the named layer (Section 5.8); derived evidence was serialised with its lower warrant bound only, collapsing UNKNOWN to DEAD on restart; a promoted dialogue claim cited its bridge by record id, so revoking the bridge's assumptions missed the claim; and a population coverage certificate could promote a selected statistical output to LIVE. Each defect now has a fix and regression tests, and fresh replays of the authored scenarios reproduce the historical descriptive values, including the M12 deterministic block. Those replays are engineering evidence: the programme labels M11 as an engineering revalidation with its adoption cells reopened and M12 as requiring protected re-evaluation, and no result in this paper is renewed by them. The cross-domain transfer comparison (six machine cells against four parent cells) is likewise an evaluation-design obligation needing prospectively matched cells. The V4-R re-run (Section 5.9) shows the V4 primary residual unchanged under the corrected gate, but as an engineering regression, not as a new protected study; the pre-registered V5 study (Section 5.9) is the corrected runtime's scientific terminal for the lifetime residual; the historical M11 and M12 V2 self-repair cells remain reopened and are reported as such.

**Parents are the ones the issues named.** A learned failure classifier over traces, an overlap-aware community parent, a Bayesian experimental-design parent and a fine-tuned or adapter language learner were not built; each is a stronger parent the backlog names and would need the same candidate channel and information receipt. The self-repair benchmark's counterfactual channel is the machine's own registered candidate repairs; on history no such channel exists, so the replay audits governance only.

**Novelty is not established.** Every core mechanism is parent-owned in the subtraction tables; the contribution claimed is the executable coupling, the checkers and mutants, the discipline, and the measured residuals over the matched parent buildable here.

## Methods summary

The reference implementation is a Python package with exact rational arithmetic in the core checkers, a JSON event ledger with SHA-256 content ids and a single reducer, and receipt tools that bind every file a milestone depends on by hash and verify the chain in continuous integration. Studies ran on two hosts (a Linux workstation as principal and a laptop as replica); the local reference model ran on the laptop GPU through Ollama with temperature 0 and a fixed seed. Statistical tests are exact binomial computations implemented in the evaluation module and reproduced by the theory batches' checkers (the D1 sizes, the F2 block sizes, the G8 sizes and powers); no test uses an asymptotic approximation. Receipt values are printed rounded to the decimals shown. Language-model assistance was used for drafting and editing the prose under the receipt-bound claim discipline; every number was checked against its receipt by the claim-verification script listed under Data and code availability, and the authors are accountable for every statement.
