# Machine epistemics: one persistent cognitive machine with explicit, revisable evidence state learns language, work, science and self-repair across a lifetime, and where a matched parent is sufficient

Manuscript draft V2 (2026-09-05, after the pre-submission pipeline). Archetype: methods and evaluation, with a pre-registered matched comparison and a negative-results section. Claim authority rests with the receipt chain listed under Data and code availability, never with this prose; every number below is copied from a receipt-bound file, and a number that no file contains is written as [NOT MEASURED].

## Abstract

Large language models acquire broad competence by absorbing text, but the evidence behind any answer they give is not an explicit object that can be inspected, revoked or re-derived. We report a programme, machine epistemics, whose working hypothesis is that a machine should learn methods of doing with an explicit, revisable evidence state, absorb the strengths of statistical learners as governed operators rather than as authority, and be evaluated only against the strongest matched parent that can be built from known components with identical information. The runtime object is a KnowledgeSpace: atoms carry warrant intervals whose liveness composes exactly under conjunction and alternative by Kleene's strong three-valued logic; revocation reopens exactly the dependents whose liveness changed; authority composes by lattice meet, so nothing internal can promote itself; and every acquisition enters through a registered channel, with feedback never becoming warrant. One persistent instance was built along a roadmap of thirteen milestones, from a bounded microworld language (134 of 134 protected utterances interpreted exactly), dialogue, continual acquisition and a conversational alpha (42 of 42 scripted steps, 0 integrity incidents), to work transfer, scientific reasoning and governed self-repair, and was then evaluated across a heterogeneous lifetime without reset. Against the matched whole-system parent, a pre-registered study of eight paired lifetimes on fresh per-lifetime protected streams rejected on its primary family, conversations, in 8 of 8 lifetimes (one-sided exact sign test, p = 0.0039), with six pre-registered secondary families also at 8 of 8 but flagged as one coin because their differences do not vary across streams, and ties wherever no revision, identification, role typing or repair is needed; the result was replicated byte-identically on a second host, and an earlier frozen study under a weaker rule had found ten families at 8 of 8 and six ties. Where the parent already suffices (learned organisation at the scale studied; work success without revision) we report it as a result. A local open-weight language model, run as a labelled reference rather than a comparator, answered 237 of the 240 questions that the given facts do not settle, 199 of them truly, from pretraining, while learning the world's lessons unevenly (25 of 48 acquired). Residuals over frontier models, blinded human usefulness and external benchmarks remain undetermined by design of the environment; no novelty, consciousness or human-equivalence claim is made.

## 1. Introduction

Three properties are wanted from a machine that reasons on behalf of people, and current statistical learners deliver at most one of them at a time. The first is competence acquired by learning rather than by hand-writing rules. The second is an evidence state that is explicit: for any answer or action one can ask which observations, lessons, demonstrations or checks warrant it, revoke one of them, and see exactly which later commitments must be reopened. The third is governance: the machine cannot promote its own conjectures to truth, cannot adopt a change to itself without an external decision, and cannot act beyond the authority it was given. Foundation models supply the first property at scale [1] and are the strongest existing parent for language, but their evidence state is implicit, so the second and third properties must be bolted on from outside.

Machine epistemics is the programme of building the second and third properties into the substrate and then learning the first on top of them. Its working thesis, recorded at the start of the build, is that the machine should learn methods of doing, not imitate a transformer; that the strengths of language models should be absorbed where they earn their keep, as operators whose outputs enter with an unknown warrant and a score outside the evidence lattice; and that whatever non-human form of intelligence results should evolve under an external constitution that owns checking, authority, metering and commitment [2]. The programme is assimilation-first: every mechanism names the prior work that already owns it, and a mechanism is reported as parent-owned unless a pre-registered test rejects that reading. The parents are old and mostly exact: assumption-based truth maintenance [3, 4], provenance semirings [5], Kleene's three-valued logic [6] with Belnap's information reading [7], rough-set lower and upper approximations [8], lattice information flow [9, 10], personalised PageRank [11], Kemeny–Snell lumpability [12], Kleene algebra with tests [13], version spaces [14] and Gold-style identification limits [15]. What the programme adds is the coupling of these parents into one executable object with exact checkers and planted mutants, and a discipline for measuring whether the coupling produces anything the parents do not.

This paper reports the whole build as one artifact and one evaluation: the runtime and the theorems that fix its semantics, the milestones that grew one persistent instance from a bounded language to self-repair, the matched-comparison methodology without which the results are meaningless, the results with their pre-registered terminals including the families where the parent is sufficient, the reference arm, the two loops that ran beside the build (a ledger applying the machine's own discipline to its builders, and a theory loop that returned runtime defects), and limitations that are structural rather than incidental.

Three claims are not made anywhere below. We do not claim a residual over a frontier foundation-model agent; no such matched parent can be built in an environment without network access or model weights, and the reference arm is labelled as unbound. We do not claim human usefulness; the blinded rating protocol is frozen but was not run. We do not claim novelty of any mechanism; every registry row that names a parent is barred from any novelty statement, and the general-novelty terminal of every receipt reads not established.

## 2. The KnowledgeSpace and its runtime

### 2.1 Warrant intervals and three-valued liveness

A KnowledgeSpace is a finite typed hypergraph of atoms and relations over an evidence universe E. Each atom carries a warrant interval ⟦L, U⟧: L is the antichain of exhibited sufficient evidence sets, U the antichain of every evidence set that could still suffice, and L ≤ U in the semiring order of antichains [5]. A profile is certified when L = U, partial when U = {∅}. Under a revoked set R ⊆ E an atom is LIVE when some exhibited warrant avoids R, DEAD when no possible warrant avoids R, and UNKNOWN otherwise. Conjunction ⊗ and alternative ⊕ act coordinate-wise on the two antichains.

The central semantic fact is that liveness is an exact homomorphism from (⊕, ⊗) on intervals to Kleene's strong (∨, ∧) on {LIVE, UNKNOWN, DEAD} (obligation KS-T21) [16]. Certified intervals never read UNKNOWN, and refinement (a certificate that raises L or lowers U) can move UNKNOWN to LIVE or DEAD but never flip LIVE and DEAD. A single completeness bit does not compose: the counterexample that forced the interval object is recorded as the first row of the self-application ledger (Section 7). The interval is Pawlak's lower and upper approximation [8] applied to the ATMS label [3]; the connectives are Kleene's [6]; nothing here is new mathematics. The checker enumerates every valid interval over three evidence ids (168 intervals) and every revocation, 225,792 homomorphism checks, and two planted mutants that read UNKNOWN as LIVE or as DEAD are caught.

Two consequences carry through the whole build. A FEEDBACK-admitted atom carries the certified-zero interval and is DEAD under every R, so reward or preference signals never enable anything (KS-T18); this is proof-carrying admission [17] with the empty ATMS label. A summary over exported constituents has warrant Λ_corr ⊗ ⨂ Λ(x): it is LIVE only when every export is LIVE, and the majority aggregation is the planted mutant (KS-T23).

### 2.2 Composition, authority and scope

Composing components under a bridge or operator yields warrant ⊗, authority meet and scope intersection (KS-T20). Authority is a product lattice of named non-negative ranks with an undeclared coordinate read as zero, and meet is the coordinate-wise minimum, so composition never raises any coordinate. This is Biba's low-water-mark [10] and Denning's lattice flow [9]. Its consequence for governance is exact: an atom produced by any internal operator has commit rank zero, and no chain of internal operations produces commit authority; only an external action receipt can (theory batch 1, T1). The same rule makes discourse safe: a record that a speaker said p has speaker authority only, ten speakers repeating p compose to the same bottom, and the machine's own claim about p becomes LIVE only through a bridge from a warranting channel (batch 2, B1). Scope is a context set with a half-open epoch; supersession is a revocation family indexed by time, and its reopening is exactly the reopening report of the ended evidence (batch 2, B5).

### 2.3 Revocation and reopening

Revoking evidence changes liveness only for atoms whose intervals cite it. The impact cone is the least dependency-closed superset of the changed set (KS-T09, Knaster–Tarski). The reopening report partitions the cone into REOPEN (liveness changed), RECHECK (a dependent that kept an alternative live warrant, so only derived content must be re-evaluated) and UNAFFECTED (KS-T22); an irrelevant revocation is a no-op. This is what an ATMS label update gives once the label is an antichain [3, 4], and the DRed over-deletion set intersected with the changed-derivability set of incremental materialisation [18, 19]. Reinstatement restores the space exactly; relearning admits a new evidence id with a lineage link so that behaviour is equal but the lifecycle differs (batch 2, B6). Structural changes made by a governed Jump are stamped with the Jump certificate, and rollback is revocation plus quarantine, never deletion (batch 1, T4).

### 2.4 Navigation, extraction and the four-valued outcome

Navigation is a restart walk over the warranted matrix, a = α s + (1 − α) Pᵀ a, whose denominators are frozen on the registered structure and never renormalised, so that revocation is exact-share pruning at the fixed point (KS-T04, KS-T04b, KS-T04c) [11]. The outcome of a query is four-valued: FOUND, GAP (target absent or not reached within budget), OBSTRUCTION (only when the ungated ceiling walker also fails) and CANNOT_CHECK (zero budget or an unresolved UNKNOWN on the path) (KS-T19). Timeout alone is a gap, never an obstruction; this is the completeness-threshold discipline of bounded model checking [20, 21] with Bruns–Godefroid's third value [22]. The budget bracket that makes a FOUND within budget sound for the fixed point is theorem T2 of batch 1; its proof surfaced that the runtime iterated from the wrong start vector, the first of the theory-reported defects counted in Section 8.

### 2.5 Acquisition channels and the learner contract

Every admission names its channel: INSTRUCTION, DEMONSTRATION, INTERACTION, EXPERIMENTATION, FEEDBACK or EXACT_CHECKER. A gap of kind target-absent is closed only through the learning channels, a learner proposal is admitted only when the version space agrees on the registered query family, and the admitted atom's warrant is the per-input antichain of minimal agreeing example sets (batches 2 and 3, B2, B3, C6) [14]. Revoking one example therefore reopens exactly the inputs whose every agreement set contained it. Per-channel acquisition bounds on the registered finite classes (teaching dimension, membership-query depth, exact identification from random aligned pairs) are tabulated and parent-owned [23, 24]; a channel that identifies below its bound has received information from outside the channel, which is the laundering detector used in the information accounting of Section 4.

### 2.6 Constitution and self-model

The organisation of the space is data under an external constitution 𝔠 = (Check, Authority, Meter, Commit). The self-model is a fibre whose atoms are observations about the machine's own runs, with authority {self_model: 1} and scope self; an edge with a self-model tail may only have self-model heads; a proposal is ⟦0, U⟧ until an external commit receipt adopts it; and a self-model record cannot close an object-level claim because its scope does not meet the task scope (batch 5, E1). Adoption is a stamped double-pushout rewrite whose rollback is hash-exact including compiled caches (batch 5, E6) [25, 26]; proposals are metered so that the meta-level loop cannot livelock (batch 1, T8; batch 5, E7) [27]. The Gödel-machine reading [28] is kept honest by construction: any proof about the machine is a certificate produced outside the self-model, never by it.

### 2.7 What the runtime is

The reference implementation is the ORION Cognitive Machine (OCM), a Python package with an event-sourced ledger, a single reducer as the only writer, replay-exact persistence and restart, and an obligation registry of 123 rows across twelve registries (87 proved, proved on a finite fragment or proved with a clause, 25 finite calibrations, 3 parent-owned, 6 open, 2 undetermined) each with a checker, a planted mutant and a named parent. Twenty-three event families cover admission through revocation, learning, Jump adoption, action receipts and snapshots. The historical results of the predecessor repository, eight controlled terminals, replay under the unified runtime without any upgrade of their authority.

### 3.1 Language (M3 to M6)

The language stack is a construction grammar over a bounded microworld with a given vocabulary: a lexicon with morphology and live-exception overrides, phrase constructions built bottom-up, clause constructions that bind phrases, a canonical meaning graph (exact canonical labelling for fragments of at most seven vertices, undetermined beyond, because Weisfeiler–Leman hashing is not canonical [29, 30]), and an interpretation whose candidates each carry their own interval, so that ambiguity is a set of live candidates and collapse is an evidence event, never a score (batch 1, T6). The dialogue workspace adds speaker-indexed commitment records, proposition-indexed correction and supersession, reference resolution over a persistent state, clarification as an information action valued in expected cells on the registered query family (batch 3, C1) [31], and an external commitment gate that commits a surface only if its decoded meaning equals the plan's, every asserted proposition is LIVE with sufficient authority and every marker follows the interval (batch 3, C2) [32]. Continual acquisition runs under five disclosed regimes (explicit lessons, aligned demonstrations, raw corpus, grounded interaction, and curricula over them) with every word, lesson, demonstration and annotation counted. The conversational alpha binds these to a 55-fact bounded world (52 verified, 3 reported from sources) and runs eleven demonstration points from a clean checkout: answer, follow-up with a pronoun, explanation, clarification on consequential ambiguity, honest unknown, an unverified source reported as such, teach a word, reuse it, restart and retain it, revoke its evidence and watch the capability stop, unrelated knowledge intact.

### 3.2 Work (M9), science (M10) and self-repair (M11)

Work is represented as task contracts (allowed and forbidden actions, hidden checker state, budget, authority, rollback), operators with registered roles and lineage, skill capsules with an invariant skeleton and per-domain adapters, and transfer maps whose warrant is the source warrant ⊗ the correspondence warrant; a transfer is refused when the target operator's registered role differs from the source role, whatever the surface similarity. Three self-contained environments with exact oracle state (enterprise support, software operations, data-analysis preflight) each have a version 2 in which the policy table changes so that a stale binding fails the hidden checker. Science is represented as tasks with an estimand, costed and risked experiments and a hidden oracle; observations where corroboration counts distinct sources and replicates never corroborate; hypotheses in symbolic, statistical, causal, simulation, program and formal layers; conclusions that carry the identification assumptions a causal claim needs; and a proof kernel whose kernel warrant and formalisation-correspondence warrant are kept apart. The self-model diagnoses a failure as a distribution over layers D0 to D8 from counterfactual evidence, names the minimum sufficient layer, raises an architecture alarm only under a valid obstruction certificate (every registered lower alternative tried with a LIVE warrant and failed, plus a ceiling witness), and proposes a change in classes C0 to C6, where C6 (constitutional) is a recommendation packet only. Governance runs the challenger in shadow on a frozen snapshot, applies the same hard gates, requires the pre-outcome prediction to be realised on tasks the proposer never saw, adopts only through an external adopter (self-approval has no token), meters adoptions per window, and rolls back exactly with stamped-evidence revocation.

### 3.3 The lifetime (M12)

One persistent instance, one ledger root, shared by the chat session, the work skills, the science ledger and the self-model, was run through seven phases: A language and social (the protected conversation, factual, lesson and negative-transfer suites), B enterprise work, C software work, D data and science, E cross-domain transfer, F revision (revoke a fact's source, retract an observation under three conclusions, revoke a demonstration), and G self-repair (a fault planted on the work machine, undisclosed). Version 2 ran one lifetime per ordering of the phases under three orderings; version 3 ran eight paired lifetimes, each on its own protected stream generated by seeded substitution inside the bounded world; version 4 re-registered the analysis with one primary family and ran on eight fresh streams. The matched whole-system parent combines the language matched parent, the work skill library, the science parent procedures and the two repair parents, with identical manifest, lessons, demonstrations, plans and budgets.

## 4. Matched-comparison methodology

The evaluation discipline was fixed before the first protected outcome was read and then tightened by the theory loop.

**Strongest faithful matched parent.** For every family the comparator is the strongest parent that can be built here from known components with identical knowledge, lessons, corrections, demonstrations and budget. For language it is a retrieval memory over the same 55 facts, a dialogue-state memory with string-level supersession, an in-context lesson table and a template renderer; it has no warrant intervals, no reopening cone, no version space and no gate. For work it is a skill library with name-similarity transfer, a trajectory-replay memory and a fresh-start learner; for science, naive regression, entropy-driven experiment selection and the same pre-registered analysis plan; for repair, parameter search over the router and revocation settings and reflection-retry. A template floor (fact glosses without dialogue state) is reported beside them. Matched means matched discipline as well as matched information: when the first work study's fresh-start parent acquired each domain more cheaply, it turned out to have skipped the withheld acceptance test every other arm pays; the parent was matched and the run relabelled (ledger row S27).

**Pre-registration and freeze before outcome.** Every study has a pre-registration document whose hash is bound into the receipt before any protected outcome is read. Any code change after outcome access relabels the run as development calibration, which carries no claim, and requires a new versioned study on new protected content. This happened at M7 (three defects fixed after the first run; a new hand-authored suite for V2), at M9 (the parent-discipline mismatch), and at M12 (an identity split in the harness, pooled pseudo-replication, and a mislabelled fault; V2 froze the fixes before its outcome).

**Exact tests and their scale.** Paired families are decided by an exact McNemar-style test on discordant pairs with a pre-registered equivalence margin δ = 0.05 on the paired rate difference and two one-sided tests at α = 0.05; a residual requires the one-sided exact test to reject and the difference to exceed δ; equivalence requires both one-sided tests to reject. The minimum n for an inferential terminal is 40 items; below it the family is reported as descriptive with its counts and interval. Theory batch 4 (D1) proved the size of these rules exhaustively over small tables, computed the size inflation of the three hostile practices (stopping when leading, post-hoc exclusion, reading p > 0.05 as equivalence), and showed that equivalence at margin 1/10 on the discordant scale needs at least 76 discordant pairs, so every equivalence verdict now names its scale [33, 34]. The predecessor terminal of zero discordant pairs in 540 was relabelled from parent-sufficient to a rate-difference equivalence at margin 7/1000 (row S28).

**Unit of inference.** Within one lifetime, tasks are dependent through shared state. Batch 6 (F2) proved that pooling the three re-orderings of one frozen stream multiplies the discordant count without adding information (on the M12 row the p-value falls from 1/4 to 1/64 at three replicas with zero between-replica variance) [35, 36], and gave the exact size of an item-level sign test under block dependence at n = 54. The V2 claims are therefore made on the principal ordering only, the conversations family carries an item-exchangeability caveat, and V3 makes the lifetime the unit: eight independent paired lifetimes with distinct stream seeds and an exact sign test over lifetime differences, which cannot reject below five pairs and has power 0.81 at eight pairs when the per-lifetime win probability is 0.9. Batch 7 (G8) then showed that V3's rule was under-specified (no primary family, an unbounded family count, and an implemented two-sided unanimous test with power 0.43 rather than the one-sided test of the design), so V4 re-registered the analysis before running on fresh streams: one primary family at α = 0.05 with the one-sided rule that rejects at 7 or more of 8 (size 9/256), six secondary families at α/6 (which reject only at 8 of 8), every other family descriptive, a collapsed-one-coin flag for any family whose eight differences are identical, and a decision that rests on the primary family alone (row S37).

**Kill gates.** A run is invalid on any hit of: protected exposure, external input or output, identity reset or split (the ledger chain head recorded at each phase boundary must be a prefix of the later chain), a LIVE commitment resting on revoked evidence after the revision phase, a self-change touching a protected target, a missing phase outcome, an undetermined verdict counted as success, or a stream leak. All gates read zero in every protected run reported here.

**Hostile mutations.** Each study carries planted mutants that an automated check must catch, among them contamination of a lesson set by a protected utterance, a comparator denied annotations the machine received, gold surfaced through a forbidden channel, a marker hard-coded rather than following state, post-hoc item removal, and stopping when the machine leads.

**Information and resource accounting.** Every arm's receipt records knowledge facts (55 for every language arm), lessons, statements, interaction turns, protected exposure (0 for every arm), persistent bytes, ledger events, wall time and peak memory; external input and output is 0 for every arm.

**Replication.** The same frozen code and pre-registration were run in a fresh Python 3.11 environment on a second host; the deterministic block of the result must be byte-identical. Wall time and memory are excluded from the comparison by construction.

## 5. Results

### 5.1 Core and runtime (M1, M2)

The core registry closed with 25 of 30 obligations proved, 2 finite calibrations (the two-direction hub witness and the old-versus-new equivalence), 2 parent-owned rows, and 1 open row (lifecycle-safe consolidation, KS-T12, later given an exact clause by batch 7). Revocation checks ran 360 cases each of prune equality, reinstatement exactness, reopening partition and retraction monotonicity on random spaces. The unified runtime replayed all eight historical terminals of the predecessor without upgrading any: the whole-solve comparator outcome stays parent-sufficient (navigation-only 38 of 50 on the development split, not significant against a random-walk-with-restart parent at 32 of 50 and a case-based parent at 34 of 50, n = 50), and the formal-proof channel stays parent-sufficient. The extraction revival study changed the surprise background from a uniform to a propagated model and raised found-by-navigation from 38 to 47 of 50 on the development split with the hub theorem, translator invariance and no-atom-lost guards all held; the default was not changed pending a protected re-run, and the receipt's named lever (a seed-count-conditioned background) was proved a no-op by linearity (row S7; batch 4, D4). A sparse navigation path agrees with the exact solver to 10⁻¹⁴ and handles 10⁴ atoms in 0.66 s.

### 5.2 Language understanding, dialogue and acquisition (M3 to M5)

On the microworld (240 utterances, 106 development, 134 protected, held-out lexemes absent from development by construction), the frozen system interpreted 134 of 134 protected utterances with the exact meaning and the correct construction, mean role F1 1.0, across five families (negation 32, passive 28, transitive 31, transitive with adjective 26, yes/no 17). The transitive construction was learned from one demonstration in a hypothesis class of six; the ambiguity suite of three items had full candidate recall and no false collapse; six paraphrase pairs were meaning-equivalent; revoking the demonstration reopened the transitive construction and left the passive intact. The first receipt had read 0 of 26 on the adjective family, because the learned transitive pattern was flat; recursion of noun phrases as their own constructions was the repair (row S13).

On the dialogue microworld (120 dialogues, 618 turns, 49 protected, 18 restarted midway), 251 of 251 expected acts were committed; clarification was asked 4 times of 4 needed with 0 unnecessary; corrections reopened 9 of 9 dependent answers and recorded 9 of 9 supersessions with 20 of 20 unrelated answers intact; 11 of 11 answers cited their evidence; 7 of 7 contradictions were reported with both sides; 0 of 51 user assertions became machine belief; unique pronouns resolved 7 of 7, ambiguous pronoun candidates recalled 4 of 4, and references after a gap of at least twelve turns resolved 5 of 5.

Acquisition started from a frozen system with three lexemes and the transitive construction removed, which reads 22 of 134 protected and 0 of 97 held-out (Table 2). Explicit lessons (six lessons, one demonstration) restored 134 of 134 and 97 of 97. Aligned demonstrations (seven, plus three taught words) reached 115 of 134 and 78 of 97; the remaining gap is a participle reading of one verb and is disclosed. A raw corpus of 661 words produced 88 form hypotheses and 0 consultable meanings, with semantic gain exactly zero, as Gold's limit predicts for positive text alone [15]. One grounded interaction reached 37 of 134. Of four curricula, the two that include explicit lessons reached 134 and the two that do not reached 115. After the demonstration regime, new gain was 93 of 112 with 0 of 22 old loss and 0 of 22 unrelated change. A subject-object-verb ordering under the English inventory returned an unknown construction rather than a wrong reading, and forcing the English order did not yield correct roles.

Batch 7's intake added two registry rows: the nested-lift clause for Jump improvement (KS-T116, proved with clause) and the paired-lifetime inference rule with a primary family, a bounded secondary count, the collapsed-one-coin flag and the world-true out-of-scope half (KS-T117, finite calibration, discharged by V4). Two batch-7 obligations remain for the next self-model revision: the level of a Jump is a pair of tower and registered class, never a bare integer; and the deconsolidation decision is parent-owned MDL [42] but its exception count must be gated by liveness.

## 9. Limitations

**No matched frontier parent.** The strongest whole-system parent buildable here has no learned language model in it. A residual over a frontier foundation-model agent is undetermined by construction, and the reference arm's information channel is unbound, so its numbers are a reference and not a comparator. This limitation is structural to the environment (no network, no containers, no weights beyond a local 7B model) and is the first item of the revival backlog.

**One bounded world.** Every language stream is a substitution of one bounded world with about 60 lexemes and 7 constructions. The V3 and V4 streams are exchangeable within that world and are not samples of natural language. Coverage of BLiMP and of the Universal Dependencies treebank under the frozen admissibility protocol is 0, and the BabyLM and CHILDES comparisons were not run because their data and terms were not pinned. The environments for work, science and repair are authored by the programme with exact oracle state; five external agent benchmarks, three external science benchmarks, the miniF2F and Lean 4 route and any frontier target are undetermined.

**No human rating.** The blinded rating protocol (seven criteria, three arms plus a labelled external reference, a recorded randomisation seed) is frozen but was not run. Usefulness to a person is not claimed anywhere.

**Small n and the one-coin collapse.** Within one lifetime the work, science, transfer and repair families have n between 4 and 12 and are descriptive. At eight paired lifetimes the sign test rejects only near unanimity, so every rejecting family is one where the machine won every lifetime. In V4 all six secondary families are flagged as collapsed: their differences are identical across streams because the work, science, transfer, revision and repair phases are deterministic functions of the planted design, which the language substitution does not vary. The lifetime-level inference therefore rests on one family, conversations, in both V3 and V4; making the secondary families vary across lifetimes needs per-lifetime variation of the planted faults, revision events and transfer cells, which together with the generator's manifest-licence check (row S38) is the next study and not part of this paper.

**Ablation channel.** The self-repair benchmark's counterfactual channel is provided by the machine's own registered candidate repairs; the historical replay can audit governance but not diagnostic accuracy, for which no counterfactual channel exists on history.

**Parents are the ones the issues named.** A learned failure classifier over traces, an overlap-aware community parent, a Bayesian experimental-design parent with priors over effect sizes, and a fine-tuned or adapter language learner were not built; each is a stronger parent the backlog names, and each would need the same candidate channel and information receipt to be a fair comparison.

**Novelty is not established.** Every core mechanism is parent-owned or parent-product-owned in the subtraction tables. The contribution claimed is the executable coupling, the checkers and mutants, the discipline, and the measured residuals over the matched parent buildable here.

## Methods summary

The reference implementation is a Python package with a src layout, exact rational arithmetic in the core checkers, a JSON event ledger with SHA-256 content ids and a single reducer, and receipt tools that bind every file a milestone depends on by hash and verify the chain in continuous integration. Studies ran on two hosts (a Linux workstation as principal and a laptop as replica); the local reference model ran on the laptop GPU through Ollama with temperature 0 and a fixed seed. Statistical tests are exact binomial computations implemented in the evaluation module and reproduced by the theory batches' checkers. No test in this paper uses an asymptotic approximation. Language-model assistance was used for drafting and editing the prose of this manuscript under the receipt-bound claim discipline described above; every number was checked against its receipt by the claim-verification script listed under Data and code availability, and the authors are accountable for every statement.
