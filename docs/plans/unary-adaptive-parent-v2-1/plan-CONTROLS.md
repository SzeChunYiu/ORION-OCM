## Meaningful controls before integration

1. Clean shared-route equivalence: same authored task/rule/library yields the same verified
   semantic status, exact selected rule, structural binding, cover, and recipe branch in both arms.
   Every/no and existential polarity, distinct witnesses, inconsistent premises, no-match fallback,
   Boolean substitution, and all four query statuses retain current semantics.
2. Prepare once and attempt the rule before fallback; trap any complete/solve before learned use.
   A warm nested-expression call must retain real cache-key visits and binding work while reducing
   mask compilation where reused. A changed query must get a fresh task-bound Prepared.
   Replay of an old Prepared, changed registry, or counter reset hiding earlier work must fail.
3. Acquisition genuinely calls the same miner: duplicate semantic supports/renamings do not
   become two supports; malformed training results cannot be dropped to make a smaller batch succeed.
   Authored fixtures may exercise small inputs; assay admission separately requires the fixed 32/16.
   Two-premise rule acquisition is the assay path; no fabricated three-premise acquisition claim.
4. Development scoring has paired positive/zero/negative-benefit examples and no-match fallback;
   verify exact query-counter deltas rather than cumulative preparation counts.
   Test each ranking tie level, max-eight truncation, rule_id routing, and empty/no-benefit results.
   Candidate errors invalidate selection and retain observations; never delete the failing candidate.
   Tampered ranking/library receipt, non-pool rule, altered source/input, and authored-selected alias refuse.
   Test matching/canonicalization/checker overhead retention even when excluded from the ranking score.
5. Parent and OCM acquire independently from identical authored inputs: same pool/ranking/library
   identities, distinct physical acquisition receipts. A deliberate mismatch blocks paired use.
   No caller-supplied method body can bypass mining, measured selection, and schema checking.
6. Clean durable admission/use has a paired interrupted write, missing payload, altered hash/CAS,
   orphan USE_PREPARE, duplicate completion, and source-drift control. Cold restore refuses incomplete
   issuance/use and never reports zero work as though a failed attempt had not occurred.
   Parent atomic batch semantics are checked on their own; no artificial second ledger is required.
7. Exact indexed use telemetry includes built/visited entries, probes, candidate order, rejected
   eligibility, structural attempts, applied recipe, replaced branch, verifier work, and fallback.
   Cache hits must remain bound to current sources/library/dependency revisions.
   Forged method ID, changed cover/result/request, stale binding and unavailable checker refuse.
8. Remove an actually used premise and invalidate that precise old binding; allow a legitimate
   alternate match. Restore original bytes and require a fresh check. Do not assert every deletion
   must stop all rule use, or count restored/presentation variants as independent discoveries.
9. Withdraw/reinstate schema, discovery and utility separately. Match both adapters' role effects.
   Schema withdrawal disables recipes but leaves the independent exact fallback available.
   Discovery withdrawal changes attribution; utility withdrawal changes eligibility, not validity.
   UNKNOWN liveness cannot masquerade as LIVE; current proof and historical use are distinct.
10. Actual authored A/B/C lifetimes use one portable, explicitly bound Python3.11.14 profile.
    A mines/selects/persists/exits. B receives no training, restores then uses actual recipes.
    C starts from an A snapshot for task interventions; a separate cold audit reads B's prior-use
    history. Role withdrawal and reinstatement require mutation/exits followed by fresh solve processes.
    Verify exit/reap/source/import/profile records, absent implicit acquisition, empty restart caches,
    and OCM's counted fixed callback rebind versus the conventional direct dispatcher.
11. Parent and child profile mismatches, startup failure, timeout, post-child source failure,
    matcher refusal, result verification failure and journal failure preserve known work/raw evidence.
    Unobserved partial counters are UNAVAILABLE, not zero. Keep the actual observed exit code.

results/postchecks; preserve its receipt outside its own self-hashed inner payload.

Do not sum process A, acquisition, selector, schema, store, and journal nested spans as independent costs.

it is not complete recorder cost. A later full assay controller must close that scope.

Keep actual preparation, full prepared-byte decoding/hash/validation, mask and key traversal,
matching, canonicalization, schema verification, training semantic-key construction, development
trials, index/revision work, persistence/fsync, restore, source scans and refused attempts.
Logical counters omit Python hashing/equality/allocation and operating-system work; total cost cannot
be inferred from constraints_checked alone. No per-query source scan is described as constant work.
Observe each adapter's actual custody implementation instead of imposing OCM scans on its parent.

Copy/generation/orchestration are recorded once physically, with root's declared comparison allocation.
Exact parent may skip acquisition and rule machinery; that saved work is legitimate.
A+B64 amortization and C/role totals remain separate. No imaginary future queries or unobserved break-even.
