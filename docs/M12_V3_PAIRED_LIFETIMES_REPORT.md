## 1. Why V3

V2 had one lifetime per ordering on identical task streams, so its only inferential family was
turn-level. V3 makes the *lifetime* the unit: eight paired (OCM, whole-system parent) lifetimes,
each on its own protected stream generated by seeded substitution inside the bounded world
(agents ↔ agents, objects ↔ objects, regular ↔ regular and irregular ↔ irregular verbs, verified
facts ↔ verified facts of the same relation, lesson words → nonce words absent from lexicon and
manifest; expected patterns rewritten with the same maps; leak check 8/8 clean). Work and science
ids are disjoint per lifetime; the ordering cycles through the three V2 orderings.

## 5. What V3 does and does not show

* It shows a lifetime-level residual over the matched parent on language, causal identification,
  communication, transfer, revision integrity and self-repair, replicated, under the pre-registered
  sign test at n = 8 lifetimes — the smallest n at which the test can reject, so every rejecting family
  is one where OCM beat the parent in all eight lifetimes.
* Work success (B/C), experiment selection, the shared pre-registered analysis and the kernel are
  ties: the parent is sufficient wherever no revision, identification, role typing or repair is needed.
* The streams are substitutions of one bounded world; they are exchangeable within the world, not
  samples of natural language. Human rating and frontier parents remain CANNOT_CHECK.
