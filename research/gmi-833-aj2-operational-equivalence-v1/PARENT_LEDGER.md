# AJ2 strongest-parent / ownership ledger

AJ2 is an integration and reduction result; it does not rename established equivalence theory as GMI novelty.

- **Operational / general probabilistic theories** own the treatment of preparations/states and effects/tests by operational roles and outcome statistics. Chiribella–D'Ariano–Perinotti are a canonical operational-probabilistic parent lane.
- **Programming-language and process-calculus semantics** own observational/contextual equivalence as indistinguishability by a declared class of observations/contexts.
- **Labelled transition systems and bisimulation** own transition-matching behavioural equivalences; coincidence with AJ2's test equivalence requires additional assumptions on the test language/contexts.
- **Coalgebra** owns broad behavioural-equivalence and quotient constructions for state-based systems (e.g. Rutten's universal-coalgebra programme).
- **Trace equivalence** is a distinct parent based on observable traces and is neither silently identified with nor ordered against AJ2 without a registered system/context theorem.

AJ2 residual: the explicit `I->A` / `A->I` reduction from the AJ1 process frame, a finite exact response-quotient microscope, independent quotient reconstruction, and a hostile showing that enlarging the admissible test family can refine the quotient.
