# AJ4 strongest-parent / ownership ledger

AJ4 does not claim machine organization, feedback, automata or circuits as new mathematics.

- **Katis, Sabadini & Walters, _Feedback, trace and fixed-point semantics_ (RAIRO 36(2), 2002, DOI 10.1051/ita:2002009)**: category-with-feedback / Circ free-feedback construction; strongest structural parent for adding stateful feedback to a monoidal process base.
- **Mealy/Moore finite-state machine theory**: finite step maps with internal state and input/output behaviour are classical automata objects. Contemporary categorical treatments recover ordinary DFAs/Moore/Mealy machines as special cases rather than inventing them anew.
- **Sequential/digital circuit semantics**: acyclic gate wiring gives combinational circuits; delay-guarded feedback supplies sequential state. This is standard circuit theory and categorical circuit semantics.
- **Coalgebra / labelled transition systems**: state-based recurrent dynamics and behavioural equivalence are parent frameworks; AJ4's operational quotient does not supersede their stronger equivalence results.
- **Register/counter machines and structural operational semantics**: small-step `(pc, registers)` transition organizations are classical. Existing #868 retains ownership of the registered G0 counter/register embedding.

AJ4 residual: placement of those parents above AJ1–AJ3's operational bottom, exact finite `M_B(O,S)` enumeration/quotienting at one registered scope, explicit substrate charging for delay, and a machine-checkable separation of process possibility / organization expressibility / realizability / reachability / selection.
