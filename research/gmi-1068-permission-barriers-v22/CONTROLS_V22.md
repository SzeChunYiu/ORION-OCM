# V22 falsifiers and repaired interpretations

Read [CORE.md](CORE.md). These are mathematical countermodels with separately checked

## V4.1 — conjunction, alternatives and distinct minimality criteria

A two-edge path requiring a then b needs both permissions. From empty baseline,
no singleton enables it; {a,b} does. Restricting search to one-step additions
would miss a genuine compound enabling condition. Retain combined interventions.
Two alternative target histories requiring {a} and {b} give two minimal enablers;
blocking both requires {a,b}. A unique missing condition is not determined.

Supports {a} and {b,c} give inclusion-minimal enablers of cardinalities1 and2.
Assign positive prices price(a)=3 and price(b)=price(c)=1. The smaller-cardinality
choice costs more. Retain every inclusion-minimal answer unless a separately
declared optimization criterion is being applied.

For supports {a},{b}, deletion B={a} has a private witness for its sole element
but is not a blocker: {b} survives. Empty B vacuously has private witnesses but
also fails to block. Require both the hitting condition and private witnesses.
Conversely one support {a,b} makes either singleton a minimal blocker; deleting
both is a blocker but has no private witness for either element and is not minimal.

## V4.2 — available permissions, identity and partiality

Two models with empty baseline image may require a versus b to reach the same
value. The common image identifies neither missing permission. Preserve the
actual requirements and declared intervention semantics.
Two different histories with the same target value and different supports give
separate permission alternatives. Deduplicating by value loses that distinction.
Grouping equal supports preserves the Boolean answer only if history backpointers
are retained wherever witness identity is claimed.

If a target-looking value lies outside original P, outside E, or on a failed
physical word, it is not an eligible witness. Keep ILLEGAL, UNDEFINED, physical
absence and true target failure separate. Defined values on illegal histories
are permitted ambient data but cannot leak into the target image.
A support {b} unavailable in U={a} cannot become enabled by adding subsets of U.
It likewise must not force a deletion from an already unavailable path.

A return-to-start loop can be the sole evaluated target history when E admits
only words of length2. An endpoint/BFS representative choosing the empty word
then erases that witness. Preserve raw history-sensitive evaluation; do not
infer a complete target-support family from terminal-state reachability alone.

## V4.3 — monotonicity premise failures and repairs

Suppose the old target history costs1 under capacity1. Enabling a new permission
adds mandatory overhead1 to every history. Its actual total becomes2 and the
old witness becomes infeasible. This family violates fixed-semantics optionality.
Charge overhead in the changed resource/context model, rather than applying the
gate theorem to it. No parent theorem has been contradicted.

The condition "a enabled and b absent" succeeds at {a} but fails at {a,b}.
No positive-support containment representation can describe it: such a
representation is monotone by subset transitivity. An explicitly intervention-
indexed family of contexts can represent this negative permission condition;
it must relinquish the positive-gating monotonicity conclusion.

An already successful baseline has empty minimal addition. A listed extra
permission that preserves its success is not evidence of repairing impossibility.
An empty target-witness family instead permits no enabling addition at all.
An empty-support target witness makes every deletion fail. Each needs its own
valid output. Finite examples do not establish infinite minimal-set existence;
the tail-family countermodel is proved in BLOCKERS_V22, independently of tests.

## V4.4 — executable and claim boundaries

Validate complete inputs even when an early transition fails, selection is empty,
or the target is empty: all suffix actions, annotations, history dimensions,
unused transitions, targets and intervention indices remain part of the contract.
Reject Boolean/integer aliases, duplicate set members/raw histories and invalid
absent-edge annotations; distinguish an empty valid result from missing evidence.

The original AF optional-extension statement supplies the correct discipline.
Its BFS-selected profile helper is not a full history-support enumerator. AF's
barrier-label validator is not a contradiction prover. Permission enabling here
is relative to declared dynamics, requirements, evaluation and target; none of
these countermodels establishes causal identification or resolves every barrier.
