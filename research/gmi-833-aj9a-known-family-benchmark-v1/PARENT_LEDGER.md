# AJ9a known-family benchmark — strongest-parent ledger

The benchmark is a **post-hoc adjudication registry**, not a claim that GMI invented these machine-intelligence families or that the list is ontologically complete.

## Registered anchors

- **K01 neural/feed-forward** — McCulloch & Pitts (1943), *A Logical Calculus of the Ideas Immanent in Nervous Activity*, DOI `10.1007/BF02478259`; Rumelhart, Hinton & Williams (1986), *Learning representations by back-propagating errors*, Nature; LeCun, Bottou, Bengio & Haffner (1998), *Gradient-Based Learning Applied to Document Recognition*, Proceedings of the IEEE 86(11):2278–2324.
- **K02 recurrent/stateful** — Elman (1990), *Finding Structure in Time*, Cognitive Science 14:179–211, DOI `10.1207/S15516709COG1402_1`; recurrent links are used to provide dynamic memory.
- **K03 local/shared-transform** — LeCun et al. (1998), convolutional/local receptive-field and shared-weight document-recognition systems.
- **K04 attention/dynamic-routing** — Vaswani et al. (2017), *Attention Is All You Need*, arXiv `1706.03762`, plus earlier neural-attention literature. AJ9's fingerprint is deliberately broader than one Transformer implementation.
- **K05 symbolic/rewrite/search** — classical term-rewriting systems, Knuth–Bendix completion and symbolic/state-space AI search. Parent ownership remains with those formal-language/rewrite/search traditions.
- **K06 probabilistic/Bayesian** — Bayesian inference/decision theory; Pearl (1988), *Probabilistic Reasoning in Intelligent Systems*, Morgan Kaufmann, as a canonical AI probabilistic-reasoning anchor.
- **K07 planning/control** — Bellman's dynamic programming/principle of optimality, Markov decision processes and optimal-control literature. The benchmark admits model/lookahead or value-recursive control-equivalent mechanisms rather than one named planner.
- **K08 retrieval/memory** — Hopfield (1982), *Neural networks and physical systems with emergent collective computational abilities*, PNAS 79(8):2554–2558, DOI `10.1073/pnas.79.8.2554`, as a canonical content-addressable-memory anchor; broader associative/content-addressed memory remains parent-owned.
- **K09 evolutionary/population search** — Holland, *Adaptation in Natural and Artificial Systems* (original 1975; MIT Press revised edition 1992), foundational genetic-algorithm/adaptive-systems work; evolutionary computation remains parent-owned.
- **K10 program synthesis** — Alur et al. (2013), *Syntax-Guided Synthesis*: synthesis from a semantic correctness specification plus a grammar-constrained candidate implementation set. Grammar bias is explicitly retained.
- **K11 self-modifying/developmental** — Schmidhuber's Gödel Machines / self-improving program work and the repository's #233 HST/HSG developmental theory. The AJ9 fingerprint is broader than proof-based self-modification and separately scores governance/verification if claimed.

## Critical precedent

Real et al. (ICML 2020), *AutoML-Zero: Evolving Machine Learning Algorithms From Scratch*, PMLR 119:8007–8019, is a direct precedent for low-level, reduced-human-bias search rediscovering two-layer neural networks trained by backpropagation. It is **not** sufficient for AJ9 closure: AJ9 requires a frozen multi-family benchmark, explicit family holdouts, no-smuggling gates, post-hoc adjudication, alternate presentations/searches for flagship families, and failure terminals.

## Ownership rule

Recovery of a registered fingerprint is not theorem novelty for the family itself. GMI novelty, if any, lies in deriving/recovering the family from lower architecture-uncommitted premises, predicting its operating regime, or discovering a candidate outside the frozen known-family taxonomy under AJ10.
