# Parent literature and merged-repo anchors (E8 / #897)

No novelty claim is made for any parent fact below; each is imported, pinned, and
subtracted. The residual is the exact #897 grammar-growth contract at the frozen
`{a,b,c}` scope: conservative monotone extension with cycle rejection, charged
deterministic recursive invention, strict train/held-out separation, charged
held-out burden comparison, and fail-closed controls.

## External parents

- **#233 HST-T04** (prefix-coded Levin allocation): shortening a target encoding
  by `Δ` multiplies allocation weight by `2^Δ`. Motivates why symbol-level
  savings compound under allocation; here the effect is *measured exactly* as an
  enumerated-index burden (T3) rather than assumed.
- **#233 HST-T05** (lifecycle macro acquisition): beneficial iff future reuse
  savings exceed build + maintenance + expected revision cost. Instantiated
  exactly as `H_eff·Δ > K` (T5) with integer arithmetic on the frozen horizon.
- **DreamCoder** (Ellis et al., wake/sleep library learning): abstraction
  admission from compression of a success corpus; recursive reuse of learned
  abstractions. The registered INV-1 rule is the deterministic, fully charged,
  exact-integer instance of that idea.
- **Stitch** (corpus-guided library compression) and the MDL/refactoring family:
  reusable abstractions are those that compress the corpus. The charged
  `o(b−1) > b+κ` criterion is the strict-positivity form with maintenance.
- **Levin search / breadth-by-description-length enumeration**: the canonical
  bias program-length-first enumeration used for discovery burden.

## Merged-repository parents (pinned by blob in `MANIFEST_V1.json`)

- `research/g2-utility-gated-parents-v2` (`UTILITY_GATED_LIBRARY_BEATS_PRIMITIVE`,
  predecessor `HARMFUL_TRANSFER_LIMIT`): on the polynomial grammar, frequency/
  compression-ranked macro admission can *harm* held-out search, and disjoint
  utility gating is what beat the primitive baseline. This child's admission
  rule is fixed by #897 as charged corpus compression; the held-out test
  therefore measures that registered rule, and the H− control plus the null
  preserve the parents' warning rather than assuming transfer.
- `research/g2-acquisition-economics-v1`
  (`CHEAP_SEARCH_AWARE_SELECTION_REPRODUCES_THE_TOURNAMENT_CHOICE`):
  `COMPRESSION_PER_TOKEN`/`MDL_COMPRESSION` selectors failed the utility argmax
  on the polynomial grammar. Recorded as the standing risk for compression-based
  admission; the registered scope here (exact enumeration, charged costs,
  combination-scale held-outs) is where compression admission is tested, and the
  negative control + null quantify where it does not help.
- `research/h1-amortized-rewrite-v2`
  (`LIBRARY_ACQUISITION_EXCEEDS_LATER_SAVINGS`) and
  `research/h1-amortized-acquisition-v1`
  (`NO_AMORTIZED_ACQUISITION_LATER_NOT_CHEAPER`): library acquisition must be
  charged against later savings, and widening the token alphabet inflates
  enumeration volume. Both are load-bearing here: every verdict charges
  `K_total`, and the H− regression is the exact widened-frontier effect (T4).
- `research/gmi-833-g0-grammar-bias-v1` and
  `research/gmi-833-g0-cost-privilege-v1`: description-length and cost are
  grammar-relative; grammar growth is a bias operation. Growth here is
  registered as conservative and charged, with no unbiased-growth claim.

## Position of the residual

None of the parents provides: (i) an exact conservative-extension certificate
with fail-closed cycle rejection at a frozen token grammar; (ii) deterministic
charged admission with definition+maintenance arithmetic and a syntax-
independent tie-break; (iii) a two-generation recursive witness with an
independent-oracle recompute; or (iv) an exact charged held-out burden
comparison with a frozen-before-invention split, a preserved negative control,
and an equal-size random-admission null. That bundle is this package's
contribution, at its explicitly finite scope.
