## 2. External parents, with citations

Model-free reinforcement learning, temporal-difference value estimation and
reward-frequency propensities are not novel here and are not claimed to be.
Neither is the descriptor construction, the cell partition, or the charged-cost
model.

- R. S. Sutton (1988), "Learning to predict by the methods of temporal
  differences", *Machine Learning* 3(1):9–44. DOI `10.1007/BF00115009`. The
  value estimate updated by experienced outcome, which is the mechanism this
  package reads out as accumulated outcome feedback at a state.
- R. S. Sutton and A. G. Barto (2018), *Reinforcement Learning: An
  Introduction*, 2nd ed., MIT Press. Tabular value estimation and the
  model-free / model-based distinction: a stored value per state, updated from
  experienced reward, with no model of the environment's dynamics. The
  protected interface of this package is a boolean threshold on exactly that
  stored value.
- C. J. C. H. Watkins and P. Dayan (1992), "Q-learning", *Machine Learning*
  8(3–4):279–292. DOI `10.1007/BF00992698`. Model-free control by experienced
  outcome; the state-action value updated from received reward, no dynamics
  model.
- J. G. Kemeny and J. L. Snell (1960), *Finite Markov Chains*, Van Nostrand.
  The occupation/reward-frequency average of a finite state space: the exact
  form of the registered label `2 * P(s) >= N(s)`.
- R. Herrnstein (1961), "Relative and absolute strength of response as a
  function of frequency of reinforcement", *Journal of the Experimental
  Analysis of Behavior* 4(3):267–272. DOI `10.1901/jeab.1961.4-267`. The
  matching law: response propensity tracks experienced reinforcement
  frequency — the behavioural parent of the reward-propensity readout.
- D. E. Knuth (1997), *The Art of Computer Programming, Vol. 2*, 3rd ed.,
  Addison-Wesley. The multiplicative-hash permutation used as the registered
  presentation lever.
- G. Salton, A. Wong and C. S. Yang (1975), "A vector space model for
  automatic indexing", *Communications of the ACM* 18(11):613–620.
  DOI `10.1145/361219.361220`. Descriptor-term indexing over a vocabulary; the
  experience table is the same construction on a word list.

## 3. What is NOT claimed novel

- No model-free reinforcement-learning method, value estimator or propensity
  rule here is new.
- "Short contexts are followed by vowels at a rate above one half" is a
  property of the corpus, not a discovery.
- The readout language, the recovery procedure, the post-hoc classifier, the
  charged-cost model and the eleven-coordinate ledger are the parents' forms,
  re-implemented here rather than imported.

## 4. The residual contribution of this tranche

One thing, methodological and small: **the family-blind recovery of an
accumulated-outcome-feedback (reward-propensity) readout as the winner of a
registered recovery over a closed, pre-outcome language that includes the
already-closed sibling rows' mechanism readouts, at real scale, with a matched
negative control that preserves the received-outcome marginal while destroying
every cell's alignment, a matched feedback-shuffle design null, and a
matched-presentation control** — and the demonstration that the recovered class
`REWARD_PROPENSITY_ACCUMULATION` is distinct from the siblings'
(`STORED_EXEMPLAR_MEMBERSHIP`, `CUE_ASSOCIATION_FANOUT`,
`THRESHOLD_CONJUNCTION`) on the same source. The neighbouring Section-H rows
are therefore separated by their readouts, not by relabeling: this package's
winner reads a value accumulated from experienced outcomes, which no sibling's
winner does.
