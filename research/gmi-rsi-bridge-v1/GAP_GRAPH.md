# Recursive GMI ↔ RSI gap graph

> **Ledger rule:** recursion stops only at a registered terminal: theorem/formal reduction, protocol, counterexample, integration boundary, inherited `CANNOT_CHECK`, or explicit empirical nonclaim. A terminal negative is closure of the *ledger obligation*, not evidence for the positive phenomenon.

Every root is recursively decomposed into six atomic checks: `definition → mechanism → observable → test → counterexample → boundary`. The machine-readable source of truth is `CLAIM_LEDGER.json`.

| Root | Gap | Terminal | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| `RSI-D0` | Self boundary | `CLOSED_DEFINITION` | The modifiable self is an explicit registered modification surface Σ; the external constitution C is outside agent self-authorization. |
| `RSI-D1` | Development operator | `CLOSED_FORMAL_REDUCTION` | D is the improvement-generating process: diagnosis, proposal/search, experiment, challenger construction, evaluation orchestration, migration and successor production. Recursive evolvability requires D itself to be addressable by a verified self-intervention. |
| `RSI-D2` | Meta-identifiability | `INHERITED_CANNOT_CHECK` | Meta-observability Ω_D is defined, but ORION G6 lacks the raw disjoint intervention traces needed to estimate it on real self-change incidents. The bridge inherits that negative rather than fabricating labels. |
| `RSI-D3` | Meta-locality | `INHERITED_CANNOT_CHECK` | Meta-locality κ_D is defined as the affected closure fraction for interventions on D, but real self-change κ remains unmeasured in G6. |
| `RSI-D4` | Meta-searchability | `INHERITED_CANNOT_CHECK` | Meta-searchability χ_D is defined over admissible D-repairs, but G6 has no calibrated repair posterior; catalogue size is not χ. |
| `RSI-D5` | Metaproductivity | `CLOSED_PROTOCOL` | Immediate task quality is separated from future descendant-production value. Primary evidence is the full resource/performance descendant profile; a scalar synthetic μ is allowed only under a preregistered utility. |
| `RSI-D6` | Causal attribution | `CLOSED_PROTOCOL` | R3 requires same-root, same-ecology, resource-matched mutable-D versus frozen-D counterfactuals, with hidden human intervention excluded. |
| `RSI-D7` | Evaluator integrity | `CLOSED_PROTOCOL` | A self-improving internal evaluator cannot certify its own success. If evaluator proxies mutate, R3/R4 evidence requires a frozen external/shadow evaluation channel under C. |
| `RSI-D8` | Heredity | `INHERITED_CANNOT_CHECK` | R4 requires an earned D change to persist into at least one later registered descendant. The bridge supplies the criterion, while current G7 only earns T0/T1 and explicitly leaves governed self-evolution D6 unrun. |
| `RSI-D9` | Stepping stones | `CLOSED_COUNTEREXAMPLE` | Monotonic current-generation selection is not required: the registered descendant metric accepts a temporarily worse intermediate state when later held-out descendants are better. |
| `RSI-D10` | Generality | `OPEN_EMPIRICAL_NONCLAIM` | Finite cross-domain transfer can support scoped transfer only. No finite capsule establishes domain-general improvement competence. R5 therefore remains an explicit empirical nonclaim. |
| `RSI-D11` | Open-endedness | `OPEN_EMPIRICAL_NONCLAIM` | Finite generations cannot establish open-ended improvement. R5/open-ended RSI is not awarded by this classifier regardless of finite replication count. |
| `RSI-D12` | Resource law | `CLOSED_PROTOCOL` | All causal RSI comparisons carry a resource vector for compute, data, evaluator effort and human engineering. A scalar cost is synthetic-assay-only; GMI resource profiles remain primary. |
| `RSI-D13` | Safety and identity boundary | `CLOSED_INTEGRATION` | Machine state, self-model and authority remain distinct. D may be mutable, while adoption authority and protected invariants remain governed by external constitution C. |
| `RSI-D14` | Limits and acceleration | `CLOSED_COUNTEREXAMPLE` | RSI is separated from recursive acceleration and intelligence explosion. Positive descendant meta-gain does not logically imply increasing per-generation improvement increments; the synthetic assay contains an R4 case that is not strictly accelerating. |

## Dependency spine

```text
M_t=(F_t,O_t,Π_t,C)
      │
      ├── G6: can M change itself under C?
      │       │
      │       └── D_t: process that generates/diagnoses/tests/adopts candidate improvements
      │               │
      │               ├── κ_D  meta-locality
      │               ├── Ω_D  meta-identifiability
      │               ├── χ_D  meta-searchability
      │               └── matched descendant metaproductivity
      │                         │
      │                         └── R3 recursive evolvability
      │                                   │
      │                                   └── G7 persistence/heredity → R4
      │
      └── C remains external adoption / invariant authority
```

R5 (general/open-ended RSI) is deliberately outside positive closure. Finite transfer or finite generations can strengthen a scoped empirical claim but cannot prove open-endedness by relabelling.
