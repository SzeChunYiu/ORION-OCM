The two global rows under audit are:

```text
R1-E  Every microscope fully enumerable with certificate.
R1-N  Every positive microscope paired with a minimal negative twin.
```

Neither row may be reconciled unless its derived pass count is exactly `14/14`.

## R1-E pass contract

Each class must pin one canonical merged microscope with all of:

```text
class_id
canonical_artifact
claim_scope
finite_universe_definition
certificate_or_exact_oracle
certificate_replay_or_machine_receipt
proof_evidence_class
claim_ceiling
```

A class passes only when the registered claim universe is finite and completely checked at its declared scope by exhaustive enumeration, exact dynamic enumeration, or a finite exact oracle over every registered object. Sampling, examples, or the mere existence of a result file do not count.

The checker must verify referenced repository paths exist and that the manifest's certificate kind is one of the frozen exact forms.

## R1-N pass contract

Each positive microscope must pin one negative twin with:

```text
negative_twin_artifact
shared_universe
changed_coordinate_or_atomic_intervention
negative_effect
minimality_order
minimality_proof_or_exhaustive_certificate
prospective_or_explicit_corrective_status
```

The twin passes only if:

1. positive and negative share the frozen comparison universe and all non-intervention coordinates;
2. the twin removes/reverses the registered positive effect or proves the relevant failure;
3. no strictly smaller registered intervention suffices under an explicit finite perturbation order;
4. minimality is proved or exhaustively certified;
5. confirmatory evidence was frozen before the protected outcome, or else the row is explicitly labelled corrective/hostile and is not misreported as prospective.

A generic negative example without a minimality proof is a `GAP`.

## Hostiles

The audit must fail closed for:

- missing any of the 14 IDs;
- duplicate class aliases;
- nonexistent authority/certificate paths;
- sampled evidence labelled exhaustive;
- missing finite-universe bounds;
- negative twins changing more than one uncontrolled coordinate unless that joint intervention is explicitly the atomic perturbation;
- missing finite minimality order or proof;
- hard-coded pass counts;
- manifest expected outcomes changed without changing authority provenance.
