# V28 — exact witnesses and the information retained by observation

## Question and model signatures

Original R2 freeze target 9 asks to mechanize its finite irreducibility witnesses
in Lean. The original source contains five theorems. Their literal types establish
two inequalities, unequal evaluator functions, a difference between two reachability
predicates, and two scalar inequalities. They do not themselves mention every
process, context or incomparability premise suggested by their names.
This study retains those types and supplies the missing constructor equalities.

Let a model m have an observation o(m) and a target t(m).
Recovery means an actual function d on the attained image of o with
d(o(m))=t(m) for every m. Equality of observations with unequal targets rules
out every such d. Conversely, target constancy on each observation fiber defines
a unique attained-image decoder. No arbitrary extension to unattained codes is
required. This is the classical factorization principle implemented in V9.

Different observations yield different fibers. In particular, an ambient evaluator,
its active domain, all three tagged responses, and the complete admitted raw source
are different observations. No result here quantifies over all possible signatures.

## AB1 — fixed process, two contexts

[FORWARD_V28.md](FORWARD_V28.md) constructs the free category on one object and
the two original action generators. Both generators interpret as processStep,
but remain distinct paths. The two partial contexts share admission and definedness
and evaluate singleton paths by the original ctx0 and ctx1.

Their singleton value functions differ and their induced history-order relations
differ. Thus a constant process observation cannot recover either target on this
two-model class. This is stronger than a model-name inequality because the
evaluators and comparisons are computed from actual histories.

## AB2 — same weak evaluator, different lawful processes

[REVERSE_V28.md](REVERSE_V28.md) constructs the thin categories of equality and
equality plus false→true. Both retain the same original stateValue function.
The weak ambient state observer therefore collides, whereas the actual
false→true Hom/admission target differs. This establishes nonrecovery for that
specified observation, not for the full process-context record.

The common ambient graph fixes vertex and arrow labels. Raw-history admission is
generated by requiring every traversed edge to be admitted. Thus the differing
primitive arrow also supplies a differing admitted singleton history.

## AB3 — the positive converses

[OBSERVERS_V28.md](OBSERVERS_V28.md) proves two recoveries.
First, a primitive edge is admitted exactly when its labelled singleton belongs
to the complete generated raw source. Second, P(h) holds exactly when the actual
V15 observation of h is not ILLEGAL. These supply explicit decoders.
The reverse pair cannot retain equal stronger observations.

If E is empty, the active domain P∩E is empty for every P. It therefore loses
admission information. Distinguishing ILLEGAL from UNDEFINED restores it.
The apparent conflict between irreducibility and recovery is resolved by naming
the observation and the omitted data.

## AB4 — exact scalar reversal

[SCALAR_V28.md](SCALAR_V28.md) binds the two original Nat score functions to actual
V12 finite Int sums on (1,0) and (0,1). The profiles are incomparable, both weight
vectors are strictly positive, and the four scores are 2,1,1,2.
This is a particular positive-weight reversal, not a theorem that every evaluator
is a fixed weighted sum or that its weights describe environmental probabilities.
