# R17 — recursive fixed-point closure audit

R17 adds no new machine-intelligence primitive. It governs how the fixed-point theory reacts to later defects.

Given a theorem DAG with edge A->B meaning B depends materially on A, a material defect at node A reopens the least reverse-dependency closure Desc(A), including A itself. No descendant may remain scientifically GREEN solely because it was closed before the defect was discovered.

A defect is resolved only when:
- the defective theorem/result has a successor adjudication;
- every affected descendant either re-verifies against that successor or is downgraded/retracted;
- the final claim ceiling is recomputed.

Empirical boundaries such as missing independent replication are preserved as boundaries. They block empirical-complete promotions but do not invalidate a finite/formal theorem unless that theorem depended on the missing evidence.

The R17 certificate means only:
"No new material defect was found by the registered R17 audit at the declared scope after dependency propagation and all known defects/boundaries were reconciled."

It does not mean no unknown unknowns exist.
