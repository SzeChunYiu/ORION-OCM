# Review of C6 — the natural-intelligence bridge

## 1. Are the species parameters (E, R, V) operationally defined, or numerology?

**Numerology, by the programme's own standard.**

The contract that governs these rows specifies what `E_bio` must contain — `BIOLOGICAL_BRIDGE_CONTRACT_V1.md` §L0.1: *"the species' ecology in GMI coordinates: resource budgets (energy/day, sensor bandwidth, actuator latency), lifespan/developmental timescale, social structure ... niche descriptors (foraging risk, predation, variability)."* Those are measurable quantities with units.

What the frozen registry actually contains (`PREDICTIONS_REGISTRY_V1.json`, PRED-NH-01) is `"E": 8.0, "R": 5.0, "V": 7.0` with no unit, no measurement, no source, no estimation procedure and no uncertainty. No artifact I was permitted to read states how any energy/day, bandwidth or latency figure becomes `R = 5.0`. `BIOLOGY_PREDICTIONS_THEOREM_V1.md` §Parameters gives only prose glosses — *"R (Resource Abundance): Brain tissue cheapness relative to body, social support for development"* — which is not an operational definition and does not license a corvid/rodent ordering of 5.0 vs 7.0.

Three further defects:

- **Taxonomic granularity is a category error for comparative cognition.** A single scalar triple is assigned to "cephalopod", "rodent", "carnivore" and "nonhuman_primate" — classes and orders spanning thousands of species with radically different ecologies. The registry itself concedes the incoherence while still assigning the scalar: rodent `"social_structure": "colony_variable"`, carnivore `"social_structure": "solitary_or_pack"`. If sociality varies within the taxon, `V` ("social or per-step verification requirements", `BIOLOGY_PREDICTIONS_THEOREM_V1.md` §Parameters) cannot take one value for that taxon.
- **No phylogenetic control.** Seven taxa are treated as independent data points. Comparative inference across species requires correction for phylogenetic non-independence; no artifact mentions it.
- **The ordering reproduces a rejected scheme.** The values run rodent (5,7,3) → carnivore (7,5,5) → corvid/cephalopod/cetacean (8,·,·) → nonhuman_primate (9,8,9) → human (9,9,9), i.e. a single ascending ladder with humans at the apex. That is the *scala naturae*, which modern comparative psychology explicitly rejects: *"Our current model of nature rejects the validity of the scala naturae"* (Marino, "Classification: The Scala Naturae", *Encyclopedia of Human-Animal Relationships*, pp. 220–222, opened at https://www.kimmela.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Marino-scala-naturae.pdf, p. 222). Nothing in the artifacts justifies a unidimensional ranking; the numbers look chosen to reproduce a prior intuitive ranking rather than measured.

**The deeper problem (OBJ-1): the quantitative machinery has zero discriminative content.** The only computation in the entire chain is `BIOLOGY_PREDICTIONS_THEOREM_V1.md` §Theorem Statement: `M* = arg min_M B_M(E, R, V)`, returning one of {neural, symbolic, probabilistic}. In `PREDICTIONS_REGISTRY_V1.json`, **all seven rows return `"phase_winner": "neural"`** — corvid, cephalopod, rodent, primate, cetacean, carnivore, human alike. A function that returns the same value for every input distinguishes nothing. All of the actual cross-species content therefore lives in the `C_pred.descriptors` strings (`"high_episodic"`, `"mid"`, `"graded"`, `"shallow"`, …), and **no artifact states any function, threshold or rule mapping (E,R,V) to those strings.** `FINAL_TARGET_NATURAL_HALF_THEOREM_V1.md` §2 offers only qualitative direction laws (*"niche variability ↑ → episodic weight ↑"*) with no cut-points telling you why E=8 yields `high_episodic` and E=5 yields `spatial_episodic`. The claim that the profiles are "derived from ecology/resource/verification parameters" is therefore unsupported by anything in the artifact set: the profiles are asserted, and they are asserted at exactly the values a comparative-cognition textbook would give (corvids: caching/episodic + planning; primates: ToM; cetaceans: culture; cephalopods: flexible solitary problem-solving; rodents: spatial navigation). That is the retrospective story-fitting the capsule forbids itself — `FINAL_TARGET_NATURAL_HALF_THEOREM_V1.md` §1.2: *"Retrospective story-fitting of known ethology is assimilation food, never closure evidence."*

## 2. Are the predicted profiles falsifiable? Is any contradicted by evidence?

**They are not operationally falsifiable as written, and at least one row is contradicted by published behavioural work.**

### 2a. The holdout is empty, so the voiding rule can never fire (OBJ-2)

`BIOLOGICAL_BRIDGE_CONTRACT_V1.md` §L0.4 requires `D_holdout` to be *"the biological observations, held out from theory fitting where possible, with provenance (species, study, n)."* Every row in `PREDICTIONS_REGISTRY_V1.json` instead reads `"status": "HELD_OUT", "provenance_slot": "corvid_caching_phenotype_v1", "content": null` — no study, no n, no dataset. Nothing was sealed; a placeholder string was written. `FINAL_TARGET_NATURAL_HALF_THEOREM_V1.md` §3 makes voiding conditional on unsealing: a row is voided *"after `D_holdout` is unsealed under a later protocol"* — and no such protocol is named anywhere in the artifacts. A falsifier whose trigger condition references a dataset that does not exist and a protocol that is not specified is inoperable by construction. This is not "not yet tested"; it is "cannot be tested as registered."

### 2b. The descriptor values have no decision rule (OBJ-3)

`FINAL_TARGET_NATURAL_HALF_THEOREM_V1.md` §3 voids a row whose phenotype *"contradicts the frozen ordinal profile on ≥2 descriptors."* But nowhere is `"mid"` vs `"high"`, `"simple_stop"` vs `"graded"`, or `"shallow"` vs `"depth_ge_1"` tied to any measurement, task, or threshold. Two competent reviewers could not agree whether a given experiment contradicts `"D_meta": "simple_stop"`. An ordinal scale with no anchors cannot adjudicate anything, so the ≥2-descriptor rule is unusable even if data arrived.

### 2c. Several registered "failure modes" are straw men

`PRED-NH-03` (rodent): *"held-out phenotype shows primate-level protocol culture under rodent (E,R,V)"*. `PRED-NH-02` (cephalopod): *"held-out phenotype shows high social-TOM investment despite solitary ecology"*. `PRED-NH-07` (human): *"architecture profile collapses to a single undifferentiated memory regime under adult (E,R,V)"*. These name outcomes no informed researcher expects. A registered failure mode that is antecedently near-impossible does not make a prediction risky.

### 2d. Rows contradicted by published behavioural evidence

**PRED-NH-03 (rodent) — contradicted on two descriptors, which is exactly the registry's own voiding threshold.**

- `"D_meta": "simple_stop"`. Foote & Crystal, "Metacognition in the Rat", *Current Biology* 17, 551–555 (2007), DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2007.01.061 (opened at https://shinyverse.org/al4ai/extras/FooteCrystal_MetcognitionInTheRat_CurrentBiology2007.pdf) report that rats declined a duration-discrimination test at rates that *"increased as the difficulty of the discrimination increased"* and that accuracy was lowest on difficult tests that could not be declined; the authors conclude *"a nonprimate has knowledge of its own cognitive state."* That is graded, difficulty-scaled confidence — `"graded"` in the registry's own vocabulary, not `"simple_stop"`. I flag that this literature is contested; the registry provides no means to adjudicate the dispute, which is itself the objection.
- `"D_plan": "shallow"`. Miller, Botvinick & Brody, "Dorsal hippocampus contributes to model-based planning", DOI 10.1101/096594 (bioRxiv v2, opened at https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/096594v2; a *Nature Neuroscience* version exists, which I could not open) report a multi-trial rat task producing planned behaviour and *"the first causal evidence directly linking dorsal hippocampus with planning behavior."* Model-based planning in rats is not `"shallow"` under any stated criterion.

Under `FINAL_TARGET_NATURAL_HALF_THEOREM_V1.md` §3, two contradicted descriptors void PRED-NH-03 — except that, per OBJ-2, the voiding clause cannot fire because nothing was sealed. The row is simultaneously wrong and unfalsifiable by its own rules.

**PRED-NH-02 (cephalopod) — `"D_comm": "none"`, `"social_structure": "mostly_solitary"`.** Scheel, Godfrey-Smith & Lawrence, "Signal Use by Octopuses in Agonistic Interactions", *Current Biology* (2016), reported field observation of *Octopus tetricus* producing colour and postural displays during conspecific agonistic interactions, with dark/"towering" displays associated with aggression and pale colouration with retreat. I could not open the publisher's copy (cell.com and ScienceDirect both returned HTTP 403); the account I actually opened is the co-author's own summary page, https://metazoan.net/33-octopus-signals/, which I cite as secondary and which does not print the volume/pages. Treat this as a flagged contradiction requiring primary confirmation rather than a closed one — but note that the registry gives no definition of `"none"` for `D_comm` that would let anyone settle it either way.

**PRED-NH-01 (corvid)** — the descriptor `"D_mem": "high_episodic"` and the failure mode *"held-out phenotype lacks episodic-like caching advantage under matched spatial controls"* restate the best-known result in corvid comparative cognition. See §3 below for why this is fatal to the "frozen before phenotype" claim.

### 2e. The "frozen before phenotype" claim collapses into a dilemma (OBJ-4)

`BIOLOGICAL_BRIDGE_CONTRACT_V1.md`, "Next step (queued, highest cost)": *"The first real prediction candidate: corvid caching (episodic-like profile) vs rodent navigation under the memory-regime partition — registries first, phenotype data held out. Requires a biology-literature saturation pass (assimilation-first) before any `C_pred` is frozen. Not started."*

`PREDICTIONS_REGISTRY_V1.json` nonetheless freezes exactly that `C_pred` (PRED-NH-01 corvid `"high_episodic"`, PRED-NH-03 rodent `"spatial_episodic"`), and `"frozen": true`.

Either (a) the mandatory saturation pass was not performed, in which case the contract was violated and the rows are inadmissible; or (b) it was performed, in which case the phenotype literature was consulted and `C_pred` is not pre-phenotype. Both branches void the ceiling item `C_pred_frozen_pre_phenotype` listed in `PREDICTIONS_REGISTRY_V1.json` `claim_ceiling.admissible`. There is no third branch: the contract text says the pass is required *before* freezing, and the same corvid-vs-rodent pair is the frozen content.

Independently: "pre-registration" against facts already in every comparative-cognition syllabus is not prospective prediction regardless of file timestamps. `BIOLOGICAL_BRIDGE_CONTRACT_V1.md` itself names *"corvid caching (episodic-like profile)"* — i.e. the answer — in the sentence proposing the experiment.

## 3. Does any artifact claim a neural mechanism? Is function separated from implementation?

**Yes, explicitly — and no, the separation does not hold across the composed set. This trips the final-target capsule's own falsifier (OBJ-5).**

`FINAL_TARGET_NATURAL_HALF_THEOREM_V1.md` §4 excludes *"any mapping to cortical areas, white-matter tracts, neurotransmitter systems, or clinical lesion topography"*, and §7 declares the capsule void if *"claim surface asserts `HUMAN_COGNITION_EXPLAINED`, G10 empirical, or neuroanatomy."*

But §5.3 of that same file composes with `gmi-natural-intelligence-bridge-v1` (*"Composition with `gmi-natural-intelligence-bridge-v1`: morphology→taxon pressure classes remain consistent"*), and that parent's §6 table assigns, as the "Neural Architecture J(T)" for named taxa:

- Primates → *"Prefrontal cortex + Wernicke's"*
- Corvids, rodents → *"Hippocampal + prefrontal"*
- Carnivores, raptors → *"Visual cortex + cerebellum"*
- Cetaceans, primates → *"Wernicke's + mirror neurons"*

§3 of the same file adds *"Memory organization → Hippocampal-cortical consolidation"*, and §9 instructs the reader to *"Compare J(M) to known neural architecture of T"* and *"Falsify if predicted J(M) ≠ actual J(T)"*. These are cortical-area identity claims about named taxa. The composed claim surface therefore asserts neuroanatomy, which is the condition `FINAL_TARGET_NATURAL_HALF_THEOREM_V1.md` §7 says voids the capsule.

The specific attributions are also stale or unsupported:

- *"Wernicke's"* is applied to primates and cetaceans. The term has no stable referent even in human neurobiology: Tremblay & Dick, "Broca and Wernicke are dead, or moving past the classic model of language neurobiology", *Brain and Language* 162, 60–71 (2016), DOI 10.1016/j.bandl.2016.08.004 (opened at https://myweb.fiu.edu/adick/wp-content/uploads/sites/252/2016/02/tremblaydick_2016.pdf) *"demonstrate that there is no consistent anatomical definition of 'Broca's and Wernicke's area'"* and argue the Classic model *"is based on an outdated brain anatomy"* and should be replaced. Exporting an eponym with no consistent human definition onto non-human taxa is not a formal mapping.
- *"mirror neurons"* is given as the architecture supporting social learning in cetaceans. Neither the artifacts nor any source I opened reports single-unit mirror-neuron recording in cetaceans, and the action-understanding interpretation the table relies on is contested: Hickok, "Eight Problems for the Mirror Neuron Theory of Action Understanding in Monkeys and Humans", *Journal of Cognitive Neuroscience* 21(7), 1229–1243 (2009) (opened at https://pages.ucsd.edu/~jpineda/COGS260Mirroring/readings/Hickok%20-%208%20problems%20for%20MN%20theory%20of%20action%20understanding.pdf) concludes *"there is no evidence from monkey data that directly tests this theory, and evidence from humans makes a strong case against the position."*

Two further separation failures:

- `NATURAL_INTELLIGENCE_BRIDGE_THEOREM_V1.md` §1 says it *"does NOT make biological claims"* while §3, §6 and §7 make behavioural and neuroanatomical claims about named taxa. That is an internal contradiction in the parent, and the child imports the parent.
- `PREDICTIONS_REGISTRY_V1.json` `lesion_alignment` maps cognitive descriptors onto `L_mem`, `L_social`, `L_att`, `L_plan`, `L_causal`, and `FINAL_TARGET_NATURAL_HALF_THEOREM_V1.md` §5.2 states these are *"the registered deficit footprint that a future empirical unseal must respect."* "Lesion" and "deficit footprint" are neurological constructs, and binding natural cognitive descriptors to them while excluding *"clinical lesion topography"* is at best equivocal. I **could not check** what `gmi-derived-lesion-v1` defines these as — that package is outside the set this brief permits me to read. I record that as unchecked, not as fine. The one alignment I can inspect is unjustified on its face: `D_pred`, defined in §2 as *"predictive-motor demand"*, is mapped to `L_causal`, with no stated reason why a motor-tracking descriptor maps to a causal-reasoning lesion.
