### Basis of the objections (artifact quotes)

- Citation status, crosswalk header: *"`VERIFIED` = the reference was checked against a live source during tranche build (2026-09-15); otherwise the reference is a canonical field anchor entered from field knowledge and MUST be re-verified by the AC re-run before any manuscript freeze."* By my count only ~10 cells carry `VERIFIED`; the rest are `CITE-TF`.
- Addendum, header: *"Every reference below is `CITE-TF` — a canonical field anchor entered from field knowledge and **not** checked against a live source during this tranche."* And: *"It does not verify any citation. Every entry is `CITE-TF`; AC05 stays open."*
- Addendum, Part 2 preamble: *"Eight of the 48 crosswalk rows carry a citations cell with no dated or authored anchor — they cite the AB audit list, a house discipline, or a sibling artifact of the same tranche, none of which is a parent."* And the file *"supplies only what the AC audit measured as missing"* — i.e. the parent record for those eight was produced by a later audit of terms already named.
- Row 48 cites *"WHAT_IS_ACTUALLY_NEW template (this package)"* as its citation; the addendum concedes *"the sibling template is not"* a parent.
- Crosswalk Coverage statement: *"Rows 20–48 above, plus the migration table, discharge the AB banned/avoid box and the terminology-consistency mandate of AB."*

Note on the verdict path: limb 1 of AC02 ("every construct has both a canonical **and** a modern parent recorded") fails outright at 43/48 rows, so the verdict is fixed at NOT_SATISFIED regardless of how the ordering question resolves; UNDECIDABLE would only apply if limb 1 passed and only ordering were murky.
