## 1. C1 — the state-class choice law

### OBJ-S1 (material) — the "licensed band" is algebraically identical to the outcome it is used to predict

`gmi-833-real-transition-receipts-v1/FREEZE_V2_AMENDMENT.md` §A4 defines the band by deriving exactly the two conditions that constitute the transition:

> "persistent wins at `lambda_low` iff `lambda_low*B_persist + p*err_persist < p*E0`" … "stateless wins at `lambda_high` iff `p*E0 < lambda_high*B_persist + p*err_persist`" … "the transition `PERSISTENT_STATE -> STATELESS` between the frozen endpoints is licensed iff `1/8 < E0 < 3/8`."

Solve the same inequalities for the crossover price. With `J_c(lambda) = p*err_c + lambda*B_c` and `err_persist -> 0`, the price at which the two candidates swap is

```
lambda_c = p*(E0 - err_persist)/B   <=   p*E0/B
```

and the frozen law is `lambda* = p*eta/(2B)`, which at the registered `eta = 0.5` (`"eta_registered": 0.5` in every record of `RECEIPTS_V1.json`) is `p/(4B)`. Therefore

```
lambda_c / lambda* = E0 / (eta/2) = 4*E0
```

and since `lambda_low = lambda*/2`, `lambda_high = 3*lambda*/2`,

```
1/8 < E0 < 3/8   <=>   1/2 < lambda_c/lambda* < 3/2   <=>   lambda_low < lambda_c < lambda_high.
```

The band is, term for term, the statement *"the true crossover price lies strictly between the two probe prices"* — which is the statement *"the transition will be observed."* A system is licensed if and only if it must transition; a system is unlicensed if and only if it cannot. `CORE.md`'s headline —

> "every one shows the pre-registered `PERSISTENT_STATE -> STATELESS` transition … 3 unlicensed systems … correctly predicted NOT to transition at either endpoint: the licensed-band boundary `1/8 < E0 < 3/8` is demonstrated on both sides by real data"

— is therefore not a prediction that survived a risk of failure. Both arms are arithmetic consequences of `E0`, a quantity measured on the same run. The control cannot fail. The only empirical content is "different data sources have different stateless error rates."

### OBJ-S2 (material) — the amendment retracted the premise but kept the constant derived from it

`FREEZE_V2_AMENDMENT.md` §A5 states plainly:

> "the analytic-uniform expectation `1/2` is recorded but NOT asserted on real data (V1's assertion is retracted by this amendment)"

Yet §A2 states:

> "Everything else in V1 (task family, pools, prices, `lambda_low = lambda*/2`, `lambda_high = 3 lambda*/2` with `lambda* = p/(16H)` … ) is unchanged."

`lambda*` is *derived from* the retracted premise. Writing `lambda* = p*eta/(2B)` as `p*(eta*(1/2))/B` shows that the `1/2` inside `lambda*` is exactly the stateless delayed floor the amendment just retracted. The amendment measured the correct replacement — `floor_del`, recorded in every receipt — and did not propagate it into `lambda*`. Instead it introduced a tolerance band wide enough to absorb the resulting error: `1/8 < E0 < 3/8` is, at `eta = 1/2`, precisely `1/4 < floor_del < 3/4`.

The receipts confirm the decomposition to three decimal places (`E0 ≈ eta * floor_del`, immediate error ≈ 0):

| receipt | `floor_del` | `eta*floor_del` | recorded `E0_err_all` |
|---|---|---|---|
| R01-gutenberg-1342 | 0.44958 | 0.22479 | 0.22446 |
| R04 (Noise.wav) | 0.37529 | 0.18764 | 0.18680 |
| R05-stdlib-json | 0.40178 | 0.20089 | 0.20020 |
| R07-frankenstein | 0.45490 | 0.22745 | 0.22776 |
| R08-dict-words | 0.48350 | 0.24175 | 0.24007 |

So the corrected boundary is `lambda_c = lambda* * 2*floor_del`. The programme held the instrument's floor to the data and left the law's floor at the retracted constant.

### OBJ-S3 (material) — the boundary-tie control shows the frozen law's boundary is wrong, 5 of 5, and is not scored as a failure

The frozen law predicts a tie at `lambda = lambda*`. `gmi-833-heldout-20-transitions-v1/HELDOUT_TRANSITION_FORMALIZATION_V1.md` §4 states this is the point of the control:

### OBJ-S14 (material) — KE-3's 161,632 is conditioned on an outcome-selected event that the same package shows is not obtainable

The success register entry `SU-KE3` reads: "The capability law is wrong on 0 of 161,632 (input, truthfully-registered-world) pairs across three real populations." The conditioning event — that the world is *truthfully registered* — is established by comparing the registration against the measured outcome, so the retained subset is selected on a success indicator correlated with the thing under test. `CORE.md` reports how often that event holds: "Truthfulness went 19/32 → 28/32 → 26/32". ### OBJ-S15 (material) — `NULL_MODAL` is a straw null evaluated on an arena the predictor selects

> "`NULL_MODAL` (always emit the modal externally-measured capability) is unsound on every point-emitting input; head to head on the inputs where `F` emits a point, the null is wrong on all of them and `F` on none."

Two defects. First, the comparison set is "the inputs where `F` emits a point" — `F`'s own abstention policy chooses the arena, and `F` abstains on 69–75% of inputs (per KE-6). Second, the non-degenerate emissions are values like `4/11`, `5/11`, `9/11` (CORE.md, KE-1), so a modal-capability null is guaranteed wrong on exactly the non-degenerate set by construction. An informative null would be abstention-matched — the best constant, or the best registration-blind rule, restricted to the same inputs with the same answer budget. No such null is present.

### OBJ-S16 (material) — the KE-3D terminal is justified by an inference three counts cannot support

> "Truthfulness went 19/32 → 28/32 → 26/32 — not monotone, which is the signature of an optimization threshold rather than a missing clause. A fourth law fitted to those counterexamples would be tuning to outcomes"

At n = 32 and p ≈ 0.8, one binomial standard deviation is about 2.3. That non-monotonicity is offered as the diagnostic signature distinguishing "an optimization threshold" from "a missing clause", and that diagnosis is the stated ground for terminating the revival chain. A mechanistic conclusion and a stopping decision are being carried by a difference the data cannot resolve. (Separately: three laws each frozen on a new population and each falsified is genuinely creditable evidence, and the row being kept open rather than closed on synthetic data is the most honest move in the whole set. ### OBJ-S17 (material) — exact arithmetic is being used to carry statistical weight

"Stdlib only, exact `Fraction`/`int` arithmetic, no floats in any claim" (CORE.md) is a statement about numerical representation. ## 4. Direct answers to the brief's questions

**Do the nulls and controls actually discriminate?** Partly, and not where it matters. The remint/rescale invariance pair (`F4a`/`F4b`) is a genuinely well-designed control pair for the reason given ("an invariance checker that returned `0` for everything would be a constant `False`"), and the hostile set HE1–HE8 includes a real self-correction (HE1's first version was undetected and was replaced) and a negative control on the freeze-order check itself (HE8). Those are real. But the load-bearing controls are not discriminating: the licensed band cannot fail (OBJ-S1), the shifted-boundary control is guaranteed to fail whenever the outcome is `STATELESS` and is blind across a factor-3 interval the programme itself proves blind (OBJ-S4), KE-7's in-universe census cannot fail (OBJ-S13), and `NULL_MODAL` cannot win (OBJ-S15).

**Is "0/200 randomized controls" a meaningful null?** No, in both places it appears. In Z15 it is the measured shadow of a theorem over a one-parameter family that excludes the relevant alternative. In `BASELINE_V1.md` A12 it is an uncharacterized number.

**Do coverage/calibration numbers carry their abstention rates honestly?** The abstention rates are carried alongside, which is better practice than most work of this kind, and `SCOPE_V1.md` names the right hazard explicitly. But the figure that the same document identifies as the decisive one — coverage on point emissions only — is not reported anywhere in the supplied artifacts, at abstention rates of 69–75% where it dominates the result, and at the one alpha where the mixture arithmetic points below the bound (OBJ-S11). Honest framing, incomplete reporting.
