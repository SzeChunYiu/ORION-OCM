> `FREEZE_V2_AMENDMENT.md` A1: "Root cause (one stage): V1 Section 4 assumed uniformly distributed bits… The analytic constant `1/2` is therefore not the stateless floor on real data."

### 3. The decisive contradiction at λ = λ\*

With `eta_registered = 0.5` (recorded in every receipt), `lambda*·B = p·eta/2 = p/4`, so persistent wins at `lambda*` iff `E0 > 1/4 + err_persist`.

Measured `stateless_floor.E0_err_all` in the five receipts: **0.2245, 0.1868, 0.2002, 0.2278, 0.2401** — every one below 1/4, so stateless wins **strictly** at `lambda*` in 5/5, for any `err_persist ≥ 0`. The receipts confirm it directly: `boundary_tie_control: {"counted_as_transition": false, "lambda": <lambda*>, "winners": ["c1"]}` in all five. The winner is identifiable as the stateless machine, not by assumption: A2 defines `floor_del` as the "delayed-mode eval error of the trained `B = 0` candidate", and each receipt records `stateless_floor.candidate_id: "c1"` — so `c1` is the `B = 0` candidate, and it wins alone at `lambda*`.

Substrate A predicts the opposite at the identical test point ("preserve both property classes as ties rather than forcing a winner"). Same closed form, same distinguished point, opposite outcomes — and the deviation is one-sided in all five receipts (the real crossing lies below `lambda*`: from A4's inequality the crossing is at most `p·E0/B`, i.e. 0.75×–0.96× of the recorded `lambda_star`).
