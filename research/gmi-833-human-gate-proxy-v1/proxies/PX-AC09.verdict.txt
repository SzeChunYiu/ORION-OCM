## 3. Required changes, and whether the flagship has a closer parent

**Required changes to the crosswalk.**

1. **A missing area, not a wrong row — this is the material finding.** The crosswalk carries no row for the flagship's own central object. Its Coverage statement asserts "Every AB-addendum box is represented: morphology, machine species, ecology, niche, selection (5 kinds), phase law/phase diagram, prior-free, …" — true of AB's list, and false of the flagship theory as FREEZE_V1 states it: "a price `lambda` charged per bit of persistent state". No row covers pricing persistent state, no row covers a cost-threshold selection rule, and no canonical term is offered for either, although canonical terms plainly exist in the opened sources (endurance shadow price; decision-centric rate–distortion; forgetting boundary; memory–distortion frontier; rate-priced state abstraction). A new row (or rows) is required, with a parent-comparison to Chen arXiv:2606.18144, Zou et al. arXiv:2605.10870 and Rosas arXiv:2606.06123, and a note on the η symbol collision between the flagship's requirement weight and Chen's endurance shadow price.
2. **Row 44 must change.** Its definition cell — "distinguish aleatoric (irreducible) from epistemic (reducible)" — is contradicted by Young (arXiv:2606.12646) and by Bickford Smith et al. (arXiv:2412.20892, ICML 2025). Its match column must drop from `EXACT` to `PARTIAL` (or `NON` pending resolution), and "adopt canonical definitions for each uncertainty construct" must be rewritten to acknowledge that the canonical definition is under live dispute.
3. **Row 2's citation must change.** ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018 is being superseded: the IEEE P29148 revision PAR was approved 10 Sep 2025 and the DIS was registered in July 2026. Cite the 2018 edition with its revision status, not as current.
4. **Row 37's migration rule ("pick one of seven") is now incomplete** — "self-evolving architecture" (Jafari et al., Frontiers in AI 2026) is a live eighth sense for structural change over a system's lifetime, which is exactly GMI's stated meaning of "development".
5. **Refresh-only, no claim change:** rows 6, 14, 19, 24, 25, 29/30, 32, 38/39, 40/47/48 gain in-window anchors as tabled above.
6. **Do not cite arXiv:2605.05066** (Impossibility Triangle) anywhere: withdrawn 6 Aug 2026 for errors in the main theorem proof.

**Does the flagship claim have a closer parent than the crosswalk records?** Two separate answers, and they must not be merged.

- *Closer parent in window:* **No.** Nothing I opened from 2025-01-01 to 2026-09-19 predicts the stateless-versus-persistent choice from task parameters alone, before a search is run, with a threshold linear in the probability that a scored moment needs an earlier input. Chen prices persistence and gets a threshold but measures its pivot from logs; Zou et al. bound forgetting within a budget; Rosas prices abstraction resolution; Zhang separates memory budgets by complexity class. The nearest in-window neighbour is Chen arXiv:2606.18144 on form, not on content.
- *Parent search completeness:* **The crosswalk never searched this area at all**, which is a different and more serious problem. Two out-of-window sources I opened show the area is populated and old: Chiuchiù, López-Suárez, Neri, Diamantini & Gammaitoni, "Cost of remembering a bit of information", *Phys. Rev. A* 97, 052108 (2018), https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.05544 (energetic cost of *retaining* a bit, beyond Landauer's reset cost); and the lossy-compression/perception literature it sits beside, which reports a critical transition separating low- and high-fidelity regimes. Until a parent search is run over "cost of retaining a bit", "shadow price of memory", "policy compression / rate–distortion", and "memory-versus-compute tradeoff", the flagship's novelty is unestablished — not refuted, unestablished.

**Scope limits of this re-run, stated plainly.** (i) `EXPERT_LITERATURE_LANES_V1.md`, which WHAT_IS_ACTUALLY_NEW_TEMPLATE_V1.md names as the protocol executing all 11 lanes ("The template is executed by the parent-literature check protocol in `EXPERT_LITERATURE_LANES_V1.md`"), is not in the Artifacts list and rule 1 barred me from opening it; whether 11 lanes were ever executed is unverifiable from the permitted artifacts. (ii) Four publisher pages refused retrieval (listed in §1) and I cite nothing I could not open. (iii) This re-run is itself model-executed, and Sinhahajari et al. (arXiv:2606.12071) is direct evidence that model judges overestimate novelty relative to domain experts; treat my "no closer parent in window" as a model-proxy result, not an expert clearance.
