the donor's universal consequence. Every target is a Boolean tautology; the other
12 have countermodels to that targeted branch. Thus there is no essential composite
target opportunity among these 16 tasks.

left/right expressions and their complements, quotiented by exact finite semantics.

matcher does not recognize. Every substitution is redundant for its target.
This is a semantic completeness limitation, not an invalid accepted answer.
It is not an absence claim over arbitrary Boolean functions.

selection rerun was made. The next question is coverage of genuinely composite
dependencies, with shared primitive methods and experience-varying structures;
