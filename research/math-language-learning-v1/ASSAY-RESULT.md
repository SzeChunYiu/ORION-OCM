# Fixed unary experiment: result and next mechanism

Source-qualified result, 8 September 2026.
The experiment tests acquisition and reuse of checked Boolean/unary methods through
formal and supplied controlled-language presentations. It uses no neural network.

## Observed result

The separately registered corrected execution finished under its original 1,200 s
clock. All four episodes report `NO_METHOD_ACQUIRED`. This is zero successful
causal-learning episodes; it does not mean the four episodes failed to execute.
The method library remained empty in both adaptive arms. No method-use advantage,
language transfer or OCM-specific novelty follows from this result.

The run emitted 2,532 checked service rows: 1,024 in the formal/text phase and
1,508 in the premise-revision phase. Every recorded independent answer check
passed and corresponding arms agreed. Rows include repeated presentations and
support variants; they are not 2,532 independent semantic problems. Separately,
192 method-role call slots were `NOT_APPLICABLE/NO_USE_REFERENCE`.

The earlier registered execution is retained separately: it generated the same task
stream but failed at the producer/consumer `train` field boundary before acquisition.
Its result remains `CANNOT_CHECK`. The correction does not convert the earlier
attempt into a success and is not an independent replication.

## Located acquisition obstruction

Both adaptive arms generated candidate counts of 10, 4, 4 and 3 by episode.
Independent essential-composite checking accepted 2, 0, 1 and 0 respectively.
Every surviving candidate had only one distinct semantic training support, below
its frozen requirement of two. Thus no candidate entered development ranking;
selection and persistence did not cause the empty library.

The two-support requirement is this experiment's acquisition criterion, not a
logical requirement for soundness after an independent universal proof.
Keep it fixed in the first revival to isolate the representation change. Improve how
checked training traces expose repeated structure, then test that change against
an equally adaptive conventional parent with the same inputs and learner.
The original outcomes and the original final partition remain fixed.

## Measured cost and scope

The fixed external observer measured preparation at 2.15 s and launch at 447.08 s,
including their process exit and product persistence. Their sum is 449.23 s with
0.01 s resolution per observation. Observer retention and work between phases
are outside that sum. Nested CPU observations are not a complete lifetime total.
The frozen cost closure retains `ECONOMICS_CANNOT_CHECK`.

There is already a concrete serving-cost problem. Across the four 64-presentation
B batches, observed OCM-enabled call wall was 12.755–12.856 s; the equally adaptive

These spans cover process launch through child readback before coordinator audit;
they exclude acquisition, C/revision work and complete lifetime overhead.
They locate a performance deficit in this experiment, not a general speed ratio.

## Post-study failure-handling correction

Later review reproduced a separate cost-reader bug: a refused or failed child can
retain `facts=None`, which the old reader dereferenced. The successor explicitly
refuses missing/untyped facts as `UNAVAILABLE_COST`. Nine authored A/B/C controls
reproduced the crash through the real coordinator call boundary; all nine then
passed within 54 focused controls, with no skips. These use simulated child outcomes.
The original scientific snapshot, results, clock and cost closure were not rerun or changed.
