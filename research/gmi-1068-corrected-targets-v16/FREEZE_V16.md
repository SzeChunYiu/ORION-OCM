## P3 — finite linear representation under primitive scalar laws

F:(Fin n→α)→α on the full vector domain. Assume actual additivity and
F(c*v)=c*F(v) for every c∈α. Rational homogeneity alone is not the generic premise.
Derive F(0)=0 and the coordinate-basis expansion; define w_i=F(e_i) and prove
F(v)=Σ_i w_i*v_i for all v, uniquely. Under these linearity assumptions,

monotone normalized functional. Zero weights are allowed, not only positive ones.
For n=0, normalization is impossible because the empty vector is zero and0≠1.
