`informed_parent` intentionally shares SV, donor modules and checkers with OCM. It is a same-information selection ablation, not an independently implemented whole-vessel comparator or strong LLM comparison. The parent result concerns representation selection/reconstruction only.

The positive fixture has 14 atoms, including exactly 8 added background atoms. A 6-state quotient reconstructs all 14 exact values with global background 1/14. Incoming-edge, mixed-warrant and stale-binding controls fall back to full navigation.

Three original consumer withdrawal errors remain in every arm; these are uncheckable functions, not successes. A separate core repair must earn a separately bound successor result.

No timing, scaling, O(k), persistence, quantum-advantage or broad capability claim. Dense preparation and full-output materialization remain counted.
