LANGUAGE LESSONS (explicit supervision):
L1: In this taught clause order, the subject noun phrase comes first, then the object
noun phrase, then the past-tense verb: 'the robot the door opened' means agent=robot,
verb=open, patient=door, tense=past. 'the' marks a noun phrase.
L2: dax means cat. L3: crate means parcel. L4: lifted is past tense of lift.
The names robot and door denote themselves. Follow the taught order on new examples.

PROCEDURAL DOMAIN: A program is a list of at most four operations applied left to
right to input x. inc adds one; dec subtracts one; double multiplies by two; square
squares the current value. Find any program realizing the requested polynomial
identity for EVERY rational x. A few example matches are not enough. The public
checker is tools/check_polynomial.py; it checks the full exact coefficient identity.
Use python3 tools/check_polynomial.py --program inc,square --coefficients 1,2,1
as an example of its interface, not as the answer to the task. Optional --x evaluates
the same program at a rational input. Do not edit this checker or other tool files.
