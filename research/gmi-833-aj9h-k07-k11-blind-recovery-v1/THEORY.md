## Crucial bias correction

Family labels and post-hoc fingerprints are hidden, but that alone is not literal prior-freedom. The following remain explicit priors and are recorded in `BIAS_LEDGER_V1.json`:

- task/ecology authorship;
- primitive basis;
- horizon/budget;
- resource accounting;
- acceptance evaluator;
- search-procedure family.

Therefore the earned result is conditional recovery under a frozen task and primitive basis. It does not prove that a civilization with no historical knowledge would inevitably rediscover each family, and it does not prove universal optimality.
