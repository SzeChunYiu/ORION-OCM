## 2. External parents, with citations

Nearest-neighbour and exemplar memory are not novel here and are not claimed
to be. Neither is the descriptor-closure construction, prototype voting, or
the charged-cost model.

- E. Fix and J. L. Hodges Jr (1951), "Discriminatory analysis: nonparametric
  discrimination: consistency properties", USAF School of Aviation Medicine
  Report 4(3):261–279. The k-nearest-neighbour rule. No DOI.
- T. M. Cover and P. E. Hart (1967), "Nearest neighbor pattern
  classification", *IEEE Transactions on Information Theory* 13(1):21–27.
  DOI `10.1109/TIT.1967.1053964`. The asymptotic error bound of the 1-NN rule
  against the Bayes error — the reason prototype voting over stored exemplars
  is a legitimate family, not a lookup.
- P. Hart (1968), "The condensed nearest neighbor rule", *IEEE Transactions on
  Information Theory* 14(3):515–516. DOI `10.1109/TIT.1968.1054155`. The
  condensed exemplar set, whose addressability is what this row's recovered
  readout (`CNT>=1`) measures.
- D. E. Knuth (1997), *The Art of Computer Programming, Vol. 2*, 3rd ed.,
  Addison-Wesley. The multiplicative-hash permutation used as the registered
  presentation lever.
- G. Salton, A. Wong and C. S. Yang (1975), "A vector space model for
  automatic indexing", *Communications of the ACM* 18(11):613–620.
  DOI `10.1145/361219.361220`. Descriptor-term indexing over a document
  vocabulary; the descriptor-closure vocabulary is the same construction on a
  word list.
- D. R. Wilson and T. R. Martinez (2000), "Reduction techniques for instance-
  based learning algorithms", *Machine Learning* 38(3):257–286.
  DOI `10.1023/A:1007626913721`. Stored-exemplar reduction/selection — the
  resource dimension the store ladder (R07) measures.

## 3. What is NOT claimed novel

- No machine-learning method here is new. No nearest-neighbour rule, no
  exemplar table, no prototype vote, no descriptor index is new.
- "A query that occurs in the stored exemplar table is more likely to be a
  shared (multi-word) descriptor" is a property of the corpus, not a discovery.
- The readout language, the selection procedure, the post-hoc classifier, the
  charged-cost model and the eleven-coordinate ledger are the parents' forms,
  re-implemented here rather than imported.

## 4. The residual contribution of this tranche

One thing, methodological and small: **the family-blind recovery of the
addressable stored-exemplar readout as the winner of a registered selection
over a closed, pre-outcome readout language, at real scale, with a
matched-presentation negative control that makes the registered presentation
lever load-bearing** — and the R09 correction to a symmetric half-split,
registered with its measured-asymmetric failure attribution before the
executor existed.
