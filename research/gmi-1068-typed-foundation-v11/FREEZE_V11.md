## Constructive law targets
For an arbitrary typed graph G with individually identified generators,
construct endpoint-indexed finite paths, empty paths and concatenation.
Prove endpoint preservation, associativity and both units by induction.
Do not assume these source laws as fields and merely project them.

For fixed object and edge interpretations into a lawful category C, construct
path evaluation and prove preservation of identities/composition and uniqueness.
The target category laws are assumptions; never label them constructed here.
The interpreter may be nonfaithful. Parallel edges, loops and repeated
occurrences are distinct syntactic paths even if evaluation identifies them.

For an arbitrary typed congruence on paths (an equivalence on each Hom stable
under concatenation), construct the quotient's operations and prove their laws.
An arbitrary equivalence relation without congruence is insufficient.
For every small category C, evaluation Path(U C)→C is onto each Hom via
singleton paths. Its kernel is a typed congruence, and the induced quotient
is isomorphic to C, fixing objects and preserving composition and identities.
Prove inverse maps, not only matching cardinalities or an informal analogy.
This bridge covers lawful extensional process equations; not all categories
are free, and no arbitrary physical substrate is assumed already categorical.

Retain V5's exact unchanged-object admission condition: identities admitted
and closure under composition. A resource-at-most-one history cutoff fails;
an explicit resource-state lift repairs typing where its stated model applies.
Deleting an identity symbol is not deleting uniquely recoverable unit structure.
No universal primitive-count minimality or unique ontology is proposed.
