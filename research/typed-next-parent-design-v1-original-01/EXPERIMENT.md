# Prospective experiment: an acquired ordinary subproof

Status: engineering design only. An execution protocol must bind actual source
and allocations after the training-only opportunity check; this document runs nothing.

## 1. Freeze the inherited machinery and exposure

Use a separately versioned exposed corpus slice, never the historical protected
evaluation. Pin grammar, trusted axioms, native checker, library prefix P, parser,
search objective, complete ordinary substitution domain, action order and limits.
Generate the shared bank from registered syntax closure over legal inputs,
never from held-out proof intermediates; use the same bank in every arm.
Keep the qualified finite-bank search as the scoped first consumer. Report its
bounded applicability; it is not a general native-search benchmark.

Publish every supplied item: training proofs, source labels, bank construction,
candidate language, cut-size bound, syntax rules, ordering and all cost limits.
No candidate sequent, desired intermediate, chosen method ID, or target-specific
search route may be supplied as training supervision.

Training proofs may use only P, or have later references expanded back to P by a
registered dependency normalization. This work is charged. Do not weaken P or
remove useful ordinary assertions to manufacture a nonalias.

## 2. Separate source families before discovery

A split custodian groups the exposed pool before the learner runs. Keep together
exact/alpha-renamed sequents, direct theorem aliases, source-derived variants and
whole-proof copies or common generation-template siblings. Use full typed
contracts and normalized whole-proof skeletons, not filenames alone.
The learned subproof motif may recur across different enclosing families:
forbidding every shared subproof would remove the mechanism being tested.

Use chronological training/development/evaluation blocks, purging cross-boundary
family leakage. Freeze at least two training source families, one development
family and two evaluation families. This is a mechanism witness allocation,
not statistical evidence of broad generalization. Admit all eligible goals from
the registered evaluation blocks; do not choose only goals a mined lemma fits.

Neither producer nor policy selection sees evaluation proofs. A separate
custodian can inspect them solely for split/dependency auditing, seal that work,
and provide the consumer only goal, allowed hypotheses and declared context.
Exclude evaluation theorem labels and any available theorem whose proof depends
on an excluded target. Do not exclude unrelated base axioms or common lemmas.
All removal rules derive from the frozen information boundary, not solve outcomes.

## 3. First opportunity check and learner

First enumerate rooted cuts along an essential-premise edge containing exactly
two logical assertion applications in training proof DAGs, with one conclusion. Preserve every ordered incoming premise
occurrence, mandatory floating hypothesis, syntax proof and DV obligation.
Syntax-building nodes remain in the proof, but do not count as logical steps.
Record shared DAG nodes and proof-tree duplication separately.

Keep existing schematic variables; initially generalize only by typed alpha
normalization. Do not add anti-unification or infer new type rules. The learned
choice is the subproof boundary and its reusable native assertion. Whole supplied
theorems are the imported-fact comparator, not discovery candidates.

The conventional baseline P1 is P plus every independently checked whole training
theorem, under exactly the same exposure. Screen cuts for alpha equivalence and
one-assertion subsumption against P1 over the fully qualified ordinary interface,
including composite/repeated substitutions and relevant premise permutations.
An unresolved alias test is not NONALIAS. This is bounded baseline nonalias,
not proof that no equivalent theorem exists elsewhere.

Require a candidate recurring in both training families. Choose one using
training/development utility under the registered objective; publish all rejected
candidates and costs. Do not choose a named theorem or hard-code a winning cut.
No candidate means NO_NONALIAS_OPPORTUNITY_AT_THIS_SCOPE.

## 4. Admission and ordinary consumption

Project the selected cut into `$f/$e/$d` context and an ordinary `$p`, with
its proof reconstructed solely from allowed original assertions. Add no axiom.
A fresh library/checker/index binding must qualify that exact successor.
Preserve a source-to-candidate-to-proof dependency receipt and current eligibility.

Before real tasks, qualify authored composite/repeated/type/DV/ordered-port
controls for the actual consumer and added-label normalization. Do not infer
this capability from the completed alias-only control.
Producer then exits. A fresh consumer loads only sealed ordinary theorem state;
all discovery traces are unavailable for target-time hinting.

Use the theorem through ordinary assertion search and report its substitution,
premise proofs, selected search path and occurrence in the checked final proof.
A logged proposal, unused label, verbal explanation or successful hinted replay
does not establish causal consumption.

## 5. Minimal comparison and causal controls

- P0: unchanged base prefix, no acquired state.
- P1: P0 plus all whole training theorems; the main conventional comparator.
- P2: P1 plus the selected learned cut as an ordinary theorem.
- Removal: identical resident P2 state and loading costs, but disable every
  action/label alias of the learned cut; ordinary fallback remains.
- Matched control: P1 plus a training-only, natively checked nonselected candidate
  matched as closely as possible on size and acquisition/matching cost.
  Freeze the choice before evaluation and report matching differences; if none
  exists, mark this control unavailable rather than inventing one.

No privileged OCM wrapper is needed now. Export the same theorem state for use
by the unchanged ordinary host; this is the mechanism transplant. A later OCM
policy comparison must give that host the identical acquired information.

The main transfer witnesses must include a genuinely different enclosing
source family and a non-atomic substitution or new composition where qualified;
atomic renamings alone count only as interface reuse. Neither witness may be
selected retrospectively. If the frozen pool supplies none, report that boundary.

## 6. Complete costs and decision

Charge source preparation, training solving or imported-proof verification,
cut enumeration, alias screening, failed admission, development selection,
projection, indexing/grounding, resident/serialized bytes, cold load and restart,
search (including failures), proof construction, normalization, native checking,
eligibility lookup, one registered removal/restart, and ordinary fallback.
Report operation vectors, wall/CPU/RSS and bytes. Separate shared from
incremental costs; neither free training nor warm-only timings justify lifetime gain.

For the sealed demand order and resource regime, report cumulative cost versus
P1, every failure/timeout, and the first observed repayment point if one exists.
Keep censored timeouts explicit. Preserve both existing scheduler variants where
qualified; choosing the strongest by development data must precede evaluation.

Decision requires both real unhinted use and a reproducible causal cost/capability
difference from removal and P1. A native-valid method can be acquired yet fail to
repay: record that distinct result. One fast task or a shorter theorem-label proof
does not establish lifetime improvement or superiority outside this finite scope.

## 7. Diagnose and revive one stage

If two-node cuts are all aliases, one preregistered revival permits three logical
nodes on the same training allocation; keep P1 and evaluation sealed.
If recurrence remains absent, freeze a new exposed training opportunity tranche.
If a valid cut never fires, diagnose matcher/bank/selection separately; if matching
dominates, try an ordinary applicability index. If whole-theorem P1 suffices,
adopt P1. Only a measured control-flow/selection failure motivates options B/C.

## 8. Language-transfer boundary

Shared symbolic deduction or a controlled-language renderer is apparatus only.
A later language study must separately test acquisition from language,
paraphrase/composition holdouts, grounded interpretation and empirical warrant.
Imported formal facts, generated proof verbalizations and causally used acquired
methods are separate receipts. Formal validity cannot certify sentence meaning
or truth about the external world. No neural model enters OCM cognition.
