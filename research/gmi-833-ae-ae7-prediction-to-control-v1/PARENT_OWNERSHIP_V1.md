## The named residual of this tranche

1. **An exhaustive exact-rational census, in both directions, with counts.**
   The parents establish that predictive and control sufficiency differ. They
   do not say how often, nor in which direction, over a completely enumerated
   sensor lattice. Here all `4140` sensors are enumerated and both directions
   are attested with exact counts — including the counts that are `0`, which are
   reported rather than omitted, so the claim's utility-dependence is visible.

2. **The Helly failure of mergeability.** That a block is free iff its states
   share an optimal action is elementary. That this is **not** a pairwise
   property — a pairwise-compatible triple with empty common optimum exists and
   is exhibited — is the part a pairwise checker gets wrong, and it is made a
   detected hostile rather than a remark.

3. **The priced sensor ladder with prospectively frozen predictions.** Five
   falsifiable statements about how the selected representation moves as the
   sensing price rises, committed before any result existed, each recorded
   `HELD` or `FAILED` with its numbers, and the ladder proved non-vacuous.

4. **Exactness as a discipline, not a claim.** Every quantity is a `Fraction`;
   the test rejects a float literal or a logarithm anywhere in either route by
   AST scan. This is not novel mathematics — it is the reason every number in
   the reconciliation lines is reproducible from the receipt.

## What is explicitly NOT claimed

No general-intelligence claim. No infinite-horizon or continuous-state
extension. No energy or wall-clock measurement — the price is a declared
abstract unit. No architecture-selection law. The registered forbidden
promotions in `MANIFEST_V1.json` name each of these.
