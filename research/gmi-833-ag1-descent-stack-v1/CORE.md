## The result that matters

Two systems identical through `F0`–`F5` — same foundation, same process theory, same signature,
**same grammar**, same machine organization — diverge first at `F6`. Their capability coordinates
are exactly `1/2` and `0`. So no predicate evaluated at or below the grammar layer can separate
them. That is the layer-indexed form of "a grammar is not the intelligence boundary", and it is
consumed from `gmi-833-aj6-hst-layer-map-v1`'s pinned receipt (51 such pairs), not re-derived here.

## Honest residual, displayed rather than hidden

One node — `GRM_G0_EXTENSIONS_UNADJUDICATED` at `F4` — carries `lowering_status: UNKNOWN`. It covers
the registered stochastic, local-graph, channel/tool and governed-self-change operator families.
AJ5's `primitive_status` ledger covers only the five core opcodes. Assigning these four families an
AG5 status (`DERIVED / RESOURCE_IRREDUCIBLE_AT_SCOPE / SEMANTICALLY_IRREDUCIBLE_AT_SCOPE /
PRESENTATION_ONLY / UNKNOWN`) with a charged overhead is the next tranche.
