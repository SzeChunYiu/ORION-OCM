# GMI #833 Section Z / Z3 — invariance and representation independence

Closes the five rows of `### Z3` in issue comment `5684819296`.

## What it establishes

A registry of ten transformations covering all six categories the row names, and
over the registered `65552`-candidate binary mechanism universe and the `60`
frozen worlds:

- **invariance** `IV-2`: the group generated by surface renaming, state-label
  negation and input/output conjugation preserves `(bits, e_now, e_delay)`
  candidate-wise and leaves every winner class set unchanged — `0` alarms over
  `200` renamings, whole-universe conjugation (a bijection), whole-universe
  state-label negation and composite re-encoding (`65536` machines each, `0`
  breaks), and a direct full-scan control;
- **equivariance** `IV-3`: under `(eta, lambda) -> (c*eta, c*lambda)` the
  objective scales by `c`, `lambda*` scales by `c`, and the verdict is a function
  of `(p, lambda/eta)` alone, with boundary `mu* = p/2`;
- **the non-invariant quantity** `IV-4`: `12544` of the `65536` declared-stateful
  candidates are behaviourally identical to a stateless one, so declared state
  bits do not descend to the behavioural quotient. Charging effective state while
  labelling declared state flips the verdict on exactly `20` of `60` worlds;
- **the invariant replacement** `IV-5`: `bits_eff` agrees with the declared
  convention on all `60` worlds and survives dead padding that moves the declared
  one — so row 4's *both* branches land, not just the cheaper disjunct;
- **grammar bias** `IV-6`: `21904` behavioural classes, multiplicity ratio exactly
  `785`, `16` classes at description length `4` against `21888` at `16`, and exact
  reachability shares by radius (`40198` of `65552` nodes within radius `8`).

## What it does not establish

Representation independence in general; invariance under transformations outside
the registry; search or reachability invariance inferred from semantic
invariance; real compiler or hardware substrate invariance; grammar neutrality.
The declared-bit accounting is **not** shown to be wrong — it is internally
consistent and invariant under the whole registered group.
