# Z3 named results — invariance, equivariance and the non-invariant quantity

Scope for every result: the registered finite universe `U` of `65552`
architecture-name-free binary mechanisms (`16` stateless output tables over
`(mode, cur)`; `65536` one-state-bit `(nxt, table)` pairs over
`(state, mode, cur)`), scored over all `8` length-`3` input words in both modes
at `t = 1, 2` (`N = 16` scored moments per mode), objective

```
J(u) = eta * ( (1-p) * e_now(u)/16 + p * e_delay(u)/16 ) + lambda * bits(u),
```

and the `60` registered worlds of `gmi-833-heldout-20-transitions-v1`. Exact
rational arithmetic throughout; no float appears in any statement.

---

## `IV-1` — the transformation registry

**Statement.** `TRANSFORMATION_REGISTRY_V1.json` registers ten transformations
covering all six categories the row names — renaming, semantics-preserving
recoding, compiler/substrate changes, equivalent grammar transformations, unit
changes, equivalent state encodings — each with a status
(`CLAIMED_IRRELEVANT`, `CLAIMED_IRRELEVANT_EQUIVARIANT` or `DECLARED_RELEVANT`),
an exact definition, and the named quantity it must or must not move.

**Falsifier.** A `CLAIMED_IRRELEVANT` entry that moves its quantity on clean
data; a `DECLARED_RELEVANT` entry that cannot be shown to move it; a row-1
category with no entry.

**Forbidden extrapolation.** `COMPLETE_TRANSFORMATION_REGISTRY`. The registry is
what this package registers, not a claim that no other transformation exists.

## `IV-2` — invariance under the semantics-and-structure preserving group

**Statement.** Let `G` be the group generated by `T1` (surface renaming), `T2`
(state-label negation, with the start state transported), `T3` (simultaneous
input/output symbol negation) and their composite `T4`. For every `g in G` and
every registered world, `sigma = (bits, e_now, e_delay)` is preserved
candidate-wise, the multiset `{sigma(u)}` is preserved, and the winner class set
is unchanged.

**Evidence.** `200` seeded renamings: `0` verdict alarms, `0` multiset breaks,
and a direct full scan of the permuted `65552`-candidate list on three seeds x
`60` worlds agrees on every verdict. `T3` applied to the whole universe: `0`
`sigma` breaks, `0` verdict alarms, and it is a bijection of the universe
(`conj_counts == base_counts`). `T2` and `T4` applied to the whole
universe (`65536` machines each): `0` breaks. Both routes agree.

**Assumptions.** `T2` acts on the pair (machine, start state); the registered
universe fixes the start to `0`, so `T2` maps a machine started at `0` to its
relabelled twin started at `1`, and it is that pair whose `sigma` is preserved.

**Falsifier.** One `g in G` and one candidate with `sigma(g u) != sigma(u)`.

**Strongest parents.** `gmi-833-remint-equivariance-v1` owns finite semantic
presentation-relabeling equivariance and already proves identity, inverse and
composition, and canonical fingerprint invariance. Nothing of that is claimed
here. The residual is the transport of that notion onto the model selection
verdict over the registered worlds.

**Forbidden extrapolation.** `SEARCH_INVARIANCE_FROM_SEMANTIC_INVARIANCE` —
the parent explicitly refuses that inference and so does this package;
`REAL_COMPILER_OR_HARDWARE_SUBSTRATE_INVARIANCE`.

## `IV-3` — equivariance under transformed resource accounting

**Statement.** Under `T5`, the common positive rational rescaling
`(eta, lambda) -> (c*eta, c*lambda)`, every objective value scales by `c`, the
threshold scales as `lambda* -> c*lambda*`, and the argmin class set is
unchanged. Consequently the prediction is a function of the dimensionless pair
`(p, mu)` with `mu = lambda/eta` alone, and the boundary is `mu* = p/2`. The
error weight and the state price are not separately meaningful; only their ratio
is.

**Evidence.** `0` equivariance breaks over `60` worlds x a `6`-rung rational
ladder; the `60` worlds carry `15` distinct `(p, mu)` keys with `0` determinism
breaks; both routes agree.

**Falsifier.** A world pair with equal `(p, mu)` and different winner class sets;
any `c` for which the argmin value is not exactly `c` times the original.

**Forbidden extrapolation.** Nonlinear or non-additive resource accounting is not
covered.

## `IV-4` — the declared state-bit count is **not** a semantic invariant

**Statement.** Define behavioural equivalence as identity of the full `48`-symbol
output trace (8 words x 2 modes x 3 timesteps). Then `12544` of the `65536`
declared-stateful candidates are behaviourally equivalent to a stateless
candidate. Declared state bits therefore do not descend to the behavioural
quotient.

**Exact census.** `12544` behaviourally stateless declared-stateful candidates;
the *table-ignores-state* syntactic family is `4096`, so the syntactic
definition undercounts by exactly `49/16`; `8448` behaviourally stateless
candidates lie outside it, and `272` of them have a constant next-state table.

**Consequence — the adversarial recoding.** A model-class verdict requires an
*accounting* convention (what `lambda` is charged on) and a *labelling*
convention (what class name is reported). If accounting charges effective state
while labelling reads declared state, the winner class set changes on exactly
`20` of the `60` registered worlds — every high-side endpoint — from
`{STATELESS}` to `{STATELESS, PERSISTENT_STATE}`. Dead-padding the population
(replacing every stateless candidate by its behaviourally identical `16`-bit
state-ignoring encoding, which changes no external behaviour) moves the purely
declared convention on `40` worlds.

**What this is and is not.** It is not a falsification of the boundary law. The
registered corpus charges and labels declared bits, which is internally
consistent, and `IV-2`/`IV-3` show that convention is invariant under the whole
semantics-and-structure preserving group. What fails is the *cross-convention*
statement: `bits` is a property of the presentation, not of the behaviour.

**Falsifier.** A behaviourally stateless declared-stateful candidate that does
not exist (`n_bs = 0`), or a mixed-convention run that moves no verdict.

**Strongest parents.** Myhill–Nerode state minimisation (A. Nerode, *Linear
automaton transformations*, Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. **9** (1958) 541,
DOI 10.1090/S0002-9939-1958-0135681-9) owns the fact that declared states can be
redundant. This package does not claim that. The residual is the exact census in
this registered universe and the demonstration that the redundancy reaches the
model-class verdict through a convention mismatch.

**Forbidden extrapolation.** `DECLARED_STATE_BIT_ACCOUNTING_IS_WRONG`,
`FLAGSHIP_THEORY_FALSIFIED_BY_RECODING`.

## `IV-5` — the invariant replacement, and row 4's first branch

**Statement.** Let `bits_eff(u) = min { bits(v) : v behaviourally equivalent to
u }`. Charging **and** labelling `bits_eff` gives, on the registered worlds,
exactly the same verdicts as the declared convention (`60/60`), and is invariant
under dead padding (`0` flips) where the declared convention moves (`40` flips).

Together with `IV-2` and `IV-3` this delivers **both** branches of row 4, rather
than the disjunction taken to avoid the work:

- *branch 1 — the conclusions survive*: under the group preserving semantics and
  declared structure, every registered verdict is unchanged;
- *branch 2 — the non-invariant quantity is characterized*: it is the declared
  state-bit count, its exact failure census is `12544`, and its invariant
  replacement is `bits_eff`.

**Falsifier.** Any world where the two consistent conventions disagree; any dead
padding that moves the effective convention.

## `IV-6` — description-length and reachability bias

**Statement (description-length bias).** `U` carries `21904` behavioural classes.
Class multiplicity ranges from `1` to `785`, a ratio of exactly `785`; the
largest class occupies `785/65552` of the universe against a uniform share of
`1/21904`. Exactly `16` classes have minimum description length `4` bits and
`21888` have `16` bits: the grammar spends `16` of its short words on `16`
classes and its remaining `65536` long words on `21904`.

**Statement (reachability bias).** Under the registered mutation graph —
single-bit flips inside a description word plus the canonical widen/narrow edge
between a `4`-bit stateless word and its zero-next-state dead-padded `16`-bit
encoding — starting from the all-zero stateless word, `40198` of `65552` nodes
are reached within radius `8`. Declared-stateless candidates occupy `1/4097` of
`U` but `1` of the radius-`0` ball, `5/6` of radius `1`, `11/32` of radius `2`
and `15/214` of radius `3`: a bias of `+20479/24582` at radius `1` decaying to
`+61241/876758` at radius `3`. Effective-stateless candidates occupy `785/4097`
of `U` and `99/107` of the radius-`3` ball.

**Falsifier.** A uniform multiplicity distribution, or a radius where the
declared bias is exactly `0` (either would mean the grammar is unbiased and the
row has nothing to quantify).

**Strongest parents.** `gmi-833-g0-grammar-bias-v1` owns the separation of
semantic coverage, description bias and reachability bias, and owns the
isometric-relabeling invariance theorem together with the same-semantics
counterexample that reverses a grammar-relative choice. This package transports
those three quantities to the binary transducer universe and reports them; it
does not re-derive the separation.

**Forbidden extrapolation.** `GRAMMAR_NEUTRALITY`. The numbers quantify the bias;
they do not remove it, and nothing here licenses a claim that search behaves the
same under a different grammar.
