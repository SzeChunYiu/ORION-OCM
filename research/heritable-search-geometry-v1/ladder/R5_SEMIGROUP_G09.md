## Semigroup nuance HSG records (not owns)

⟨O⟩-closure mixes NON-identical kernels K_t (non-homogeneous products). Dobrushin's
nonstationary bound covers exactly this: the contraction of a product is at most the
product of coefficients, so coalescence needs only Σ_t (1 − δ(K_t)) = ∞, not
sup δ < 1. This stronger quantitative form is the parent's actual theorem (it is the
tool his 1956 CLT uses); HSG cites, does not re-prove.
