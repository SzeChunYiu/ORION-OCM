# A_T18 @ R7 — burden telescoping under task inflow; @ R6 self-chosen ε

**Verdict: LIFT_CONDITIONAL.** Two named conditions: FLUX-DRIFT (R7) and
EPS-PRECOMMIT (R6). Without them the telescoping bound is not merely weakened
— it is ill-posed, because B_0 is not fixed and measures are not conserved.
**PARENT_STATEMENT_UNVERIFIED_TEXT**: Baez–Fong 2015 (⚠) used only for the
stationarity-under-flux vocabulary; no exact theorem claimed.

## Part 1 — @ R7: telescoping with non-conserved burden

Frozen-scope form (parent): total verification burden telescopes to a fixed
budget B_0 — each level's burden is charged against an initial endowment, so
Σ_t b_t ≤ B_0.

Under task inflow the sum acquires new summands after launch: tasks arrive at
rate λ with per-task burden drawn from a distribution with mean b̄, and
processing retires burden at rate μ. The frozen statement fails in the strong
sense: with λ b̄ > μ, Σ b_t diverges and there is no finite B_0 at all.

What survives (exact statement): with T the random set of admitted tasks,

  Σ_{i ∈ T} b_i  ≤  B_0 + Σ_{i ∈ T_inflow} b_i   (admission ledger form)

and the telescoping BOUND is meaningful iff the open system is stable:
burden backlog is positive recurrent iff λ·E[b] < μ (drift condition).
Then expected steady-state total burden is finite, O(B_0 + λE[b]/(μ − λE[b])),
and the frozen bound reappears as the closed-system corner λ = 0. Burden is
not conserved; it is a queueing quantity, and the honest R7 bound is a
drift-conditional one. Admission control (which tasks enter T) is the only
lever that keeps the condition satisfiable — inflow governance is therefore
part of the bound, not an operational afterthought.

## Part 2 — @ R6: self-chosen ε

If the process selects its own tolerance ε_t (acceptance threshold, exit
criterion), the telescoping charge depends on a quantity chosen by the charged
party. Two regimes:

1. ε chosen BEFORE the evidence it is applied to (pre-committed in C_t):
   charges are honest; the telescoping bound survives with ε_t in place of a
   fixed ε, and each ε amendment is billed like any amendment (T04 CHARGE).
2. ε chosen AFTER observing evidence: the process can lower ε exactly where
   lowering is cheap and raise it where retiring burden is expensive. This is
   a selection channel (T08 leak mode; T17 tamper channel) and it unbounds
   the telescoping inequality from inside — no amount of initial budget B_0
   fixes it, because the per-step charge is now self-negotiated.

Condition named: EPS-PRECOMMIT — ε_t is fixed in the constitution prior to
the observation it judges; violations are treated as tamper-channel events
(R7), not as parameter choices.
