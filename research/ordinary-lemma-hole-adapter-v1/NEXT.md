# Next: qualify a real native caller

The structural adapter is ready for a separately designed caller. It matches one
supplied target node and one unfolded child-port path; a local NO_MATCH is not a
complete search or a certificate that reusable structure is absent.

The current interface has three homogeneous wff/class parameters, empty
source/active/applied distinct-variable obligations, at most 256 target nodes,
2–8 semantic body applications and 4,096 emitted normal proof labels. Existing
constructor transport excludes zero-arity assertion steps. Unsupported cases
remain explicit; these controls do not widen the qualified native context.

Before relying on a replacement, bind the currently admitted lemma and its
proof, current library/axiom inventory and source order, exact target statement,
typed external hypotheses, current evidence/registry liveness and complete new
proof bytes. Obtain a fresh exact native check of the rewritten whole proof.
Do not substitute a saved synthetic trace marker or a caller-provided digest for
that authority. Do not retain a target-assertion shortcut or an unissued label.

Then test actual retrieval and causal reuse against the equally equipped ordinary
persistent-lemma parent, with appropriate withheld/chronological closure and all
acquisition, retrieval, verification and revision costs. Neither a native gate
nor such a reuse experiment is created by this source/control publication.
