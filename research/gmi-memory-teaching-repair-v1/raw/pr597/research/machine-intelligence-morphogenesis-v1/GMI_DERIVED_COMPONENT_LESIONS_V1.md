## The result: a derived double dissociation

> **Removing procedural memory costs +148.3 % in serving and *zero* bits of retention.**
> **Removing semantic memory costs +300 % in retention and *zero* serving cost.**

A double dissociation is the standard evidence that two systems are distinct rather than one system
described two ways. Here it is **derived rather than observed**: the two lesions load on orthogonal
coordinates of the charged ledger because the conditions that produced them are different conditions —
PVR-3's reuse threshold for one, CSR-1's retention width for the other.

That independently strengthens the partition in `GMI_MEMORY_REGIME_PARTITION_V1.md`, which had argued
distinctness from four different growth laws. Growth laws and dissociation are independent arguments for
the same conclusion, and both hold.

The episodic and working lesions have a different character again: episodic loss is **graded** and set by
the gap between `N_t` and capacity, while working loss is **total** rather than graded, because
within-event state is not a store to be made smaller — without it no multi-step event can be served at
all. Four components, three distinct deficit *shapes*.

## Scope, stated plainly

**These are lesions of derived components inside this accounting**, not of biological structures. The
double dissociation is a statement about which ledger coordinate each condition controls. **No claim is
made that it predicts any neuropsychological finding**, and items 25–28 remain absent — that comparison
would require the biological half the corpus does not have.

**Prediction status:** derived, not prospective. Each figure follows arithmetically from the law being
tested, so agreement confirms internal consistency rather than supplying independent evidence. The
non-trivial content is the *dissociation structure*, which is not something either law states directly.

**Falsifier:** a lesion of one derived component that moves the ledger coordinate assigned to another —
for example a procedural lesion that changes retention width, or a semantic lesion that changes serving
cost. Either would show the components are not separable in the accounting, collapsing both this and the
partition.
