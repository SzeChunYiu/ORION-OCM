# Bounded alias and one-assertion subsumption screen

Prospective specification. No checker or screening result is implemented here.

## Freeze what complete means

P1 contains the permitted base assertions and every whole training theorem.
Bind its exact typed contracts and the consumer's qualified grammar, variable
universe, allowed terms, syntax/DV context, hypothesis-use rules and resource
bounds. Include valid composite and repeated bindings; do not reduce the ordinary
interface to the recipe's six atomic cases.

Specify either a complete decision procedure for this domain or a finite
enumeration with coverage evidence. Finite term/bank restrictions qualify only
that domain, not unrestricted Metamath substitution. A resource cutoff,
unsupported construct, incomplete enumeration or unknown syntax proof yields
UNKNOWN, never a negative alias claim. Charge and preserve each cause.

## Account for every premise occurrence and allowed use

Screen alpha-equivalent full assertion frames and derivation of the candidate
boundary by a direct allowed hypothesis return or one existing logical assertion,
together with required syntax proofs.

Retain all ordered mandatory floating and essential ports, including repeated
occurrences, and bind the conclusion and DV constraints. Match each essential
occurrence to an allowed supplied hypothesis proof in the candidate context under
one consistent typed substitution. Preserve emitted stack order even when the
chosen hypotheses have another input order.

Do not require a bijection between hypothesis lists. Native-valid reuse of a
supplied proof can discharge repeated ports; candidate hypotheses can remain
unused when the registered context permits it. Account for ambient hypotheses
only if the input contract supplies them. A one-to-one matcher or equality of
premise counts is not a complete test.

A one-assertion alias receives no uncharged logical derivations of missing premises.
A broader subsumption test allowing such derivations must declare its additional
proof-search scope, native warrant and costs. Use the actual native discipline,
not an assumed structural rule; see the ordinary interface in MECHANISMS.md.

## Results

- ALIAS_FOUND: retain an explicit substitution, every hypothesis-port witness,
  syntax proofs and a natively checkable boundary proof.
- SCREENED_NEGATIVE_IN_DOMAIN: complete coverage of the declared interface found
  no alias; record its boundaries and completion evidence.
- UNKNOWN: incomplete or unqualified comparison; keep the candidate unresolved
  and exclude it from nonalias-based conclusions.

A witness suffices for a positive finding; absence needs coverage. These results
do not decide global theorem novelty or useful search-control learning.
Known mathematics can still support acquired ranking/applicability utility.
Current theorem-acquisition eligibility remains unchanged; a control-selection
study requires separate registration and relevant search telemetry.
