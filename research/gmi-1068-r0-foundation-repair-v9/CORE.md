# Foundation recovery: read first

This successor addresses two different questions: whether the original R0
governance requirements were delivered and checked, and when one declared
semantic input can be recovered from another.

- [Semantic theorem and countermodels](THEORY_V9.md).
- [General kernel proofs](RecoverabilityV9.lean).
- [Original R0 adjudication](R0_ADJUDICATION_V9.md) and [independent review](REVIEW_V9.md).
- [Freeze](FREEZE_V9.md), [source ownership](PARENTS_V9.json) and
  [replay receipt](RESULT_V9.json).
- [Independent semantic reconstruction](independent_semantic_v9.py).

Recoverability means a decoder exists on the attained image. It is equivalent
to the target being constant whenever the retained input is equal.
A collision refutes every such decoder. This property survives declared
invertible re-encodings, while a count of primitive symbols does not.

The process/context countermodels use one ambient history domain. They do not
hide admissibility inside the evaluator's type. The examples support semantic
independence in that model class; they do not prove a unique ultimate ontology.

R0 closure is governance evidence only. Mathematical derivations, all-family
recovery, real-scale experiments and novel verified predictions remain separate
requirements of #1068. Historical #833 evidence and original R0 bytes remain.
