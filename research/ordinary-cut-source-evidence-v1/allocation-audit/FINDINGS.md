# What the metadata establishes

## Corpus and index

The public corpus is set.mm commit 7ddd528c948ae375618ad1ca476b28b9a0eec7d6,
historically acquired as 51,126,635 bytes, SHA-256 7b70cd8cca88aeb72a8dd97029d0b506015fb0325afec581cdc9add8ca0c8547.
Acquisition, readiness, both census contracts and the authored preparation manifest
agree. The audit checked metadata and the current file size; it did not reread or
rehash the corpus. A later authorized consumer must bind its actual bytes.

The qualified historical region ends before ssind.1: 1,757,201 raw bytes,
4,095 proved assertions and 96 trusted assertions. The preparation source closed
one active scope, then used unchanged trace_source.index to generate all 4,191
ordinary contracts. The closed prefix and PARENT have distinct, retained hashes.
See [LINEAGE.json](LINEAGE.json) for the exact chain.

There is no newly established index for ordinals 4,096–4,223. The fixed chronological
128-root proposal is meaningful without looking at outcomes, but its labels,
dependency exclusions and additional axiom inventory require the separately gated
custodian release. All released whole theorems must enter P1 even when their cut
interface is unsupported. Original P0 cannot be reduced.

## Allocation reconciliation

Both exact Metamath census contracts say that full per-root decisions remain
internal and that no train/dev/hidden assignment was made. The acquisition readiness
also records partitions_created=false. The 24-task programme and its 6/6/12
necessary screen therefore do not establish a protected set of native labels.

The six public proof anchors remain exposed apparatus material. The subsequent
constructor, class chunk and typed-wff lifecycle contracts use those anchors or
explicit authored extensions, while preserving the same ordinary P0 catalogue.
Internal census outcomes remain unopened in this audit.

A genuine older allocation was found: the Lean F1 registry freezes four identities
from a separate Lean commit, with reference support explicitly exposed. Its complete
assignment metadata was read, while proof blobs and outcome rows were not.
The unary assay also has real split rules, but its inputs are generated ASTs rather
than set.mm labels. All generated rows, semantic keys and final outcomes remain
unopened. No claim of semantic independence across those domains is made.

## What remains unknown

The search followed explicit input/authority records and examined older registration
families discovered from complete top-level inventories. The inventories are
navigation evidence, not a proof that no other registry exists. Other hosts,
unregistered sessions and unreferenced lanes were not exhaustively audited.

Consequently this is a bounded attestation draft. Its empty known Metamath
protected-label list cannot satisfy a universal completeness declaration. Root can
define the authoritative registry universe for the new training-only release,
supply any additional allocation metadata, and issue the appropriate scoped
declaration. If that declaration is bounded, the release input contract must say so
instead of reusing its current global-coverage sentinel.

The proposed release remains exactly ordinals 4,096–4,223, with no replacements.
Later corpus bodies remain sealed. A protected P0 assertion is a conflict; protected
new roots or dependency descendants are excluded and retained. Native trace export,
fresh authority and complete P1 construction are separate unopened gates.

## Read and execution limits

[SOURCE-METADATA.json](SOURCE-METADATA.json) distinguishes full metadata/source reads
from selected-field inspection of decoded JSON. The historical readiness/inspection
records contain already exposed anchor material; they were not claimed to be
byte-free of every proof detail. No new corpus body, per-root census outcome,
generated task row, native run, learner call or training release was opened.
