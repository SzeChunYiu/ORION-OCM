# Weighted transient transfer

A confidence-set certificate now connects data-selected policies to unbounded
undiscounted expected work on a common measurable or countable interface.
Geometric weighted drift supplies properness, summable tails and a uniform
cost-error bound; the selected policy's comparator stays in the same certified
class. This is an application of mature weighted transient MDP theory.

1. [The theorem and proof](WEIGHTED_TRANSIENT_TRANSFER_THEOREM_V1.md)
2. [Decisive countermodels and constructive rescue](BOUNDARIES_AND_WITNESSES_V1.md)
3. [Matched parents, exact scope and full charges](PARENTS_AND_COSTS_V1.md)
4. [Exact control receipt](RECEIPT_V1.json) and
   [validation](VALIDATION_V1.md)

Coverage alone does not produce a certificate. Empirical properness, or even
properness in two close models, does not give uniform undiscounted cost
transfer. All revealed policy-relevant signals must be represented. The
general theorem does not assert a physical kernel, observable sufficient
state, arbitrary support confidence bound, controller synthesis or new learned
capability. Finite controls do not prove its general quantifiers.

Run the static whole-payload replay with CPython 3.12:

    python3 -I -B replay_v1.py

MANIFEST_V1.json binds every other file, including the complete receipt,
sources, tests and four pinned parent documents. Replay anchors the initial
manifest and expected payload across the worker and compares all output bytes.
This checks the supplied packet's consistency; an externally retained manifest
digest identifies the intended publication. It does not authenticate a host
or the physical truth of a source record.
