# GMI recursive theory: read first

This is a conditional scientific programme, with delivered proofs,
constructive witnesses and separately unexecuted empirical obligations.
It does not assert universal completion or unique architectural necessity.

The [fifteen-question ledger](FIFTEEN_GAP_CLOSURE_LEDGER_V1.md) now links the
delivered derivations to their remaining premises. The complete
[formal derivation route](../gmi-formal-derivation-v1/CORE.md) is the compact
proof index; its [scope matrix](../gmi-formal-derivation-v1/SCOPE.md) states
what each milestone does and does not establish.

## Foundations and adaptive composition

- [Minimal axiom freeze](MINIMAL_AXIOM_FREEZE_V1.md) factors the existing
  eight-field master tuple into five architecture-neutral groups. It does
  not establish an absolute minimal or unique foundation.
- [Global adaptive validity](GLOBAL_ADAPTIVE_COMPOSITION_THEOREM_V1.md)
  requires validity relative to the complete global past. Repeated visits,
  dependent rows, predictable births and unbounded monitoring preserve that
  conditional theorem, not arbitrary sampler truth.
- GAC5 now requires founded dependencies with established bases or an
  independently initialized invariant. Its [full correction](../gmi-grand-unification-v1/GAC_WELL_FOUNDED_COMPOSITION_CORRECTION_V1.md)
  preserves the original source and infinite-chain countermodel.
- The [learning/memory theorem](FORMAL_LEARNING_MEMORY_THEORY_V1.md)
  complements the formal unit's LEARNING and MEMORY results. Evaluation
  classes, complete decoder information and convergence modes are explicit.

## Downstream derivations

The formal route supplies contextual representations, finite realizations,
conditional optimization, memory/revision, causal identification, planning,
paid metareasoning and a certified executable-structure learner.
Each theorem requires its declared model, access, obligation, realization and
cost contracts. Representability does not select an architecture; finite
witnesses do not prove infinite quantifiers or authenticate deployment.

The learner is a supplied construction with conditional progress/convergence,
not an established worldwide novelty or empirical superiority result.
[Prospective falsifiers](../gmi-formal-derivation-v1/FALSIFIABILITY.md)
and broad experimental comparison remain unexecuted. Neural realizability
is an existence/comparator result and does not alter OCM's non-neural
executable-structure cognition route.
