# Exact finite evidence and portable replay

This packet contains synthetic finite relations and proofs, not measured GMI
performance. The source-bound receipt is regenerated by the stated exact
algorithms and checked field for field; optimized Python must agree too.

Run in this directory with CPython 3.12 and the standard library only:

```sh
python3 -m unittest -v test_joint_relation_v1
python3 -O -m unittest -v test_joint_relation_v1
python3 replay_v1.py
python3 -O replay_v1.py
```

All actual verification is performed on the Linux laptop. Replay requires no
Git repository, external package, network, timing sample or ecology calls.
Internal parent snapshots preserve their original bytes. The manifest binds
all source, documentation, parent and receipt files without binding itself;
the receipt binds its seven direct scientific inputs, including its checker.
Source binding establishes byte identity, not truth or historical execution.

The local census covers all 343 nonempty three-input/three-action relations,
comparing the solver with actual adequate selectors and encoder/decoder tables.
The joint census covers all pairs of binary-action relations with input sizes each
ranging from one through three. Every adequate joint selector is enumerated, and the
constructed encoder is checked on every pair against the original relations.
Exact singleton obligations retain the 729-pair function multiplicativity test.

The triangle additionally enumerates every one of its 262,144 joint selectors.
The C5 proof uses an independent 32-selector local optimum, every local-selector
pair for the optimized product parent, the row incidence lower bound, and all
25 joint executions. It does not enumerate the 4^25 joint selectors. Its
64,566 tested eight-subsets are construction work, not a search proving that
seven symbols are impossible. That lower result comes from 15 incidences and
at most two first-input rows per message.

Named countercontrols challenge pairgraph substitution, multiplicativity
outside its function/product scope, area-only lower bounds, bit-rounding
claims, missing inputs, altered outputs, invalid action/message labels and
manifest byte mutation. Positive controls include constant actions, exact
functions, redundant unused actions and unbalanced product input registers.

## Observed validation

CPython 3.12.13 on the Linux laptop passed all 15 tests normally and all 15
with optimization. The derived receipt contains 1,521 joint relation pairs,
1,045,984 actual joint selectors and 10,404 checked constructed input pairs.
The local 343-task census examines 1,728 adequate selectors and 5,579
encoder/decoder tables through the first witness at the minimum alphabet.
Normal and optimized portable replay compare the complete derived receipt.
