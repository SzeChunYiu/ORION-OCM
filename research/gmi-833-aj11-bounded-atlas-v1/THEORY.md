### Why quotienting does not erase presentations

All-word operational equivalence preserves registered task capability, but four operational classes contain both stateless and one-bit realizations with different raw resource vectors. Reachability also lives on presentations. The complete atlas therefore stores presentation records and a semantic quotient map rather than pretending the quotient alone contains every scientific coordinate.

Under these exact finite conditions, the scoped terminal `COMPLETE_GMI_ATLAS_AT_BOUND_B` is justified. It means complete enumeration/quotient/evaluation/development accounting for this frozen universe only.

## Unbounded theorem boundary

For standard unbounded Turing-complete computation, program syntax is effectively enumerable, but a general computable complete semantic classifier would decide nontrivial extensional semantic properties/program equivalence and thereby conflict with standard Rice/halting/equivalence undecidability results. Those theorems remain parent-owned.

Thus finite completeness does not extrapolate to a terminating semantic catalogue of all unbounded machine intelligences. The required unbounded stance is instead:

`open generativity + relative barriers + blind recovery/prediction + explicit UNKNOWN`.
