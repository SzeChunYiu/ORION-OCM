# The six urgent requests: exact closure map

Read [CORE](CORE.md) first. “Proved” below means an explicit mathematical
derivation with finite executable witnesses, not proof-assistant verification.
All six stronger research goals remain distinguishable from scoped theorems.

| Request | Concrete result in this unit | What it does not yet establish |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Derive known families without templates | Complete raw finite tables yield inferred affine support/symmetries and scalar realizations; generic Boolean syntax yields conditional/eager programs; arbitrary coded-state search yields memory selection; scalar semiring laws yield constraint/probability/min-cost computation. | Unique MLP, LSTM, Transformer, MoE, GNN or RAG architecture from unrestricted raw experience. |
| 2. Quantitative architecture laws | Exact bias–variance–resource law for fixed affine subspaces; acquisition–retention–retrieval optimum; bounded-program instruction-price transitions; width-sensitive elimination costs. | Universal Transformer-versus-RNN or hardware-neural-versus-memory phase diagrams. |
| 3. Neutral rediscovery | Executable bounded generic program generation, all retained-code encodings, exhaustive finite permutation inference, and complete finite elimination-order search with charged work. | A prior-free or encoding-invariant search over all computational structures. Factorization and arithmetic laws remain explicit premises. |
| 4. Held-out family prediction | Predictions frozen before function/parameter/action/graph holdout execution, with source-bound falsifiers. | A genuinely hidden architecture family independent of theory construction. That experiment is still required. |
| 5. A new intelligence form | Operational reduction criteria and positive quantitative rediscovery mechanisms; historical parents explicitly credited. | A previously unknown form surviving all appropriately scoped reductions. An implemented computable candidate cannot escape an unrestricted program class. |
| 6. Complete domain basis | Constructive coverage of every finite explicit factor instance in the registered algebras; repairs to analog, quantum, local-oracle and complexity reduction claims. | An exhaustive independent taxonomy of physically possible intelligence domains. |

## Why these are substantive additions

The former V2/V3 neutral runners evaluated supplied competitor costs. This
unit generates and executes implementations. The raw-table path does not
receive the locality, symmetry or family names used to characterize its
output. The memory lower bound quantifies over arbitrary retained encodings
and adaptive reads; its optimum is not assumed to be a cache. Factor
elimination handles cyclic and relational instances with a compiled solving
cost, beyond merely saying that finite constraints can be encoded.

[Known-family derivations](KNOWN_FAMILY_DERIVATIONS.md) makes the premises
and missing selection steps explicit for every named family in the request.
[Predictive closure](PREDICTIVE_CLOSURE.md) gives the global certificate and
corrects the old single-witness rejection rule.

## Concrete next obligations, not silently discarded gaps

1. Register operational definitions and a sufficiently rich low-level
   candidate universe for neural nonlinear layers, recurrent gates,
   attention, retrieval-generation and planning compositions. Show coverage
   or publish the unresolved residue. Prove why their identifying properties
   beat every admitted alternative under a common resource model.
2. Have an independent holder choose families after the prediction map and
   grammar freeze. Prevent family/template information from entering the
   search; compare generated executable results and all tied properties.
3. Calibrate acquisition, synthesis, execution, memory, communication and
   precision costs on realistic substrates; test size transport prospectively
   against strong matched parent implementations.
4. For any proposed new form, state the exact uniform reduction class and
   a separation theorem or lower bound before claiming domain novelty.
5. For wider domain coverage, define physical interfaces and precision
   contracts before treating analog, constructive or quantum behavior as
   cheaply reducible to finite digital programs.

These are scientific requirements. They cannot be discharged by marking a
receipt green or assuming the desired architecture in an added axiom.
