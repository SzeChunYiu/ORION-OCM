# GMI: executable mechanism derivations

This unit turns several architecture-property laws into executable finite
derivations. It adds actual program generation, inferred finite ecological
structure, arbitrary-code memory selection and compiled factor inference.
It preserves the earlier formal1858 unit and grand theorem register.

**The six stronger requests are not all closed.** Known mechanisms are
derived under explicit contracts; hidden-family prediction, realistic-scale
selection and a previously unknown intelligence form remain unestablished.
Start with the [six-gap map](SIX_GAPS.md) for that distinction.

[Evidence and replay](EVIDENCE.md) records the registrations, source bindings,
independent controls and exact scope. [Source audit](NEUTRAL_SOURCE_AUDIT.md)
explains why historical supplied-cost comparisons were insufficient.
[Primary sources](ECOLOGY_SOURCES.md) distinguish inherited mechanisms from
the new integration. No neural learner is introduced into OCM cognition.

The generated programs are conventional computational mechanisms with
established parents. This work adds rigorous explicit derivations and
prospective finite tests; it does not establish invention of those mechanisms.
