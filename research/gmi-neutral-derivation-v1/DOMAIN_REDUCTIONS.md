# What the domain reductions establish

Status: scoped synthesis and a new constructive factor-compilation bridge.
Historical domain files remain frozen. This note does not turn a finite
compiler or universal interpreter into a complete taxonomy of intelligence.

## DR1: the existing completeness claim is conditional coverage

The prior domain-derivation note distinguishes catalogue, reduction map,
necessity map, predictive laws, neutral recovery, discovery and recursive
closure. Its explicit status is DC0 strong, DC1/DC2 partial, DC3–DC6 open.
The later `GMI_CLASSICAL_DOMAIN_REDUCTION_COMPLETION_V3.md` terminal means
no known unreduced ordinary-classical candidate under registered matched
primitives. It does not prove D1–D8 are a complete independent basis.

A reduction must preserve the protected execution/development response on
the registered interventions and declare its resource transformation. A
family-level theorem requires one effective uniform compiler and a bound
across the family. "Every fixed finite size is simulable" is weaker than a
polynomial-family or constant-factor reduction. One-way simulation is not
bidirectional equivalence, and the accepted transform class must be closed
under the actual composition rule. Products of relative overhead ratios
cannot silently be treated as composition of total-size transforms.

## DR2: exact placement of the requested unusual forms

| Form | Established reduction and decisive assumptions |
|---|---|
| Relational/constraint/sheaf | Finite local sections and explicit restriction/compatibility maps become CSP variables/factors. Protected gluing is a satisfying assignment. Encoding is not yet a solver complexity theorem. |
| HDC/VSA | Finite componentwise binding, addition, permutation and similarity become array programs. Componentwise work is linear in dimension; clean-up also depends on codebook size. |
| Cellular/local field | Fixed finite local rule, bounded degree and synchronous topology become one process per cell with matched communication and synchronization. |
| Constructor/autocatalytic | Explicit finite local constructor rules become distributed programs when granted the same construction ports, actuator semantics and replication schedule. |
| Population/hereditary | Explicit finite population and computable charged fitness/variation become population programs with matched processors. |
| Analog/reaction | Finite encodings or a certified approximation become stochastic/dynamical programs; this does not establish matched physical precision, energy, latency or sensing costs. |

These are the content of CR-1–CR-4 in
`GMI_CANDIDATE_DOMAIN_REDUCTION_THEOREMS_V2.md` and CR-5–CR-9 in the later
completion note. They preserve useful mechanism distinctions. They do not
establish a new domain through a renamed state carrier.

## DR3: positive closure supplied by the new compiler

FC1–FC4 construct an exact algorithm for every finite explicit factor instance
over the three registered semirings, including cyclic graphs. The derivation
uses finite distributivity; its circuit consists of scalar operations and
table accesses, with no global-solver primitive. Every variable order is a
sound program, and finite complete order search returns its true minimum
registered score, with ties and search work retained.

For a finite local-section system, create one variable per local section and
compatibility factors. Restriction-map equalities can instead introduce an
overlap variable with factors \(o=r_i(s_i)\), \(o=r_j(s_j)\). This retains
linear-size explicit restriction tables; materializing every compatible pair
of local sections can have quadratic size and must be charged if chosen.
The Boolean compiler then decides whether compatible global sections exist.
Its width-dependent bound provides the missing constructive solving cost.

This closes finite scalar-query semantics for that entire encoded family,
and supplies Bayesian partition and min-cost specializations under their
additional factor assumptions. It does not prove a tight reduction to a
native unit-price "global glue" oracle: native cost and accepted overhead
must still be compared. A supplied factor model is also different from
discovering its variables, factors or semiring from unstructured experience.

## DR4: concrete boundaries and repairs

**Local consistency is insufficient.** Three Boolean variables constrained
pairwise unequal on a triangle admit support for each value on each edge,
but no global assignment. A generic unsatisfiable CSP need not have a short
single-edge/cycle certificate. Elimination or full enumeration solves this
finite example; bounded width supplies a positive complexity condition.

**Native operations need lowering.** A computable local transition does not
by itself have fixed interpreter overhead relative to a unit-priced native
oracle call. Decompose it into matched charged instructions or provide its
own cost bound. Otherwise the general matched-primitive reduction hides work.
Likewise circular-convolution HDC is not componentwise binding: a direct
compiler costs quadratically in dimension, while FFT bounds need their
arithmetic/precision assumptions. Implicit permutations shift work into access
and composition; those costs are not erased by a metadata representation.

**Finite stochastic state is not finite-rational exact flow.** A finite CTMC
with rational generator \(Q\) has time-\(t\) law \(e^{tQ}\), whose entries
need not be rational. State enumeration supplies the generator. A computable
representation or certified approximation with stated tolerance is required
for finite numerical outputs. Similarly, numerical ODE integration needs
existence, stability/regularity and an error bound; finite horizon alone does
not turn an approximate trajectory into exact physical semantics.

**Finite quantum simulation is not uniform polynomial reduction.** A state
vector simulation costing \(2^n\) at each fixed \(n\) does not establish
polynomial overhead across unbounded \(n\). It certifies only its explicit
finite-range or exponential transform. Physical access/oracles remain part
of the comparison. This repairs the finite-size wording in the historical
DC1–DC9 note without importing a quantum-advantage claim.

## DR5: consequences for requested derivation, reduction and basis

1. **Deriving known families:** the new bridge derives Boolean constraint,
   rational factor inference and weighted finite optimization circuits from
   one explicit algebraic contract. Factorization and algebra remain supplied.
   No unique neural or symbolic architecture follows from GMI's core alone.
2. **Reducing new forms:** a candidate with the finite relational interface
   above receives an executable compiler and an explicit cost certificate.
   A mismatch yields a missing interface/semantics premise or compiler defect;
   a larger resource requirement can remain scientifically meaningful.
3. **A complete basis:** the entire finite registered factor family is covered.
   This is family completeness, not completeness of physical or computational
   domains. Unrestricted effectively computable systems have a universal
   program basis at computability-only resolution; that does not identify
   resource-domain classes or their favorable ecologies.

No finite discovery record determines how many arbitrarily rare or
grammar-inexpressible domains exist. A finite exhaustive completeness claim
requires finite encodings, precision, interface, candidate family and a
decidable reduction relation; otherwise semantic equivalence and reachability
can contain undecidable program questions. Finite observations cannot remove
these premises. A new-domain theorem instead needs a registered parent class
and a resource separation beyond the accepted transformation, followed by the
distinct empirical claims of recovery and held-family validation.

The positive compiler and its width law assimilate established
[bucket elimination](https://ics.uci.edu/~csp/r48b.pdf) and
[generalized distributivity](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/sw1pm-bwj40).
They extend the repo's explicit derivations without claiming the parents'
mechanisms as newly discovered science.
