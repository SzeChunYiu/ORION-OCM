# Conditional predictive closure and its boundaries

This unit supplies executable instances of the bridge. It does not replace
the [architecture-emergence theorem](../gmi-architecture-emergence-v1/ARCHITECTURE_EMERGENCE_THEOREM_V1.md)
or assert an unrestricted completion of the six scientific requests.

## PC1 — an end-to-end finite certificate

Fix a finite nonempty set X of protected inputs, an exact response obligation,
a finite encodable program class P, terminating interpreters with decidable
trace costs, and a registered selection order. Define a structural property
predicate F from program artifacts or traces, before reading the search result.
The input/ecology and all description, acquisition and deployment prices are
fixed independently of which candidate wins. Suppose:

1. a theorem from this problem predicts a nonempty set S of optimal properties;
2. synthesis enumerates every member of P or a context-congruent quotient
   preserving adequacy, the charged objective and all reported properties;
3. every retained realization is executed/verified on all X;
4. every optimum, including ties, is retained at the reported property level;
5. a source-bound independent check verifies the prediction and enumeration.

Then agreement establishes the claimed property selection on X and P.
Proof: exhaustive semantic checking identifies exactly the adequate members.
Complete ranking identifies their exact optima. Property-preserving quotienting
and complete optimum fibers recover precisely the set-valued answer. The
theorem's pre-search prediction is thus tested against executable realizations.

This is an application of finite correctness, completeness and minimization,
not a new optimization principle. Its substantive content is supplied by the
independent resource laws and the actual generation/execution of witnesses.
P must be justified for any conclusion outside P. A finite grammar bound is
declared coverage, not proof that all physical architectures were searched.

If costs concern hardware, calibration and error bars must enter the theorem;
exact rational prices cannot supply empirical hardware facts. Factorial or
exponential discovery cost is included separately and can prevent practical
deployment even when its finite result is correct.

## PC2 — correct rejection and tie semantics

For adequate candidates let a=min_{p:F(p)} J(p) and
b=min_{p:¬F(p)} J(p), with an empty-class minimum+∞. If both are+∞,
the problem is infeasible. Otherwise:

- a<b: every optimum has F;
- a=b<∞: F and its complement coexist among optima;
- b<a: no optimum has F.

These statements follow directly by comparison of the **class minima**.
A non-F candidate beating one supplied F witness does not disprove optimal
F membership. The costs F_witness=2, F_other=1 and non_F=1 are an exact
counterexample. This corrects the overly strong NS-1 consequence in the
historical neutral-search adequacy note; its sampling bound requires its
separate independent-discovery and probability premises.

With approximate bounds, strict U_F<L_notF suffices to exclude the other
class only when L_notF covers every adequate competitor and U_F belongs to
one actual common witness. Overlapping intervals are unresolved, not ties.

## PC3 — what template blindness can mean

No probability distribution on a countably infinite program set is invariant
under every permutation of program identities. Such invariance would give
each singleton the same mass c. If c=0 their union has mass0; if c>0 finite
partial sums eventually exceed1. Thus literal uniform ignorance over all
programs is not a probability model.

A finite fixed instruction grammar can be label-blind and fully enumerated.
Its encoding, resource model, primitives and bound still express assumptions.
Neutrality here means those assumptions and the synthesis algorithm do not
change after seeing which historical architecture wins, and no named-family
macro supplies that winner. It does not mean the authors lacked historical
knowledge. Changing encodings requires a proved translation and its actual
overhead; agreement of alternate evaluators of supplied costs is insufficient.

## PC4 — held-out prediction is an evidence condition

Let a theorist choose a fixed map from public task descriptors to predicted
property sets, before a protected generator supplies new instances. A replay
can test that fixed map without revising it. If the generator's family was
already used to construct the map or the case was authored by the theorist,
passing it is not evidence of an unseen-family prediction. Mathematical
coverage of an entire class is distinct from that empirical provenance claim.

The present registrations test authored function, parameter and graph-action
holdouts. They were frozen before execution, and all failed predictions must
remain visible. A successful run cannot retroactively make them hidden-family
tests. An independent protected family campaign is a remaining empirical
obligation. Finite successes also do not supply realistic-scale transport.

## PC5 — novelty and completeness need a reduction resolution

If “programmatic” includes every effectively computable realization under
the same allowed external interfaces, any implemented computable candidate
already belongs to that class: its implementing program is a witness.
Consequently no such candidate can prove nonmembership in the unrestricted
program class. This statement is about computability; it gives no efficient
simulation or physical cost equivalence. Universal-machine computation is
established prior work, not a GMI novelty:
[Turing's original construction](https://www.cs.virginia.edu/~robins/Turing_Paper_1936.pdf).

A meaningful new-mechanism claim instead fixes comparator interfaces,
uniform resource-preserving reduction bounds and deployment obligations.
A strict separation can then be proved relative to those classes. Merely
renaming a program, failing to find a reduction, or searching a bounded
archive does not establish it. The memory, conditional-computation and
factor-elimination mechanisms in this unit have established parents.

Completeness at computability-only resolution is therefore much weaker than
a complete resource-domain basis. The [domain analysis](DOMAIN_REDUCTIONS.md)
provides constructive finite reductions and exposes the precision, native
operation and uniform-cost premises still needed for broader physical forms.

## Remaining falsifying obligations

A valid counterexample to an analytic inequality under its premises is a
mathematical defect. A lower-cost generated candidate, missed tied property,
invalid execution trace or omitted finite program refutes the corresponding
implementation/certificate. A hardware ranking reversal challenges empirical
transport only if hardware costs were actually in the registered premises.
These outcomes require different repairs; none can be silently relabeled as
an unconditional success.
