## 0. Why every earlier Z lane declined Z17, and what this package builds instead

Rows 1 to 6 of `### Z17` speak of *central conclusions*, plural; rows 7 and 8 of
*the theory* and *the flagship claim*, definite. Running the eight axes against
one conclusion chosen by this lane and ticking the rows would be closure by
narrowing. The missing object is a **committed register of the programme's
central conclusions**, sourced from what the programme itself has frozen, never
authored here. This package builds that register first, freezes it, and only
then runs the axes against **every** entry.

exists; `RESULT_V1.json` must carry that sha256 and the axis executor must refuse
to run against a register whose sha256 differs.

## 2. The eight axes — cell vocabulary and closure rule, fixed now

Each axis produces one cell per register entry. The only admissible cell values:

- `RERUN_HOLDS` — the conclusion was actually re-derived under the axis's
  perturbation by this package and held, with exact numbers;
- `RERUN_MOVED` — re-derived and the conclusion changed; the change is recorded
  as a finding with exact numbers;
- `AXIS_INAPPLICABLE` — the conclusion has no free dimension of this kind (a
  corpus count has no random seed, no search law and no grammar); the reason is
  stated per cell;
- `NOT_RERUN_HERE` — the axis applies but this package did not execute it; the
  requirement is written to `REQUIREMENT_TABLE_V1.json` with the package and the
  axis named;
- for rows 7 and 8 only, `NOT_THE_FLAGSHIP_CLAIM` — the row's own text names
  *the theory* / *the flagship claim*, singular, so the cell is defined only for
  `F1` and the law forms `F2`..`F4` that state it; every other entry is so marked.

**Closure rule.** A Z17 row closes **iff no register entry carries
`NOT_RERUN_HERE` on that axis**. Predicted before running: Tier C carries
`NOT_RERUN_HERE` on axes 1 to 5, because re-running 84 packages' executors
under seven perturbations each is a programme, not a package; rows 1 to 5 will
therefore stay open with the exact cell counts disclosed. Row 6 closes only if
the second-route status of every Tier C entry is determined and every present
second route is actually executed. Rows 7 and 8 close on the designated flagship
claim if their predictions below hold.

## 3. What is actually re-run, per tier

**Tier F (all eight axes, computational).** The registered universe of the
flagship claim: `L = 3` binary sequences, mode bit `M`, 16 stateless tables on
`(M, X)` and `256 x 256` one-bit transducers on `(S, M, X)`, scored at
`t in {1, 2}`, objective `J = eta*((1-p)*e_now + p*e_delay)/16 + lambda*bits`,
the 20 registered `(p, eta)` worlds at `lambda in {lambda*/2, lambda*, 3*lambda*/2}`
(60 worlds). For identification and for axis 1 the declared union population of
`Z1` is re-enumerated independently: `eta in {1,2,3}`, `p in k/8`, environment
families {uniform binary; six skewed input laws `q in {1/5,1/4,1/3,2/3,3/4,4/5}`;
uniform alphabets `A in {3,4}`} — nine environment families, 243 two-level
instances — plus the 135-world three-mode ladder of `Z13` at `L = 4`.

- **Axis 1 (benchmark families removed in turn).** Families: the 9 environment
  families, the 3 `eta` rungs, the 9 `p` rungs, the 3 `lambda` positions.
  Re-run: the endpoint verdicts on the remaining worlds, and the identification
  of the law inside the declared 289-member grid `eta*p*(a + b*R0)`,
  `a, b in k/16`. *Prediction:* identification stays unique under every single
  removal; it is lost only when every non-uniform family is removed at once,
  where exactly `9` grid laws tie (`a + b/2 = 1/2`). The retired `F5` form is
  never identified on the ladder under any removal.
- **Axis 2 (alternate grammars).** The same semantic universe is generated by
  bounded straight-line `NAND` programs `G_NAND(k)` and `NOR` programs
  `G_NOR(k)` over inputs `(S, M, X)` with at most `k` gates, `k in {0..5}`; a
  candidate is admitted iff both its output function and its next-state
  function are expressible. Re-run: the argmin class on all 60 worlds under each
  grammar, and the `IC-1` envelope threshold recomputed from the restricted
  profile. *Prediction:* the stateless optimum is reachable at `k = 0`; the
  one-bit `(0, 0)` point needs a 2:1 multiplexer, reachable at `k = 4` and not
  below, for both gate types; verdicts are unchanged on all 60 worlds for
  `k >= 4` and move on a computable subset for `k <= 3`; the recomputed
  `IC-1` threshold reproduces the restricted-universe argmin at every `k` with
  `0` disagreements.
- **Axis 3 (alternate search and development laws).** Exhaustive enumeration;
  deterministic coordinate descent from the all-zero tables; seeded multi-start
  hill-climb over table bits (20 seeds x 8 restarts x 400 steps); a
  developmental growth law (take a stateless optimum, then admit one state bit
  keeping the `s = 0` rows and searching the rest); a seeded uniform sample of
  2,000 candidates. *Prediction:* exhaustive and multi-start hill-climb agree
  with the flagship verdict on 60/60; the growth law reaches the one-bit floor
  from exactly 2 of the 4 tied stateless optima; the sampler's class verdicts
  agree 60/60 even in seeds that miss the exact `(0, 0)` point, because the
  best sampled one-bit candidate still beats the stateless optimum at
  `lambda*/2`. Any stall of the single-start descent is reported, not hidden.
- **Axis 4 (alternate resource accounting).** `A1` `lambda*bits` (registered);
  `A2` `lambda*bits_eff` with `bits_eff` the behavioural minimum of `Z3` `IV-5`;
  `A3` `lambda*(2^bits - 1)`; `A4` `lambda*` (table entries: 4 stateless, 16
  one-bit). *Prediction:* the closed form `eta*p/2` holds under `A1`, `A2`, `A3`
  (60/60 each) and moves under `A4` to `eta*p/24`, exactly the `IC-1` marginal
  under that accounting; `IC-1` holds under all four with `0` disagreements.
- **Axis 5 (seeds and relabeling controls).** 200 seeded permutations of
  candidate identifiers, 20 hill-climb seeds. *Prediction:* `0` verdict changes
  over `12,000` world-checks; hill-climb agreement `60/60` in every seed.
- **Axis 6 (independent implementations).** Route A (per-candidate scan) and
  Route B (a summary histogram built by an independently written simulator)
  must agree on every cell; both must reproduce the on-`main` receipt counts of
  `gmi-833-heldout-20-transitions-v1` (65,552 candidates, 146 summaries, 40/40,
  20 ties) and the `Z15` counts. *Prediction:* full agreement.
- **Axis 7 (every free theoretical parameter, rows 7).** The flagship closed
  form has environment-declared parameters `eta, p, lambda` and one computed
  constant, the stateless delayed floor `1/2`. Exact partials
  `d lambda*/d eta = p/2`, `d lambda*/d p = eta/2`; per world the exact
  multiplicative factor on each of `eta`, `p`, `lambda` that flips the verdict,
  and the exact additive margin on the floor constant. *Prediction:* the
  smallest flip factor over the 40 endpoint worlds is exactly `2` (or `2/3`),
  and the floor margin is exactly `1/4` at every endpoint world; on the
  three-level ladder the sensitivity to each of the four floors is reported per
  world with the collapse condition's dependence on `R0(d2|b=1)` made explicit.
- **Axis 8 (smallest supporting evidence subset, row 8).** (a) the exact
  minimum number of population instances that identifies `IC-1`'s coefficient
  pair uniquely inside the 289-law grid, by exhaustive subset search; (b) the
  smallest candidate certificate for the two floors; (c) the artifacts on `main`
  that the flagship claim's own manifest pins. *Prediction:* (a) is exactly `2`
  and `1` is impossible; (b) is `17` of `65,552` (all 16 stateless tables plus
  one one-bit `(0, 0)` witness).

**Tier A (axes 1 and 6 executed; 2 to 5 classified; 7 and 8 not the flagship).**

**Tier C (axis 6 executed; 1 to 5 `NOT_RERUN_HERE`; 7 and 8 not the flagship).**

(`NO_SECOND_ROUTE_SIGNAL`), owed to the REV-L46 programme, not hidden.

**Tier R.** Retired entries are not re-run; `F5` and `F6` are re-run as planted

## 4. Two routes, hostiles, null, vacuity

Route B may not import Route A. Every hostile must move the quantity it
perturbs: a scoring-window shift, a wrong floor constant, a wrong accounting
exponent, a semantics-permuting pseudo-relabeling, a truncated grammar ladder.
Null: 200 seeded random coefficient pairs in the 289-law grid, none of which may
reproduce the enumerated thresholds on the full population. Every bound reported
(floor margins, flip factors, minimal subset sizes) is classified for vacuity:
a bound that no candidate can violate is retained but not counted.
