# AJ6 — HST/HSG as developmental dynamics above process organizations

## 1. Layer placement

AJ6 consumes AJ1–AJ5 and preserves #233 as the primary theory of inherited changes to future search/development. HST is **not** the operational substrate ontology.

The #233 developmental state is

`Sigma_t = (L_t,Q_t,H_t,E_t,R_t,V_t,C)`.

At the AJ spine:

- `L_t`: legal organization/presentation language over AJ4 organizations and AJ5 presentations;
- `Q_t`: proposal/search kernel over organization or presentation changes;
- `H_t`: inherited persistent capital—current organization reference, archives, libraries, modules, methods, failures and reusable state;
- `E_t`: task/environment ecology supplying registered requirements;
- `R_t`: resource-allocation policy over raw AJ4/AJ5 resource coordinates;
- `V_t`: evaluator/verifier whose observations use AJ2/AJ3 operational tests and registered decision criteria;
- `C`: external constitutional/governance boundary for legal adoption, authority, metering and protected evaluation.

## 2. There is no unique map M -> Sigma

A process organization `M_t` alone does not identify its developmental state. The correct construction is a contextual lift

`iota_chi(M_t) = Sigma_t`,

where `chi=(L,Q,H_aux,E,R,V,C)` and `H_t = H_aux ⊕ current(M_t)`.

Different contexts around the same current organization can induce different reachable futures. The finite checker enumerates 32 developmental worlds over the same four Boolean organizations and finds 51 pairs with identical current organization/current capability but different best reachable future capability.

Therefore:

`current organization/capability != developmental potential`.

## 3. Development is a higher-order transformation

#233 already defines development as

`Sigma_(t+1) = U(Sigma_t,x,e)`.

AJ6 reinterprets its coordinates over the lower GEN layer. A legal developmental transformation may alter, with provenance and resource accounting:

- the current organization inside `H`;
- `L`: representation language / derived operators / grammar;
- `Q`: proposal/search law;
- `H`: archive, library, reusable modules and inherited failures/methods;
- `R`: resource policy;
- where the scientific protocol allows it, `E` or `V`, but these must be provenance-tagged as environment/evaluator changes rather than silently called machine self-development.

`C`, substrate laws and AJ1 process-frame assumptions are not ordinary endogenous development variables unless the scope explicitly grants governed modification of them. Changing the physical/computational substrate or `Adm_S` changes the scientific context; it cannot be laundered as improvement under the old context.

## 4. Existing HST concepts on the lower spine

- **inherited capital** -> persistent reusable substructure in `H` plus charged storage/maintenance;
- **operator invention** -> an `L` extension by a derived macro/process organization with explicit lower semantics; a genuinely new primitive capability requires a substrate-scope change;
- **representation change** -> a charged transformation of `L/H` and organization interfaces, with AJ2/AJ3 operational consequences audited;
- **library formation** -> persistent `H` objects plus optional reusable `L` macros;
- **search-law change** -> update of `Q`;
- **recursive grammar growth** -> `L_t -> L_(t+1)` where each new operator is derived/compiled or explicitly tagged as a new substrate premise;
- **evolvability/useful-descendant mass** -> property of `(M,Sigma,Q,E,R,V)` rather than of `M` alone.

## 5. Parent theorem placement

The #233 theorem programme keeps its ownership and scopes:

- HST-T01 optional-inheritance monotonicity: reach/strategy-set dominance at the developmental layer; mandatory overhead correction remains binding.
- HST-T02 reach expansion: new operator/primitive changes closure/reach; AJ5 now distinguishes derived presentation macro from genuinely new lower substrate capability.
- HST-T03 representation insufficiency: now sits directly on AJ2/AJ3 operational/decision distinctions.
- HST-T04/T05 coded-search acceleration and macro amortization: presentation/search/resource layer; AJ5 strengthens the warning that code length is presentation-relative.
- HST-T08 Blackwell information dominance: AJ3 decision/accessibility layer; information costs remain explicit.
- HST-T09/T10 transfer/evolvability: developmental capability-response layer, not current-capability identity.

The lowering does **not** remove meta-no-free-lunch, Rice/halting, Goedel or Blum-speedup boundaries. Those results apply under their own effective-computation/proof/search premises. Escaping one requires an explicit premise/substrate/contract change, which is an AF/AJ12-style barrier displacement rather than a contradiction.

## 6. Hidden assumptions exposed by the operational bottom

AJ6 records five assumptions that were easy to hide when `Sigma` was treated as the top-level object:

1. `M -> Sigma` requires developmental context; it is not unique from machine state alone.
2. Every searchable/developable organization requires some representation in `L`; presentation and compiler costs therefore matter.
3. `V` can only distinguish what its admitted AJ2/AJ3 tests/resources make accessible; verification is not automatically omniscient.
4. `operator invention` inside one substrate means deriving/adding a legal process organization or macro; changing substrate laws is a scope change.
5. Changes in `E`, `V`, or `C` have different provenance from endogenous machine development and must not be credited to the machine without a causal/provenance argument.
