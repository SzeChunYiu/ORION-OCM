# Authored native learning control — completed lifecycle

A method extracted from two supplied proof traces was admitted by the native checker,
persisted, restored in a fresh process, and actually used in search on a different
authored full-task meaning. Removing its actual canonical ID reversed the search
reduction; restoring that ID reproduced the enabled derivation and work exactly.

This qualifies the conventional mechanical chunking apparatus. It does not establish
OCM-specific novelty or superiority, general autonomous discovery, language transfer,
a corpus result, or a publication-ready scientific claim.

## Research consequence

Use this now-qualified constructor/learner/search interface for the registered broader
curriculum and an equal OCM transplant. Keep conventional chunking as the matched parent.
The dominant measured cost is cold ordinary instance compilation; any persistent
index/cache improvement must be applied equally and charge maintenance/revision costs.
Require task-family separation, no-use/harmful transfer, revision and lifetime tests
before any mechanism or novelty claim. Do not interpret this one authored composition
as a solution to FLT, open-ended language, scalability, or the programme as a whole.
