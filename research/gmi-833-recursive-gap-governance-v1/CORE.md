# Core claim

This package operationalizes the #833 recursive-loophole constitution at a declared governance scope. A scientific result must carry explicit assumptions, dependencies, falsifiers, strongest parents, prior disclosure, logic/scope metadata, counterexample methods, forbidden extrapolations, and unresolved gap ids. Experiments additionally disclose leakage, search space, cost model, evaluation and sampling bias.

The live gap graph is a **mechanism and schema**, not a claim that the entire GMI corpus has already been entered or exhausted. Independent hostile review, corpus saturation, replication and real-scale closure remain separate evidence obligations.
