## Target claim

A universal statement that OCM is strictly beneficial in every environment is false whenever cognition can cost resources while useful reusable structure is absent. The defensible architecture-level target is conditional and falsifiable:

> Under registered information, lifecycle and resource conditions, a baseline-inclusive OCM controller acquires or retains cognition only when the resulting downstream and reusable-state value exceeds acquisition, policy-use, verification, storage, maintenance, invalidation and replay cost; across independently frozen native lifetimes satisfying those conditions, the resulting protected lifecycle cost is lower than the strongest equally equipped conventional parents.

This separates two obligations:

1. **General decision theorem:** the controller may always bypass cognition and must pay for the controller itself. Strict benefit requires a positive paid-cognition residual after every modeled cost.
2. **Native instantiation:** multiple independently frozen populations must actually satisfy those premises with protected output/lifecycle equivalence and matched parents.

The existing finite Bellman and lifecycle results in this directory supply the first mathematical spine. They do not by themselves satisfy the second obligation.

## Proof-readiness gates

A native lane counts toward the architecture claim only when all are true:

1. **Operational population:** prospectively frozen nontrivial tasks/lifetimes, not a handful of authored cells chosen after outcome.
2. **Endogenous acquisition:** useful structure is acquired from permitted prior experience rather than inserted because the evaluation target is known.
3. **Causal later reuse:** removal/restoration or another exact intervention proves the acquired structure changes later cognition.
4. **Protected equivalence:** output, authority, audit trace contract, revision/revocation behavior and fallback obligations remain valid.
5. **Strong matched parents:** conventional derived lemmas, exact caches/indices, analytic thresholds and online/metareasoning parents receive equal information and applicability.
6. **Complete cost vector:** acquisition, compilation, policy use, solving, checking, storage, maintenance, invalidation, checkpoint/replay and source/output custody are measured or explicitly bounded.
7. **Net-positive region:** benefit survives a prospectively registered nontrivial resource-price/lifecycle region; a local search-only win is insufficient when whole-process cost changes sign.
8. **Independent replication:** the same theory-predicted boundary appears in structurally different populations or domains rather than surface variants of one authored fixture.

The general architecture claim remains closed until multiple native lanes pass all eight. A failure is retained as a limitation result.
