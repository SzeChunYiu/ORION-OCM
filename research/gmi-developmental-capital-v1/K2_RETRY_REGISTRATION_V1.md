## 4. Parent subtraction is required, not optional

Facilitated variation and meta-learning parents already target higher-order
improvement of future adaptability. A K2 positive therefore requires, in
addition to clearing the bar:

- an explicit comparison against the strongest applicable parent mechanism;
- a statement of what the OCM route supplies that the parent does not;
- `PARENT_SUFFICIENT` recorded as the honest terminal if the parent matches it.

Clearing the bar without this subtraction establishes nothing about GMI.
