## Residual contribution of this tranche (what is new, and only this)

Parents (3) and (4) closed the lexical and semantic-fingerprint routes and both reported
that the structural question — whether a grammar's own cost geometry makes its declared
target cheapest — was out of their mechanical reach. This tranche supplies exactly that
second, independent **structural/cost route**:

- a name-blind, exact-integer target-exclusivity criterion computed from cost tables rather
  than identifiers (GME-1), with its deletion dichotomy (GME-2);
- the resulting **identified and adjudicated population**: 7 of 12 targeted grammar
  instances in the extractable population encode their declared target, all disclosed and
  charged, 0 undisclosed (GME-5);
- a detector whose power is demonstrated rather than assumed, anchored on #891's own
  registered counterexample (V1) and on planted encodings in real corpus grammars (V2);
- two previously unrecorded lexical false-positive classes (`SUBSTRING_COLLISION`,
  `PARENT_LITERATURE_ATLAS`) (GME-6);
- the constructive confirmation of the parents' own caveat: every encoding found in this
  corpus carries a neutral production name and is invisible to both lexical routes.
