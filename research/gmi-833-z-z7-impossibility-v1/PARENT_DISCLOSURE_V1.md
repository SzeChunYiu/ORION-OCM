## Residual contribution of this tranche

1. **Uniformity of the impossibility**: the whole `bits = 0` family sits at
   exactly `N/2` delay errors, so the impossibility is a property of the
   resource class rather than of its optimum.
2. **Exact impossibility regions**: `286` of `289` lattice cells unattainable at
   `bits = 0`, `146` at `bits = 1`, and `3656` of `7680` cost-grid cells
   infeasible, split `2976`/`680` by budget.
3. **Prospective adjudication with honest labels**: five qualitative predictions
   and six quantitative ceilings frozen before the enumeration, each carrying
   `[DERIVED-AT-FREEZE]`, `[UNCOMPUTED]` or `[NAIVE]`, so the record distinguishes
   replication from surprise.
4. **A real counterexample and its repair**: the `[NAIVE]` scarcity hypothesis is
   refuted by an explicit witness, the failed assumption is named, and the repair
   — that the mode bit indexes disjoint table entries, so the single state bit is
   not shared between channels — is recorded with its claim-ceiling consequence.
5. **A structural family impossibility**: the Moore family's copy-channel frontier
   `{(8,0)}` against the Mealy family's `{(0,0)}`, at identical state budget.
6. **Tightness as the null's discriminator**: `6/200` random impossibility claims
   are true-and-tight against `5/5` frozen ceilings, with `153/153` witness
   soundness — so the frozen set is shown to be special, not merely true.
