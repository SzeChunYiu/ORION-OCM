a unique model winner across compilation. The software returns UNKNOWN when
the intervals cannot establish strict separation. Bounds are evidence premises;
the software does not infer compiler overhead or scientific usefulness.

## Falsification and limits

The oracle independently derives minimal costs, checks all witness truth values
and compiled executions, and attacks missing/altered certificates and programs.
Restricted grammars and premature termination must fail completeness. Changing
the resource model can change the model winner; semantic translation alone does
not preserve optimization. No finite sample is used as an empirical
generalization claim.

Formal Boolean synthesis and compiler methods are established parent work.
The contribution is a concrete repair to this programme's evidence chain.
No conclusion about every known or unknown form of machine intelligence follows.
