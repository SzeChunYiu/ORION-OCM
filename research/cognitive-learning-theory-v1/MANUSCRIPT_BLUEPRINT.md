## One-page editorial significance packet

**Problem.** Persistent learning systems must correct their accumulated
knowledge and mechanisms. Learning a detailed self-model may itself cost more
than the repairs it enables. Missing dependencies can preserve unsupported
beliefs; indiscriminate invalidation wastes valid competence.

**Central hypothesis.** Learning enough structure to choose an acceptable
repair can be cheaper than identifying an entire causal architecture. Useful
structure must reduce complete future acquisition/repair cost while preserving
externally fixed epistemic obligations.

**Candidate mechanism.** Maintain competing effect models and their evidence.
Probe disagreements that matter for the current repair. Admit a change only
when the evidence/checker establishes its required preservation and authority
conditions. Retain useful learned constraints through restart, and reopen them
when their supporting assumptions cease to hold.

**What would change scientifically?** A demonstrated boundary between the
information needed for full structure recovery and the information needed for
safe, economical repair, accompanied by an actual-source mechanism that
exploits that boundary beyond the strongest faithful learning/repair parent.
Decision-region determination already owns decision-sufficient stopping and
identifiability: [Javdani et al., 2014](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.5886).
The formal reconstruction is adopted prior art; the claimed empirical residual
is NOT YET ESTABLISHED.

**Strongest alternative explanation.** Existing version-space learning,
active experimental design, assumption-based truth maintenance, incremental
computation, library induction and adaptive optimization already explain the
mechanism and its benefit. All are allowed to compose in the strongest parent.

**Current evidence.** An earlier real-M11 dispatcher pilot retained two
existing-library changes and repaid counted development work in a fixed
continuation, but lacked wall-time payback, an earned third change and a
controller residual. Its field decomposition was supplied. Exact finite theory
checks and a new narrowly scoped support-learning pilot are reported separately
when available; neither can retrospectively validate the earlier hypothesis.

**Decisive causal result still needed.** Under matched development information,
learned decision-relevant structure improves fresh repair cost at the same
quality/preservation floor; removing that structure removes the benefit;
strong structured/adaptive parents do not fully reproduce it.

**Generalization result still needed.** The same learned mechanism survives a
prospectively withheld family or validation regime with its identity frozen,
complete acquisition/maintenance costs, negative-transfer controls and disjoint
replication. New masks of one previously exposed method are not a new domain.

**Main limitation.** Scope-conditional proofs, internal exact computations,
and an architecture-specific development pilot do not establish a general
machine-cognition principle or top-tier novelty.

## What “complete” can mean

1. **Formal closure at scope:** every object has semantics; assumptions are
   explicit; proofs cover the stated finite class; counterexamples delimit
   failure; exact algorithms agree within an enumerated universe.
2. **Mechanism closure at scope:** acquisition, use, provenance, persistence,
   withdrawal/reopening and compensation-aware removal have executable evidence.
3. **Empirical closure of a registered claim:** the frozen primary test,
   strongest parent, uncertainty, negative twins and replication all have a
   bounded disposition, including a negative one.
4. **Submission preparation:** supported claim, manuscript, figures, code/data,
   independently checked references, accountable human authors and journal
   requirements are complete.

No finite collection of favorable domains proves an unrestricted complete
theory of cognition. Broad theory remains revisable. Do not substitute an
unfinished implementation checklist for missing evidence or force positive
terminals to meet a publication ambition.

## Claim and result writing rules

- Describe fixed-rule support retention separately from identifying arbitrary
  reinduced rule outputs, complete dependencies or full causal architecture.
- Count human-designed candidate libraries, grammar/equation priors and supplied
  intervention handles. Learned parameters, learned methods and new primitives
  are separate categories.
- A known classical algorithm installed in OCM is an engineering improvement.
  A contribution beyond that algorithm requires measured evidence.
- Use all complete independent trajectories/families in uncertainty analyses;
  never treat correlated masks or theorem checks as independent cognitive trials.
- Keep formal proof, exact verification, development observations, confirmatory
  evidence and replication in separate claim rows.
- Report source-level attribution and custody limitations, all negative results,
  incomplete accounting and every unavailable original trace.
