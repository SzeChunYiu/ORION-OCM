# Publication readiness: bounded internal review

## Review setup

**Input scope.** Research specifications, implementation, exact-model proposals,
prior-art notes and the recorded self-evolution development pilot. A preliminary
manuscript, `MANUSCRIPT_DRAFT.md`, was supplied during this review and read in
full. Its final reviewed version contains an abstract, Results, Methods and
Discussion, including the frozen support-study results and raw-evidence links.
The earlier pending-result checkpoint is superseded by that final consistency
review. There is no finalized submission package. This is a bounded internal
review, not an editorial decision letter.

**Assessment boundary.** The three reports below are simulated emphasis variants
within one AI-assisted internal audit. They are not three independent human
referees. This reviewing session previously contributed inverse-parent code and
methodological criticism to the programme; it is not an external or
authorship-independent replication. No protected study was rerun for this review.
The review uses the supplied local Nature reviewer source basis and programme
issues #143/#144/#145. It does not certify current venue-specific requirements.

**Shared fact base.** The published pilot selected two existing dispatcher
alternatives through M11 and failed to earn a third change. Its registered
continuation reports 1,024,703 evolved counted units, including 686,855 initial
development units, versus 2,613,144 initial-dispatcher units: a 60.7866% reduction
in that work model, with first payback at episode 12. Complete five-revision
episode quality was 24/24 versus 0/24; this denominator does not mean every
individual incumbent revision failed. Including recorded initial development,
wall time was 4.46 s versus 4.22 s: no wall-time advantage. These totals were
recomputed from the saved rows, without executing the assays.

The retained object is a dispatcher configuration and its M11 history; donor
fields and methods are reconstructed for each task. A conventional finite
search over the same alternatives implements the controller. These are useful
bounded engineering results with explicit parent sufficiency, not operator
invention or evidence of a distinct general cognitive scaling regime.

## Reviewer 1 — emphasis on the scientific object

**Overall assessment.** The strongest current contribution is the disciplined
separation of tested repair sufficiency, unidentified cause and governed
admission. Formalizing those distinctions could produce a useful research
instrument. Neither the pilot nor an elementary finite theorem establishes
the requested completeness of cognitive theory.

**Interested readers.** Researchers studying program synthesis, diagnosis,
truth maintenance and continual adaptation could use exact countermodels that
expose when a repair is licensed and when observations are insufficient.

**Strengths.** The packet retains failed generations, labels supplied algorithms
as existing alternatives, and does not turn successful routing into a new
primitive. It distinguishes mathematical scope from empirical generalization.

## Reviewer 2 — emphasis on evidence and measurement

**Overall assessment.** The saved work/quality improvement is real within its
declared deterministic accounting model and authored continuation. Its reporting
is unusually careful about failures and costs. It is not a confirmatory estimate
of general improvement, and the stronger runtime resource claim is unsupported.

**Interested readers.** Researchers building persistent reasoning systems may
care about the observed tradeoff between dependency discovery and lazy
recomputation, especially the concrete conditions under which acquisition fails
to amortize.

**Strengths.** Rejected probes, incumbent and challenger shadow work, fresh-use
ablations and development expenditure are included in the reported source work
sum. The 24-episode continuation runs its declared horizon, rather than stopping
when payback first appears. Negative wall-time evidence is retained.

**Interested readers.** A narrowly framed, reproducible set of counterexamples

## Cross-review synthesis

**Consensus strengths.** All reports recognize the distinction between supplied
mechanisms and machine selection, the preservation of negative results, and the
value of explicit finite assumptions and cost receipts.

**Consensus concerns.** `CLT-NOV-01` is shared by Reviewers 1 and 3: no residual
beyond matched parents is established. `CLT-EMP-01` is shared by Reviewers 2 and
3: the development trajectory does not establish population generalization or
an open-ended cognitive language. These are substantive overlap, not votes by
independent referees.

**Different emphasis.** Reviewer 1 requires a sound formal/causal object;
Reviewer 2 requires matching between estimand, units and resource evidence;
Reviewer 3 requires one significant contribution and an assessable manuscript.

**Decisive test and successor.** The bounded F4 census now tests learned
incumbent-retention predicates against equally informed antichain, exact-table
and lazy-cache parents. The conventional antichain learner uses the least
counted work among correct registered arms; the explicit version-space arm
uses fewer queries but costs more. Adopt the useful parent-owned mechanism;
there is no established novelty residual here. A successor must hide currently
supplied grouping, charge its acquisition and maintenance, and test a separately
registered lifecycle consequence on disjoint source-faithful ecologies with
equally capable adaptive parents. The completed census does not establish
hidden dependency-group discovery or whole-function repair.

**Readiness disposition.** Formal closure, evidence closure, novelty and
submission packaging are separate gates. The current positive engineering
findings and negative parent results are usable research evidence. They do not
jointly imply theory completeness, a submission-ready flagship or likely
journal acceptance. `READINESS_GATES.json` records the claim-dependent open gates.

**Unsupported or not-assessable claims.** Universal/minimal cognition,
Transformer replacement, a uniquely new primitive basis, field establishment,
independent external replication and journal acceptance are unsupported. A
finalized submission package, human accountability and full submission compliance
are not assessable from the supplied preliminary draft; missing material is not
evidence of misconduct.

### New-artifact assessment

**Formal reconstruction.** `FORMAL_CORE.md`, FC1–FC7, now supplies explicit
objects, assumptions, proofs and counterexamples. Internal proof/source review
found no unresolved defect in the stated elementary propositions. The
fixed-order source mapping supports monotonic retention of the exact
`(preperiod, period, values)` representation; it does not prove a general
monotonicity law for arbitrary learners. FC6 is a standard conditional quotient
proof. The FC7 numerical check is proposed only; its displayed affine
accounting proposition has an elementary algebraic proof. There is no
proof-assistant certification or external mathematical review here.

**Exact verification.** `exact_verification.py` and
`results/exact-verification-v1.json` compare distinct algorithms on complete
registered finite universes. Source and result-section hashes were checked in
this review. The decision test covers 87,808 combinations of three hypotheses,
three acceptable-action options, two binary probes and positive probe costs;
explicit action trees and version-space dynamic programming agree. The 40,000
cases allowing a decision without full identification are a census count in
this supplied universe, not an empirical success probability. All 20 monotone
three-variable functions match independently enumerated support antichains;
11 have relevant variables missed by full-state single-deletion sensitivity.
The largest deletion-only lifecycle instance has 160 states, 480 transitions,
two immediate-answer classes and 20 continuation classes. Restorations and
authority-changing actions are outside that alphabet. These results close
finite reconstruction checks, not acquisition efficacy or novelty.

**Parent disposition.** `LEARNING_OPERATOR_DONOR_MAP.md`, L0–L9, identifies
decision-region determination/HEC and EC2 as direct parents for the
decision-sufficiency objective. Alternative supports, abstraction learning,
failure constraints, refinement and routing also have direct parents. No
`RESIDUAL_SURVIVES` row is established. This materially strengthens
`CLT-NOV-01`; the new proofs do not remove it.

**Preliminary manuscript.** `MANUSCRIPT_DRAFT.md`, “Learning fixed-rule supports
for governed repair”, was read in full after the initial research-packet review.
The abstract, populated source-study Results and availability section were
reviewed again after the frozen outcome receipt arrived. Their numerical
claims agree with the saved census. The draft separates exact
representation-retention from replacement-rule learning, reports the stronger
antichain parent's lower counted cost, and retains the older pilot's negative
wall result. It explicitly limits reload to serialized-state reconstruction
inside a worker process, excludes a final-storage comparison, and links the
registration and raw outcomes. No material claim inflation was found. It does
not claim an original HEC/ATMS theorem or submission readiness. This resolves
the earlier absence of assessable prose and pending empirical results; it does
not resolve novelty, independent evidence, complete availability or human
accountability. `CLT-G11-MANUSCRIPT-PACKAGING` remains PARTIAL.

**Actual-source support pilot.** `support_effects.py` and
`run_support_development.py` were inspected before execution. The learner gets
four candidate block handles and the monotone hypothesis class. Its active
acquisition continues until the entire four-bit retention function is
identified. Thus it does not directly test decision-region early stopping
against full-model identification. The response is retention of a fixed rule
representation, not the arbitrary replacement rule. Matched antichain,
eager-table and lazy-cache parents are essential. The final
`STRUCTURE_LEARNING_PROTOCOL.md` was read in full: it correctly limits the
claim to a new E2 development census, separates within-method interpolation
from transfer, retains common structural gifts, and distinguishes the counted
operation proxy from audit-inclusive process time. Source occurrence IDs and
payloads jointly identify revocable evidence; equal payloads are not treated
as one evidence occurrence.
