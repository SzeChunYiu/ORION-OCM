# Formal scope V27

The pinned Lean 4.19 replay checks 48 source modules: 29 immutable imports
and 19 new modules. Its static audit registers 282 explicit types and prints
the assumptions of every registration. This is a source-level mathematical
result, separate from Python execution and the successor authority gate.

## Read first

The frozen target is the bounded seven-parent translation requirement,
original GMI2-R1-009. The proofs do not establish every process formalism,
a unique ontology, full GMI, or derivation of every form of intelligence.
The classical parent mechanisms remain attributed in PARENTS and the ledger.

Every registered constructor or operation is tied to its actual definition.
The checker copies the complete dependency closure into a fresh temporary
directory, compiles each source, then elaborates the static expected statements.
It does not infer replacement statements from the current source during replay.
It rejects forbidden proof constructs and inspects each registered declaration.
Standard Lean logical principles, including classical choice where used,
remain visible in the printed assumption reports.

## AA1: relation transitions, coalgebras and the same finite path

RelParentV27 constructs arbitrary relations with equality identities and
existential composition. Its laws are derived, including empty types.
The actual TotalRel category includes faithfully into this relation category.
A relation from an inhabited type to Empty is permitted; totality is stronger.

LTSCoalgebraV27 implements the actual successor-set and relation decoders.
Both composites are identities by extensionality. powerMap is the existential
image of labelled successors, with derived identity and composition laws.
hom_iff proves the coalgebra equation equivalent to Forward AND Back.
graph_bisimulation connects that equation to the actual graph relation.
Forward simulation alone is explicitly insufficient.

LTSPathsV27 uses the immutable V11 inductive Path over generators
{a // R s a t}. The generators recover transition triples.
mapPath recursively maps those generators; nil, cons, append and label-list
equations are registered. The actual path ProcessMap preserves evaluation.
labels_append is also proved.

lift_path constructs a source path for each actual finite target path under
Forward and Back. Its conclusion is equality of dependent pairs containing
the target endpoint AND the mapped path. It is stronger than equality of
label words and does not assume that two edges share only their labels.
No injectivity, uniqueness, effective lift selector, or infinite-path theorem
is asserted. These LTS generators have no duplicate multigraph edge IDs.

map_eval concerns the actual target Path.category interpretation.
The broader paper assertion about an arbitrary lawful category interpretation
is not a separately registered V27 kernel theorem.

## AA3 and AA4: kernels, events and labelled tests

The immutable V14 Weight interface is retained in full: a nontrivial
semiring-like carrier, zero-sum-free addition and absence of zero divisors.
OrderedWeight adds a partial order, bottom zero, monotone addition and both
multiplications, and commutative multiplication. These are scalar premises;
matrix bounds and event-category conclusions are derived, not fields.

OrderedWeightsV27 derives sum monotonicity and row composition bounds.
SubEventsV27 constructs actual matrix Events with row sums at most one,
including zero events into zero-dimensional outputs. Matrix laws derive
its Category. The normalized Kernel inclusion is faithful, and toKernel
is inverse on the normalized Event slice, preserving every coefficient.

FiniteOutcomesV27 defines actual finite shapes by atoms Fin n and binary
products. Carrier and total are recursively defined, and the sum identities,
exchange, monotonicity and term bound are proved for those operations.
Encoder retains an injective external labelling; its paired constructor
uses actual ordered pairs.

EventTestsV27.Test begins with one raw matrix per outcome and a normalized
aggregate. component derives each Event bound from the aggregate equation.
Composition keeps actual paired outcomes and actual matrix products.
aggregate_product and Test.comp_aggregate derive the aggregate-composition
identity, and the resulting aggregate normalization is proved.
Zero events and equal-valued distinct outcomes are retained.
no_empty_outcomes uses inherited one_ne_zero for inhabited inputs.
Empty input dimensions are covered by empty_input.

This is pair closure of tests, not a globally bundled category of labelled
tests with product reassociation, nor an adaptive-test construction.
Python starts from checked Events; Lean starts from raw matrices and proves
their component bounds. The finite correspondence checks this interface
difference separately.

EventObservationsV27 constructs actual basis preparations and effects.
observation_entry extracts each matrix coefficient and separation derives
equality of Events from all such observations. Closed scalar composition
is multiplication, not conditional normalization.

natOrderedWeight witnesses consistency of the generic scalar premises.
Nat-normalized channels alone provide no nontrivial probabilistic model.
fractional_scalar_control and equal_aggregate_different_tests are exact
Std.Internal.Rat arithmetic controls, not Event Rat or Test Rat instances.
The inherited V14 seven-arrow rational matrix model is freshly replayed.
General nonnegative rational/real scalar interpretations and the actual
Fraction Event/Test calibration are respectively paper and executable
evidence; no complete Real/Rat OrderedWeight instance is fabricated.

## AA5: declared task interfaces

TotalRel between their subtypes. Category laws come from the real operations.
decode retains ambient labels; decoded_domain is exactly the source
interface and decoded_range is contained in the declared target.

RegularTasksV27 builds regularTask from canonical arrows and an explicit
TotalRel graph of Ran R inclusion into Dom S. decode_regular proves the
exact relational composite; regular_domain retains exactly Dom R.
The declared output remains Ran S, not necessarily Ran (R;S).

The actual five-label R/S/T control proves that inferred exact-image
regularity fails strong partial associativity. Declared interfaces and
explicit inclusions supply the stated repair, retaining unused outputs.
TaskControlsV27 supplies a closed identity-only possibility class, excluded
swap whose square is included, and a separate nonclosed candidate class.
The actual V26 restriction requires identity/composition closure as input.
Physical feasibility of any arrow or inclusion, repeatability and resource
laws are not inferred. The immutable same-path resource controls replay.

## Registration, executable evidence and exclusions

ParentBindings, EventBindings, TaskBindings, SystemBindings and ControlBindings
bind actual Hom types, identities/composition, decoder/encoder operations,
finite sums, matrices, task relations, controls and scalar observations.
ProofTargets supplies three independent full-statement leaf contracts for
source-valid mutation checks; it does not replace the other registrations.

The Python corpus, independent oracles, row ledger and custody guards provide
separate finite correspondence and governance evidence. Lean does not verify
their implementations, experiment counts, CI orchestration or ledger prose.
Generic monoidal/symmetric reduct extraction, arbitrary measurable kernels,
quantum operational systems and general ancillary equivalence remain paper
interpretations of their parents, not constructions claimed by this kernel.
