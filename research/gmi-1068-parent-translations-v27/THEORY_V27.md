## AA3-B — arbitrary relations and the total slice

For arbitrary types A,B define Rel(A,B) as predicates R(a,b).
Set id_A(a,b) iff a=b and (R;S)(a,c) iff ∃b, R(a,b) and S(b,c).
The identity equations follow by eliminating the equality witness; conversely
choose b=a or b=c. For associativity, either bracketing is equivalent to
∃b,c, R(a,b) and S(b,c) and T(c,d). Reassociate the witnesses and conjunctions.
Extensional equality of predicates gives the category laws.

Total(R) means ∀a,∃b,R(a,b). For total R and S, choose an R-successor and then an
S-successor, yielding a total composite. Equality is total, with successor a.
Thus the actual V14 TotalRel category embeds by forgetting only the proof.
This same-object inclusion preserves identity/composition literally and is
injective on each Hom because relation equality determines the total-relation
record up to proof irrelevance. Its image is exactly the total relations.

It is not surjective on every Hom: when A is inhabited and B empty, the empty
relation is a valid Rel(A,B), whereas totality would produce an impossible b.
When A is empty, totality is vacuous and the empty relation is a legitimate arrow.
Neither convention should be replaced by an arbitrary default output.
