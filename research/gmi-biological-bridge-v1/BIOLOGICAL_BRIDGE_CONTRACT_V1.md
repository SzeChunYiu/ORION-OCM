## L0. The bridge schema

A cross-species prediction is a quadruple `(E_bio, M, C_pred, D_holdout)`:

1. `E_bio` — the species' ecology in GMI coordinates: resource budgets
   (energy/day, sensor bandwidth, actuator latency), lifespan/developmental
   timescale, social structure (from the new `other_agents` / `population`
   axes), niche descriptors (foraging risk, predation, variability).
2. `M` — the GMI-derived morphology/capability law applied (e.g. TOM-3 probe
   pricing, META-1 confidence, memory-regime partition) with its scope.
3. `C_pred` — the predicted cognitive profile, stated BEFORE consulting the
   held-out phenotype data, with at least one registered way to fail.
4. `D_holdout` — the biological observations, held out from theory fitting
   where possible, with provenance (species, study, n).

A prediction counts only when all four are frozen prospectively. Retrospective
"explanations" of known biology are assimilation food (nearest work saturated
first), never closure evidence.
