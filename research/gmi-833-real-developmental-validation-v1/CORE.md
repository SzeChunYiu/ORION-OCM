## A frozen statement that turned out FALSE, reported not edited

`FREEZE_V1.md`'s `OI-3` claimed `Lstar(n) = min{l : Phi(n+1,l-1) >= Phi(n,l)}`
is the depth above which a `gamma=1` admission is guaranteed-increasing. **It is
not** — 860 counterexamples, the first at `n=2, l0=4`. `Lstar` is the
guaranteed-loss depth at `gamma=0`. The frozen definition is not edited; the
corrected converse is carried by a new companion
`Mstar(n) = min{ l>=2 : Phi(n+1,l-2) >= Phi(n,l) }`, which satisfies the same
frozen bracket. Recorded as deviation D2.
