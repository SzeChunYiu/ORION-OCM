# Current qualifications of historical claims

These qualifications govern the scientific reading of earlier source records.
The original results and registrations remain available unchanged; a later
correction does not manufacture a new measurement.

| Earlier inference | Current warranted conclusion |
|---|---|
| Successful registered output proves the whole theory complete. | Source-bound replay supports that exact output. General quantifiers require a valid proof, and physical or semantic premises require their own evidence. |
| Known update-law labels plus prices identify the best learner. | Actual optimization premises, adequate implementations and a common charged objective must hold. Bayesian entropic updates can overlap a mirror-descent representation. |
| A lower-bound proof without a cardinality assumption preserves its numeric value under every class expansion. | The order argument persists; the infimum can fall. Preserving a numerical lower bound needs target-total accounting coverage. |
| A cheaper non-family program than one family program excludes the family. | Exclusion compares the entire admitted family minimum against a retained adequate competitor. One losing witness cannot establish that minimum. |
| The old V2/V3 neutral runners show executable rediscovery. | [The source audit](../gmi-neutral-derivation-v1/NEUTRAL_SOURCE_AUDIT.md) distinguishes supplied-cost comparisons, template-bearing sources and winner agreement from generated executable mechanisms. |
| The new finite mechanisms establish all named architectures or a previously unknown intelligence form. | [Predictive closure](../gmi-neutral-derivation-v1/PREDICTIVE_CLOSURE.md) and [the six-gap map](../gmi-neutral-derivation-v1/SIX_GAPS.md) retain explicit grammar, data, identifying and comparison assumptions. |
| Twelve reported tasks identify population niche frequency. | They give finite reported counts. Population claims need a justified task law, joint achieved-score/baseline event and retained sampling evidence. |
| A mean score is a family ceiling. | A ceiling bounds every admitted member or a defined family optimum. An achieved mean does not supply that bound. |
| Complete PROGRAM intervention establishes acquisition of a new grammar. | [The current assay](../gmi-current-program-assay-v1/CORE.md) identifies body replacement within the supplied finite search mechanism; task exposure, retained state and every recorded charge remain part of its scope. |
| Geometric transfer failure defeats all proper unbounded transfer. | [Additive potentials](../gmi-additive-potential-transfer-v1/CORE.md) revive a polynomial regime while retaining the earlier weighted-transient regime. Certificate existence is still an explicit premise. |
| Current-response state equivalence licenses arbitrary future forgetting. | Future teaching and updates can distinguish such states. Safe compression requires preservation of the declared future interaction behavior and charged conversion/replay. |

The detailed correction proofs and exact countermodels are linked from
[CORE](CORE.md). A proposed new experiment is not an executed protected test.
An exposed refinement must retain its earlier attempt and must not be described
as a previously fixed prediction.
