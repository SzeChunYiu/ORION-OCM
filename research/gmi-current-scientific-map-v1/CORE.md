# Current GMI scientific results

result keeps its mechanism, admissible class, evidence and cost assumptions.
There is no established universal architecture winner or completed physical
theory of intelligence.

Read [current qualifications](QUALIFICATIONS.md) before interpreting historical
closure labels. [Open scientific problems](OPEN_PROBLEMS.md) states the next
falsifiable step for each remaining claim; an unresolved empirical premise is
not repaired by restating a theorem that assumes it.
