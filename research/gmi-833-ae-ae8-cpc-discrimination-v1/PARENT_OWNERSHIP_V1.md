## What is explicitly NOT claimed novel

None of the eight comparator principles, none of their properties, and none of
the information-theoretic machinery. In particular this package does not claim
to have discovered rate-distortion, MDL, predictive information, expected free
energy, control as inference, bounded rationality, resource-rational analysis or
algorithm selection, and it does not rank them. It emits
`OBSERVATIONALLY_EQUIVALENT_AT_REGISTERED_SCOPE` where the registered roster
does not discriminate, and forbids promoting either member of such a pair.

It also does not claim that CPC is false. The registered terminal is
`CPC_MASTER_LAW_CLAIM_WITHHELD`: a refusal to make a claim, not a claim to the
contrary. `CPC_MASTER_LAW_CLAIM_WITHHELD_IS_A_NEGATIVE_RESULT_ABOUT_PARENTS` is
a registered forbidden promotion for exactly that reason.

## The named residual contribution of this tranche

1. **The computed collisions.** The irreducibility result is not the observation
   that a function of `v` cannot separate arguments with equal `v` — that is
   arithmetic. It is that over a `4110`-model space, for four of five named
   phenomena, pairs of *structurally different* models with *exactly equal
   derived* term vectors, differing in exactly one computed structural
   attribute, are found by an exhaustive search in a frozen order. The equality
   is derived from the model objects, never stipulated, and the test asserts
   both that the objects differ and that the vectors are equal element-wise.
   The fifth, verification, is reported as not proved.
2. **A falsifiable bag-of-laws baseline.** Laws are validated on their own
   coverage; one is reported refuted on the roster and excluded; the baseline
   covers `10` of `12` worlds, so it can be missed as well as beaten.
3. **The classifier applied in the register's own order**, with the ladder
   published, so the `DECOMPOSITION` verdict is auditable against the frozen
   criteria rather than asserted.
4. **Exact logarithmic comparison without bounds.** Information quantities over
   a uniform eight-point universe are irrational; they are carried as rational
   combinations of prime logarithms and compared by a single integer product
   comparison, which decides `<`, `=` and `>` exactly. No float and no interval
   arithmetic appears anywhere.
5. **A disclosed decision rule and two disclosed register gaps**, so the reader
   can see precisely which choices the register did not pin.
