# Mechanical OCM execution contract

[Programme](CORE.md) · [FLT reconstruction](FLT_RECONSTRUCTION.md).

Governing operator direction, 6 September 2026. This is a prospective requirement,
not a claim that the current repository already enforces or qualifies neural absence.

## Required boundary

No neural network may contribute to OCM runtime cognition, acquisition, retrieval,
search, premise selection, policy, generation, criticism or teaching. A neural parser,
encoder, embedding service or hidden external model violates this boundary even when
its output is checked later. Non-Transformer, CPU-only and small-model execution are
not substitutes for non-neural execution.

OCM must still learn. Permitted structures include explicit rules, typed proof terms,
method schemas, constructions, failure records, cost tables and symbolic policies.
Numeric statistics are not inherently neural; proposal scores never become proof
warrants. Do not implement a blanket ban on `BackendKind.STATISTICAL`: its name does
not identify the mechanism, and an opaque PROGRAMMATIC callback could invoke a model.

Human or AI help writing research software is disclosed engineering input. It cannot
supply target-specific proof plans, tactics, route selectors or learned methods during
a scored episode. Freeze generic mechanics before protected target access. Separately
account for authored background mathematics, grammars and search priors.

## Qualification of a successor

Register a closed source/import/operator/binary/library/artifact set, candidate grammar,
file-input set and launch contract. Use an isolated executor with controlled inputs and
no unregistered network/model access. Audit transitive imports, dynamic loaders, callbacks,
child processes, native libraries and restored state; record actual dispatched operators
and external calls. Enforce refusal of undeclared dependencies or dispatch, and test it
with planted forbidden-import, hidden-model-call and restored-state controls.

A keyword scan, backend label, no observed network call, or a successful proof cannot
alone establish neural absence. Combine the declared closure, enforced execution boundary,
source inspection and hostile controls; state remaining observation/trust limits. Failed
or incomplete boundary checks produce CANNOT_CHECK for that episode's qualification.
Do not infer a whole-repository guarantee from one closed experiment.

For the first proof successor, use the fixed proof package and registered non-neural
background, explicit term data, generic mechanical search and a pinned Lean checker.
Do not load the G1 model archives or hosted-model modules. The current checked-text slice
imports neutral helpers through `g1_field`, which imports `udpipe_donor.sha256`; its reviewed
path does not invoke that lazy model loader, but strict source/dependency containment needs
a separately qualified neutral helper/requirements successor.

## Preserve historical attribution

UDPipe1 and Stanza G1 experiments actually attached neural syntax donors to OCM runtime
calls: [G1 vessel](../ocm-prototype/g1_vessel.py), [Stanza vessel](../ocm-prototype/g1_stanza_vessel.py).
They were not merely external comparators. Preserve those sources, results, manifests and
receipts. Under this new direction, future neural routes belong only to separately labelled
external reference/comparison work, never the mechanical OCM arm.

The inspected installed chat and checked-text paths identified no neural inference;
the repository as a whole is not yet qualified or architecturally enforced as neural-free.
The operator registry's warrant discipline does not establish that stronger property.

Published neural-authored proofs may be evaluator/reference artifacts or explicitly supplied
background givens in a reconstruction condition. Such givens are not OCM-acquired structures;
that condition cannot establish end-to-end neural-free acquisition of them. Disclose upstream
neural contribution and cost uncertainty. Strict acquisition arms exclude neural-produced
target methods and teaching traces. Withheld answers and routes stay outside learner access.

No historical evidence is rewritten to meet this successor contract. Every new scored arm
must carry its own exact source, input, mechanism and execution-boundary qualification.
