# Mathematical proof experience → warranted language reasoning

Research critique, 7 September 2026. Prospective design; no acquisition, build,
theorem selection, learning run or qualification rerun was performed.
Read [current concepts](../outputs/ocm-research-plan/18-ORION-CONCEPTS.md), the live
`proof-corpus-coverage-v1/NATIVE_CONTRACT.md`, and `proof-environment-v1/README.md`.

## Decision

Use **many mathematical developments**, with FLT as one demanding case.
Start with mathlib across independently registered subject/module families;
add Metamath as the next distinct proof representation. Do not make five new
prover integrations a prerequisite to demonstrating useful explicit learning.
Rocq/MathComp, Isabelle/AFP and HOL Light then test whether the mechanism survives
different foundations and proof styles. Source diversity is not semantic independence:
ported theorems, common textbook proofs and descendants can cross corpus boundaries.

Current OCM machinery can capture an exposed Lean reference and check an expression
against an independently registered target in a reconstructed environment.
Capture does not discover a method; reference replay does not reconstruct a missing proof.
The missing research step remains checked experience → explicit executable generalization
→ persistence/restart → causal consumption on untouched tasks → net future benefit.

These are candidate sources, not newly registered revisions or claims of uniform
current compatibility. A common OCM catalogue should store native foundation IDs,
typed signatures, dependency/support edges, proof references and method proposals.
Do not erase native semantics into one unqualified AST or merge similarly named
theorems. Cross-foundation transports require their own checked interpretation.

Retain both kernel evidence and available discovery traces. A proof term certifies
the conclusion; it does not reveal all failed attempts, search costs or human insight.
Thus corpus ingestion has an observed cost, while original discovery cost may be unknown.
No neural component may run in acquisition, retrieval, proposal, policy or checking.
Compilation can execute tactics/plugins: pin and qualify an offline executable profile.
Neural-authored upstream proofs remain disclosed reference data, not OCM inventions.
A strict end-to-end acquisition arm needs a separately provenance-qualified teaching set;
a non-neural replay of upstream AI material cannot establish neural-free acquisition.

## One falsifiable experiment: mathematical order methods in language entailment

**Hypothesis:** a method learned from mathematical set/order proofs reduces the total
cost of resolving fresh quantified-language obligations after restart, beyond the
benefit of storing the same new theorems and beyond an equally adaptive symbolic parent.
This tests transfer of reasoning methods through supplied language semantics.
It does not test acquisition of English grammar or general language understanding.

**Training:** freeze a population of checked set/order proof episodes independently
of language test instances. Mine repeated typed subproofs by mechanical anti-unification
and dependency backtracing; candidate generalizations must receive fresh kernel proofs.
Possible discoveries include monotonicity composition or order reversal under complement;
these are hypotheses, not rules secretly inserted as the learned outcome.
Register supplied primitives, the generalization grammar, search policy and all failed
candidates. Persist a method as typed premises/conclusion, proof-producing implementation,
applicability conditions, support IDs and measured retrieval/application cost.

**Language task:** a fixed, deterministic controlled grammar over unary predicates,
Boolean noun phrases, and `every`, `no`, `some`, `not every`; no pronouns, implicit
existence, binary relations or pragmatic/world knowledge in the initial fragment.
Translate compositionally to set inclusion, disjointness and nonempty intersection.
Example: “Every violinist is a musician; no musician is asleep” entails “No
violinist is asleep.” Reversing the first universal or asserting existence need
not follow. Generate negative, unknown and inconsistent cases as well as entailments.
Unsupported/ambiguous inputs refuse; lexical relations must be supplied premises.

**Independent answer check:** register each semantic obligation separately from the
learner's candidate. Check premise consistency first. Entailed/contradicted answers
need proof certificates; unknown needs models for both hypothesis polarities.
For this unary Boolean fragment, finitely many predicate-membership regions permit
an exact decision procedure, rather than an arbitrary finite-domain approximation.
Qualify its translation and certificate checker before using it as an oracle.
Inconsistent premises receive a distinct result; logical explosion is not useful answering.

**Strong parents:** use [LangPro's symbolic natural tableau prover](https://aclanthology.org/D17-2020/)
on matched logical forms/CCG derivations, excluding external statistical/neural parsing;
its [actual input boundary](https://github.com/kovvalsky/LangPro) is explicit.
Also use an optimized complete Boolean-set/BDD or SAT-based solver for this exact fragment.
Choose the best parent on a separate development set, retain all parent results,
and give an adaptive parent the same mathematical episodes and proof-producing chunking.
[Soar already learns reusable rules by dependency-based chunking](https://soar.eecs.umich.edu/soar_manual/04_ProceduralKnowledgeLearning/);
neither chunking nor monotonicity reasoning is itself OCM novelty.
[Natural logic already exploits monotonicity and exclusion](https://nlp.stanford.edu/~manning/papers/natlog-cm.pdf).

**Matched arms:** frozen parent; same-added-theorems library-only; equally adaptive
symbolic parent; OCM learned method; OCM with acquired method disabled but all
facts/indexes retained. Share semantic inputs, axioms, parsing, persistence,
verifier and compute allocation. Log actual method applications that changed search;
a method name in the final explanation is insufficient causal evidence.

**Withholding:** split by mathematical dependency/method family and language semantic
composition, not random theorem rows or paraphrases. Keep an IID test and a separate
unseen-composition test; do not claim generalization merely from renamed nouns.
Retain a renamed-vocabulary control and a math-notation presentation of each language
obligation to locate benefit in reasoning versus parsing. Cross-corpus duplicates and
direct aliases are grouped before splitting; do not promise complete semantic deduplication.

**Falsifiers:** wrong variance, double negation, vacuous universal, empty predicate,
unlicensed existential inference, irrelevant learned method, and withdrawn premises.
After restart, withdraw discovery provenance while preserving an independent correctness
proof; the method may remain eligible. Withdraw an applicability premise and affected
answers must invalidate. Restore it and recheck eligibility without fabricating a proof.

**Score:** warranted answer coverage/correctness, refusals, search expansions, candidates
retrieved, checker work, cold/warm latency, storage and incremental-revision work.
Charge ingestion, method discovery, rejection, compilation, indexing, restart, query,
verification and revision across a preregistered future-task horizon. Report paired
uncertainty by independent task family and harmful transfer; do not sum nested timings.
Success needs causal use plus lower cumulative cost at matched quality against the best
matched parent. No crossover is **NO_AMORTIZED_BENEFIT_OBSERVED**, not hidden success.
Matching the adaptive parent is **PARENT_SUFFICIENT**, an assimilation success but no
distinctive OCM effect. A positive result remains narrow semantic method transfer.

## Next engineering decision

Finish the registered Lean semantic-coverage gate, then implement one shared learning
episode contract across broad mathlib families. Build the above language experiment
around the same persisted method objects, not another independent demonstration.
The already locked four-row FLT assignment remains unchanged. Completing full FLT
is not a prerequisite for this early language-transfer experiment.
Add Metamath once that loop is measurable; use the other ecosystems to challenge its
generality. The OCM-specific question is revision-safe, cost-effective learned reuse,
not the mere presence of a theorem library or a new name for symbolic deduction.

## Narrow review of the companion language design

The root's `21-MATH-TO-LANGUAGE.md` correctly separates theorem application,
verbalization and learned method transfer. [GF](https://www.grammaticalframework.org/doc/gf-refman.html),
[GF-HoTT](https://www.grammaticalframework.org/~aarne/gf-hott/) and
[ACE-in-GF](https://github.com/Attempto/ACE-in-GF) support its grammar-parent choice.
Two precision fixes were sent to the root: scheduling possibility requires a model
or impossibility certificate, not merely an entailment label; contradictory premises
must be distinct. Shuffle only independently warranted methods/rankings or use valid
unrelated methods: type compatibility alone cannot authorize a shuffled procedure.
