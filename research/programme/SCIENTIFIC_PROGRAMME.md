# OCM scientific programme — current research objective

Operator direction, 6 September 2026. This is a research charter, not a manuscript or a finding of novelty.
[Read first](CORE.md) · [experiments](EXPERIMENTS.md) · [nearest parents](PARENTS.md).

## Objective and order

Finish decisive scientific research, produce a small set of substantial papers suitable for top-tier journal review, then develop a real product. Formal science and the [four-level FLT programme](FLT_RECONSTRUCTION.md) are the flagship, with F0 exposed mechanical proposer qualification first. Richer language follows as an independent capability claim.

The intended advance is that useful experience improves future capability or reduces complete future cost while commitments remain justified through change. Better training/acquisition economics, task cost, revision behavior, reliability and scaling are distinct claims. A positive on one axis cannot promote the others.

Spend effort on the smallest experiments that resolve the largest scientific uncertainties. Reuse mature infrastructure and qualified non-neural mechanisms under the [execution contract](NO_NEURAL_CONTRACT.md). Historical neural OCM donor use stays accurately attributed; future neural systems are external references only. Paper writing, presentation and product polish cannot compensate for a missing mechanism or weak comparator.

## Two candidate contributions

| Programme | Scientific question | Required new result | Current status |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1: certified local execution under revision | When can an answer depend on a small active field despite a large, changing persistent library? | A precise algorithm with sound omission/refinement and update contracts; characterized assumptions; measured complete-task benefit against a conventional indexed/incremental/provenance parent | Local extraction and candidate indexes are apparatus; a full composed certificate and whole-task scaling remain open |
| S2: revision-aware library learning | Can acquisition, retention and method selection improve future verified solving while pricing checking, revision and harmful transfer? | A concrete rule using only past information, useful learned structure on new semantic tasks, causal consumption and lower lifetime cost or higher matched-cost capability | Existing primitive aliases and saved-program reuse do not establish this result |

Lead with whichever programme earns a substantial result first. Develop a second paper only if its mechanism and central evidence are independently substantial; otherwise integrate it into one stronger paper. Do not create a paper for each of the 28 proposed subsystems.

A breakthrough remains the ambition. Its scientific substance must be a result that survives the strongest comparisons and external scrutiny, not the number of components, theoretical names or manuscript pages.

## Comparator and attribution design

Equip native parents with the same permitted non-neural algorithms, indexes, persistence, evidence information, independent checkers and adaptation opportunities. Fix capability asymmetries before interpreting a residual. The proposed new mechanism is the experimental treatment; do not silently give it to every baseline and erase the contrast.

Where possible use native-fixed, native-adaptive, OCM-fixed and OCM-adaptive arms. An additional ablation removes the specific proposed mechanism while preserving other powers. If transplanting the mechanism improves a conventional host too, report portability; the scientific contribution can be the mechanism rather than an exclusive architecture brand.

An external-only LLM comparison must freeze the actual model/version and give equivalent documents, tools, feedback and memory. Compare equal-quality cost or equal-cost capability. Disclose pretrained competence and authored priors separately; there is no meaningful claim that a two-function hand-seeded solver trained more cheaply than a general language model.

Do not assume observations within one lifelong session are independent. Select the number of independent lifetimes and quality margins using development precision/power and task consequences before protected outcomes. Report clustered paired uncertainty, failures/timeouts, multiple tested endpoints and both positive and adverse-transfer regimes.

## Paper readiness

A paper is ready for submission only when it has a clear central mechanism or theorem, a targeted nearest-work account, strong matched comparisons, complete resource accounting, meaningful held-out generalization, reproducible code/data and independently auditable claim-to-result bindings.

A theorem must state its model, assumptions, quantifiers and computational interface. Exact enumeration is finite evidence unless a proof covers the declared general family. Refusal and incompleteness retain their meanings through all layers.

Current journal selection is deferred until result and audience are clear. Top-tier standard is the working quality bar; acceptance is an external editorial decision. Manuscript and figure work follows the vendored nature-* package and current claim authority. Existing historical paper-plan tables are not automatically current scientific claims.

## Research closure discipline

A failed hypothesis triggers one-stage diagnosis, a mechanism-based revival and a separately specified retest. Preserve the original negative. Continue generalization through an explicit improvement path, without tuning outcomes or deleting adverse regimes.

Stop expanding an experiment when its declared question is resolved. Select the next experiment by expected information and scientific value per effort. This conserves work while preserving the evidence standard.
