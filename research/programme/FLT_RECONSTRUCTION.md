# FLT reconstruction and mechanical transfer

[Programme](CORE.md) · [no-neural contract](NO_NEURAL_CONTRACT.md) · [parents](PARENTS.md).
Prospective protocol: no FLT gate is claimed passed. Formal science is the flagship;
richer language remains a later independent claim. Exact checking gives a clearer
correctness boundary, not a promise that mathematical search is easier.

## Five gates, including apparatus qualification

| Gate | Engine receives | Engine must construct | Permitted conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| F0: exposed apparatus | Small public logical goal, allowed lemma signatures, finite term grammar | Proof term through generic matching/composition/search | Proposer/checker/custody work at this scope; no FLT progress or discovery |
| F1: masked support | Registered library, target statement and visible boundary of a withheld proof region | Missing supporting lemmas, dependency connections and checked proofs | Reconstruction under the recorded information boundary |
| F2: major landmarks | FLT and only registered major mathematical landmark statements plus allowed background | Intermediate goals, auxiliary objects, lemmas and proofs | Formalization of a supplied route; no unsupplied-strategy claim |
| F3: no supplied route | Exact FLT statement and declared permitted library | Auxiliary structures, decomposition, strategy and checked proof | Route construction relative to that library; supplied route or target-specific script invalidates this claim |
| F4: subsequent transfer | Frozen post-experience machine and independent target families | New checked proofs with measured use of acquired structure | Better future cognition only if matched attribution and full costs support it |

Run early transfer after small episodes; full-FLT transfer is the culminating experiment.
Register each stage separately. F3 describes the frozen machine's access boundary, not
researcher ignorance: FLT and its known route are public and already discussed. Freeze
target-independent mechanics before target access; audit target-specific engineering and
post-freeze human/AI hints. There must be no hardcoded theorem-to-route selector.
F1/F2/F3 are isolated information conditions, each restored from its separately declared
initial snapshot. F2 cannot inherit F1's detailed proof decomposition. F3 starts from a
clean frozen pre-FLT machine/library/history snapshot, not a machine exposed to F1/F2 answers or landmarks. Exclude their persisted methods, indexes, summaries,
failure memory and caches. A cumulative-history arm is separately labelled and cannot
receive the same no-supplied-route credit. F4 records exactly which prior episode supplied its learning.

## Immediate implementation: F0, then a small F1 region

The existing [fixed proof package](../proof-replay-v1/README.md) pins Lean 4.19.0, eight
Foundation theorems and one authored two-lemma composition. It has no Mathlib and performs
fixed replay, not search. The installed science API's default kernel is propositional.

Create a separate successor; preserve the fixed package's manifests, sources and receipts.
For exposed commissioning, mask `Composition.lean`'s body while allowing the two Foundation
lemma signatures and exact target. The theorem is already public/inspected, so this is not
held-out discovery. Generic typed AST constructors, unification, binder introduction and
lemma application must find the term through deterministic bounded search. Retain all
attempts and refusals; do not select the known two-lemma script by theorem name.

Candidate input is registered proof-term data, not unrestricted Lean source. A trusted host
translates permitted constructors; reject new imports, commands, tactics/plugins or arbitrary
code outside the contract. Lean elaboration/builds can execute code: the fixed replay tool is
not an arbitrary-candidate isolation service. Candidate generation and trusted checking need
separate controlled environments and independently pinned inputs.

Controls include missing premise, wrong type/statement, injected axiom/admitted proof, leaked
target constant, forbidden dependency/dispatch, expired applicability, harmless renaming,
restart and deterministic search limits. Qualification must actually exercise them. After F0,
select a small real number-theory region fitting the qualified laptop envelope; register its
corpus, definitions, masks, mechanical parents and checker before evaluating it.

## Generic mechanisms and learned structure

Start with head-symbol/type/arity indexes and discrimination trees; typed application and
unification; backward AND–OR subgoal search; explicit typed constructors/witnesses; and
proof-producing rewrites/transport. A retrieval miss does not prove non-existence. Every
auxiliary definition or representation introduces its actual side obligations.

Abstract successful proof fragments into typed executable schemas; retain failure/context
records and inspectable decision lists, cost tables or symbolic selection programs. Update
from past episodes only. An acquired operator cannot authorize itself or weaken acceptance.
Correctness warrant, applicability, discovery provenance and permitted scope remain separate;
withdrawing discovery evidence need not invalidate an independently proved method.

## Withholding and theorem acceptance

Keep the independently registered target statement as an obligation, never an available
proved premise or target axiom. Remove registered withheld proof bodies, proved target
constants and structurally dependent accessible declarations. Audit known aliases/re-exports,
descendants, cached `.olean` files, generated C, tactic caches, filenames, documentation and
method-library entries. Rebuild allowed imports independently; hiding one source file is
insufficient. Keep answer artifacts and the full published route outside learner access.

Do not promise an algorithm that proves no logically equivalent theorem exists anywhere in
the background. State the scoped dependency/alias review, use deliberate-leak controls and
independent inspection, and refuse inadequately established boundaries. The allowed background
must still support deduction. F2's supplied landmarks are information, not free discovered work.

Every scored proof must match the independently fixed formal target under the allowed
constants/definitions, pass transitive permitted-axiom inspection, and undergo fresh trusted
kernel replay with complete dependencies. Weak/vacuous replacements, `sorryAx`, or a target
smuggled as an axiom fail. Independently check informal/formal correspondence; famous landmark
names do not certify unrestricted classical statements. A second checker helps only under its
own audited source, patches, input and trust contract. Missing checks are CANNOT_CHECK.

## F4 attribution and complete lifetime economics

Freeze retained structures and policy before held-out targets; use independent semantic proof
families and lifetimes. Compare base state; a library-only arm with exactly the same added
verified lemmas/proofs but initial methods/policy; that same library plus learned methods with
a fixed selector; the full learned method/policy machine; and a strong adaptive symbolic parent
with matched history, checker and acquisition opportunities. This separates extra mathematical
facts from executable-method learning and policy/organization learning.

Add learned-method removal, matched irrelevant-library and harmful-transfer controls. Require
actual use after restart and a causal difference under otherwise matched removal; merely
printing or invoking a learned name is insufficient. Report failures and negative transfer.

Charge acquisition and authored inputs, compilation, retrieval/index building and maintenance,
all search attempts, elaboration, checking, persistence/storage, reorganization, revision and
relearning. Report a resource vector or justified unit-bearing weights. Warm query savings,
acquisition-inclusive crossover and quality are separate endpoints; neither a stored answer
nor a larger theorem database establishes improved lifetime cognition.

## Published FLT reference and resource boundary

The inspected published pin is [aa2d8b34692b16c70f699536de0d8e75b9a3e9ef](https://github.com/anthropics/fermats-last-theorem/tree/aa2d8b34692b16c70f699536de0d8e75b9a3e9ef).
Its [build definition](https://github.com/anthropics/fermats-last-theorem/blob/aa2d8b34692b16c70f699536de0d8e75b9a3e9ef/lakefile.lean)
uses Lean 4.33.1 and Mathlib pin `db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d` (v4.33.0).
This differs from our fixed 4.19.0 package; compatibility/ports require new qualification.
Keep the historical 4.19.0 replay unchanged. A new scored proof successor must pin and
qualify a release containing applicable kernel soundness fixes (candidate 4.33.1), rather
than inherit 4.19.0 for convenience; see the official [4.33.0 soundness fixes](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/releases/v4.33.0/).
This does not establish that the historical fixed proofs are invalid.

[FinalCheck](https://github.com/anthropics/fermats-last-theorem/blob/aa2d8b34692b16c70f699536de0d8e75b9a3e9ef/FinalCheck.lean)
checks the three permitted axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` and derives
Mathlib's FLT proposition. The independent [comparator challenge](https://github.com/anthropics/fermats-last-theorem/blob/aa2d8b34692b16c70f699536de0d8e75b9a3e9ef/verification/comparator/Challenge.lean)
states both elementary and Mathlib targets; its `sorry` placeholders specify obligations,
not accepted proofs. Pin the actual resolved comparator/exporter/kernel and patch hashes.
The published comparator wrapper's recorded fake-landrun mode is not an isolation guarantee.

The [authors' README](https://github.com/anthropics/fermats-last-theorem/blob/aa2d8b34692b16c70f699536de0d8e75b9a3e9ef/README.md)
reports source-build peak RAM 153 GB, comparator peak 230 GB (300 GB recommended), and a
successful separate patched-nanoda check. These are author-reported verification outcomes,
not rerun here or proof-generation cost. Full verification exceeds the current laptop envelope;
qualify suitable RAM/storage/concurrency before scheduling it. Begin with bounded regions.

The neural-agent campaign follows substantial existing mathematics and a known route. It is
an external reference, not a matched non-neural or theorem-only baseline. Do not import its
answers/route into stronger discovery arms. This plan schedules bounded preparation first; full replay follows a qualified compute/checking contract.
