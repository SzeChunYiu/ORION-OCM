# OCM nearest-parent assimilation matrix

Checked 2026-09-06. Research planning only; no manuscript, novelty clearance, or new experimental result.
Scope: ten selected primary research parents spanning library induction, support/provenance, incremental execution, representations and language-agent memory.
All proposed OCM residuals below are hypotheses to falsify, not claims established by these sources.

## Decision

Keep two possible paper programmes: **certified local execution under evidence revision** and **revision-aware library learning with measured lifetime benefit**.
Do not write a paper per subsystem. Persistent memory, executable skills, support alternatives, indexed retrieval, append-only history, compression and incremental updates are existing mechanisms.
Assimilate the mechanisms first; a combined native implementation gets the same mechanisms before testing any OCM-specific residual.

## Governing implementation boundary

The [no-neural contract](NO_NEURAL_CONTRACT.md) applies to every OCM arm. Full DreamCoder recognition, LILO, Voyager and neural language/memory-agent configurations remain external-only references. Extracting explicit library, graph or search mechanisms is an adapted non-neural component study, not a faithful reproduction of the complete neural parent. Match substantive symbolic parents without neural premise selection, embedding retrieval or policy assistance. Preserve historical UDPipe/Stanza cognition inside prior OCM arms.

The [FLT protocol](FLT_RECONSTRUCTION.md) is the next flagship. Candidate symbolic baselines, not yet compatibility-qualified with FLT's 4.33.1 toolchain:

## Representation boundary

For any proposed Representation Atlas, define concrete semantics, abstraction/concretization and operator soundness before implementation.
Analogy or geometric similarity cannot establish equivalence. Operator-relative certificate invalidation needs a separate focused review of abstract interpretation/refinement before any claim.

## Candidate paper A — certified local execution under revision

**Question:** can a demand-driven algorithm return the same warranted task result as a complete reference, while charging mainly the actually necessary region plus its update dependencies?

Required contribution:
- A precise algorithm and soundness argument composing navigation error bounds, complete candidate eligibility, both support bounds, checker obligations and snapshot-bound invalidation.
- A certificate says why omitted state cannot change the committed result; uncertainty yields refinement/refusal, not an answer.
- An implementation integrates these obligations in the real task path and demonstrates reduced complete work against the combined indexed/ATMS/provenance/IVM parent.
- Identify graph/obligation/workload assumptions. Do not infer general locality from disconnected distractors or a theorem for a different transition kernel.

Falsifying panel:
- Fixed relevant task plus 10×/100×/1000× irrelevant growth; local support withdrawal, upper-only changes, alternate proofs and new applicable operators.
- Connected weak distractors, near-tied routing scores and an omitted decisive operator.
- Global quantification/shared-premise changes where large work is genuinely necessary.
- Cold construction, index maintenance, residual repair, refinement, replay and all checks charged.
- Refutation: any unsound committed answer; or no useful cost frontier beyond the equally equipped conventional parent.
- The publication candidate is the composed algorithm and its proven/measured scope. Combining familiar components by itself is not a result.

## Candidate paper B — revision-aware lifetime library learning

**Question:** can acquisition/retention/selection that prices future verification and revision improve later cognition beyond equally adaptive program-library parents?

Required contribution:
- A concrete learning/selection rule, using only past/training information, that predicts later benefit rather than selecting by test outcomes.
- Acquire from verified experience; normalize aliases; retain typed current justification separately from discovery provenance.
- Demonstrate causal use of acquired structure on new task structures, followed by independently checked results.
- Improve the complete capability/resource frontier, including failed induction, maintenance, invalidation and relearning.

Falsifying panel:
- Native fixed, native adaptive and OCM adaptive; additionally an OCM adaptive ablation with the proposed rule removed.
- Same tasks, priors, semantic specifications, permitted non-neural algorithms, primitive/library search space, storage, verifiers and feedback; add the same-new-theorems library-only control before attributing proof transfer to learned methods/policy.
- Future related families, unrelated families, harmful transfer and changed/revoked applicability; independent correct proof can retain a method.
- Remove the learned library/selected abstraction and observe a change in actual search or verified executable use.
- Track cumulative verified solutions and total resource cost over increasing lifetime; report break-even and uncertainty, not warm-call timing alone.
- Refutation: aliases only, no causal later consumption, no amortization, or a strongest parent reproducing the benefit.
- LILO/Voyager adaptations with different domains or donors are labeled adaptations; publish their differences rather than claiming exact reproduction.

## Measurement and claim discipline

- Use a capability/cost vector, or preregister dimensional weights; bytes, time and verifier calls cannot be added without units.
- Distinguish training, development feasibility and protected evaluation. Fit/choose representations and parameters without protected future tasks.
- Treat UNKNOWN, unsupported language and failed verification as separate terminals; a retrieval miss cannot establish non-existence.
- No GPU-versus-symbolic comparison with unequal information or pretrained competence. External model pretraining is disclosed; no pretrained neural competence enters the mechanical OCM arm. Unequal-information external comparisons cannot establish an OCM mechanism residual.
- PARENT_SUFFICIENT is a valid positive engineering result, not an OCM novelty claim.
- Source verification here does not establish that no nearer 2026 method exists. Each surviving algorithm must receive a targeted nearest-work search after its exact mechanics are fixed.
- Do not open a third paper on a generic library/router/atlas. Representation invention is deferred until a distinct obstruction, mechanism and parent-exceeding result exist.
