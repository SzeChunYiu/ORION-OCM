## 4. The shared neutral grammar `G`, frozen before any data is read

This is the shared neutral grammar of the programme (the census, tranche A
`SIGMA_HA`, tranche C `SIGMA_TC`) — the same specification, unchanged:

- Leaves: `x0 x1 x2 x3 x4 x5 x6 x7` (the standard interface), `0`, `1`.
- Operations: `NOT` (arity 1), `XOR`, `AND` (arity 2).
- **Cost** `c(E)`: `1` per leaf, `1 + c(kids)` per operator node
  (node count).
- **Interface**: each scope uses the interface `(a, b)` of leaves it reads.
  Semantics are total on `{0,1}^a`.
- **Semantic quotient**: expressions are grouped by their full-interface
  denotation (truth table over all 256 input points); one canonical
  representative per class (fewest nodes, then smallest rendering).
  Quotienting is response-independent and runs once, before any target.
- **Search**: complete enumeration of every expression class at cost `<= 5`
  (718 classes on the full 256-point interface, verified by the six-layer
  census below); a candidate is recovered on the protected interface by exact
  match then minimum cost then canonical rendering. No family label and no
  family menu reach the search.

**Six-layer census** (complete semantic classes by exact node cost, computed
on the full 256-point interface): `c=1 -> 10`, `c=2 -> 8`, `c=3 -> 56`,
`c=4 -> 112`, `c=5 -> 532`, cumulative `<= 5 -> 718` (10,050 syntax trees).
The grammar digest is `16289266d044cb1c1e26e48e7df20c71767ee1064d9b19da65dbfa60dbea1b34`,
the same digest as tranche A `SIGMA_HA` and the census — the `R02` shared
neutral grammar is a statement about one grammar, not nine.

**No family names anywhere in the grammar or search.** Family names are
attached only after recovery, by an external post-hoc structural classifier
that reads expression trees and never a family label. The forbidden-token
list and the semantic audit are in §12.

The registered channel roles follow the frozen registry: `x5` is the
stochastic-source probe, `x6` the update/learning-feedback probe, `x7` the
external peer/tool/participant probe, `x4` the persistent-state probe, `x3`
the addressable-context probe. The registry supplies channel labels only.

## 6. The nine protected interfaces and their rationales (R01)

All rationales are static, above-budget mechanical identities on the frozen
grammar `G` — no sampler, no simulator, no PRNG, no tuning. Each family is
defined AS A CONTRACT OVER OBSERVABLES, and the protected channel is the
coordinate whose read is the registered observable.

- **Evolutionary/population search** (`SIGMA_TD01`): a family is evolutionary
  search when its response is produced by iterated evaluate-recombine-mutate
  over a finite population with charged per-step costs. The observable
  contract is dependence on a registered population-source probe: the
  response reads the stochastic source channel `x5` together with a companion
  coordinate (golden `XOR(x0,x5)`, cost 3). The matched negative control
  `XOR(x0,x1)` reads two interior coordinates and no source channel.
- **Cellular/local-field computation** (`SIGMA_TD02`): a family is
  cellular/local-field when its response is produced by local-window update
  rules over a lattice with no global channel. The observable contract is
  dependence on a neighboring-cell signal: the response reads the external
  peer/tool channel `x7` conjunctively with a companion coordinate
  (golden `AND(x0,x7)`, cost 3).
- **Distributed/collective intelligence** (`SIGMA_TD03`): a family is
  distributed/collective when multiple agents share only a common observation
  channel with no central controller. The observable contract is dependence
  on an external participant signal: golden `AND(x2,x7)`, cost 3, the peer
  channel `x7` gated by the participant coordinate `x2`.
- **Tool-using/solver-routing intelligence** (`SIGMA_TD04`): a family is
  tool-using when a solver routes subproblems to specialized sub-artifacts
  under a charged acquisition contract. The observable contract is dependence
  on a tool result: golden `XOR(x1,x7)`, cost 3, the peer/tool channel `x7`
  mixed by parity with the routed coordinate `x1`.
- **Neuro-symbolic/statistical-symbolic hybrids** (`SIGMA_TD05`): a family is
  a hybrid when a statistical front-end composes with a symbolic store under
  a typed interface. The observable contract is a registered three-source
  hybrid composition: golden `XOR(XOR(x0,x1),x2)`, cost 5, three sources
  combined by one composition operator.
- **Continual-learning systems** (`SIGMA_TD06`): a family is continual-learning
  when one system is re-fit on a nonstationary task stream with charged
  retraining. The observable contract is dependence on an update-feedback
  probe: golden `AND(x0,x6)`, cost 3, the update channel `x6` read
  conjunctively.
- **Meta-learning systems** (`SIGMA_TD07`): a family is meta-learning when its
  acquisition of a per-task adaptation rule is itself learned on a charged
  outer loop. The observable contract is dependence on a learning-feedback
  probe: golden `XOR(x0,x6)`, cost 3, the update channel `x6` read by parity.
- **Self-modifying/morphogenetic systems** (`SIGMA_TD08`): a family is
  self-modifying when it rewrites its own update rule under a registered
  rewrite contract. The observable contract is dependence on a self-update
  probe: golden `AND(x1,x6)`, cost 3. The matched negative control `x4` reads
  only the persistent-state probe and no self-update channel.
- **Multi-agent emergent communication systems** (`SIGMA_TD09`): a family is
  multi-agent emergent when agents develop a channel protocol jointly,
  measured by joint achievable score minus channel charge. The observable
  contract is dependence on a peer message: golden `XOR(x0,x7)`, cost 3, the
  peer channel `x7` mixed by parity with the message coordinate `x0`.

**R01 ecology predictions** (on a DISTINCT evidence key from the recovery
class, following the tranche A addendum discipline): on the complete-cube
ecology `{0,1}^8` with budget `B=5` the probe contracts realise at exact
minimum node cost — `x5` at 1, `x6` at 1, `x7` at 1, `x4` at 1; every channel
composite of the table realises at exact cost 3; the triple composition
`XOR(XOR(x0,x1),x2)` and its `AND` twin realise at exact cost 5. These are
exact counting statements about the ecology alone, which the family-blind
search then confirms.

## 8. Resource crossover (R07) and exact lower bound (R06)

**Cost law.** `c(E)` = node count. `B = 5` (frozen in §4).

**R06.** Complete enumeration gives, for each golden, an exact minimum node
cost with zero strictly cheaper candidates; the lower bound is exhaustive and
reported per row.

**R07.** At each scope the registered resource coordinate is the generic
serving allocation for the golden's protected channel (the tranche A and
classical `A5` pattern, exact). A serve plan must provide the channel value at
each of `H` serve positions; the golden composite reads the channel `r` times
per position (`r = 2` for every row except `SIGMA_TD05` where `r = 3`), once
per read-site. Two plans:

- **replay**: every read-site re-reads the channel at every serve position:
  `cost = H * r * leaf_read`.
- **stored**: every read-site loads its own cell once up front, and each
  serve position reads the loaded cells in one pass:
  `cost = r * cell_write + H * cell_read`.

Two frozen exact integer price regimes:

| regime | leaf_read | cell_write | cell_read |
|---|---|---|---|
| `REPLAY_CHEAP` (A) | 1 | 5 | 1 |
| `STORED_CHEAP` (B) | 4 | 1 | 1 |

**Prediction (frozen before any run):** at horizon `H = 1` the winners differ
in all nine rows — under regime A the replay plan is the strict winner (cost
`2` against `11` for the `r = 2` rows; `3` against `16` for the `r = 3` row);
under regime B the stored plan is the strict winner at every `H >= 1` (cost
`8` against `3` for the `r = 2` rows; `12` against `4` for the `r = 3` row).
Under regime A the stored plan strictly overtakes at the registered crossing
horizon `H = 11` (`r = 2`) and `H = 8` (`r = 3`). The crossover is exact,
integer, and reported per row.

## 9. Independent regeneration (R09) and independent search (R10)

**R09.** Two independent-regeneration controls per row, computed from the
frozen grammar alone:

1. **Order-reversal**: the candidate list is presented in reverse enumeration
   order; the champion of each golden's class must preserve its post-hoc
   class.
2. **Coordinate-label transport**: the golden's coordinates are permuted (all
   non-identity permutations of the coordinates appearing in the golden);
   every transported spelling must preserve the golden's truth table and its
   post-hoc class.

**R10.** `independent_oracle_v1.py` is a source-separated implementation
(imports none of `grammar_td_v1` / `tranche_d_v1`, enforced by an `ast` scan)
that re-derives, for every scope, the champion's exact protected semantics by
its own enumeration and reports the same minimal-cost class. `R10` holds when
the oracle agrees with the primary on every scope's minimal-cost class and
cost.

## 11. Controls and nulls

- **Matched negative controls** are per-row contracts the row's predicted
  class must NOT satisfy (defined in §3/§6, executed by `tranche_d_v1.py` and
  certified by the exact class census). The champion of each control's truth
  table must be outside the predicted class.
- **Null batteries.** For each scope a null battery draws up to 200 distinct
  semantic classes (deterministic SHA-256 counter stream, seed
  `GMI833H-TD|<scope>`, draw `k` appends `|k`) from the pool of classes whose
  dependence set is DISJOINT from the golden's dependence set. A null
  recovers iff its champion's post-hoc class equals the row's predicted class;
  the battery must recover `0/N` (for `SIGMA_TD05` the disjoint pool is 182
  classes, so the battery is the full pool, `0/182`).
- **Hostiles.** Every hostile carries an `applicable` flag: the run fails if a
  hostile cannot move its quantity. Registered hostiles: grammar extension
  (adding a leaf or an operation moves the ensemble), a family label offered
  to the classifier, a foreign `sigma` stamped on a certificate, a float in a
  claimed quantity, a parent gate certificate offered as evidence, a relabel
  control that does not preserve class, and a null that recovers. A clean
  variant of each hostile must not alarm.
- **Non-degeneracy.** Every scope reports its exact interface sizes, the
  enumeration's class counts, the golden/control node counts and error counts;
  degenerate zeros fail the run.

- **Forbidden-token scan.** Every search-visible source (`grammar_td_v1.py`,
  the causal functions of `tranche_d_v1.py`, the oracle) is scanned for the
  family tokens `evolutionar`, `population`, `cellular`, `local-field`,
  `distribut`, `collective`, `tool-using`, `solver-routing`,
  `neuro-symbolic`, `statistical-symbolic`, `continual`, `meta-learning`,
  `self-modif`, `morphogen`, `multi-agent`, `emergent`, `communicat`. A
  positive control (a planted family-shaped token) must be caught; a negative
  control (the frozen basis) must stay clean.
- **Semantic audit.** The grammar's primitives carry no family-shaped
  signature: a planted channel macro with an eleven-coordinate signature must
  be flagged; the frozen basis must stay CLEAN. The audit is imported only for
  its form.
- **Generated, not authored.** The search is generated by the grammar
  enumeration; no family label reaches the search, and the provenance of every
  recovered structure is the frozen grammar.
