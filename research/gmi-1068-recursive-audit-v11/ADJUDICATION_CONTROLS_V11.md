# Exact adjudication controls
The first V11 successor checker preserved original identities and histories
but did not constrain the new repair scopes or individual evidence mappings.
Independent review reproduced four bypasses: unrestricted repair claims,
pending repair status, unrelated theorem citations, and coordinated receipt
and disposition broadening.

The reviewed audit contract now fixes every new repair and closing atom,
including status, scope and exact witness kind/locator. It verifies receipt
schema, all current package hashes, all three kernel-source bindings,
fourteen registered statement types, measured coverage and context evidence.

The integrated proof replay remains necessary: custody checks do not execute
the kernel. CI runs both layers and the successor's hostile regression tests
in ordinary and optimized Python. Missing inputs remain CANNOT_CHECK; invalid
claims or bindings are checked failures. The checker and its adjudication
contract remain reviewer-trusted, not a proof of their own soundness.

Only the three preregistered scientific requirements may close. None of
these controls certify all science, every architecture or absence of unknown
defects.
