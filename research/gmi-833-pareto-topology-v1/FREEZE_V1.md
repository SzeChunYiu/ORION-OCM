## Frozen target: DIST-1B / TOPO-1A

The primary transform burden must remain a nonnegative vector, including declared semantic distortion and lifecycle-resource coordinates. A single scalar price vector is a projection, not the fundamental geometry.

For a finite set of nonnegative burden vectors, let `PF(S)` be its Pareto-minimal antichain. Define finite antichain operations:

- choice: `A (+) B = PF(A union B)`;
- sequential composition: `A (*) B = PF({a+b : a in A, b in B})`;
- identity: `{0}`;
- unreachable: empty antichain.

Prove/check:

1. Pareto normalization is idempotent;
2. choice is associative, commutative and idempotent with empty identity;
3. composition is associative with `{0}` identity and empty absorbing;
4. composition distributes over Pareto choice;
5. finite multiobjective path closure therefore yields a Pareto frontier `H(M,N)` of attainable directed transform burdens rather than a fabricated coordinatewise minimum;
6. for any frozen strictly-positive scalar price vector `w`, `d_w(M,N)=min_{v in H(M,N)} w·v` recovers a directed Lawvere-style scalar pseudometric and obeys triangle inequality; different Pareto frontiers can collapse to the same scalar distance, so scalarization is information-losing;
7. define strict forward budget balls `B_b(M)={N : exists v in H(M,N), v_i < b_i for every coordinate}` for strictly-positive budget vector `b`;
8. prove these balls form a basis for a forward topology: identity puts `M` in every positive ball, and if `N in B_b(M)` with witness `v<b`, then residual budget `r=b-v>0` satisfies `B_r(N) subseteq B_b(M)` by additive composition and Pareto domination;
9. unreachable pairs stay outside every finite budget ball;
10. symmetry, Hausdorffness, manifold structure and a universal resource scalarization are not assumed.
