# R1-S1 successor freeze — typed process/path basis

Issue: #1068
Ancestor R1 merge: 30161557b66bfcf59b9261acf4b99ca2ceab2064

This freeze precedes R1-S1 outcome-bearing implementation.

## Defects under attack

1. R1 V1 counts identity, associativity and identity laws as separate retained primitive components.
2. An identity morphism was described as "no-change", which can be misread as asserting a positive-duration physical stasis operation.
3. The claim that an ordinary category is the weakest common process structure conflates structural content with a choice of mathematical presentation.
4. The six-hostile receipt does not separately perturb/remove PROCESS and COMPOSITION.

## Candidate repair

Attack the weaker process-side basis:

- interface/configuration types;
- substrate-admitted primitive/nontrivial process steps with typed source/target;
- finite typed paths, including the empty path;
- sequential path concatenation when endpoints match.

In a path presentation, identities are empty paths and unit/associativity laws follow from path concatenation. A substrate may instead present already-composed extensional processes; quotienting/equating paths is extra semantic structure.

The category C_S remains a useful canonical derived normal form when composition/equality of processes is declared, but R1-S1 must not claim it is the unique weakest encoding.

## Frozen targets

- executable finite typed-path construction;
- illegal boundary-join hostile;
- nontrivial-process removal hostile;
- sequencing-removal hostile;
- derive empty-path identity and associativity rather than assume them as independent ontic primitives;
- show two presentation styles can realize the same registered reachability/history semantics;
- narrow the irreducibility claim to requirement-relative typed process/path content;
- reconcile all R1 atomic rows and supersede the V1 six-component primitive reading.
