# Parent ownership — what this tranche does not claim

Assimilation first. Each parent below is absorbed and its result used; the residual this
tranche adds is stated at the end and is deliberately small.

## External mathematics

|---|---|---|

## Repository parents, pinned by path and blob

`gmi-833-aj5-g0-lowering-v1` owns the core five statuses and the `5t` overhead bound.
`gmi-833-g0-stochastic-update-v1`, `gmi-833-g0-local-graph-ops-v1`,
`gmi-833-g0-interaction-channels-v1` and `gmi-833-g0-governed-self-change-v1` own the operator
semantics, the registered universes and the declared resource vectors. This tranche treats all
five as authoritative and compares against them; it does not restate their results as its own.

## What is NOT claimed novel

Operator decomposition, exact rational arithmetic, matrix composition, channel semantics,
attested update, term algebra, and every named parent above. Neither is the observation that a
named opcode can be a macro — AJ5 established that for the core five.

## The residual contribution

1. An exhaustive, two-route lowering of the four registered extension families into the AJ5
   role basis, with the parents' own resource vectors reproduced rather than re-declared.
2. A twenty-one row status ledger whose every entry is read off a published measurement, with
   the status rules stated before the ledger is built.
3. Two structural residuals given as measured witness counts rather than prose: the admission
   relation cannot be folded into the internal state transform, and exactly one operator is
   priced by structure.
4. A named boundary the scope cannot cross, published with its structural reason instead of
   being absorbed into a clean number.
