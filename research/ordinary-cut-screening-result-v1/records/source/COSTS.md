# Counter and timing scope

Grammar construction attempts and completions are separate. One engine is retained for
each exact canonical parameter descriptor; constructor failures are cached as refusals.
The whole P1 is supplied to every construction. Failed construction wall time is charged;
its partial contract-read count is unavailable, not zero.

Every syntax request records status, parse/emission time, attempted parser calls and input
tokens. Successful syntax construction labels count even when used only for a type test.
Alias proof label counts record successfully returned complete assembled witnesses.
Internal Earley chart operations and partially emitted labels on failure are unavailable.

The unchanged matcher records token_states, type_checks, essential_matches and dv_checks.
P1_assertions_visited counts entered rows, not rows merely remaining in the library.
A rejected state over the registered bound is recorded separately from accepted states.
Time and work refusal leaves coverage incomplete and retains earlier positive witnesses.

The future driver interval starts before input reads and source/gate checking. The saved
measured_driver_wall_s ends before final custody, module inventory and RESULT serialization;
the separately reviewed outer observer must measure those costs. Individual SCREEN writes
are inside the driver interval. Its exact 180-second containment is not implemented here.

Original attempt costs are preserved verbatim: driver result plus caller and outer receipts.
Do not add nested intervals twice or call a fresh process an evicted operating-system cache.

Authored control costs are separate from any scientific screening measurement. The
qualification process interval includes two synthetic isolated refusal subprocesses.
Observer source/import hashing, initial setup and final receipt serialization are excluded.
