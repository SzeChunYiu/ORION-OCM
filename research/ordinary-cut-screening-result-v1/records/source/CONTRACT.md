# Retained ordinary screening: prospective source contract

This successor repairs only the syntax interface used by the existing ordinary one-step
screen. Its inputs are the original 76 CUT_PROPOSAL occurrences, in root/cut order, and
the exact saved 4,323-contract P1. It performs no extraction, training, native proof replay,
acquisition, serving admission or hidden evaluation. Duplicate canonical bodies remain
separate occurrences. No new roots or replacements are allowed.

## Mechanisms

- Five matcher functions are transplanted with identical AST. Composite and repeated
  substitutions, ordered essential-hypothesis matching, reuse, omission and current
  no-ambient-DV semantics remain unchanged.
- The reviewed contract-derived grammar, syntax engine and structural proof replay are
  copied exactly, including the corrected distinction between grammar and query coverage.
- One grammar is constructed per exact canonical body parameter descriptor and full P1.
  Grammar creation failures are retained for that context; no syntax-result cache is added.
- Full P1 source order is visited, including syntax contracts and whole demonstrated
  theorems. There is no subset selected to make a proposal appear novel.

## Outcomes and refusal

SYNTAX UNKNOWN propagates through construction, ground validation, matching and witness
emission. ValueError from the syntax callback denotes only completed nonmembership in
a supported grammar; the unchanged matcher may cache that negative type test.

A positive structural proof can be ALIAS_FOUND_PROOF_READY while other rules or grammar
coverage remain unknown. It is not native admission. Complete screen negatives require
supported grammar, every P1 row visited, and no unknown disposition. A stopped row may
lose its internally accumulated, not-yet-returned matcher candidates; earlier emitted
aliases are retained. This preserves the donor matcher's return semantics.

The three-variable homogeneous class/wff context and no ambient DV remain explicit.
Linear/no-essential/no-DV syntax compilation follows the documented Metamath $j
syntax-tool convention, not a universal constraint of the native verifier.

## Resources and costs

The original 2,000,000 matcher-state and 60-second soft window are unchanged. The window
begins at driver entry before bound input materialization. The existing syntax prototype
refuses inputs longer than 512 tokens or syntax proofs longer than 4,096 labels as UNKNOWN.
Those are explicit syntax-domain boundaries, not substitutions for the study bounds.
A running Earley call is not internally interrupted; a later separate observer must
retain the registered 180-second outer containment and partial failure evidence.

Charge cold context construction, parse/emission wall time, calls/tokens, successful
emitted proof labels, alias proof labels, parent visits and all matcher counters. Native
calls are zero. Earley internal chart operations and interrupted emission-label counts
are uninstrumented; their wall cost is retained, never reported as zero work.
No warm-cache saving beyond the declared per-context grammar reuse is claimed.

The original result, caller and outer-process receipts stay bound with the successor.
Their disjoint attempt windows remain visible; nested subprocess intervals are not
added twice. Source preparation and authored control costs are separate from a future
screening measurement.

## Gate and current scope

This tranche permits only source preparation and authored integration controls.
REQUEST-DRAFT.json has no execution gate or qualified outer observer. A separate root
review/gate and exact once-only observer are required before reading the saved inputs
through this new parser or screen. A source-control pass grants no corpus outcome.
