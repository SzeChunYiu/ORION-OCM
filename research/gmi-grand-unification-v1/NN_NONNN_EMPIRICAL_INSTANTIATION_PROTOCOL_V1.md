# Grand GMI NN / Non-NN Empirical Instantiation Protocol V1

Status: **PREREGISTRATION / EVIDENCE-ACQUISITION PROTOCOL; NO EMPIRICAL FAMILY VERDICT CLAIMED**  
Date: 2026-09-12

## 1. Why this layer exists

The formal NN/non-NN derivation grammar is closed at registered scope only in the logical sense. A real `DERIVED_NEURAL`, `DERIVED_NON_NEURAL` or `DERIVED_HYBRID` verdict still needs facts that mathematics cannot manufacture:

- the actual protected task/ecology;
- real substrate resource measurements or proved physical bounds;
- a real development/training/search law and its budget;
- deployment/generalization evidence;
- an explicit candidate universe.

This protocol turns those external premises into a preregisterable experiment packet whose output can be consumed by the existing NN/non-NN derivation certificate and family-frontier phase theorem.

It is deliberately family-neutral. Neural, non-neural and hybrid candidates are charged under the same declared accounting rules.

## 2. Non-negotiable order

A family-selection experiment is valid only in this order:

1. **freeze the GMI problem and protected metrics;**
2. **freeze the candidate universe and family definitions;**
3. **freeze the development/search budgets;**
4. **freeze resource coordinates and accounting boundaries;**
5. **freeze deployment/generalization split and interventions;**
6. **freeze uncertainty and selection rules;**
7. **freeze predictions/falsifiers;**
8. only then execute protected measurements;
9. emit a receipt without changing the frozen rules;
10. attack any unique family verdict by candidate expansion.

Changing a load-bearing item after protected outcomes are known starts a new version. It is not a continuation of the same preregistration.

## 3. E0 — problem registration

The packet must bind the full registered GMI problem:

\[
\Sigma=(\mathbf P,\mathcal E,\Omega,\rho,Q).
\]

Required fields:

- `problem_id` and immutable problem version;
- protected input/context domain;
- protected response/action relation;
- tolerance/loss definition;
- ecology distribution or finite ecology set;
- intervention/adversarial family;
- semantic quotient/response metric used by the theory;
- capability pass threshold and null/baseline controls.

If the obligation itself changes, the architecture question changed too.

## 4. E1 — operational family definitions

Family names must be converted into protected operational predicates before measurement.

At minimum define:

- `NEURAL`: the registered operational criteria that make a realization neural for this study;
- `NON_NEURAL`: the registered disjoint or complementary criteria;
- `HYBRID`: the rule for mixed regional realization and the boundary between regions.

A string label such as `Transformer`, `program` or `neuromorphic` is insufficient unless its load-bearing operational properties are registered.

Family membership must be decided from the candidate artifact/configuration, not from whether the result is favorable.

## 5. E2 — candidate universe

Every candidate record must freeze:

- `candidate_id`;
- family;
- implementation/version hash;
- substrate/hardware target;
- train/search/synthesis procedure;
- hyperparameter/search space;
- initialization/randomness source;
- permitted tools/external memory;
- precision/numerical regime;
- compilation/runtime stack;
- declared structural properties used by architecture-refinement claims.

For a question explicitly comparing NN with non-NN intelligence, the registered universe must contain at least one serious adequate candidate from each family or the result cannot exclude the missing family.

A unique winner is always candidate-universe-relative unless a separate completeness theorem exists.

## 6. E3 — development/reachability accounting

Endpoint existence is not enough. For every candidate, freeze the developmental process and budget:

- number of training examples/episodes;
- optimizer/search/synthesis/evolution rule;
- gradient/evaluation/query budget;
- wall-clock or compute budget;
- energy consumed during development when measured;
- model-selection and hyperparameter-search cost;
- number of seeds/restarts;
- early-stopping rule;
- checkpoint-selection rule;
- failure terminal when the endpoint is not reached.

Development cost and deployment cost are separate registered coordinates unless a scalarization explicitly combines them.

A candidate that exists in principle but is not reached under its registered development law is not in the realized reachable set for that packet.

## 7. E4 — resource vector and accounting boundary

The resource vector must be declared before measurement, for example

\[
\rho=(E_{dev},E_{deploy},T_{dev},T_{lat},M_{peak},M_{persistent},BW,\text{matter},\text{precision},\ldots).
\]

Every coordinate needs:

- unit;
- measurement boundary;
- warm-up policy;
- preprocessing/data-movement inclusion rule;
- batch/concurrency regime;
- amortization rule;
- tool/API/external-store accounting;
- uncertainty method.

Do not compare GPU joules, CPU time, network calls and human effort by an implicit scalar. Either retain the Pareto vector or preregister an explicit scalarization with units/weights and a justification.

When substrates differ, the semantic task stays fixed while the physical frontier is allowed to deform. Cross-substrate comparison is therefore valid only through declared common resource coordinates or a declared substrate-specific decision rule.

## 8. E5 — capability and deployment evidence

Training fit does not discharge deployment adequacy.

Freeze independently:

- development/training set;
- validation/model-selection set;
- protected deployment/generalization set;
- interventions/OOD shifts;
- leakage guards;
- external verifier when exact correctness is available.

The generalization-identifiability layer applies before extrapolation claims: if training evidence leaves a target fiber whose protected responses vary by more than the admitted tolerance, the unseen response is not identified from that evidence alone. Additional structure, probes or parent-theory guarantees are required.

Any feature used at deployment must be available from the task statement, prior registered state or charged actions. Solution-derived features are forbidden unless the run is explicitly labeled as an oracle ceiling.

## 9. E6 — replication and uncertainty

The packet must preregister how uncertainty becomes a bound consumed by the family-phase theorem.

Possible valid routes include:

- exhaustive finite enumeration;
- exact symbolic/verified bounds;
- confidence intervals from independent repetitions;
- concentration/generalization bounds with declared hypotheses;
- calibrated measurement intervals including instrument error.

For each candidate/resource coordinate produce lower and upper bounds

\[
[L_{c,j},U_{c,j}].
\]

Point estimates alone cannot support a robust family exclusion when uncertainty could reverse the ordering.

Seed count is not a ritual: randomness sources and the aggregation rule must be fixed, and failed/unreachable seeds remain in the receipt under the preregistered rule.

## 10. E7 — hard feasibility before preference

For each candidate evaluate hard gates before resource preference:

1. protected capability threshold;
2. intervention/robustness threshold;
3. precision/stability requirement;
4. physical/resource hard budgets;
5. developmental reachability;
6. deployment/generalization gate;
7. receipt/provenance consistency.

Candidates failing a hard gate do not become cheap frontier points.

If no candidate survives, the correct terminal is `INFEASIBLE_AT_REGISTERED_SCOPE` rather than selection of the least-bad family.

## 11. E8 — selection rule

Two valid default modes are supported.

### 11.1 Pareto mode

Keep the declared resource vector. Compute the robust/evidence-compatible frontier. A family property is derived only if every selected candidate/morphology has that family property under the registered uncertainty rule.

### 11.2 Scalar mode

If a scalar cost `J` is preregistered, each family/candidate has a certified interval `[L,U]`. Family A is a robust unique winner only if its feasible upper bound lies strictly below every competing feasible lower bound under the registered selection unit:

\[
U_A < \min_{B\ne A}L_B.
\]

Overlap means `UNDECIDED_FROM_CURRENT_EVIDENCE` unless another preregistered hard criterion resolves it.

Do not introduce a tie-breaker after seeing a tie.

## 12. E9 — canonical terminals

The experiment may emit only a scope-indexed canonical terminal:

- `INFEASIBLE_AT_REGISTERED_SCOPE`;
- `DERIVED_NEURAL_AT_REGISTERED_SCOPE`;
- `DERIVED_NON_NEURAL_AT_REGISTERED_SCOPE`;
- `DERIVED_HYBRID_AT_REGISTERED_SCOPE`;
- `FAMILY_COEXISTENCE_AT_REGISTERED_SCOPE`;
- `UNDECIDED_FROM_CURRENT_EVIDENCE`;
- `INVALID_RECEIPT_OR_PROTOCOL_VIOLATION`.

Every terminal must include the candidate-universe version and selection rule.

## 13. E10 — prospective prediction record

Before protected outcomes, freeze at least one falsifiable prediction that can hurt the favored theory reading.

Examples:

- a predicted family phase at a task scale;
- a resource crossover interval;
- a reachability reversal with development budget;
- a locality/symmetry-induced architectural property;
- a hybrid-vs-pure crossover after bridge cost;
- an abstention/undecided region where intervals should overlap.

The record must also state what observation would falsify the prediction and how the claim will be demoted.

## 14. E11 — candidate-expansion attack

Any unique family terminal must undergo at least one post-freeze hostile expansion round before being promoted beyond its original finite candidate universe.

The attacker should add a materially different candidate that targets the winner's claimed advantage, for example:

- a compiled exact non-neural implementation against a neural winner;
- a quantized/sparse/shared neural realization against a program winner;
- a hybrid decomposition against both pure families;
- a different physical substrate;
- a stronger development/search procedure for an apparently unreachable family.

If the winner changes, the original result remains valid at its original candidate scope and the broader uniqueness claim is withdrawn.

## 15. E12 — family-neutral controls

Minimum controls:

- best constant/null policy where meaningful;
- exact/simple baseline where meaningful;
- same protected obligation for all candidates;
- identical deployment examples/interventions;
- no answer-derived deployment features;
- development cost accounted under one rule;
- preprocessing/data movement accounted under one rule;
- tool/external-memory calls charged;
- failed candidates retained;
- receipt hashes/versions frozen;
- no hidden family-specific tolerance relaxation.

## 16. E13 — evidence packet

A completed packet should contain:

- preregistration manifest;
- candidate registry;
- raw per-run/per-seed measurements;
- calibration and hardware/software metadata;
- reachability outcomes;
- deployment/generalization outcomes;
- uncertainty intervals;
- family-conditioned frontier summary;
- canonical verdict and its proof trace;
- failed predictions and protocol deviations;
- candidate-expansion result or explicit `NOT_YET_RUN`.

A summary without raw/atomic evidence is not sufficient for a strong family derivation.

## 17. Synthetic protocol self-test

The accompanying checker does not pretend to run hardware. It validates protocol semantics on frozen synthetic evidence packets:

1. a neural candidate has `[8,9]`, non-neural `[11,13]`: neural is robustly selected;
2. neural `[8,11]`, non-neural `[10,12]`: intervals overlap, so verdict is undecided;
3. an initial neural winner is attacked by adding hybrid `[6,7]`: the broader candidate universe selects hybrid, proving why candidate scope must be carried in the terminal;
4. a cheap candidate that fails capability is removed before selection;
5. a packet missing deployment evidence cannot emit a derived family terminal.

These are logic tests only.

## 18. What this round closes

The formal closure audit said the remaining NN/non-NN gaps are external premises. This protocol makes those premises operationally acquirable:

\[
\boxed{
\text{formal GMI derivation}
+\text{preregistered real measurements}
+\text{reachability}
+\text{deployment evidence}
+\text{candidate attack}
\to\text{auditable family verdict}.
}
\]

It still does not create the measurements. The next scientific step is to instantiate this protocol on a concrete task/substrate pair and execute it prospectively.

## 19. Stop / resume rule

This protocol is complete when every required evidence field is either populated or explicitly unresolved and the canonical adjudicator refuses to overclaim.

Further theory recursion is justified only by:

- a protocol field that cannot be operationalized;
- a reproducible confound not representable by the current resource/reachability/deployment objects;
- a new candidate family requiring a genuinely new protected operational primitive;
- a prospective experiment falsifying a theorem-level prediction rather than merely supplying a missing empirical value.
