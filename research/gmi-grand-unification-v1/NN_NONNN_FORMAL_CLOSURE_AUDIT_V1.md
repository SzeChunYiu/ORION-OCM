## 1. Audit question

The target question is:

> Does Grand GMI now contain every logically necessary internal bridge needed to explain how an intelligence obligation can yield neural, non-neural, hybrid, coexistence, infeasible or undecided morphology verdicts, without silently manufacturing goals, measurements or candidate completeness?

This is a closure audit of the derivation grammar, not a claim that every real-world architecture has already been predicted.

## 2. Closure rule

Represent the explanation as a directed acyclic derivation graph. Every load-bearing edge must have exactly one of four statuses:

1. **INTERNAL_THEOREM** — proved/constructed inside Grand GMI;
2. **PARENT_THEOREM** — delegated to an external mathematical/physical theorem with explicit hypotheses;
3. **EXTERNAL_PREMISE** — a fact that must be declared, measured or observed from the world;
4. **IMPOSSIBILITY_BOUNDARY** — a stronger global claim is unavailable in principle or outside the declared scope.

An internal formal gap exists iff a load-bearing edge is required for the requested scope but has status `UNCLASSIFIED` or an internal theorem is being invoked outside its hypotheses.

Formal closure therefore does **not** mean every external premise is known. It means the theory says exactly what follows once those premises are supplied and exactly when it must return `UNDECIDED`.

## 5. Impossibility / scope boundaries

### B01 — unrestricted optimizer

No total effective optimizer over unrestricted Turing-complete/infinite morphology spaces is claimed. Finite registered certificates are decidable only at their declared scope.

### B02 — observational-equivalence normal form

For Turing-complete process languages, complete observational-equivalence normalization is undecidable in general. Domain-wide transfer therefore needs a declared decidable fragment or weaker quotient.

### B03 — noncompact attainment

An infimum need not be attained. The theory uses infima by default and requires a separate compactness/attainment theorem before saying `minimum morphology`.

### B04 — exact quotient regularity

On infinite history spaces, response equivalence remains conceptually defined but convenient measurable/standard-Borel quotient structure is not automatic.

### B05 — global family uniqueness

A scope-relative `DERIVED_NEURAL` or `DERIVED_NON_NEURAL` verdict can reverse when a new adequate candidate is admitted. Global uniqueness needs global candidate coverage, not rhetoric.
