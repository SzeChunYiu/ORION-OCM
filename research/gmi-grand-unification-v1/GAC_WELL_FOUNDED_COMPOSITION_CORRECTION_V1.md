## Countermodel to acyclicity alone

At the original [GAC5 source](../gmi-main566567-audit-v1/raw/main/GLOBAL_ADAPTIVE_COMPOSITION_THEOREM_V1.md),
countably many modules were allowed but an acyclic wiring alone was used to
license topological induction. Let module n copy Boolean input x_(n+1) to
output x_n, with contract input1 implies output1. Wire n+1 to n for all
nonnegative integers n. There is no directed cycle and each assumption follows
from its predecessor's guarantee, but there is no source/base component.
The execution x_n=0 for all n satisfies every local implication and wiring
equation while failing the proposed global obligation x_0=1.
This is a missing induction premise, not a faulty local identity operation.

## Positive repair

The [active GAC5 theorem](../gmi-recursive-theory-closure-v1/GLOBAL_ADAPTIVE_COMPOSITION_THEOREM_V1.md)
now requires a finite DAG or a well-founded predecessor relation, with
externally established base assumptions. Give each component a rank in a
well-order, strictly greater than every predecessor it relies on.
Well-founded induction establishes all guarantees: discharged predecessor
guarantees and external premises supply the next assumption, then its valid
local contract supplies its guarantee. The stated conjunction entails O.

For recurrent or unfounded wiring, an independently initialized and preserved
invariant supplies the alternative induction over execution time. It cannot
be inferred merely from the absence of a finite directed cycle.
The statistical GAC1–4 laws and finite founded cases are unchanged.
This is established assume-guarantee induction discipline, not a novel
composition mechanism. It matches the formal supplement's explicit
global-history and premise-discharge obligations.
