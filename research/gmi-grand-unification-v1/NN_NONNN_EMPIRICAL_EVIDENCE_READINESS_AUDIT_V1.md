## 1. Audit question

The formal derivation chain and the empirical instantiation protocol now exist. The next question is narrower and empirical:

> Does the current ORION evidence base already contain a complete, task-bound, family-comparative packet sufficient to emit a real `DERIVED_NEURAL`, `DERIVED_NON_NEURAL` or `DERIVED_HYBRID` verdict?

Answer: **no**.

The valid current family terminal is therefore

`UNDECIDED_FROM_CURRENT_EVIDENCE`.

## 2. Audit rule

Each empirical-protocol section is classified as one of:

- `READY_FORMAL`: the theorem/protocol needed to consume evidence exists;
- `PARTIAL_REAL_EVIDENCE`: relevant real evidence exists but does not discharge the NN/non-NN comparative field;
- `SYNTHETIC_ONLY`: only exact/synthetic theorem witnesses currently discharge the field;
- `MISSING_TASK_BOUND_PACKET`: no frozen real comparative packet currently binds the required evidence;
- `BLOCKED_OR_DEFECTIVE`: existing evidence has a known defect that prevents promotion.

A real derived-family terminal requires every load-bearing empirical field to be discharged for one immutable task/candidate/substrate packet. Evidence from different scopes cannot be stitched together after the fact as if it were one experiment.

## 17. Minimal next experiment

The smallest useful prospective experiment should deliberately avoid claiming universal intelligence architecture. It should target one finite, validated ecology and one physical substrate/accounting boundary.

Minimum packet:

1. one frozen protected task with validated instruments;
2. one neural candidate and one non-neural candidate, both exact/adequate enough to survive the capability gate;
3. identical development budget policy;
4. real task-bound latency/memory/energy-or-compute intervals with calibration semantics;
5. protected deployment/intervention set;
6. preregistered scalar or Pareto selection rule;
7. prospective prediction including an allowed `UNDECIDED` region;
8. hostile candidate expansion after the first verdict.

A successful packet would earn a **point family verdict**, not a universal law. Replication across tasks/substrates would then test whether a phase law generalizes.
