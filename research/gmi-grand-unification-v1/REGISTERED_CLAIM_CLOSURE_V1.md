## What the present mathematics establishes

The master declaration gives one obligation-relative language for response,
resources and development. Its frontier definition alone predicts no machine
family. A family verdict requires a complete admitted competitor class, sound
lower bounds, a jointly feasible construction, attainment where claimed, and
the relevant physical profile fibers. GG34 and MSC state those obligations.

The accompanying results supply constructive closures for particular admitted
interfaces. They include acquisition with changing or uncertain worlds;
joint query, message and terminal-action costs; bounded controller realization;
certified proof reuse; feasible symmetry reduction; and complete ideal one-way
quantum dimension coverage. Their proofs extend beyond the finite regression
instances only under the stated hypotheses. Parent results are credited in
each module; these applications do not claim new general dynamic programming,
confidence-sequence or real-algebraic decision methods.

## Next decisive scientific claim

The central empirical question is whether experience produces an executable
method that is causally used on fresh obligations. The retained B6 witness
and consumer census provide matched parents, not that demonstration. Structural
presence, training success and a historical carrier label do not discharge it.

A prospective comparison must fix fresh held-out obligations before evaluation,
compare the acquired method intact, removed and restored, retain a matched
experience control, and charge acquisition, search, verification, representation
and repeated deployment. Define the putative method and legal intervention
first: an evolved genotype is executable structure even without a PROGRAM
object. For the proposed causal-use claim, removal must affect the declared
held-out behavior and restoration recover it. A null removal effect does not
exclude redundant implementations. An effect alone neither establishes the
acquisition origin nor distinguishes an imported method from newly acquired
structure; ancestry and matched experience controls must do that work. Even a
positive result is limited to its intervention and transfer contract.

Another finite specialization of an established algorithm should be added only
when it resolves a named missing premise or counterexample needed by such a
claim. A new model's solvability is not evidence for the assumptions of the old
one. Reopen every affected theorem and dependent conclusion when a substantive
counterexample, missing competitor, omitted resource or invalid record appears.
