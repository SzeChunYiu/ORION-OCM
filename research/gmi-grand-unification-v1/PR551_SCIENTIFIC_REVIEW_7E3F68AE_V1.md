## Deletion resistance does not establish semantic necessity

The [new atrophy interpretation](https://github.com/SzeChunYiu/ORION-OCM/blob/7e3f68ae418ad88e8832bebbce6955d8274b66d5/research/machine-intelligence-morphogenesis-v1/GMI_B6_DEVELOPMENTAL_MORPHOGENESIS_RV_377_180_FREEZE.md#L522-L549)
infers that any surviving node must lower capability below the floor if deleted,
and treats a retained DENSE node as functionally load-bearing.
The actual [pinned deletion routine](https://github.com/SzeChunYiu/ORION-OCM/blob/5378c2b7f91f8fbc567764a2ee0c5ff6e256e814/research/machine-intelligence-morphogenesis-v1/gmi_microscope/atrophy_ir.py#L308-L362)
performs a canonical greedy sequence of typed single-node deletions for at most
24 rounds. It skips type-check failures; replay exceptions produce no accepted
capability; successful deletions must retain every registered capability floor.
It does not search general typed replacements, rewiring or semantic equivalents.
Survival alone therefore does not prove that every deletion falls below the floor,
that a general fixed point was reached, or that the retained coefficient is necessary.
At a verified terminal deletion fixed point, the warranted statement concerns the
allowed, successfully evaluated local deletion moves and their registered floor.

There is already a constructive countercontrol for coefficient necessity in this
specific recovered witness. The [consumer proof](https://github.com/SzeChunYiu/ORION-OCM/blob/f91c78edab73b1a3769764a863d13e313e9c46e9/research/gmi-witness-mechanism-v1/CONSUMER_SEMANTICS_V1.md#L16-L49)
replaces the DENSE output with a constructed zero vector. The DENSE numeric cell
has no active numeric consumer; the observed INSERT key and EVIDENCE-store behavior
are preserved. Under the stated B0 interface and finite event sequences, the
replacement preserves query values, abstention and ordered common stores.
[The finite control evidence and limits](https://github.com/SzeChunYiu/ORION-OCM/blob/f91c78edab73b1a3769764a863d13e313e9c46e9/research/gmi-witness-mechanism-v1/CONSUMER_SEMANTICS_V1.md#L51-L79)
explicitly exclude equality of resource charges, dense state, vector/tape representation
and graph encoding. The replacement changes the presence-based descriptor to KVSTORE;
it does not prove equal full physical profiles or the irrelevance of dense parameters
to every task. Report syntactic hybrid presence and deletion resistance separately
from a demonstrated hybrid learning mechanism.
