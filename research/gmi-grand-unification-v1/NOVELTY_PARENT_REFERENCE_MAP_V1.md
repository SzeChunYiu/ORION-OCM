# Grand GMI parent-reference map V1

Date: 2026-09-13. This is a parent-subtraction bibliography, not an exhaustive literature review.

## Physical/process and semantic parents

- David Deutsch & Chiara Marletto, **Constructor theory of information**, *Proceedings of the Royal Society A* 471 (2015), DOI `10.1098/rspa.2014.0540`. Parent for possible/impossible physical transformations, interoperability and substrate-independent information.
- Artemy Kolchinsky & David H. Wolpert, **Semantic information, autonomous agency and non-equilibrium statistical physics**, *Interface Focus* 8 (2018), DOI `10.1098/rsfs.2018.0041`. Parent for causal-intervention definitions of semantic information relative to viability/self-maintenance.
- Michael L. Littman, Richard S. Sutton & Satinder Singh, **Predictive Representations of State**, NeurIPS 2001. Parent for controlled state represented by action-conditional predictions.
- James P. Crutchfield / Cosma R. Shalizi computational-mechanics work on causal states, including causal-state minimal sufficient predictive representations. Parent for history equivalence by future distributions and minimal predictive state.

## Communication / information parents

- H. S. Witsenhausen and later zero-error source-coding/function-computation literature; Orlitsky & Roche coding-for-computing line. Parent for confusability/coloring and functional compression with decoder side information.
- David Blackwell, comparison of experiments / garbling theorems (1951/1953) and modern extensions. Parent for decision-theoretic information ordering under garbling.
- Shannon rate-distortion, Wyner-Ziv side-information coding, and later task/goal-oriented semantic-communication theory. Parent for noisy/lossy resource tradeoffs.
- Recent semantic-communication surveys explicitly cover semantic entropy, semantic rate-distortion, semantic channel capacity and task-dependent distortion while noting the lack of one settled universal semantic-information framework.

## Symmetry / decision parents

- Hunt–Stein invariant decision theory and later equivariant/minimax extensions. Parent for symmetry reduction and equivariant rules under group invariance and convexity/regularity hypotheses.
- Vector/Pareto optimization and robust optimization are parents for nondominance, attainment, epsilon-optimal points, lower-bound relaxations and interval certificates.

## Learning / architecture / distributed parents

- Meta-learning / learning-to-learn surveys (e.g. Hospedales et al., *IEEE TPAMI* 2022, DOI `10.1109/TPAMI.2021.3079209`) explicitly include optimization over learning rules and architecture search. Parent for broad recursive learner-search ideas.
- Finite-automata / recurrent-neural simulation literature is parent for compiling finite-state machines/transducers into neural realizations.
- Decentralized control, team decision theory, networked control and communication-complexity literatures are parents for internal information constraints in distributed systems.
- Game theory is parent for Nash, regret and strategic-equilibrium sectors.

## Task-directed acquisition parent

- Daniel Golovin, Andreas Krause & Debajyoti Ray, **Near-Optimal Bayesian Active Learning with Noisy Observations**, NeurIPS 2010 / arXiv:1010.3091. Introduces Equivalence Class Determination: experiments stop once the remaining version space lies in one decision/equivalence class. This is a direct parent of task-directed stopping before full state identification.
- Decision Region Determination and task-dependent localization extend the same idea to decision regions. GMI must therefore claim only the residual involving set-valued compatible actions plus persistent-retention/controller/resource coupling unless a stricter theorem is proved.

## Close contemporary parallel

- Mark Whitlock / TUS OS, **The Resource Geometry of Answerable Inference** v1.0 (2026-08-26), Zenodo DOI `10.5281/zenodo.22115282`.
- Mark Whitlock / TUS OS, **The Answerability Calculus** v1.0 (2026-08-28), Zenodo DOI `10.5281/zenodo.22148044`.

Public descriptions advertise irreducible answerability structure, minimal sufficient cores, certified inference-resource boundaries, contract-resource representation, lawful reduction, composition and quantum-operational treatment. Because these releases precede the present 2026-09 GMI synthesis, broad claims that obligation-relative resource structure itself is a first GMI invention are prohibited pending theorem-by-theorem subtraction.

## Interpretation rule

A parent reference does not automatically invalidate GMI. It changes what has to be new. The valid residual may be a strict theorem extension, a new composition of parent objects, a prospective prediction not made by the parent, or an empirical falsification. If no such residual is exhibited, the GMI result is an application/specialization and must be described that way.
