This audit answers a different question from theorem correctness. A theorem can be correct and still be old. A programme can also be scientifically new while using old mathematics if the new object is a nontrivial composition, a new prediction, or a new empirical falsification.

## 1. Novelty classes

Every GMI result must be placed in one of these buckets before using words such as *new*, *novel* or *first*.

1. `PARENT_OWNED_SPECIALIZATION` — the mathematical result is already contained in a known parent theory; GMI contributes typing/application only.
2. `NEW_SYNTHESIS_CANDIDATE` — ingredients are known but their joint architecture-free composition appears to be the residual contribution. This is a synthesis claim, not theorem-priority.
3. `TECHNICAL_NOVELTY_CANDIDATE` — the exact theorem statement/residual was not located in the parent search. Priority remains unproved until an independent primary-literature search passes the protocol in `NOVELTY_PRIORITY_PROTOCOL_V1.md`.
4. `PROGRAMME_EMPIRICAL_NOVELTY` — a frozen GMI prediction, negative result or measured comparison contributes new evidence even when its mathematics has parents.
5. `PROSPECTIVE_INTERNAL_NOT_HUMANITY_WIDE` — GMI predicted an unoccupied cell in its own frozen registry; this is not evidence that the mechanism was unknown to the literature.

## 3. What appears genuinely additive today

### 3.1 Strongest theory residual: the dual morphology-generating spectra

The most defensible candidate for a genuinely new GMI object is not any individual channel theorem. It is the pair

`Xi = (S*, kappa, tau)`

with `kappa` and `tau` defined from **attainable resource sets / Pareto spectra**, not coordinatewise fictitious minima, and used jointly to constrain physical morphology.

The parent literatures separately study sufficient/predictive states, communication/information requirements and computation complexity. The proposed synthesis uses both spectra to constrain a physical/developmental realization-fiber frontier. The all-partition theorem establishes a concrete width/query separation using classical constructions. Neither it nor this parent search proves that arbitrary richer spectra are irreducible to each other. This is a **candidate synthesis novelty**, not a priority or unrestricted independence declaration.

### 3.2 Architecture derivation is changed from name recovery to fiber statements

The protected K4 failure — named property vectors were not recovered and were search/seed dependent — forced the programme away from syntax recovery. GMI now asks what all realizations in an attained operational profile fiber must share. Requiring nonempty selection and **complete equal-profile fibers** before declaring a property derived is a meaningful synthesis: it blocks representative-only architecture claims.

The ingredients (Pareto sets, equivalence classes, attainment) are classical. The residual is the rule connecting operational identifiability to architecture claims.

### 3.3 Necessity-to-morphology bridge

PL-1…PL-5 now derive family-conditioned lower bounds from proved semantic/transformation necessities under an explicit accounting-soundness contract. This is scientifically stronger than taking family bounds as inputs because the bound applies to every admitted member of the structural class, including unconstructed machines. The generic relaxation proof is parent mathematics; the GMI residual is the typed bridge from task necessities to morphology-family exclusion and the proved asymmetry:

- adding a valid necessity cannot lower a lower bound;
- enlarging the structural class cannot raise it;
- necessities do not generally identify a selected family; independently supplied existence and exhaustive rival exclusion can suffice on particular inputs.

### 3.4 Programme-specific prospective and negative evidence

These are evidence contributions even when the mathematics has parents:

- K4 exact named-architecture/property prediction failed at protected scope (`0/264`) and exposed search/seed dependence.
- CL-1…CL-7 organize multiple machine families by operational channel capability rather than syntax.
- RV-377-135…138 were frozen composition laws before execution and survived exact checking.
- RV-377-200 prospectively registered an occupied channel region and an empty twin; neutral search realized the occupied region on the registered experiment while the channel ablation removed its capability.
- real-transfer phase laws were reported correct on `63/64` off-band replicates versus `53/64` for 3-fold CV while using zero additional fits versus 12–15.
- parity-family experiments give registered-scope non-neural / coexistence outcomes and have been expanded specifically to attack weak-candidate explanations.

None of these licenses “first unknown machine species in human history.” They are prospective **programme evidence** that the framework can make falsifiable predictions before search/outcome access.

## 4. What we should stop calling novel

Without a new priority audit, do **not** advertise any of the following as GMI inventions:

- predictive or causal-state equivalence classes;
- causal viability semantics;
- zero-error graph/hypergraph coding;
- rate-distortion or side-information laws;
- Blackwell garbling monotonicity;
- invariant/equivariant decision rules from symmetry;
- neural simulation of finite state machines;
- meta-learning, architecture search or evolution as search over learners;
- decentralized communication constraints;
- Nash/regret/game-theoretic machinery;
- Pareto/vector optimization, lower-bound relaxations or epsilon-optimal witnesses;
- proof caching, knapsack-style reuse or ordinary decision-tree search.

GMI may reuse these as parent theorems. Its burden is to show that the **composition produces a theorem or prediction the parent alone did not already provide**.

## 5. Close contemporary parallel: Answerability

The 2026 Answerability series is sufficiently close that Grand GMI must explicitly subtract against it before making broad-framework novelty claims. Public material for *The Resource Geometry of Answerable Inference* (2026-08-26) and *The Answerability Calculus* (2026-08-28) advertises irreducible answerability structure, minimal sufficient cores, resource boundaries, lawful reduction, contract-resource representation and composition. Those themes overlap the obligation/resource half of Grand GMI.

Therefore this audit forbids the claim “GMI is the first obligation-relative resource theory of inference/intelligence.” The defensible comparison target is narrower: whether Grand GMI's semantic-cut / transformation-spectrum pair, morphology fibers, recursive quotient-compatible development and prospective architecture-free machine-form experiments produce residual results absent from Answerability and the older parents.

## 6. Promotion rule

A `TECHNICAL_NOVELTY_CANDIDATE` may be promoted to `PRIORITY_SUPPORTED` only after:

1. the exact theorem statement and all load-bearing hypotheses are frozen;
2. primary-literature searches are run under at least three alternative vocabularies;
3. the strongest parent result is written beside the candidate and a counterexample shows why it does not imply the residual, or a formal mapping shows the residual strictly strengthens it;
4. the Answerability parallel is checked where applicable;
5. an independent reviewer repeats the search and parent mapping;
6. the dated search record is committed before public “first/new theorem” language.

Empirical novelty requires, in addition, a frozen pre-outcome prediction and a result not selected after observing the target outcome.

## 7. Current disposition

The answer to “are we adding anything new?” is **yes, but not where an architecture-centric reading would first look**.

The programme should currently claim:

- **new synthesis candidate:** one causal-process theory connecting obligation-relative semantics, information cuts, local computation, physical/developmental resources and realization fibers;
- **technical novelty candidates:** the joint `(kappa,tau)` morphology signature, quotient-compatible architecture-neutral morphogenesis, the complete-fiber rule for architecture derivation, and the task-necessity→family-bound bridge;
- **programme empirical novelty:** protected failures, frozen channel-composition predictions, prospective region realization, phase-law transfer and cross-family resource experiments;
- **no broad priority claim:** the foundational ingredients individually have substantial parents, and the contemporary Answerability programme overlaps the broad obligation/resource architecture.

That is a stronger scientific position than calling every lemma novel: it identifies a small residual that can actually be attacked.
