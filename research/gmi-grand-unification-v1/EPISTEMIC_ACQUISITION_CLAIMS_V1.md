## Parent subtraction

Adaptive query learning, optimal decision trees, causal experimental design and Bayesian/information-gain acquisition are established parents. This tranche does **not** claim their algorithms as GMI inventions.

The GMI residual is the composition of those experiment mechanics with the architecture-free semantic response quotient: the theory derives the target for *complete protected-response identification* before optimizing the experiment process. A weaker action task is governed by the decision-region parent and TDA-1--3, not this quotient cost.
