## What TCR contributes to this register

TCR does not claim a new stopping principle, dynamic-programming mechanism or
native-versus-macro cost distinction. Alongside its published decision-region
and policy-value-vector parents, FC-T5 already defeats the inference that
any adequate action makes further information valueless.

The concrete specialization is the input-labelled terminal-vector table on
every Boolean subcube. Keeping the complete table reconstructs all no-repeat
query-plus-terminal profiles. Keeping its pointwise antichains reconstructs
the exact frontier of all finite deterministic trees under nonnegative
rational charges. The executable witnesses retain joint resource outcomes,
all-input adequacy and actual action dictionaries.

The interface supplies terminal tables and excludes controller, compilation,
acquisition and physical realization costs from the reconstructed coordinate.
The donors sharpen the remaining Q1/Q2/Q3 work; neither their finite examples
nor this integration establish new learning, E2/E3 evidence, production
adoption, unrestricted stopping or full GMI completion.
