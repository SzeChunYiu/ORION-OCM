## Constructive correction: finite description ranks

Fix an optimal universal prefix machine W and distinct finite strings a,b.
Define Ua(0)=a, Ua(1p)=W(p); define Vb(0)=b, Vb(1p)=W(p).
Both domains are prefix-free and both simulate W with one extra bit.
A universal prefix machine cannot halt on the empty program: that would exclude
every other program. Thus K_Ua(a)=1<K_Ua(b)=1+K_W(b), whereas
K_Vb(b)=1<K_Vb(a)=1+K_W(a). Exact finite ranking reverses.

The positive parent theorem is invariance **up to machine-dependent additive
constants**, not exact equality or ranking invariance. The machines contain the
fixed literals in their descriptions; changing physical interpreters is not free.
Freeze and charge the reference language before a finite resource comparison.
This is an application of [Kolmogorov's original invariance construction][K].

## Constructive correction: the matched diagonal control

The S source fixes exact f(x)=x^T A x/2, A=diag(1,kappa,...,kappa),
x0=(1,...,1), d20, tolerance1/1000, kappa100 or2. Its vanilla accounting charges
one fused coordinate gradient/update touch per iteration, hence d times the
iteration count, with setup0. Dimension and kappa are public model parameters.

Use the fixed rule x_j <- x_j - g_j/A_jj. In exact arithmetic g=Ax, so all
coordinates become0 in one full update. Under that same fused-touch convention
there are exactly d touches per task, setup0, total dH; the public diagonal
pattern requires no acquired Hessian or dense factorization.

## Constructive correction: savings are conditional

For finite H, valid-at-use event V_t, integrable saving S_t and total extra
lifetime cost C, E[gain]=sum_{t=1}^H E[1_{V_t} S_t]-E[C]. Each summand is
Pr(V_t)E[S_t|V_t] when Pr(V_t)>0 and is0 otherwise.
The original eta*Delta-A-M follows when these conditional means all equal
Delta and C=A+M. Even H1 with saving0 or2 equally likely and A1/2 has positive
expected gain but loses on half its paths.

Survival dependence also matters: H2, first saving1, survival probability1/2,
and second saving0 on survival/2 on invalidation gives both unconditional
saving means1 but actual expected useful saving1, not eta*Delta=3/2.
Use conditional savings, or fixed deterministic per-use costs as in CRI.
This is linearity of expectation and geometric survival; no new renewal law.

## Exact geometry arithmetic and historical custody

Under ADDITIVE, completed-pair/half-pair counts form a sufficient Markov state.
With single-move mass a, a pair's survival function after Poisson time u=t/m is
s_a(u)=2a/(2-a) exp(-au/2)+(2-3a)/(2-a) exp(-u).
Independent pair clocks imply E[T]=m integral_0^infinity
[1-(1-s_a(u))^m]du. Binomial expansion integrates each exponential exactly.
Here T counts proposals, including rejected moves. If N is the first-hit
proposal index and tau the rate1 Poisson clock time, interarrival times
are independent of proposal marks: E[tau|N]=N, hence E[N]=E[tau].
This independent formula agreed rationally with first-step DP for both masses

Under CHUNK, strict acceptance keeps pairs00 or11; coupon collection gives
E[T]=m H_m/p_pair and exact ratio9 ([classical coupon-collection parent][ER]).
