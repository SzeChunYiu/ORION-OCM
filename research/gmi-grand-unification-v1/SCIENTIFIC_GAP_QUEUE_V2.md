## Reopening and completeness

Reopen any result when a counterexample satisfies its actual hypotheses, a
selector/witness/competitor-coverage premise is missing, a resource dimension
was omitted, or evidence fails its registered instrument. The audit must check
the revised theorem and its dependents, including a no-alarm control.

Exact unrestricted solving remains impossible by the stated undecidability
reduction. That boundary does not discharge Q1–Q9, establish all sharp laws,
or prove that all possible theory gaps have been enumerated. The overall
scientific-completion goal therefore remains open.
