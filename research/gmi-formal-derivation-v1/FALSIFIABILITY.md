# Falsifiers and the remaining empirical contract

Definitions are conventions; they are not falsifiable predictions about nature.
A mathematical theorem is defeated by a counterexample satisfying its stated
premises or an invalid proof step. An empirical GMI claim is defeated when a
prospectively fixed model prediction fails in its registered measurement domain.
Changing the domain after seeing the result creates a new claim, not a rescue
of the original one. This distinction is essential to interpreting this unit.

## Formal falsifiers, each with an explicit repair boundary

| Claim | Decisive falsifier inside its scope | Repair if a premise fails |
|---|---|---|
| Global adaptive confidence | A law satisfying the global conditional-mean and birth-registration contract whose crossing probability exceeds the stated error | Model dependencies, version changing means, prevent replay or use drift-aware targets. |
| CMP2 composition | Compatible conditional local contracts and summable allocations, but excessive first-failure probability | Expose a hidden channel, missing precondition or uncharged new failure event. |
| Recursive representation | Two quotient-equivalent histories with different quotient successors under an admitted continuation | Close the continuation family or retain the missing state. |
| Constructive neural realization | One declared discrete input/state pair on which the explicit network differs from the transition table | Correct construction/encoding; do not extrapolate an approximate real-valued network to exact logic. |
| Learning consistency | All sampling, boundedness, support and growing-class premises hold, but risk fails its bound/convergence claim | Repair a proof or estimator; poor performance outside these premises is a transfer failure. |
| Certified learner acceptance | Under simultaneous valid intervals and accurate full costs, an accepted switch violates its stated population net-benefit inequality | Restore omitted search, evaluation, invalidation or execution charges. |
| Causal identification | A structural model satisfying consistency, exchangeability and positivity violates CAU3 | Reject the theorem or proof; merely observationally indistinguishable models test identifiability premises instead. |
| Resource prediction | A full accounted execution violates its predicted registered resource inequality | Revise the accounted mechanism and re-register a matched comparison. |

The executable witnesses in this unit exercise small exact models, negative
controls and repairs. Passing them is not a mechanically checked proof of all
real numbers, histories, programs or infinite processes. The analytic proofs
carry those quantifiers. There is no Lean/Isabelle/Coq verification claim.

## Predictions obtainable now

The following are conditional predictions inherited from explicit mechanisms,
not currently established world-first GMI discoveries:

1. For a prespecified register of K candidates with equal allocation
   alpha=delta/K, ADAPTIVE's prescribed confidence radius contains log K.
   Its sufficient sample requirement for a fixed width therefore increases
   with K. This protocol-specific certification cost is not a universal
   information lower bound: unequal fixed prior allocations can leave an
   existing candidate's radius unchanged when further candidates are added.
2. Reuse can justify costly acquisition only after enough future benefit to
   repay all accumulated acquisition/evaluation debt; LEARNER gives the exact
   acceptance inequality. Increasing unpaid debt shifts that threshold.
3. A representation can become preferable when the fixed declared workload or
   substrate changes even though its semantic behavior is unchanged;
   AGENCY-RESOURCES gives the two-candidate crossing law.
4. Small one-step model error can coexist with a completely wrong
   eventual-hazard prediction; CMP5 specifies the
   failure mechanism and discounted/summable remedies.

One new-to-this-supplement design is the charged certified candidate learner
in [LEARNER](LEARNER.md), with dynamic proposal, explicit evidence and debt-aware
deployment. Its individual mechanisms have strong parents. A synthesis is an
algorithmic construction; global novelty and a stronger useful rate than the
best combined parent remain unestablished.

## Decisive empirical comparison to register before execution

Use at least three different finite-to-growing task mechanisms: finite
transducer inference, an executable symbolic/tree learner, and a controlled
hidden-state decision task. Neural networks may be external theoretical or
experimental comparators; this unit does not put neural models inside OCM.
Choose disjoint acquisition, tuning and final evaluation seeds/tasks, and record
their generation and release before final evaluation.

Compare the proposed learner with (i) a fixed strongest family learner,
(ii) ordinary validation/SRM using exactly the same candidates and observations,
(iii) a cost-aware safe selector using the same confidence method, and
(iv) an oracle over the same admitted set with its oracle status explicit.
The strongest combined parent may duplicate the proposed mechanism; then there
is no algorithmic novelty at that scope. Do not compare with a deliberately
degraded parent. Equal-sample shuffled selection is a control for selection bias.

Record per task and over the whole lifetime:

- samples, risk/verified correctness and anytime coverage;
- proposal, compilation, rejected-candidate evaluation and switching work;
- persistent and peak transient memory, including certificates/dependencies;
- final execution time/work and all reset, invalidation and external-service costs;
- transferred executable operations that causally affect new task computation.

Falsifying interventions include repeated rows, shared latent causes, delayed
feedback, dynamic candidate birth, model drift, unseen outcomes, lost state,
replayed observations and non-amortizable acquisition. Keep premise-violating
stress tests separately interpreted from within-premise theorem tests.
Report effect estimates, uncertainty, failure reasons and complete paid work.

No experiment is reported as executed here merely because this protocol exists.
Theoretical derivation is the current deliverable. Claims of improved sample
efficiency, robustness, reasoning, adaptation, compute efficiency and
generalization require those registered results and independent replication.

## Why "all of GMI is complete" is not a supported verdict

This unit supplies one explicit classical formal route across the requested
topics. It does not close arbitrary physical realization, learned sufficient
state/support, universal optimization, empirical transfer or scientific novelty.
Each broader theorem must supply the missing hypotheses or produce a
countermodel and an attainable alternative. A list of theorem names cannot
establish that every scientific gap has been exhausted.
