# GMI formal derivations: read first

This unit supplies explicit conditional proofs from an economical operational
core through adaptive inference, composition, representations and learning.
It is a classical discrete-time specialization of Grand GMI, with finite
constructive learners and carefully stated countable/infinite extensions.

The central derivation is:

\[
\text{causal/access/obligation/realization/cost contracts}
\Rightarrow\text{protected state and lawful composition}
\Rightarrow\text{constructive realizations}
\Rightarrow\text{conditional learning and planning guarantees}.
\]

Each arrow below states its additional hypotheses and proves its conclusion.
The core does not assume a successful learner, choose a utility, mandate neural
networks, or supply a free universal optimizer.

## How to interpret the result

Analytic proofs carry the general quantifiers; exact finite executable checks
challenge constructions and known failure modes. These are different evidence.
This is not a proof-assistant-checked formalization or an empirical validation.
Use [the scope matrix](SCOPE.md) for every requested milestone and the exact
remaining premise. Parent mechanisms are cited beside their derivations.

The proposed learner is a concrete synthesis, with an executable finite
non-neural hypothesis language. Its global novelty and empirical advantage are
not established. Neural realizability is proved at a declared mathematical
interface; neural necessity and arbitrary physical optimality do not follow.

This directory is owned by the formal-derivation session and is integrated with
the separate repository audit session. Source lineage, finite checks and review
corrections are recorded in [EVIDENCE](EVIDENCE.md). Existing historical evidence
and the master theorem registers retain their separate authority.
