### `research/gmi-833-developmental-potential-evolvability-v1` (#909 / #908)

Owns: `DP-1` (potential vs current capability vs headroom); `EV-1`
(`Ev_Q(U) = Q(U)`, expected first-hit `1/p`, typed `UNREACHABLE_ZERO_USEFUL_MASS`);

**Nearest miss, stated precisely.** `HIST-1` is the **accounting identity**:

charges. `CAPITAL-1` is instantiated in burden terms as this package's mandatory
`H-CAPITAL` case: `L_sol = {m1 → ababababab}` is accepted by REP-1 on its own
target (−36,890) and rejected by REP-2 on the distribution (+18,139).

### External literature

- **DreamCoder** (Ellis et al., *Phil. Trans. R. Soc. A* 381:20220050, 2023,
  DOI `10.1098/rsta.2022.0050`) — wake/sleep library learning: abstraction
  admission driven by corpus compression, with the library amortising later
  search. Parent of the *phenomenon*; supplies no rank-free criterion for when a
  representation change reduces enumerated search cost.
- **Stitch** (Bowers et al., *POPL* 2023, DOI `10.1145/3571234`) — corpus-guided
  top-down library learning with exact compression accounting. Parent of the
  *exact compression accounting*; its objective is description length, not
  enumeration burden, and the alphabet-inflation term does not appear in it.
- **Levin search / universal search** (Levin 1973; Li & Vitányi, *An
  Introduction to Kolmogorov Complexity and Its Applications*, 4th ed., 2019,
  DOI `10.1007/978-3-030-11298-1`) — the enumerate-by-length cost model that
  makes "burden" well defined, and the `2^Δ` allocation intuition imported via
  #233 HST-T04.
- **No Free Lunch for search/optimization** (Wolpert & Macready, *IEEE Trans.
  Evol. Comput.* 1(1):67–82, 1997, DOI `10.1109/4235.585893`) — the classical
  statement that averaged over all objectives no search algorithm dominates.
  `SD-1` is **not** a restatement: it is a finite exact comparison over **three
  named structured ecologies** with a derived mechanism (`SD-2a/b/c/d`)
  attributing the ordering to separability and deception. Explicitly listed as a
  forbidden promotion: no general NFL claim is made from it.
- **Deceptive fitness landscapes** (Goldberg, *Foundations of Genetic
  Algorithms* 1:74–88, 1991) — the classical construction `SD-2c` instantiates
  exactly, with the accident channel derived rather than observed.

## 3. What is NOT claimed novel

- The substrate, enumeration order, burden metric, closed form, charge model,
  held-out fixtures and `κ = 1` (all #897).
- That a library can reduce measured held-out discovery cost (#897 `HLD-1`).
- That widening an alphabet inflates enumerated prefixes (#897 `T4`).
- That acquisition must be charged against later savings (`h1-amortized-rewrite-v2`).
- That solution capital differs from search-policy improvement (`CAPITAL-1`).
- That no single admission rule dominates (grammar-growth v2 `T10`).
- That algorithms do not dominate averaged over objectives (Wolpert–Macready).
- Anything about developmental phase transitions, path dependence, hysteresis,
  escape from developmental traps, or reachability mass for predicted
  morphologies — owned by the concurrent lane on #910 / PR #924 and untouched.
  In particular `NOV-2`'s `Reach_G(B)` census is over **target words under
  grammar growth**, not reachability mass over predicted morphologies.

   `ΔNet = −Saving(T⁺) + Tax(T⁰) + K_total`, reproducing #897's published

4. **`REP-3`** — the EARNED-BY-COUNTEREXAMPLE boundary showing description
   shortening through an actually-used macro is not sufficient for burden

   expressive power at every `t`.
6. **`NOV-2`** — budget-reachability is non-monotone under growth, with the
   exact added/removed census showing growth is a trade, not a gain.
