## Evidence classes and logic

The audit itself is a P2 finite registry/custody check over exactly fourteen registered class IDs. A class may be marked enumerability `PASS` only when a finite complete universe and replayable exact certificate/oracle are pinned. A class may be marked minimal-twin `PASS` only when the twin is matched, effect-removing/reversing, and minimality is proved over an explicit finite perturbation order.

The validator derives counts from the manifest. It rejects omitted classes, duplicate aliases, missing PASS artifacts, PASS rows without a complete finite-universe statement, attempted laundering of a GAP into a PASS without an artifact, minimal-twin PASS without a finite order/proof, and uncontrolled multi-coordinate twins unless the joint intervention is itself declared atomic.
