## Question

After a fragment is *earned* by a SEARCH_AWARE scan plus a single disjoint
confirmation, charged **once**, and served as a **rewrite / operator inside
the same 4-primitive grammar**, does later related-task acquisition get cheaper

```text
C_acquire(T_j | K_t) < C_acquire(T_j | K_0)
```

and does remaining library capital recoup against that saving on a longer but
still miniature later-task horizon?

Charged coordinates (issue H1 list):

- new information (unique 4-primitive programs checked until threshold);
- examples/demonstrations (training solutions used to mine the library);
- compute (4-primitive programs visited in rewrite-cost order until first hit);
- verifier calls (`normal_form` polynomial-identity checks);
- state written (persist bytes).

Environment interactions, IO/tool calls, and human instructional burden have no
channel on this microscope (`CANNOT_CHECK`).

## Mechanism change (capital, not serving)

Serving is v2's: underlying tokens remain exactly `inc` / `dec` / `double` /
`square`. Search generates each 4-primitive word of length ≤ bound **once**.
A learned fragment `f` is a greedy-leftmost rewrite. Visit order is

```text
(operator-cost, rewritten-first, original 4-ary index)
```

v1's 5-ary MACRO product is closed. The remaining failure in v2 was the
**validation tournament**: every candidate was fully searched on the disjoint
validation stratum, and that selection work exceeded later savings.

This capsule attacks that capital three ways, together:

1. **SEARCH_AWARE scan** from `research/g2-acquisition-economics-v1/` (scan, not
   tournament). The G2 grammar-widening term is zeroed because rewrite serving
   does not add a token; the remaining term is closed-form 4-ary BFS volume
   saved by reducing operator-cost depth on already-solved training programs.
   Token operations are recorded and **never added** to enumeration compute.
2. **Charge acquisition once**, then serve many related later identities
   without re-running selection.
3. **Prune the library to the single scan-selected rewrite.**

A **single** confirmation search (primitive index vs the one selected fragment
on disjoint validation) remains so admission is still earned. That is not an
8-candidate tournament. Confirmation compute stays on the ledger. Training
search stays on the ledger. Recoup is not manufactured by dropping acquisition
cost.

## World

Exact identities generated by `inc` / `dec` / `double` / `square`. Task identity
is polynomial normal form. **New salts**, disjoint from G2 capsules and from
H1 v1/v2:

| stratum | min primitive length | n | salt |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| train (earn library) | 4 | 16 | `orion-ocm-h1-lifetime-train-v3` |
| validation (single confirmation) | 5 | 16 | `orion-ocm-h1-lifetime-val-v3` |
| later related | 6 | 48 | `orion-ocm-h1-lifetime-later-v3` |

The later horizon is longer than v2's 16 identities and still miniature
(1407 min-length-6 fingerprints exist; 48 are taken). A contiguous fragment
(length 2–4, support ≥ 2, cap 8) is ranked by SEARCH_AWARE. Frequency's winner
is recorded and is not deployed when the scan disagrees. The scan winner is
admitted only if confirmation aggregate enumeration attempts are strictly
below the primitive index.

## Arms

1. `K_0` — primitive 4-ary grammar (reset).
2. `ORDINARY_PERSISTENT` — admitted fragment stored/reloaded from ordinary atomic JSON; same rewrite serving.
3. `OCM_LIVE` — same fragment as a procedure object; persist/restart before later tasks.
4. `OCM_REVOKED` — training evidence revoked; later search must equal `K_0`.

Ordinary parent receives the identical fragment and exact rewrite search.
Later-task savings against reset, if any, are therefore `PARENT_SUFFICIENT`
versus a persistent program-library rewrite. `PARENT_SUFFICIENT` is not
programme failure.

## Lifetime capital

`C_acquire(T_j | K_t)` is later-task search given an already-earned library.
Library capital charged once:

```text
training search
+ SEARCH_AWARE token scan   (not added to compute)
+ single confirmation search
+ persist bytes
+ training demonstrations
```

then later-task `C_acquire` across 48 related identities. The 8-candidate
tournament is not run.

A first-class negative is recorded, and salts/predecessor counts are **not**
retuned, when:

- later-task compute is not strictly cheaper than reset (`NO_AMORTIZED_ACQUISITION_LATER_NOT_CHEAPER`);
- or remaining library capital exceeds later-task savings (`LIBRARY_ACQUISITION_EXCEEDS_LATER_SAVINGS`).

In the second case the **break-even horizon** (length-6 related identities at
the observed per-task saving) is reported honestly. That is a valid result.

H1 boxes are earned per charged later-task coordinate, not by pooling unlike
units. The vector inequality is false unless every charged later-task
coordinate strictly improves. Examples and state written remain library
capital, not later-task savings.
