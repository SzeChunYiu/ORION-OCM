# Prior-free GMI claim ledger v1 — OCM

## Status vocabulary

- **THEOREM** — proved under explicit assumptions.
- **FINITE EXACT** — exhaustively established on a declared finite object/domain.
- **COVERAGE** — a form maps into the operational vocabulary.
- **DONOR SPECIALIZATION** — imported theorem/mechanism specialized to the OCM/GMI setting.
- **OPEN** — a named proof, construction, recovery, or empirical obligation remains.
- **REFUTED / REPAIRED** — an earlier stronger statement has a counterexample and must not be revived without new assumptions.
