## Meaning of prior-free

`prior-free` here means:

- no probability measure over the admissible ecology is needed to define the core order, residuals, or worst-case control objects;
- no Transformer, RNN, neural, symbolic, Bayesian, program-search, memory, KSO, or other named architecture family is inserted into the premises.

It does **not** mean assumption-free. The formalism remains conditional on a causal interaction domain, a cognitive obligation, an admissible ecology, a system boundary, an operational probe resolution, and—when morphology or development is claimed—a resource model and development process.

## Main result

The theory separates four questions that must not be collapsed:

1. **prediction:** which histories have the same future obligation-relevant response profile?;
2. **control:** which histories can share an action, plan, or autonomous state?;
3. **morphology:** which operational realizations are physically nondominated under declared resources?;
4. **development:** which such realizations can actually be reached under a declared search/update process and budget?

The strongest current closure is framework-level. Predictive residuals, finite deterministic control bounds, prior-free dominance, architecture non-leakage, and the necessity/construction morphology closure criterion are formalized. Broad known-form representation coverage is available. Time-held-out recovery, prospective unknown-form prediction, general randomized-controller closure, nontrivial substrate-specific morphology closure, general developmental closure, and genuinely quantum external interfaces remain open.

## OCM evidence boundary

This theory does not upgrade any OCM protected evaluation terminal. In particular, importing the formalism does not restore historical M11/M12 scientific admission, prove ChatGPT equivalence, establish frontier mathematics for the OCM runtime, or show that the present KSO decomposition is uniquely required.

Its immediate use is stricter: architecture-benefit and reorganization claims must state their machine boundary, operational equivalence, resource coordinates, necessary outer bounds, constructive inner realizations, and evidence level.
