## Question

After a G2 macro is *earned* by a disjoint validation utility gate, does

```text
C_acquire(T_j | K_t) < C_acquire(T_j | K_0)
```

hold for registered related later tasks, when every charged coordinate is
counted?

Charged coordinates (issue H1 list):

- new information (unique expanded programs checked until threshold);
- examples/demonstrations (training solutions used to mine the library);
- compute (token-word enumeration attempts);
- verifier calls (`normal_form` polynomial-identity checks);
- state written (persist bytes).

Environment interactions, IO/tool calls, and human instructional burden have no
channel on this microscope (`CANNOT_CHECK`).

## World

Exact identities generated by `inc` / `dec` / `double` / `square`. Task identity
is polynomial normal form. New salts, disjoint from G2 capsules:

| stratum | min primitive length | n | salt |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| train (earn library) | 4 | 16 | `orion-ocm-h1-amortized-train-v1` |
| validation (utility gate) | 5 | 16 | `orion-ocm-h1-amortized-val-v1` |
| later related | 6 | 16 | `orion-ocm-h1-amortized-later-v1` |

A contiguous fragment (length 2–4, support ≥ 2, cap 8) is admitted only if
validation aggregate enumeration attempts are strictly below the primitive
index. Frequency's winner is recorded and is not deployed when it loses the
gate.

## Arms

1. `K_0` — primitive grammar (reset).
2. `ORDINARY_PERSISTENT` — admitted macro stored/reloaded from ordinary atomic JSON.
3. `OCM_LIVE` — same macro as a procedure object; persist/restart before later tasks.
4. `OCM_REVOKED` — training evidence revoked; later search must equal `K_0`.

Ordinary parent receives the identical macro and exact search. Later-task
savings against reset, if any, are therefore `PARENT_SUFFICIENT` versus a
persistent program library.

## Lifetime capital

`C_acquire(T_j | K_t)` is later-task search given an already-earned library.
Library capital (training search + every candidate's validation search + persist
bytes + training demonstrations) is reported separately.

A first-class negative is recorded, and salts/counts are **not** retuned, when:

- later-task compute is not strictly cheaper than reset (`NO_AMORTIZED_ACQUISITION_LATER_NOT_CHEAPER`), even if unique-program / verifier calls fall;
- or library capital exceeds later-task savings (`LIBRARY_ACQUISITION_EXCEEDS_LATER_SAVINGS`).

H1 boxes are earned per charged coordinate, not by pooling unlike units. The
vector inequality is false unless every charged later-task coordinate strictly
improves.
