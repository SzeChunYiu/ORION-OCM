# Matched parents and charged scope

## Mature mechanisms

- [Kotzing–Krejca, First-hitting times under drift, TCS 796 (2019),
  Theorem 7](https://hpi.de/friedrich/docs/publications/2019/TCS_a.pdf),
  pp55–56: upper additive drift on an unbounded, lower-bounded process.
  Its general-filtration observation is on p53. APT uses nonnegative stopped
  potentials and finite telescoping directly; it does not invoke an unbounded
  optional-stopping equality or remove terminal/nonnegativity premises.
- [Bertsekas, Proper Policies in Infinite-State Stochastic Shortest Path
  Problems](https://arxiv.org/html/1711.10129v2), Section II:
  nonnegative total cost and finite expected duration are distinct conditions.
  We do not import general Borel optimizer/measurable-selector existence from
  its particular disturbance model, nor a universal proper-policy synthesis.
- The bound [WTT proof](raw/parents/WTT.md) supplies the complete observable
  interface, simultaneous confidence/certification distinction, common-policy
  replacement argument and same-class setup-aware comparator. APT changes its
  geometric potential into separate duration, work and error potentials.
- [ARC](raw/parents/ARC.md) supplies one all-look confidence event for its
  finite registered row/support sampling contract. It does not produce a
  countable/Borel confidence region, physical row truth or eventual certificate.
- [SPS](raw/parents/SPS.md) supplies exact finite-known-model proper control.
  APT is conditional transfer of supplied policies, not another SSP optimizer.

## Exact finite implementation

The finite checker accepts explicit rational model vertices, nominal Q,
the common interface and supplied L,V,B,r (optionally A,epsilon). All rows,
including nominally unreachable states, are checked. It returns initial
integrals, not an estimate of physical performance.
Affinity of drift/potential inequalities and convexity of absolute model error
license the rowwise convex hull of JOINT transition/charge pairs and any
correlated subset. Transition rows and charges use the same mixing coefficients;
independently recombining their extrema needs an additional certificate. This does not
assert arbitrary continuous-region corner sufficiency, authenticate a supplied
confidence region, or equate fixed uncertainty with a switching game.
The countable identities are proved analytically; finite rational row/prefix
controls do not enumerate the general state space.

The small independent oracle is reused byte-for-byte from WTT: exact absorbing
chain elimination and explicit path execution. It never certifies the general
theorem by enumeration. No solver for arbitrary measurable kernels is supplied.

## Full acquisition and lifetime cost

All sampling, state preparation, resetting, observation, identifying support
and sufficient state, source auditing, confidence construction, potential
synthesis, robust row supremum calculation, policy search/evaluation,
certificate verification, controller/code storage, randomness and deployment
remain charged. A physical rescue/action requires independent warrant.
Registered finishing and terminal settlement work are included exactly once.

APT bounds only its stated nonnegative expected resource coordinate. Apply
it separately to other registered coordinates with separate potentials; scalar
minimization is not a global physical Pareto theorem. Use the same policy and
same setup/seed objective throughout selection and comparison.
Any claimed unconditional lifetime expectation needs an integrable bound over
the acquired dataset as well as deployment randomness, including setup costs.
Mathematical certificate existence does not supply an economical learning
algorithm, state meaning, broad family ceiling or an acquired executable method.
