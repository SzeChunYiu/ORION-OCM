# Additive-potential transfer

Separate nonnegative potentials now certify expected duration, work and model
transfer error without requiring geometric stopping tails. The supplied
countable polynomial family is an explicit additional working regime;
WTT is recovered by a constructive potential substitution.

1. [General measurable theorem and proof](ADDITIVE_POTENTIAL_TRANSFER_THEOREM_V1.md)
2. [Countable revival and decisive boundaries](COUNTABLE_REVIVAL_AND_BOUNDARIES_V1.md)
3. [Matched parents and full costs](PARENTS_AND_COSTS_V1.md)
4. [Exact control receipt](RECEIPT_V1.json) and [validation](VALIDATION_V1.md)

The proof drops nonnegative finite-horizon remainders. It does not require
the upper work potential itself to vanish in expectation; the doubling/kill
control shows why that restriction would reject valid work bounds.

The confidence event and certificate-availability event remain distinct.
Policies, setup costs and comparator classes are common. State sufficiency,
support, physical row truth, actual acquisition/synthesis costs and adequate
terminal meaning require their own warrant. No general optimizer, physical
architecture winner or empirical learned capability is inferred.

Replay the complete static payload using CPython 3.12:

    python3 -I -B replay_v1.py

MANIFEST_V1.json binds every other unit file, including complete receipt,
source, tests and the four copied parent artifacts. Replay retains the initial
manifest and expected receipt across the worker and compares all output bytes.
An externally retained manifest digest identifies the intended publication;
internal consistency does not authenticate physical premises or a host.
