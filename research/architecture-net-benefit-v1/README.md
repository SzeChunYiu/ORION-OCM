## Four review perspectives applied

These are analytical roles within this session, not four independent people or agent executions.

**Formal semantics:** require protected semantic correspondence and actual reachable lifecycle coverage; abstract negative cycles can be spurious.

**Amortized/online analysis:** count0-demand costs, initial debt and reset frequency; a nominal long lifetime does not guarantee enough reusable work.

**Systems:** include storage, validation, startup and cross-module work; a fixed cold/warm graph is not justified for an unbounded growing ledger.

**Empirical inference:** treat whole lifetimes as sampling units, retain exposure history, and separate observational timings from verified cost bounds. Direct shared-kernel equality is not independent replication.

**G3: endogenous demand and lifecycle.** Obtain prospectively sourced task/lifetime populations with discovery opportunities not supplied as preselected successful fragments. Separate development/admission/evaluation. Preserve low-reuse, drift and reset regions in the population.

**G4: architecture attribution.** Compare complete OCM with strong persistent parents and ablations using the same primitives. A win over a forgetful parent is only a persistence result. A common learner's search improvement is only a mechanism result. Demonstrate where integration removes duplicated work or improves lawful decisions after all its extra costs.

**G5: prospective external evaluation.** Freeze objective, contracts, code and uncertainty procedure before protected outcome access. Validate the sign/threshold predictions on independent complete lifetimes, then transfer the same abstract condition across genuinely distinct domains. Multiple domains support generality; they do not imply universality.

First concrete success target: one nontrivial native lifecycle family, a strong matched persistent parent, preserved protected semantics, and a positive complete-cost rate with a payable measured/verified initial debt. A narrower proven family is better than a broad unsupported claim.
