## H1 — amortized acquisition (primary residual test)

For each lifetime, construct matched related-task pairs with the same frozen acquisition channel and competence threshold. Primary coordinate: **new information units of that channel required to first reach threshold**. Do not pool unlike channel units.

Within each predeclared channel family, compute `parent_cost - OCM_cost`; positive favors OCM. Primary decision uses the designated channel family fixed in V1. With 24 non-tied lifetime differences, a one-sided exact sign test rejects at `>=17/24` positive differences (`p≈0.03196`). Ties are retained and the exact rule is recomputed on the non-tied n; the family is `CANNOT_CHECK_POWER` if fewer than 16 non-tied lifetimes remain.

A positive H1 residual additionally requires acceptable harmful-transfer performance: no statistically hidden benefit obtained by transferring indiscriminately.

## H2 — relevant-computation scaling

At 1x/3x/10x/30x state probes, report per arm:

- actual `N` by object class and bytes;
- actual identity touches `k`;
- `k/N`;
- index entries touched;
- global scan items;
- exact backend work coordinate(s);
- wall/CPU/GPU time as secondary noisy coordinates;
- preprocessing/maintenance/storage charged separately.

H2 cannot be supported from `k/N` alone. Required within-arm signature: `k/N` decreases from 1x to 30x while task correctness/coverage does not fall beyond its frozen tolerance and no hidden global scan occurs. Residual vs P6 requires a favorable paired query-work scaling difference on the predeclared exact work coordinate. If P6 scales equivalently, record `PARENT_SUFFICIENT` for H2.

## H5 — lifetime economics

No global scalar is preregistered. Cumulative vectors include acquisition + use + revision + preprocessing/index maintenance + storage + verification + external IO. Report Pareto relations and coordinate-specific crossovers `m*` only.

"OCM is globally cheaper" is admissible only if OCM weakly dominates P6 on every preregistered primary resource coordinate and strictly improves at least one without losing task quality/integrity. Otherwise use `TRADEOFF_FRONTIER_ONLY`, coordinate-specific findings, or `LIFETIME_CROSSOVER_NOT_OBSERVED`.

## Joint thesis decision

`PERSISTENT_EPISTEMIC_SCALING_SIGNATURE_SUPPORTED` requires all of:

1. H1 residual survives P6;
2. H2 measurable sparse/relevant-computation signature survives P6 or P6's equivalent is explicitly absorbed without claiming an OCM-specific residual;

## Exclusions
