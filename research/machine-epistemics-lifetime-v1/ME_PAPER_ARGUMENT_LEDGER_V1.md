## Reviewer conclusion at V1

The strongest scientifically defensible story is narrower and stronger than the initial rhetoric:

> Machine Epistemics is not being tested against a strawman "memoryless static LLM." The decisive question is whether explicit warrant/dependency governance *coupled with* reusable executable lifelong competence produces a joint measurable regime that survives modern indexed retrieval, long-term memory, continual adaptation, executable-skill and truth-maintenance parents.

Language is now the first domain where this claim becomes concrete: not “we wrote a chatbot without a Transformer,” but whether a deliberately small persistent substrate can acquire a learned listener/speaker, generalize compositionally, revise that competence locally, and—if the protected strongest-parent evidence supports it—reduce the marginal cost of learning later related linguistic structure.

Both claims are currently **open**, which is the correct status before the fresh protected studies exist.
