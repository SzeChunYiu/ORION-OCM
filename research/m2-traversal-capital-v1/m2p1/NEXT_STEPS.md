## The unifying step: one integrated developmental arm

Every OCM-specific win so far comes from a separate research script — MDL selection,
the applicability gate, deployment liveness — each compared against the parent
individually. The mission's Q4 (composition) and the "same core across domains" spine both
need them as **one controller**:

```text
CONTINUED_OCM  =  MDL selection  →  per-target applicability gate  →  D_live lifecycle
```

with `learn_generator`, `validate_generator` and `solve` still the registered units and
every success still externally verified. This is the smallest change that lets every
future run — M2-P2's authored worlds, HET-LIF-1's two domains, the R1 maths estate —
carry the full developmental controller as a single arm against `RESET`,
`STRONG_ADAPTIVE_PARENT` and `PARENT_WITH_MDL`.

Registered prediction for that arm on the refused worlds of this lane: it beats
`PARENT_WITH_MDL` wherever the library's value is heterogeneous (FV6/FV8/FOREIGN_M1
pattern) and ties it wherever the library is uniformly good (E5/E8 pattern) — never
worse, by the P1 bound.

## What would falsify the current picture

- `CONTINUED_MDL` **refuses on D2** with 7/7 recovery → hierarchical chunking does not
  dissolve the depth bound; gap 4 stays at the substrate ceiling.
- Fit-size sweep shows **no** fit-n at which marginal pays → the gate's acquisition is
  intrinsically dearer than its lifetime saving on cheap-target ecologies.
- Expanded packet shows accuracy plateaus **below** 0.8 → the cause taxonomy, not the
  case count, is the L6 bottleneck.
