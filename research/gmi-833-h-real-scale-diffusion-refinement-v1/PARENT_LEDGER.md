## 2. External parents, with citations

Iterative refinement, denoising, and the idea that a response may depend on how
many perturbations a candidate has received are not novel here and are not
claimed to be. Neither is the descriptor closure, the winner rule, the
charged-cost model, or the dominance test.

- L. Boltzmann (1872), "Weitere Studien über das Wärmegleichgewicht unter
  Gasmolekülen", *Sitzungsberichte der Kaiserlichen Akademie der
  Wissenschaften* 66:275–370. The stochastic-relaxation picture in which a
  state is refined by successive random perturbations until it settles in a
  region of the state space — the mechanism this row's protected interface
  names, stated as a step count rather than as a continuous flow.
- N. Metropolis, A. W. Rosenbluth, M. N. Rosenbluth, A. H. Teller and
  E. Teller (1953), "Equation of State Calculations by Fast Computing
  Machines", *The Journal of Chemical Physics* 21(6):1087–1092.
  DOI `10.1063/1.1699114`. Successive registered perturbations of a candidate
  with an acceptance test; the chain of steps is the quantity, not the start.
- J. Sohl-Dickstein, E. A. Weiss, N. Maheswaranathan and S. Ganguli (2015),
  "Deep Unsupervised Learning using Nonequilibrium Thermodynamics",
  *Proceedings of the 32nd International Conference on Machine Learning*,
  PMLR 37:2256–2265. The forward corruption and reverse refinement of a
  candidate as an explicit sequence of steps.
- J. Ho, A. Jain and P. Abbeel (2020), "Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic
  Models", *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems* 33:6840–6851.
  The step-indexed view of refinement: the response is a function of how many
  refinement steps a candidate has taken.
- Y. Song, J. Sohl-Dickstein, D. P. Kingma, A. Kumar, S. Ermon and B. Poole
  (2021), "Score-Based Generative Modeling through Stochastic Differential
  Equations", *International Conference on Learning Representations*. The
  continuous-time limit of the same refinement chain.
- D. E. Knuth (1997), *The Art of Computer Programming, Vol. 2*, 3rd ed.,
  Addison-Wesley. The multiplicative-hash permutation used as the registered
  presentation lever, and the source of the registered alphabet order.
- G. Salton, A. Wong and C. S. Yang (1975), "A vector space model for automatic
  indexing", *Communications of the ACM* 18(11):613–620.
  DOI `10.1145/361219.361220`. Descriptor-term indexing over a document
  vocabulary; the descriptor-closure vocabulary is the same construction on a
  word list.

## 3. What is NOT claimed novel

- No diffusion, denoising, score-matching or annealing method here is new.
- "A candidate that is far from its context's support takes more registered
  steps to come back" is a property of the corpus under the registered order,
  not a discovery.
- The readout language, the winner rule, the post-hoc classifier, the
  charged-cost model and the eleven-coordinate ledger are the parents' forms,
  re-implemented here rather than imported.

## 4. The residual contribution of this tranche

One thing, and it is a measurement rather than a method:

**A family row of the `STOCHASTIC_SOURCE_CHANNEL` registry family is recovered
at its own real scale, and is separated from its registered sibling by
measurement rather than by name.** On the registered stochastic-refinement
ecology `F33` at `SIGMA_H33R`, the family-blind winner rule over a closed
readout language recovers `REFINE<=25`
(`REFINEMENT_INDEX`, a refinement step index) at **1,479** held errors against
the majority rule's **5,197**; the registered single-draw family of the same
language makes **30,557** at its best (a factor of 20.7) and the raw cardinality
family **5,197** (a factor of 3.5); and the registered **global** design null
degrades the refinement family by **4.52x** while leaving the cardinality arm
**bit-identical**, so the three families are separated mechanically by the same
reassignment rather than by their labels. The registry's own contract entry is
recorded as a steer in the freeze and is not claimed as a certificate in either
direction.
