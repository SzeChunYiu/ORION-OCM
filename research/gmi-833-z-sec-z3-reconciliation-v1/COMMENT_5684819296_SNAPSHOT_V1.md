## Groundbreaking flagship closure rule

The programme must **not** infer groundbreaking or top-tier flagship readiness from checkbox count. A flagship-ready claim requires, at minimum:

```text
compact explanatory principle
+ architecture-uncommitted derivation
+ invariance/no-smuggling evidence
+ discriminating prospective predictions
+ calibrated capability/failure predictions
+ independent hostile replication
+ real-system validation
+ at least one genuinely surprising discovery
```

If any component is absent, the claim ceiling must say so explicitly.
