# AJ13 freeze — recursive descent stopping rule

AJ13 asks whether the registered GMI descent has reached a scientifically defensible **operational stopping layer**, not an absolute bottom of mathematics or reality.

The stop audit is conjunctive. `FOUNDATION_RELATIVE_GMI_CORE_STABLE_AT_REGISTERED_SCOPE` may be emitted only if all six registered conditions are evidenced:

1. every remaining lower assumption is explicitly tagged as foundation, logic/metatheory, physical-substrate law, resource model, or value/requirement input;
2. no MI-family-specific mechanism remains in the AJ1 operational base;
3. removing each AJ1 registered operational component has a demonstrated requirement loss/boundary at the frozen finite microscope;
4. higher semantic GMI results survive a materially different lower presentation where the relevant theorem is claimed presentation-invariant;
5. strongest-parent ownership is explicit rather than rebranding lower parent formalisms as GMI novelty;
6. further descent targets mathematics/logic/physical law/metaphysics or changes the declared substrate, rather than exposing a hidden neural/symbolic/search/etc. MI mechanism.

Failure of any condition returns `DESCENT_CONTINUES__MATERIAL_HIDDEN_ASSUMPTION_OR_INVARIANCE_GAP`.

Passing the stop audit does **not** mean the remaining assumptions are metaphysically final or uniquely true. It means the GMI-specific derivation can stop at the registered operational layer while those lower assumptions remain explicit parameters/boundaries.

Forbidden terminal: `ABSOLUTE_BOTTOM_OF_MATHEMATICS_OR_REALITY_PROVEN`.
