## Repository parents

The AJ4 organization set at the registered binary budget is the possibility space, rebuilt here
and pinned in `MANIFEST_V1.json`. The sibling result — that requirements and pricing move the
preferred form — is owned by the merged niche-repricing and objective-provenance packages, also
pinned there, and is not re-earned. The already-checked AH7 row that keeps generation,
development, capability and choice as separate maps is owned by AJ4.

## What is NOT claimed novel

Version-space narrowing, non-domination, transducer theory, and the observation that data
presentation matters. None of these is this tranche's.

## The residual contribution

1. The data axis isolated: space, pricing, requirement and horizon held fixed, with a
   fingerprint over all four so that "held fixed" is checkable rather than asserted.
2. A registered dataset family where two of five target behaviours change their preferred form
   on coverage alone, with the exact systems named.
3. The two construction properties — the answer is a function of the data, and narrowing is
   monotone — checked rather than assumed.
4. Two measurements that do not support the row, published rather than dropped.
