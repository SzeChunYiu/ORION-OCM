# K2 acquisition experiment: read first

Ledger item 10, run rather than argued.

- **K2 is positive at a registered scope.** With the `squares` library, K2 holds
  on **148 of 148** held-out reuse targets.
- **Semantic reuse is necessary at this registered scope.** Across the four
  named libraries and two disjoint populations — **1,707** no-reuse
  library-target *pairs*, each target counted once per library — there are
  **zero** K2 successes. It is *not* a general law: KAE-10 exhibits a renamed
  library that accelerates without shortening.
- **It is not sufficient.** Held-out rates are 50.99%, 100% and **39.37%**, so
  the condition is one-sided and an iff would be false.
- **Absent reuse, retention hurts**: `H` is strictly worse on 100% of held-out
  no-reuse targets, because macros inflate the search alphabet with nothing to
  shorten.

unit-charge condition stops applying, and why no law built from search-level
totals predicts the remaining cases.
