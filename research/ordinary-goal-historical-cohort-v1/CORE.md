# Historical 22-row training cohort

[COHORT.json](COHORT.json) freezes all 22 historical IDs and issued labels, with complete original cut bodies, typed contexts, proposed proofs/targets/ordered hypotheses, admission rows and corresponding screen rows. It is training-data materialization, not fresh admission or current eligibility.

The first matching released-root/cut traversal reconciles exactly with `cut-lemma-00` through `cut-lemma-21`: target, hypothesis count, proof length, source label and zero-premise fields all agree. No discrepancies were found in those comparisons; seven rows have no essential premises.

The retained frame is three class parameters for every row, but physical names differ: 20 use A/B/C and two use A/B/V. The cohort remains intact. [Frame profile and integration limitation](FRAME-PROFILE.json).
