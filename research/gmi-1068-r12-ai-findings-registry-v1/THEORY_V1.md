# R12 — academic AI findings through GMI

R12 distinguishes five scientifically different achievements:
1. DERIVED_AT_REGISTERED_SCOPE — a GMI theorem/finite derivation produces the qualitative finding under stated assumptions;
2. PROSPECTIVELY_PREDICTED — prediction was frozen before outcome;
3. CONDITIONALLY_EXPLAINED — the finding follows once additional empirically grounded assumptions are supplied;
4. ACCOMMODATED_NOT_DERIVED — GMI can represent the result but adds no causal explanation;
5. EMPIRICAL_LAW_NOT_DERIVED — GMI provides variables/frontier language but the quantitative law comes from data;
6. PARENT_OWNED_LIMIT — strongest result belongs to established parent theory;
7. UNRESOLVED.

The registry covers the 52 canonical finding families frozen in R12. It intentionally does not claim every publication or every future AI phenomenon has been exhausted.

Core unification patterns:
- scaling/efficiency results live on context-conditioned resource-capability frontiers;
- architecture findings are morphology realizations selected by locality, symmetry, temporal aliasing, routing, information and resource pressures;
- learning phenomena are paths through the developmental/update process space;
- retrieval/tools/agents expand admissible information/action processes;
- robustness/OOD/calibration failures expose a mismatch between the registered context/test family and deployment contexts;
- generative model families are different factorizations/transports/refinement processes under different objectives/resources;
- classical symbolic, Bayesian, kernel, tree and exemplar methods are specializations, not pre-GMI exceptions.

Quantitative empirical constants, benchmark scores, power-law exponents and emergence times are never derived merely by rewriting them in GMI notation.
