## Expert-cell review

### Learning and information theory

Constructive finding: evaluate a learner on the future response profiles it must distinguish, not only training accuracy. The profile includes unseen recombination and post-revocation retention.

Hostile finding: the first lower bound is a version-space information count. It does not by itself exceed query learning, teaching dimension, privileged-information or computational-trace parents.

Next obligation: replace direct bits by a natural program class and prove a nontrivial query/computational lower bound.

### Dynamic algorithms, data structures and recourse

Constructive finding: persistent proof/dependency state can buy faster, less-destructive updates after evidence changes.

Hostile finding: static dependency storage, change propagation and the two-state/three-state recoloring witness are close to established dynamic-data-structure and recourse questions.

Next obligation: derive a joint lower bound coupling what had to be learned during acquisition with future update cost and useful-retention guarantees.

### Formal methods and provenance

Constructive finding: local certificates can prevent unsupported learned procedures from silently remaining live. Scope, verifier identity, evidence epoch and alternative support paths are part of the semantic object.

Hostile finding: proof-carrying code, TMS/ATMS, provenance and self-adjusting computation already own certificate checking and dependency maintenance separately.

Next obligation: prove that the support/dependency object is learned, not supplied free, and that incremental repair is equivalent to full re-derivation on a registered fragment.

### Architecture theory

Constructive finding: the object can guide an executable OCM implementation.

Hostile finding: a recurrent/looped finite-precision Transformer with the same memory, trace, verifier, tool and update interfaces can plausibly execute the same algorithm. Raw expressivity is not a defensible novelty axis.

Next obligation: perform the parity compilation early. Retain the result as an architecture-neutral learning/resource theorem if compilation succeeds.

### ORION epistemics

Constructive finding: ORION contributes native evidence, dependence, authority, receipt, failure-knowledge and reopening objects that can become obligations on learned procedural state.

Hostile finding: vocabulary and workflow integration are not scientific novelty.

Next obligation: make false authority, unsupported retention and over-retraction explicit hard outcomes in the theorem.

### Hostile novelty review

Current rejection case: the whole proposal may be a composition of trace learning, proof-carrying execution, provenance/TMS, self-adjusting computation, ticketed/certified unlearning and a recurrent Transformer.

Only acceptable escape: a theorem must survive the exact parent product under equal information and whole-system resources. Otherwise route to `PARENT_SUFFICIENT` or an integration-standard result.

## Breakthrough candidate

Working name only: **Lifecycle-Identifiable Proof-Carrying Experience (LI-PCE)**.

A training episode yields reusable procedural knowledge plus independently checkable local support. The learner must later solve unseen compositions and respond correctly to revocation of evidence, scope, policy or verifier identity. It must retain every still-supported useful procedure, reject every unsupported one and report abstention when retained evidence is insufficient.

The first exact theorem is in `OCM_LIFECYCLE_IDENTIFIABILITY_PROOF_AND_FRONTIER_V1.md`.
