# Revocation-Complete Learning V0 — parent subtraction and breakthrough target

## 6. Strongest-parent subtraction

1. **Computational-trace learning:** already proves that traces can radically change identification and studies partial/adversarial corruption. RCL cannot claim “traces help learning.”
2. **Exact monotone-DNF / hidden-hypergraph learning:** `f_mathcal J` is a monotone DNF whose minimal terms are warrants. Learning an arbitrary warrant profile from liveness membership queries is parent-owned.
3. **Learning–unlearning space theory:** future deletion can require retained memory; ticketed and system-aware models already study exact update frontiers. RCL must not claim the generic memory phenomenon.
4. **Provenance/TMS/self-adjusting computation:** complete known alternative derivations and selective invalidation are parent-owned.
5. **Proof-carrying execution:** checking one current certificate is parent-owned. RCL’s distinction is certificate soundness versus future-warrant completeness.
6. **Authority revocation and provenance-to-forget-set systems:** exact revocation over a supplied authority/provenance object and precise record identification are parent-owned.
7. **Recurrent Transformer compilation:** the finite algorithms are implementable by an equally provisioned recurrent machine; no architecture separation follows.

No exact primary-source match for the phrase or complete joint theorem was identified in the targeted search recorded in the source ledger. That is not saturation and grants no novelty.

## 7. Surviving breakthrough theorem target

The elementary pack supports one stronger research object:

### RCL-B — joint operator-and-warrant learning frontier

Construct a natural family of compositional operator DAGs and an independently checked proof-carrying training interface. Prove all of the following under one parity contract:

1. **constructive upper bound:** learn operator semantics and a CWC warrant summary, then execute held-out/reminted compositions;
2. **strongest-interface lower bound:** endpoint, raw-trace, or sound-but-nonexhaustive proof interfaces require strictly more total information, later proof/data queries, recourse, abstention, or false-authority risk;
3. **retention:** the safe learner cannot win by deleting or rejecting everything;
4. **dynamic scope:** handle evidence, checker-version, authority-edge, rule, and scope changes—not only a supplied example forget set;
5. **corruption:** state the exact boundary for missing, corrupt, self-authored, or unavailable certificates;
6. **parent-product refusal:** show why monotone-DNF learning + exact unlearning + provenance maintenance does not already obtain the same theorem at equal resources;
7. **architecture parity:** compile the algorithm into a recurrent Transformer and contract the claim if the overhead is comparable.

A strong candidate resource vector is

\[
(B_{sem},B_{warrant},Q_{proof},Q_{data},T_{repair},Recourse,CollateralLoss,FalseAuthority,Abstention).
\]

A breakthrough requires a nontrivial Pareto or impossibility theorem on this vector, not merely the definition of RCL.

## 8. Kill conditions

File `PARENT_SUFFICIENT` or contract immediately if:

- RCL-B reduces at equal information and cost to exact monotone-DNF/hypergraph learning plus standard provenance maintenance;
- every intervention is equivalent to an explicitly supplied training-example forget set;
- the checker, trace, or certificate silently carries the target program or all future answers;
- safety is achieved by retracting everything;
- held-out transfer disappears under reminted identities;
- the same-information recurrent Transformer matches all resources;
- a primary source proves the same joint theorem;
- the intervention family is chosen after observing the result.
