# Parent ownership and the residual contribution

Assimilation-first. Everything below is absorbed, cited and left with its
classical ownership intact. What this package adds is named at the end, and it
is small.

## Classical parents — owned entirely outside this corpus

- **Finite automata, Myhill–Nerode, state minimisation.** Rabin, M. O. and
  Scott, D. (1959). "Finite automata and their decision problems."
  *IBM Journal of Research and Development* 3(2), 114–125.
  doi:10.1147/rd.32.0114. Hopcroft, J. E. (1971), "An n log n algorithm for
  minimizing states in a finite automaton." The notion that a bounded register
  induces a finite behavioural quotient is theirs, not ours.
- **Linear least squares.** Legendre (1805); Gauss (1809). The normal equations,
  the Gauss–Markov optimality, and everything this package's affine arm does are
  classical.
- **Linear prediction of speech / autoregressive filtering.** Makhoul, J.
  (1975). "Linear prediction: a tutorial review." *Proceedings of the IEEE*
  63(4), 561–580. doi:10.1109/PROC.1975.9792. That a short FIR predictor
  captures most of the short-time structure of real speech is his result, and it
  is what `E_H02` exercises.
- **Generalised linear models.** Nelder, J. A. and Wedderburn, R. W. M. (1972).
  "Generalized linear models." *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society A*
  135(3), 370–384. doi:10.2307/2344614. McCullagh, P. and Nelder, J. A. (1989),
  *Generalized Linear Models*, 2nd ed., Chapman and Hall. The link function, the
  variance function, IRLS and the log-link Poisson arm are theirs.
- **Basis expansion and kernels.** Aizerman, M. A., Braverman, E. M. and
  Rozonoer, L. I. (1964), "Theoretical foundations of the potential function
  method." Schölkopf, B. and Smola, A. J. (2002), *Learning with Kernels*, MIT
  Press. The representer form, the kernel trick, and the observation that a
  landmark representation is cheaper than an explicit expansion below a
  crossover are theirs.
- **Program enumeration and observational equivalence pruning.** Udupa, A. et
  al. (2013), "TRANSIT: specifying protocols with concolic snippets";
  Alur, R. et al. (2013), "Syntax-guided synthesis", FMCAD.
  doi:10.1109/FMCAD.2013.6679385. Enumerate-and-quotient-by-denotation is
  standard SyGuS practice and is not claimed here.

**Nothing about any of these families is claimed novel.** This package does not
discover linear regression, GLMs, kernels or automata, does not improve any of
them, and makes no statistical-methodology claim.

## Corpus parents

- `research/gmi-833-h-neutral-four-family-v1` — **the strongest parent.** It
  owns the neutral lower-grammar construction, the enumerate-quotient-select
  discipline, the post-hoc external structural classifier, the semantic
  no-smuggling audit, and the ten-gate family ledger form, for these same four
  rows. Its terminal is
  `ONE_LOWER_NEUTRAL_GRAMMAR_RECOVERS_FOUR_POSTHOC_STRUCTURAL_CLASSES_AT_REGISTERED_FINITE_SCOPE__FAMILY_GATES_REMAIN_OPEN`
  and its `open_gate` is `real_scale_test`.
  **No certificate of that package is counted here.** Its gates hold at
  `SIGMA_4F`, a finite `Z3` scope; importing one would be the
  `CROSS_SCOPE_GATE_COMPOSITION` that `FGS-2` forbids. Form is borrowed;
  evidence is not.
  Its `forbidden_promotions` bind claims that rest on its evidence. This package
  rests on none of it and says so in `FREEZE_V1.md` Section 2.
- PR #997 `research/gmi-833-h-family-gate-soundness-v1` — owns `FGS-1..FGS-6`.
  `FGS-2` (scope-gluing no-go) is the theorem that dictates this package's whole
  architecture; `FGS-3` is what makes a direct real-scale test the right
  instrument rather than an inference from the finite parent.
- PR #1023 `research/gmi-833-h-family-requirement-ledger-v1` — owns the
  scope-indexed 43-row requirement matrix, `HRL-1..HRL-5`, and the finding that
  `R11` is the universal residual and that no Section-H row is
  `BLOCKED_STRUCTURAL`. Its `HRL-5.1` observation that `R01` and `R08` share one
  evidence key at `SIGMA_4F` is why this package witnesses them separately.
- `research/gmi-833-real-transition-receipts-v1` (#903) — owns the corpus's
  real-system evidence protocol: sha256-bound real sources, registered seeds,
  committed `REAL_RUNS/` artifacts, predictions frozen before outcomes, CI
  re-running the deterministic stage. That protocol is followed here.
- `research/gmi-833-capability-predictor-evaluation-v1` (#1022) — owns the
  hollow-census and vacuous-hostile findings. Both are answered here by an
  explicit non-degenerate count per scope and an `applicable` flag that fails
  the run when a hostile cannot fire.

## Residual contribution of this tranche

1. Four real-scale scopes for the four classical Section-H rows, each with the
   eleven coordinates earned **at that one scope**, with no certificate imported
   from any other scope — the first construction in this corpus that satisfies
   `FGS-2` rather than working around it.
2. A registered, falsifiable definition of `real-scale` for classical families,
   with the scale coordinate, the thresholds, the digest-bound sources, and an
   explicit statement of what it does not license.
3. The real-scale outcomes themselves, positive and negative, at that
   definition — including the ecology and loss boundaries that the first pass
   exposed and the single-stage attributions for them.
