## 2. The scope decision that governs this package

Section H requires eleven coordinates per row: property prediction from
specification/ecology (`R01`); `P3`/`P4` grammar (`R02`); no family macros
(`R03`); neutral recovery (`R04`); negative twin (`R05`); lower bound where
possible (`R06`); resource crossover (`R07`); held-out frozen prediction
(`R08`); remint (`R09`); independent search (`R10`); real-scale test (`R11`).

`research/gmi-833-h-neutral-four-family-v1` certifies ten of those eleven for
these same four rows at its own scope `SIGMA_4F` — a finite `Z3` grammar over
an eight-point carrier — and records `open_gate = real_scale_test`
(`RESULT_V1.json:56`), `complete: false`, `issue_833_checkbox:
MUST_REMAIN_OPEN` on every row.

It is **invalid** to add an eleventh certificate at a different scope and
declare the conjunction. That is `CROSS_SCOPE_GATE_COMPOSITION`, forbidden by
`gmi-833-h-family-requirement-ledger-v1` `HRL-1`, and proved invalid by PR #997
`FGS-2` (scope-gluing no-go), whose countermodel argument covers grammar,
ecology, freeze, family row and protected-interface drift explicitly. A scope
is the tuple `(family row, grammar, ecology, budget, freeze, protected
interface)`; a real-scale ecology is a different tuple.

**Therefore this package imports no gate certificate from `SIGMA_4F`. It earns
all eleven coordinates at its own four scopes, self-contained.** The parent
supplies form (one neutral lower grammar, the ten-gate ledger shape, the
post-hoc structural classifier) and is cited as the strongest parent; it
supplies no evidence that is counted here.

The parent's `forbidden_promotions` list contains `REAL_SCALE_VALIDATION_COMPLETE`
and the four `*_FAMILY_ROW_CLOSED` strings. Those bind claims resting on the
parent's evidence. This package rests on none of it, states so here, and adds
`CROSS_SCOPE_GATE_COMPOSITION` to its own forbidden list so the consideration
is on the record rather than stepped around.

**Slice rule, frozen here.** For each ecology the row index runs `0..n-1` in

rows form the **fit** slice. The remint slice is the fit slice restricted to
`index mod 5 in {0}`; the remint held-out slice is `index mod 5 == 2`
restricted to the second half of the source, disjoint from everything above.

## 5. The neutral lower grammar `G_R`, frozen before any data is read

Leaves: `ARG` (the input value at the current fold index), `PARAM` (the
parameter at the current fold index), `ACC` (the accumulator), `S` (the
completed fold value), `BIAS` (a scalar parameter), `STATE` (the delay-cell
read), `C0`, `C1`.

Operations, all generic and of arity at most two: `ADD(a,b)`, `MUL(a,b)`,
`NEG(a)`, `ABS(a)`, `STEP(a)` = 1 if `a > 0` else 0, `RECIP(a)` = `1/a` if
`a != 0` else 0.

Control constructs, both generic: one indexed accumulate
`ACC <- ADD(ACC, BODY(ARG_i, PARAM_i))` over a registered index set, and one
delay cell `STATE_{t+1} <- OUT_t`.

A candidate program is a pair `(BODY, HEAD)`:

```
ACC_0 = 0 ;  ACC_{i+1} = ADD(ACC_i, BODY(ARG_i, PARAM_i))  for i in the index set
S     = ACC_N
OUT_t = HEAD(S, BIAS, STATE_t) ;  STATE_{t+1} = OUT_t
```

`BODY` ranges over every `G_R` expression of at most 3 nodes over leaves
`{ARG, PARAM, C0, C1}`. `HEAD` ranges over every `G_R` expression of at most 4
nodes over leaves `{S, BIAS, STATE, C0, C1}`. Both are enumerated exhaustively
and target-independently, once, before any ecology is loaded, and semantically
quotiented on a fixed exact rational probe grid.

The generator receives **no family name, no family identifier, no
family-specific candidate menu, and no target values**. Structural names
(`PERSISTENT_STATE`, `AFFINE_SCORE`, `NONLINEAR_LINK`, `LIFTED_BASIS`) are
attached only after selection, by an external classifier that reads the
selected expression tree and never the family label. A semantic no-smuggling
audit checks the grammar digest before and after every search, provenance
closure over the registered operations, totality of denotations on the probe
grid, and target-independence of the enumeration. A token scan alone is not
sufficient and is not relied on.

## 6. Arithmetic rule

Every reported quantity is an integer or a `fractions.Fraction`. Floats appear
only inside parameter-fitting routines. A fitting routine's output is
rationalised at a registered precision (`limit_denominator(10**9)`, applied
identically to every arm) **before** it is used, and from that point on it is
data. No float enters any comparison, any count, any loss, or any claim.

## 7. What `real-scale` means here — the registered definition

A test at scope `sigma` is **real-scale** exactly when all five hold:

1. **Real provenance.** Every input value is read from a source of Section 4,
   verified by sha256 at run time. Data derived by counting, windowing, or
   indexing those bytes is real; data produced by a sampler, simulator, or
   generator is not, and none is used.
2. **Scale coordinate and threshold.** The scale coordinate is the number of
   fitted rows `n_fit` and the held-out row count `n_held`, both after the
   Section-4 slice rule. Real-scale requires `n_fit >= 100,000` and
   `n_held >= 20,000` at every scope. For reference, `SIGMA_4F` evaluates a
   carrier of 8 points; the threshold is therefore at least four orders of
   magnitude above the finite parent.
3. **Fitted systems.** At each scope at least two arms are fitted at that scale
   — the structure the blind search selected and its registered comparison arm —
   with the same slices, the same rationalisation, and the same charged cost
   model.
4. **Protected evaluation.** Every claimed number is computed on the held-out
   slice only, after the structure is fixed, in exact arithmetic.
5. **Resource accounting at scale.** The charged cost model is evaluated at the
   real `n`, `d` and index-set size, not at a reduced search budget.

**Falsifier of the definition itself.** If any claimed number at a scope can be
reproduced from fewer than 100,000 real fitted rows, or from bytes not covered
by a Section-4 digest, the real-scale certificate at that scope is void.

**What this definition does not license.** It does not license: frontier-model
or deep-network real-scale claims; any statement about data outside `D1`–`D3`;
any statement about ecologies other than `E_H01`–`E_H04`; transport of these
certificates to another scope; independent-team replication; maturity `M5`;
`EV4`/`EV5`; the aggregate Section-H rows; or any of the other 39 named-family
rows. Reproducing on a second host or a second Python version licenses none of
those either — intra-package agreement is not independent replication.

## 8. Frozen predictions, with falsifiers, before any outcome exists

Each is derived from the ecology specification of Section 3 alone. `R01` is
discharged by the `a`-predictions (structure predicted from the ecology); `R08`
is discharged by the `c`/`d`-predictions (held-out numbers frozen before the
held-out slice is read). They are deliberately witnessed by different
predictions, because the parent's `R01` and `R08` share one evidence key at
`SIGMA_4F` and their measurements there are not independent.

**`SIGMA_H01` — a real character stream carries history dependence.**
- `P01a` The blind search selects a program whose `HEAD` reads `STATE`;
  the post-hoc class is `PERSISTENT_STATE`.
- `P01b` On the symbol-permuted twin ecology (identical symbol multiset, order
  destroyed), the blind search selects a program whose `HEAD` does not read
  `STATE`.
- `P01c` On the held-out real slice the selected stateful program's exact
  symbol-class error count is strictly lower than the selected stateless twin's.
- `P01d` Over 200 order-randomised control streams the stateful program's error
  count is not strictly lower in any of the 200.
- Falsifier: any of `P01a`–`P01d` fails.

**`SIGMA_H02` — a real linear-predictable target.**
- `P02a` The blind search selects `AFFINE_SCORE`: `BODY` semantically equal to
  `MUL(ARG, PARAM)` and `HEAD` semantically equal to `ADD(S, BIAS)`, with no
  `STATE` read and no non-affine transform of `ARG`.
- `P02b` On the `E_H04` twin the same search does not select `AFFINE_SCORE`.
- `P02c` Held-out exact squared error of the selected affine arm is strictly
  lower than that of the best constant arm, and the held-out exact sign-decision
  error count is strictly lower than the majority-class arm's.
- `P02d` Over 200 row-permuted design controls the affine arm's held-out exact
  squared error is not lower than the constant arm's in any of the 200.
- `P02e` There is an exact index-set size `m*` at which the charged cost of the
  compact composed program first falls below the charged cost of the tabulated
  alternative; `m*` is reported exactly.
- Falsifier: any of `P02a`–`P02e` fails.

**`SIGMA_H03` — a real over-dispersed non-negative count response.**
- `P03a` The blind search selects a `HEAD` that applies a non-affine transform
  to `S`; the post-hoc class is `NONLINEAR_LINK`.
- `P03b` The log-link arm is strictly closer to the held-out real count than
  the identity-link arm on strictly more than half of the held-out rows, in
  exact rational comparison of rationalised predicted means.
- `P03c` The identity-link arm emits at least one negative predicted mean on
  the held-out slice; the log-link arm emits zero. (A count response cannot be
  negative; this is the link doing work, exactly countable.)
- `P03d` On the `E_H02` twin the blind search does not select `NONLINEAR_LINK`.
- Falsifier: any of `P03a`–`P03d` fails. In particular, a tie on `P03b` or a
  zero negative-prediction count on `P03c` closes no row.

**`SIGMA_H04` — a real target that is a non-affine functional of the inputs.**
- `P04a` The blind search selects `LIFTED_BASIS`: `BODY` applies a non-affine
  transform to `ARG`.
- `P04b` Held-out exact squared error of the lifted arm is strictly lower than
  that of the affine arm fitted on the same real design at the same scale.
- `P04c` There is an exact landmark count `q*` at which the charged cost of the
  kernel/landmark arm first exceeds the charged cost of the affine arm while its
  held-out exact squared error is still strictly lower; `q*` and both costs are
  reported exactly.
- `P04d` On the `E_H02` twin the blind search does not select `LIFTED_BASIS`.
- Falsifier: any of `P04a`–`P04d` fails.

**Cross-family prediction.** `P00` One and the same `G_R`, unchanged between
scopes (grammar digest identical before and after all four searches), produces
all four selections; no ecology receives a grammar extension, an extra
operation, or a hand-supplied candidate.

**A prediction that fails is reported as failed and closes no row.** The row is
then diagnosed to a single stage, one revival with the matching lever is
attempted, and the revival is re-tested against a newly frozen prediction set
recorded in a `FREEZE_V*_ADDENDUM.md` committed before that re-test — never
against the set that revealed the problem.

## 9. Remint, independent search, hostiles, nulls

- **Remint** (`R09`): the whole derivation is re-run on the disjoint remint
  slices of Section 4; the selected structural class at each scope must be
  identical. A different class fails the gate.
- **Independent search** (`R10`): a source-separated oracle re-derives every
  selection and every held-out claim without importing the primary executor.
  Non-import is enforced structurally by an `ast` scan of the oracle's imports
  and by a `sys.modules` assertion, not by a comment.
- **Hostiles**: every hostile carries an `applicable` flag. The run **fails**
  if a hostile is vacuous — that is, if the quantity the hostile perturbs is
  not moved by the perturbation. A hostile that cannot fire tests nothing.
- **Nulls**: the randomised controls of `P01d` and `P02d`, and the no-alarm
  assertion that the true run raises no hostile.
- **Non-degeneracy**: every scope reports its non-degenerate row count
  explicitly — held-out rows with non-constant response, designs with non-zero
  variance in every used column, and decision arms that emit both classes. A
  scope whose held-out predictions are degenerate closes no row, however good
  its aggregate number looks.
