## Protocol (hard rules)

1. **Prospective fits only.** A scaling law is fit from data collected after its functional form + variables are frozen; never from retrospective data.
2. **Held-out scale prediction before each larger run.** Predict point + interval at the next scale; register the prediction receipt; the run adjudicates. Two consecutive prediction failures ⇒ law refuted at that scope (CORRECTED, retain).
3. **Physical denominator always** (P3-meters clause; DATABASE_PARENT_SUFFICIENT lesson): count-only claims invalid.
4. **No cross-level inference.** A synthetic-ecology positive never licenses a GPT-level capability claim (ladder rule); scaling laws are claimed only at registered scope.
5. Family-wise error controlled (Holm) across endpoints per study; fixed horizons; no interim looks.

## Candidate functional forms (to be frozen per study, from parents — not assumed)

- Acquisition vs history: search-prior formation curves (DreamCoder compression-over-order; Soar practice power laws) as nulls to beat.
- Amortisation vs target count N: crossing form from view-benefit economics (build cost amortised over query mix, maintenance term, budget knapsack) — validated against M1C's observed ladder {8,32,128,512}.
- RSI: ℓ(G) linear-in-log-G decline is the earned form; flat = AMORTIZER_ONLY.
- Cost-of-thought vs capability: bounded-optimality measurement discipline (fixed-budget net utility as sole terminal metric).

## Ecological validity precondition

Every scaling run's ecology passes the viability gate (useful prior NOT derivable from public specification; learnability condition; value-of-history > 0 measured cheaply BEFORE the expensive arm). Independently authored ecologies (P1-E3 unit; M2-P2 in flight) for any claim above assay scope.
