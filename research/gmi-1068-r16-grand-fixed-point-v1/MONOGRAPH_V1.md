# Grand Unified GMI — Fixed-Point Theory V1

## One-sentence theory

Grand GMI is the theory of how substrate-admitted compositional processes transform **contextual attainability**.

## Minimal relative core

At the registered GMI scope the only surviving GMI-specific axes are:

1. **PROCESS_CORE** — a substrate-relative typed category of admissible sequential processes.
2. **CONTEXT** — externally supplied observational/evaluative maps into preordered result spaces.

They are mutually non-derivable in general: identical process dynamics can support incompatible contexts, and identical contexts can be paired with different admissible process sets.

Everything else in the registered GMI vocabulary is derived.

## State and representation

State is not primitive. Histories equivalent under all registered future tests share a contextual-equivalence class. A correct representation cannot merge histories separated by a required future test. Representation is a sufficient quotient/refinement selected under resource constraints.

Morphology/species are quotient classes, not intrinsic architecture names.

## Geometry and resources

Transformation burden is the Pareto/scalar cost of admissible transforms. It is directed and may be infinite. Symmetry is not universal. Resource theory remains parent mathematics.

## Learning

All update laws have one type: U:(organization,evidence)->organization'. Trial search, directional updates, reverse accumulation, Bayesian conditioning, hypothesis elimination, memory writes, library formation, population updates, meta-updates and code self-modification are specializations selected by information and resource structure.

## Information and control

GMI explicitly separates marginal bias, dependence, predictability, causal/interventional relevance, control relevance, compressibility and computational accessibility. Information existence alone does not imply accessible or useful intelligence.

## World models / physical AI

A world model is a predictive process on a representation sufficient for future contextual questions. Latent prediction is advantageous when omitted raw distinctions are context-irrelevant enough to justify resource savings; raw-detail hostile worlds refute universal JEPA superiority. Planning is search over predicted future attainability. Embodiment is sensor/action process boundaries plus physical substrate constraints and physical consequences.

LeCun/JEPA/world-model work is therefore a rich comparison/empirical specialization, not a new GMI primitive.

- independent external-team replication;
- physical embodied robot trial;

Therefore the final claim is a **declared-scope theory fixed point**, not empirical or metaphysical completeness.
