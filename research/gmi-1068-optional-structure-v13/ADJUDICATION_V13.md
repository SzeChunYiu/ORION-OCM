## GMI2-R1-004 — adjudicate parallel composition

**Original target.** Original R1 freeze result4 asks for valid process models
without universally required enrichments; the hypothesis explicitly lists
parallel/monoidal tensor as not assumed universal without a forcing witness.

**Adjudication.** Tensor is not universally required for every typed sequential
process category. The one-object category of actual bit functions e,z_0,z_1
has associative typed composition and identities, yet admits no monoidal
structure whatsoever on that unchanged category.

**Proof warrant.** M1-A proves actual function semantics, closure, distinctness,
noncommutativity and that only e is invertible. M1-C considers arbitrary tensor
and weak unitors: invertibility forces both unitors to e; naturality gives a
common unit; bifunctoriality gives interchange. M1-B then forces commutativity,
a contradiction. An omitted tensor field or one unsuccessful proposed tensor
would not establish this claim. The proof quantifies over every arrow operation.

**Closure gate.** Exact frozen finite calibration and hostile controls must pass,
including all 19683 operations, exactly 81 with the common unit, a genuine failed
interchange equation for each such candidate, and the valid C2 control.
Kernel registration must check the actual general interchange and finite
obstruction statement types, with formal/paper boundaries stated explicitly.
Only then may the successor mark this requirement CLOSED.

**Excluded claims.** No prohibition of useful physical parallelism, no exclusion
of monoidal enlargement, no absolute minimality theorem and no closure of the
separate stochasticity/higher-cell requirements.

## GMI2-R1-005 — test symmetry necessity

**Original target.** The original hypothesis lists symmetry/braiding among
structures not assumed universal. Test necessity even after a lawful tensor
is actually supplied, so the tensor-free M1 example alone is insufficient.

**Adjudication.** A valid monoidal process model need not admit a braiding for
its registered tensor. M2-A constructs the discrete category on M with tensor
its noncommutative product. M2-B adds C2 loops, yielding nonidentity processes
and the same obstruction. Both models satisfy the stated monoidal laws.

**Proof warrant.** In the C2 product, typed composition is XOR and tensor uses
the M product on objects and XOR on arrows. XOR commutativity gives interchange;
its associativity/unit laws and those of M give naturality and strict coherence.
Any braiding component for (z_0,z_1) would have type z_1→z_0, but that hom-set
is empty. Absence of even a component excludes braiding and hence symmetry;
no claim about checking selected hexagon equations substitutes for this fact.

**Closure gate.** Independently verify the discrete and C2 product categories,
typed composition, tensor functoriality, coherence and the precise empty hom-set.
Nonidentity loops, malformed witnesses, label permutations, domain collapse and
valid C2 symmetry control must be exercised. State actual Lean coverage without
silently identifying a necessary-data structure with a full category library.
Only a successful bound receipt and independent review justify CLOSED.

**Excluded claims.** The obstruction concerns the registered tensor. It does
not prove that the underlying categories admit no alternative braided tensor.
It proves no AI architecture recovery or empirical universality result.
