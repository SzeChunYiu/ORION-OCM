# Inference parents and constructive scope

## Assimilate the established mechanisms first

[AEM1–7](raw/parents/ARCHITECTURE_EMERGENCE_THEOREM_V1.md) already separates
sampled candidates, complete compatible models, full fibers and attainment.
Its MSC/ARCH parents certify the compared selection under adequate coverage and a
common profile/cost interface. This correction adopts that distinction.
The mature bounded-optimality parent is
[Russell, sections3–6](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~russell/papers/aij-cnt.pdf):
the environment, machine and performance measure are part of the problem.

[PNIR](raw/parents/INTERVAL_COST_CORRECTION_V1.md) already requires sound upper
and lower bounds in one declared resource before transferring a cost verdict.
[The previous551 audit](raw/parents/PR551_MATRIX_AND_LINEAGE_DELTA_REVIEW_V1.md)
rejects treating a tested SEARCH configuration as a full-family supremum.
[The consumer repair](raw/parents/CONSUMER_CORRECTION_V1.md) distinguishes
port/scheduling evidence from causal coefficient use and campaign performance.
None of these scope boundaries changes because more row summaries arrive.

For sampling, [Hoeffding1963](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1963.10500830)
studies probability bounds under independent bounded summands.
It does not license independence from a public seed or convert a selected
registry into a population draw. We do not assign a confidence interval here:
the actual sampling and observation conditions are not authenticated.
A valid future fixed-sample design can fix the configuration/predicate and
target distribution before draws, retain complete outcomes and distinguish
sampling units; sequential/data-selected analyses need their own guarantees.
This is an applicability boundary, not a new concentration theorem.

The algorithmic parent is [Mouret–Clune2015, section3/Figure2](https://arxiv.org/html/1504.04909v1).
MAP-Elites evaluates a candidate before archive insertion and uses user-chosen
descriptor cells. Empty cells may remain unvisited. A cost descriptor is an
adaptation of this established mechanism, not a new search algorithm.
We apply only its finite archive-update semantics below.

## FEE-4 — finite observations do not identify a family ceiling

Fix a task, score range[0,1] and a family F containing observed subset S.
The observed maximum m=max_{s in S}q(s) supplies m<=sup_{f in F}q(f)<=1.
If an unmeasured admitted configuration has unconstrained score, completions
with that score0 and1 preserve all observed entries but can have different
suprema when m<1. The checker fixes E_fresh4, retaining its observed gradient_h2=.8229 and
gradient_h4=.8594 plus every other original observation in both abstract
completions. Their maxima on that fixed task are.8594 and1. It asserts no realized new learner.

Positive repair: a sound family-wide upper bound U and an attained observed
value m=U identify the supremum; exhaustive finite coverage also identifies it.
For m<=sup<=U with U−m<=epsilon, the observed candidate is an epsilon witness.
Coverage and bound validity must concern the same task, scoring rule and family.
These completions concern partial score registers that permit an unmeasured
member; they do not exhibit a member of a concretely fixed native family.
Any independently proved constant-family optimum remains available at its
declared ecology and scoring interface. This is the existing mechanism, not an inference from
five successful rows. Universal negative family claims need the upper side.

A baseline percentile alone supplies no family upper bound. Even under a
hypothetical margin24(q−b), b=7/8 and q=15/16 yield margin3/2 and q>.85.
This score-level completion shows that the baseline premise alone does not
force admissibility into a .2-percent low-baseline niche. It is not a physical
learner or a realization within the full native protocol.
If scores are bounded by1 in that same margin model, feasibility only requires
b<=23/24 for a margin of at least1; lower baselines alone are not sufficient.

## FEE-5 — specify the population before stating a frequency

A sampler over coefficient tuples induces its pushforward distribution over
semantic tasks. It need not be uniform on distinct tasks: three equiprobable
representations denoting(A,A,B) give taskA mass2/3, versus1/2 under uniform
sampling of distinct tasks. The finite control evaluates these two measures.
No assertion that the actual coefficient map has this exact duplication
pattern is needed for the general logical obstruction.

Without a justified sampling design, a count is a count over its registered
objects. A reported empirical percentile is not automatically an exact
population percentile. Purposeful and sampled sets can support different
questions; neither the direction nor amount of a prevalence difference is
determined merely by a different baseline distribution.
One can revive a population statement by defining the measure and the
predicate, establishing sampling/coverage, and retaining the relevant data.
The .2-percent report and selected learner outcomes do not discharge that work.

## FEE-6 — what cost bins constructively preserve

For a finite sequence of already evaluated candidates with fixed scores and
descriptor values, archive insertion retains a maximum-score candidate in
every visited cell, with the declared first-on-ties convention.
Induction: the empty cell accepts its first member; subsequent replacement
occurs exactly for an improved score. This inherited rule is all that is used.

If a cheap(.8,cost1) and expensive(.9,cost100) candidate share a former cell,
the former archive retains expensive. If a new cost axis separates their bins,
both are retained after those visits. If the binning does not separate them,
cheap is still discarded. Another better candidate in its new bin may also
replace it, so “every cheap variant survives” is false.

Both supplied evaluations have already cost101 in either archive.
Adding a descriptor cannot refund that cost or limit the cost of the next
candidate. It also changes the parents available for future selection,
so grammar preservation does not imply an unchanged search distribution.
This motivates a separately registered comparison with actual evaluation,
acquisition and lifetime costs; no such experiment is performed here.

All finite controls are symbolic arithmetic on supplied numbers. The result
is a source correction and inherited inference repair, not a mechanism
discovery, a completed cost experiment or a claim about all constructions.
