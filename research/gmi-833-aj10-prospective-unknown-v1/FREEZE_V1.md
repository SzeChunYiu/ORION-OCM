# AJ10 freeze — prospective UNKNOWN discovery protocol

This file freezes the prospective discovery contract before the held-out environment, search outcomes, taxonomy matching, or strongest-parent review are committed.

## Frozen generative substrate

The causal generator is a finite architecture-label-free Boolean mechanism space over three binary inputs. Two presentation families are permitted only as generic executable representations:

1. expression programs from terminals `x0,x1,x2,0,1` and generic `NOT/AND/OR`, with at most five operation nodes;
2. complete 8-row Boolean response tables.

Raw resource vectors are primary and have two coordinates `(evaluation_operations, persistent_description_cells)`. No scalar winner is assumed. Expression cost is `(operation_nodes,0)`; table cost is `(1,8)`.

Two materially different search procedures are frozen:
- breadth-by-operation expression enumeration plus complete table enumeration;
- semantic dynamic programming over truth vectors plus direct protected-table construction.

## Prospective separation

The held-out Boolean environment/truth vector is not part of this freeze and must be committed after this freeze. Search/evaluation may read only that protected I/O task and the generic mechanism rules above. It may not read AJ9 family names, family fingerprints, post-hoc taxonomy results, or strongest-parent adjudication.

Capability/resource/developmental profiles must be frozen before taxonomy matching. Developmental profile is `FIXED` for this finite microscope; no learning/development claim is made.

## Frozen post-hoc taxonomy and novelty protocol

After outcomes are frozen:
1. compare generated candidates against the already-frozen AJ9 K01–K11 registry;
2. candidates with no registered family match enter `UNKNOWN_MORPHOLOGY`;
3. only then perform strongest-parent review against broader known computational mechanisms;
4. distinguish novelty levels: implementation, morphology/architecture, algorithmic mechanism, computational class, capability profile;
5. if a strongest known parent explains the candidate, record `PARENT_REDUCED_KNOWN` and do not call it novel;
6. if evidence cannot identify one morphology or novelty cannot be established, preserve `UNKNOWN` rather than force a family/novelty claim.

A claimed novel form would require an independent replication. This microscope does not require a positive novelty claim; a negative/UNKNOWN result is scientifically valid.
