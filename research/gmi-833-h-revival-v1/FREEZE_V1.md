### 5.1 `V02` — a real linearly predictable response with a genuine intercept

Rows: every file of `D3` in ascending path order, decoded `latin-1`, split on
`\n`; every line with at least one character is a row, in order. This is the
row set `gmi-833-h-real-scale-classical-v1` registered for its `E_H03`; the
response and the design are different.

Design, twelve target-independent per-line character-class counts, each scaled
by a registered power of two: leading spaces `/16`, letters `/64`, spaces `/64`,
`_` `/8`, `=` `/4`, `(` and `)` `/8`, `[` and `]` `/8`, `,` `/8`, `.` `/8`,
`"` and `'` `/8`, `#` `/4`, digits `0`–`9` `/8`.

Response: `y = (len − 79) / 64`, where `len` is the byte length of the line
and 79 is the line-length limit of PEP 8 — an external constant, not a fitted
one. The decision label is `[y > 0]`: the line exceeds the PEP 8 limit.

Why this ecology answers the attribution. The line length is the sum of the
twelve counted classes plus the count of every other byte, so the response is
affine in the design up to a non-negative residual with strictly positive
mean, and the constant `−79/64` guarantees the intercept is not zero. There is
an intercept to identify, which `F02` did not have. The response is **not**
exactly attainable — the uncounted bytes vary across lines — so the squared
error is strictly positive and the gate cannot be won by an identity.

Protected interface: the exact held-out sum of squared errors, and the exact
held-out count of decisions `STEP(OUT_t) != [y_t > 0]`, both in `Fraction`.

### 5.2 `V03` — a real over-dispersed count response under its own likelihood

Rows and design: exactly the `F03` construction of
`gmi-833-h-real-scale-revival-v1` `FREEZE_V1.md` section 4 — tokenise every
file of `D3` with `[A-Za-z_][A-Za-z0-9_]*`; a row is a `(token, file)` pair
occurring at least once, ordered by file path then first-occurrence offset;
the response is the exact occurrence count of that token in that file, an
integer `>= 1` by construction; the design is the twelve spelling-and-file
columns (token length, leading-underscore, trailing-underscore, uppercase
count, lowercase count, digit count, underscore count, vowel count,
first-character-is-underscore, file bytes, file lines, file distinct tokens),
each scaled by the smallest power of two strictly greater than the column's
maximum absolute value over all rows. The construction is carried over
unchanged because the attribution is to the loss and to nothing else; a
changed ecology would confound the lever.

**Registered response family and loss.** The response is a count. The
registered loss is the Poisson deviance

```
D(mu; y) = 2 * sum_i [ y_i * ln(y_i / mu_i) − (y_i − mu_i) ]
```

with `y ln(y/mu) := 0` when `y = 0`, and with `D = +inf` whenever any `mu_i
<= 0` on the rows scored: a predicted mean outside the support of the
registered family is inadmissible under its own likelihood, and the loss says
so without a separate rule. `D` is the negative log-likelihood of the Poisson
family up to the saturated term, which is constant across arms.

This loss is part of the ecology's specification, which `R01` already
licenses the derivation to read. It names no link. Every candidate program of
`G_S` is fitted and ranked under it by one generic routine; the grammar
contains no exponential and receives none, so whatever link the search
recovers is one it built from `ADD MUL NEG ABS STEP RECIP`.

On the tension `RSR-4` named — that a proper scoring rule is family
information — this freeze takes the position the row forces: a generalized
linear model is defined by a link **and** a likelihood; the likelihood is what
the ecology measures, the link is what the search must recover; measuring the
row under squared error measured a different family. That position is stated
here so it can be disagreed with, and `PARENT_LEDGER.md` records it as the
residual.

Protected interface: certified deviance comparisons on the held-out slice
(Section 9), and the exact held-out count of non-positive predicted means per
arm.

### 6.2 `G_H` for the derivational scopes, with the one classifier change

Exactly `gmi-833-h-successor-constructs-v1` `FREEZE_V1.md` section 4 and its

operation, leaf, budget or construct is added or removed.**

The **one** change is to classifier rule 2. Read on the probe grid, never from
a family label:

```
 1 kind != NONE and head depends on RESP                  -> RESPONSE_SPACE_SEARCH
 2 some fold bank combines with MUL AND that bank is
   head-read                                              -> MULTIPLICATIVE_ACCUMULATION
 3 ... 11 unchanged
```

A bank is **head-read** when the program's output depends on that bank's fold
value: at `L = 1`, bank `k` is head-read iff the head depends on `S_k`; at
`L = 2`, the stage-1 bank is head-read iff `BODY2` depends on `U` and the head
depends on `S1`, and the stage-2 bank iff the head depends on `S1`. A program
whose `MUL` bank feeds nothing is not a multiplicative accumulation; it is
whatever its head makes it, and rules 3–11 name it.

The grammar digest incorporates the classifier rule text so that `G_H` here
has a different digest from the parent's — it *is* a different scope — and is
recorded before and after every search, control, null and hostile.

## 8. The two derivational levers, stated exactly

### 8.1 The matcher pool

At each derivational scope the **matcher pool** `M` is the set of every
well-formed `G_H` program of charged cost at most `B_MAX = 10` that reproduces
the response on every row of the search slice exactly, in `Fraction`
arithmetic, with the delay cell reset to `0` at the first search row. The pool
is enumerated exhaustively — no cost stratum is skipped once a match is found —
and its size is reported per cost. The recovered program is the cheapest pool
member, ties broken by the registered rendering order (parent A8).

### 8.2 The constructed held-out slice

A second registered stream, `c_0 = 20260921`, same recurrence, same channel
maps as the scope, supplies **candidate rows** `k = 0, 1, 2, ...`, at most
480. At `SIGMA_E34` candidate row 0 has its `A` and `P` overridden to the
pinned held-out tie row above and is **always** the first held-out row.

The held-out slice is built by one pass over the candidates, before any
candidate's response is read, from the pool's *outputs* alone. Every pool
member starts with delay state `0`. At candidate `k`, each member emits its
output given its current state. If the emitted outputs are **not all equal**,
row `k` is appended to the held-out slice and every member advances its state
with its own output; otherwise the row is skipped and no state moves. The pass
stops when 12 rows have been appended or the candidates are exhausted.

Only then are the held-out responses computed from the functional and
compared. This construction reads programs and channels, never responses, so
the held-out slice is chosen blind to the answer; a test asserts that the
construction function receives no response vector.

### 8.3 What `R08` means here, and the structural branch

`R08` is **earned** at a scope iff (i) the recovered program reproduces every
row of the constructed held-out slice exactly, and (ii) at least one pool
member fails the held-out slice — the slice discriminated. Both counts are
reported whichever way they fall.

If the pass appends **no** row — every pool member agreed on every one of the
480 candidates — then no held-out slice drawn from the registered channel
domain can discriminate the pool, and `R08` is reported
`NOT_EARNED_POOL_EXTENSIONALLY_EQUAL_ON_CANDIDATE_DOMAIN`. That outcome is
then **proved in two tiers, not assumed**: (a) every pool member is reduced by
the registered normal form of Section 8.4, and the count of members whose
normal form equals the recovered program's is reported as *provably equal*;
(b) the remainder, if any, is reported as *empirically equal on 480 candidate
rows and not provably equal*, with its members listed. In that case the
held-out slice for the sanity check (i) is candidate rows `0..11`, and (i) is
still required.

If the pass appends between 1 and 11 rows the slice is used as constructed.

### 8.4 The registered normal form

A syntactic rewriting to a canonical tree, applied to every body, second-stage
body and head: commutative operands of `ADD` and `MUL` sorted by rendering;
`ADD(x, C0) -> x`; `MUL(x, C1) -> x`; `MUL(x, C0) -> C0`; `NEG(NEG(x)) -> x`;
`NEG(C0) -> C0`; `ABS(ABS(x)) -> ABS(x)`; `ABS(C0) -> C0`; `ABS(C1) -> C1`;
`STEP(STEP(x)) -> STEP(x)`; `STEP(C0) -> C0`; `STEP(C1) -> C1`;
`RECIP(RECIP(x)) -> x`; `RECIP(C0) -> C0`; `RECIP(C1) -> C1`; applied to a
fixed point; together with the bank swap at `r = 2`. Every rule is an identity
of the exact semantics on all of `Q`, and a test checks each on the probe grid.
Two programs with one normal form are equal on every input. No rule uses a
fact about the channel domain, so domain-only equalities (such as `ABS(ARG) =
ARG` where `A_i >= 0`) are deliberately **not** provable by it and land in tier
(b).

### 8.5 The base-rate null under the revised classifier

The registered null is the parent's: the exact structural-class census of the
whole well-formed enumeration, classified by the Section 6.2 classifier.
Two populations are counted and both are reported:

- the census up to the **recovered cost** — the programs the search actually
  examined, the parent's adjudicating population, which for a scope whose
  recovered cost equals the parent's is the same *set of programs* under a
  different classifier; and
- the census up to **`B_MAX`** — a population the revealing run never counted.

*Applicability.* The recovered class must have a strictly positive count in
both censuses; a null that cannot produce the class tests nothing and fails
the run.

*The prediction, at every derivational scope.* The recovered class is **not**
the modal class and holds strictly below one half, in **both** censuses. `R04`
is earned only if both hold and the `R01` class prediction held. This is
strictly harder than the parent's single-census rule, and it is registered
before any census is run.

## 9. Arithmetic rule and the certified deviance comparison

Every reported quantity is an integer or a `fractions.Fraction`, except where
a quantity is a **certified interval** as defined here. Floats appear only
inside fitting and screening routines. A fitting routine's output is
rationalised by the fixed-denominator operator of
`gmi-833-h-real-scale-revival-v1` `FREEZE_V1_ARITHMETIC_ADDENDUM.md`,
`rationalise(x) = Fraction(round(x * 10**9), 10**9)`, applied identically to
every arm, before it is used; from then on it is data. No float enters any
comparison, count, loss or claim.

The Poisson deviance contains logarithms and is not rational. Every deviance
claim is therefore a **certified interval**: for rational `mu > 0` the
logarithm is bracketed by two rationals `[lo, hi]` obtained from a correctly
rounded 40-digit decimal logarithm widened by one unit in the last place on
each side; sums of brackets are exact sums of `Fraction`s; a difference of two
deviances is a rational interval; a comparison is **decided** only if that
interval excludes zero, and is reported `UNDECIDED` otherwise, which counts as
a failed prediction. The `y ln y` terms cancel exactly in every difference
and are never bracketed. A hostile checks that a bracket that does not
contain its logarithm is caught, using the correctly rounded exponential of
its endpoints. This is the banded-classification discipline of
`gmi-833-capability-predictor-evaluation-v1` `FREEZE_V3_ADDENDUM.md` applied
to a transcendental loss: a claim is made only outside the band where the
arithmetic could be wrong.

## 10. What `real-scale` means here — the registered definition

Adopted unchanged in substance from `gmi-833-h-real-scale-classical-v1`
`FREEZE_V1.md` section 7, because a definition that moves with the package it
certifies certifies nothing. A test at scope `sigma` is **real-scale** exactly

`n_fit >= 100,000` and `n_held >= 20,000` after the slice rule; at least two
arms fitted at that scale on the same slices with the same rationalisation and
charged cost model; every claimed number computed on the held-out slice only,
after the structure is fixed, in exact arithmetic or as a certified interval;
resource accounting charged at the real `n`, `d` and index-set size.

**Falsifier of the definition itself.** If any claimed number at a scope can be
reproduced from fewer than 100,000 real fitted rows, or from bytes not covered
by a Section-5 digest, the real-scale certificate at that scope is void.

**What it does not license.** Frontier-model or deep-network claims; any
statement about data outside `D2`–`D3`; any statement about ecologies other

none of those either.

## 11. The search procedure, identical at both real-scale scopes and both controls

1. Enumerate `BODY` and `HEAD` exhaustively and quotient each by its exact
   denotation on the probe grid, once, before any ecology is loaded.
2. **Screen**: fit every enumerated pair by the generic routine on the first
   400 rows of the ranking-fit part at a reduced iteration budget, rank by the
   scope's registered loss, keep the best 40.
3. **Rank**: refit each survivor on the whole ranking-fit part and score it on
   the ranking-score part it has not seen. Order by (loss finite, loss, node
   count, `BODY` rendering, `HEAD` rendering). Under the deviance an
   inadmissible candidate has infinite loss and therefore ranks last; no
   separate admissibility rule exists or is needed.
4. Fit the winner and every registered comparison arm on the fit slice,
   rationalise, and evaluate on the held-out slice.

**The generic fitting routine**, one for every candidate, every scope and
every control, receiving no family information: when the prediction is
jointly affine in the parameters and the loss is squared error, closed-form
least squares with a registered ridge of `1e-8`; otherwise first-order descent
on the loss with a central-difference gradient and backtracking line search,
started from the best-loss point among a registered start set — the zero
vector, the least-squares point when the prediction is jointly affine in the
parameters, and six draws from a seeded (`20260919`) standard normal — with a
fixed iteration budget identical for every candidate. Under the deviance a
start whose loss is infinite is simply never the best start.

No step receives a family name, a family identifier, or a target-specific
candidate list. The structural class is attached after step 3 by the
classifier, which reads only the winning expression trees.
