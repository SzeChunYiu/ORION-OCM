## What is NOT claimed novel

Not novel: any of the four equivalence notions; the Myhill-Nerode theorem; bisimulation;
clone/term equivalence; compiler correctness; the partition lattice. Not novel: that
different presentations can present the same theory — that is the AG3 row's own premise and
Lawvere's result.

## The residual contribution

1. The exact inclusion matrix between the four strengths over one registered finite universe,
   with a separating pair for every strict inclusion (`AG3L-1`).
2. The finding that bounded mutual simulation of the external actions does not imply equality
   of the presented object — `367,372` counterexample pairs and a two-state minimal one
   (`AG3L-2`).
3. The identification of fixed-overhead compiler equivalence as a **tolerance** rather than an
   equivalence, with an explicit non-transitive triple at `k = 1` (`AG3L-3`).
4. The generated lattice: five elements, one new, not a chain, with `k* = 2` and the ladder
   saturating at `k = 2` so the parent's factor `5` is not strict at this scope (`AG3L-4`).
5. A constructed witness for the one named strength that had none in the merged corpus —
   definitional/term equivalence — placed at its exact level (`AG3L-5`).

## Compatibility with the parents' own forbidden sets

`gmi-833-parent-equivalence-v1` forbids `ALL_GMI_EQUIVALENCE_IS_MYHILL_NERODE`. This package
does not assert it; it is the strongest available evidence against it, because model
equivalence is computed here as the **coarsest** of four distinct levels with three strictly
finer ones certified below it. `gmi-833-g0-grammar-bias-v1` forbids `ALL_GRAMMARS_EQUIVALENT`
and `GRAMMAR_REPRESENTATION_INVARIANT_UNIVERSALLY`; the computed order refutes both at this
scope rather than asserting them. `gmi-833-aj5-g0-lowering-v1` forbids
`COMPILER_MAKES_SEARCH_BIAS_INVARIANT`; it is carried into this package's own forbidden list.
