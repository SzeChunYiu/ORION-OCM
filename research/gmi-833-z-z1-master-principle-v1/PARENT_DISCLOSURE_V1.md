# Z1 — parent ownership disclosure

## What is not claimed novel

**`IC-1` is not new mathematics.** The statement that minimisers of a scalarised
objective `error + lambda * resource` trace the lower convex envelope of the
achievable set, and that the switching thresholds are the envelope's supporting
slopes, is standard. It is the Lagrangian / generalised-multiplier method, it is
the convexification step in rate-distortion theory, and it is the scalarisation
theorem of multi-objective optimisation.

- Everett, H., "Generalized Lagrange multiplier method for solving problems of
  optimum allocation of resources", *Operations Research* 11(3):399-417, 1963,
  doi:10.1287/opre.11.3.399 — the multiplier sweep and the fact that it can only
  reach convex-envelope points.
- Geoffrion, A. M., "Proper efficiency and the theory of vector maximization",
  *Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications* 22(3):618-630, 1968,
  doi:10.1016/0022-247X(68)90201-1 — which Pareto points a linear scalarisation
  can and cannot recover.
- Shannon, C. E., "Coding theorems for a discrete source with a fidelity
  criterion", *IRE National Convention Record* 4:142-163, 1959; Berger, T.,
  *Rate Distortion Theory: A Mathematical Basis for Data Compression*,
  Prentice-Hall, 1971 — the rate-distortion Lagrangian and the convexity of `R(D)`.
- Rissanen, J., "Modeling by shortest data description", *Automatica*
  14(5):465-471, 1978, doi:10.1016/0005-1098(78)90005-5; Grünwald, P. D.,
  *The Minimum Description Length Principle*, MIT Press, 2007 — the strongest
  parent for "a principle that trades fit against description cost", and the one
  a reader will reach for first.
- Dantzig, G. B., "Discrete-variable extremum problems", *Operations Research*
  5(2):266-288, 1957, doi:10.1287/opre.5.2.266 — the greedy/LP-relaxation
  reading of the same envelope in the knapsack setting.
- Simon, H. A., "A behavioral model of rational choice", *Quarterly Journal of
  Economics* 69(1):99-118, 1955, doi:10.2307/1884852; Russell, S. and Subramanian,
  D., "Provably bounded-optimal agents", *JAIR* 2:575-609, 1994,
  doi:10.1613/jair.133 — bounded and resource rationality, the parent family the
  Z6 lane already showed to be observationally equivalent to the registered law at
  its scope.

**The registered laws being unified are also not ours.** `lambda* = eta*p/2` and
`lambda*(A) = eta*p*(1 - 1/A)` are `gmi-833-z-z5-critical-phenomena-v1` `CP-2`
and `CP-5`; `lambda* = eta*p*R0` is `gmi-833-z-z6-discrimination-v1` `DS-5`; the
three-level ladder prediction is `gmi-833-z-z13-property-prediction-freeze-v1`.
All three live on sibling branches (PRs #1034, #1039), not on `main`, and are
cited as parents. Their numbers are **re-derived here from scratch** rather than
imported, which is why the `16 / 729 / 65536` table counts and the `1/2, 2/3, 3/4`
floors appear again in this package's own receipt.

   **zero** free theoretical constants — and the exact identification of that

   constant in the examined corpus (`IC-4`).

principle. No claim that `IC-1` explains anything outside additive costs with a
