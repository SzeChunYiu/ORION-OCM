elaborated theorem, captures its independent type/universes and exposed reference

## Remaining commissioning boundary

The source/compiler/module-artifact correspondence and evaluator build profile
remain trusted external obligations. A range can be authored by source; range
uniqueness plus a header check is not universal source attestation. The capture
binary imports registered compiled material with loadExts=false, but its host
development executions do not qualify the future isolated acquisition/build or
capture profile. Fixture modules are absent from the generic link-object list;
this alone does not establish the complete dynamic execution closure.

The manifest separately records borrowed source, new linked artifacts, toolchain
and load paths. No old 47-case receipt is reused for this changed adapter, and no
old environment source was changed. No actual corpus assignment was opened,
built or exported by these tests. Real coverage still requires the independent
resource/offline profile and exact acquired source/build provenance.

Timing is per test process or recorded process envelope; it is not additive
lifetime cost, a speedup, complete CPU/RSS accounting, learning or locality.
No whole-OCM neural-free, FLT or novelty result follows.
