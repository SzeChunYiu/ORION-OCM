# AJ9b strongest-parent / novelty ledger

This tranche does **not** claim invention of threshold networks, neural computation, generic program synthesis, or architecture search.

## Strong parents

- **McCulloch & Pitts (1943), _A Logical Calculus of the Ideas Immanent in Nervous Activity_**: classical threshold-unit/network parent for logical computation with weighted aggregation and thresholding.
- **Rumelhart, Hinton & Williams (1986), _Learning representations by back-propagating errors_, Nature 323:533–536, DOI 10.1038/323533a0**: classical learning/training parent. AJ9b does not claim its learning result.
- **LeCun, Bottou, Bengio & Haffner (1998), _Gradient-Based Learning Applied to Document Recognition_**: strong parent for multilayer/shared local neural architectures; not reclaimed here.
- **Real, Liang, So & Le (2020), _AutoML-Zero: Evolving Machine Learning Algorithms From Scratch_, ICML/PMLR 119:8007–8019**: especially close parent. AutoML-Zero searched a generic space of basic mathematical operations and rediscovered two-layer neural networks trained by backpropagation. AJ9b therefore cannot claim that generic low-level search recovering a neural family is novel in itself.
- **Enumerative/program synthesis and superoptimization literature** owns bottom-up finite program search, semantic deduplication and cost-bounded synthesis techniques.
- **Threshold logic / Boolean circuit theory** owns the XOR/threshold expressibility facts at this scale.

## GMI residual claimed here

The scoped residual is the integration of the already-frozen AJ operational-bottom programme with a **pre-frozen leave-family-out benchmark**, explicit Git-custodied no-smuggling separation, two search/presentation routes, raw-resource accounting, and a post-hoc family adjudicator that cannot influence generation.

The result is therefore a **validation protocol + bounded recovery result**, not a new theorem about threshold networks.

`AJ9B_K01_BLIND_STRUCTURAL_RECOVERY_AT_FROZEN_FINITE_SCOPE`

Forbidden novelty claims: first automatic neural discovery, first low-level architecture search, neural-learning derivation, backpropagation rediscovery, universal architecture derivation, or full GMI.
