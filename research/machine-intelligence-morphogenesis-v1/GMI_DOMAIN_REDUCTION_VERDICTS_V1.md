# GMI Domain Reduction Verdicts v1

Status: **HOSTILE REDUCTION AUDIT / PLAIN TECHNICAL LABELS**

Status date: 2026-09-12.

Purpose:

> Record why proposed carrier/operator families do or do not currently justify structural-domain status. A negative verdict is a scientific result and should remove duplicate theory rather than be defended rhetorically.

---

# 1. Verdict scale

```text
KEEP-OPEN       parent separation genuinely unresolved at registered overhead
KINGDOM         useful structural family, but reduced to a broader domain
MECHANISM       important operator/state law inside existing domains
SUBSTRATE       mainly physical implementation/resource effect
REDUNDANT       already implied by an existing GMI theorem/state variable
KNOWN-DOMAIN    established nonclassical or external paradigm; calibration target, not novelty
```

---

# 2. Relational local-to-global state

Verdict: **MECHANISM / KINGDOM, not a new H2/H3 domain on current exact families**.

Why it is real:

- local sections can have global compatibility obstructions;
- arbitrary graph-XOR systems have `beta_1=m-n+c` independent obstruction coordinates;
- the exact microscope confirms the obstruction and solution-count laws.

Why it is not enough:

- a spanning-forest symbolic algorithm extracts the same obstruction vector in `O(n+m)` time;
- modern sheaf-theoretic distributed-computing work explicitly treats solutions as global sections and cohomology as obstruction structure, so the mathematical mechanism has strong prior parents;
- no protected burden separation from CSP/program/message-passing parents is established.

Promotion condition:

> find an obligation family where local-section/gluing representation has a frozen H0-H2 lifecycle law that no symbolic/distributed parent can match.

# 3. Constructive closure state

Verdict: **KEEP-OPEN only at tight H0-H2 lifecycle resolution; reduced at H3/H4 for explicit monotone systems**.

Why it is real:

- identical present behavior does not determine future construction capability;
- `k` independent future build capabilities require `k` bits of developmental state;
- closure and prerequisite depth exactly predict reachability and unlimited-parallelism deadline capability.

Why it is not yet a new domain:

- explicit monotone closure is computable by linear-time symbolic forward chaining;
- classical construction theory, universal constructors, self-reproducing automata and autocatalytic-set theory are strong prior parents;
- a D8/D4 system can explicitly store the constructor rules/closure.

What remains genuinely promising:

```text
physical materialization where constructed components change future resource geometry
local repair/construction without global state rewrite
parallel construction whose wall-clock/communication law is native
construction that creates new sensing/actuation channels rather than only software modules
```

Promotion requires a lower bound against compiled D4/D8 lifecycle cost, not another closure theorem.

# 4. Event partial-order state

Verdict: **KINGDOM/MECHANISM inside symbolic/distributed computation**.

Positive result:

- partial-order state can represent `n!` interleavings or `2^n` snapshot states with `O(n)` event nodes on independent-event families.

Reduction:

- event structures, Petri-net unfoldings and process formalisms already provide the native representation;
- the carrier is itself a finite symbolic relational object;
- translation into D4 is essentially representational reminting rather than a costly simulation.

Do not spend P0 domain-novelty budget here unless a new lifecycle lower bound appears.

# 5. Quotient/invariant state

Verdict: **REDUNDANT as a domain; foundational GMI mechanism**.

Reason:

- task-relative quotienting is already required by the semantic quotient theorem;
- the exact invariant compression result is an instance of that theorem;
- no new carrier/operator variable is needed.

Keep topological/algebraic invariants as possible implementations of semantic quotient state, not a separate domain by default.

# 6. Hyperdimensional vector algebra

Verdict: **KNOWN KINGDOM/FRAMEWORK; domain separation open only at tight resource resolution**.

Reasons against novelty:

- extensive HDC/VSA literature already exists;
- HDC/VSA is classically computable and even computationally universal in known formulations;
- strong neural, symbolic and associative-memory reductions/relationships exist.

Why it remains important:

- binding/bundling/permutation give a distinct native algebra;
- high-dimensional concentration yields precise capacity/noise laws;
- those laws are valuable calibration tests for GMI capability prediction.

GMI goal is to derive and predict its niche, not claim invention.

# 7. Local-field / cellular state

Verdict: **probable D6/D7 kingdom; KEEP-OPEN at H0-H2 only**.

Reasons against domain status:

- cellular automata and neural cellular automata are established;
- a local field with computable update rule is classically simulable;
- global behavior can often be typed as dynamical-state plus distributed communication.

Hard weakness law:

> finite-radius local updates have a finite causal light cone. A site cannot depend on information initially farther than `r*t` after `t` synchronous rounds.

Thus global obligations impose unavoidable latency unless the substrate includes long-range communication or changes topology.

Potential positive niche:

- local damage/repair where the desired response is itself local and massive centralized communication would be wasteful.

---

# 8. Energy-landscape relaxation

Verdict: **probable search/dynamical kingdom or substrate**.

Reasons against domain status:

- Ising/annealing/oscillator formulations have strong optimization/search parents;
- special hardware may change constants without changing the structural carrier class.

Hard weakness law:

> a native operator restricted to strictly energy-decreasing local moves cannot escape a strict non-global local minimum.

Therefore any claim of general intelligence from pure local relaxation is false without nonlocal moves, temperature/noise, tunneling, restarts or morphology change.

Those added mechanisms must be charged separately.

---

# 9. Population-hereditary state

Verdict: **probable D5/D8 kingdom; KEEP-OPEN only if lineage state yields an unreduced history law**.

Hard weakness law:

> selection alone cannot create a variant outside the support of the current population.

Therefore discovery requires mutation, recombination, immigration, constructive operators or another novelty source. A population system with no novelty operator has a hard capability ceiling determined by initial support.

Promotion requires showing that a population/lineage representation predicts future capability with materially lower lifecycle burden than a compressed search/morphogenesis state.

---

# 10. Reaction-network state

Verdict: **probable physical/dynamical kingdom; H0-H2 in-situ niche open**.

Reasons against domain status:

- chemical reaction networks have explicit classical mathematical descriptions;
- broad CRN models have known computational-universality results under some semantics;
- exact stable deterministic CRN computation can also have strong restrictions, so semantics matter critically.

Possible niche:

> sensing and computation occur in the same physical medium, avoiding digitization/transduction costs.

Any experiment must therefore include molecular preparation, sensing, reset, readout, stochastic error and latency. Ignoring I/O would manufacture a fake domain advantage.

---

# 11. Quantum-coherent state

Verdict: **KNOWN nonclassical calibration domain, not novel**.

Unlike the classical candidates, quantum state evolution may support complexity separations not known to be efficiently classically simulable. But machine-intelligence advantage remains task- and I/O-dependent.

GMI must predict both:

```text
native quantum-data/oracle regimes where quantum structure matters
classical-data regimes where state preparation/readout/error correction erase the benefit
```

Do not transfer algorithmic quantum speedups into general intelligence claims without end-to-end accounting.

---

# 12. Research allocation rule

Demote a candidate when:

1. its carrier is a direct bounded encoding in a stronger parent;
2. its native operators compile with the accepted overhead;
3. its natural complexity coordinate adds no held-out predictive power;
4. its apparent advantage comes from comparing against a deliberately weak parent;
5. its advantage disappears after full I/O/search/verification/materialization cost.

Increase research priority only when the parent-separation atom remains unresolved after the strongest known compiler is implemented.

Current highest-value open directions:

```text
constructive closure under physical/material lifecycle constraints
hyperdimensional capacity/resource calibration (known, not novel)
local-field repair versus communication lower bounds
nonclassical quantum calibration
neutral search for a carrier/operator law whose complexity coordinate cannot be absorbed into current domains
```
