# Representation-phase parent no-go v1

Status: **direct parent subtraction for resource-conditioned representation / hierarchy phase claims.**

## Claim attacked

A broad Track-B claim might be:

> as resource constraints or task relevance change, an intelligent system undergoes phase transitions to different internal representations/hierarchies.

That phenomenon already has strong information-theoretic parents.

## Information Bottleneck

The Information Bottleneck formalism optimizes a trade-off between representation compression and predictive relevance:

\[
I(X;Z)-\beta I(Y;Z).
\]

Later work explicitly analyzes **phase transitions** in the optimum as `beta` changes, including discontinuous changes in learned representation/prediction behavior and thresholds for learnability.

Anchors:

- Wu & Fischer, **Phase Transitions for the Information Bottleneck in Representation Learning**, ICLR 2020, https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.01878
- Wu, Fischer, Chuang & Tegmark, **Learnability for the Information Bottleneck**, UAI/PMLR 115 (2020), https://proceedings.mlr.press/v115/wu20b.html
- Still et al. / predictive information-bottleneck and resource-constrained optimal prediction literature; an explicit example of representation-dimensional phase changes appears in **Optimal prediction with resource constraints using the information bottleneck**, PLoS Computational Biology 2021, DOI `10.1371/journal.pcbi.1008743`.

## Bounded rationality and hierarchy

Information-theoretic bounded-rationality work models computation/information processing as a limited resource and derives abstractions, specialization and hierarchical decision organizations from expected-utility versus information-cost tradeoffs.

Anchor:

- Genewein, Leibfried, Grau-Moya & Braun, **Bounded Rationality, Abstraction, and Hierarchical Decision-Making: An Information-Theoretic Optimality Principle**, Frontiers in Robotics and AI 2:27 (2015), DOI `10.3389/frobt.2015.00027`, https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/robotics-and-ai/articles/10.3389/frobt.2015.00027/full

Related systems of bounded-rational agents derive specialization/division of labor from information constraints and priors.

## Predictive/minimal-state connection

Computational mechanics / causal-state theory and predictive-state representations already study minimal predictive representations. Shalizi & Crutchfield explicitly discuss relationships between Information Bottleneck, causal states and statistical relevance bases.

Thus several Track-B themes already meet in parent literature:

```text
task relevance
+ compression/resource constraint
-> abstraction / representation
-> possible phase transition / hierarchy / specialization
```

## What this kills

Track B gets no novelty credit for:

```text
resource price changes representation complexity
compression/relevance tradeoff causes discrete representation changes
limited information processing produces hierarchy/specialization
minimal predictive state exists under a fixed predictive obligation
```

These are already mature theory families.

## Stronger Track-B residual

The Track-B target must be **developmental and morphology-generative**, not merely representational:

1. the state/representation itself may change by learning, library growth, topology change, operator invention or consolidation;
2. the theory must charge acquisition/update/maintenance/verification, not only representation information rate;
3. the predicted phase should concern a complete developmental morphology/factorization and its future learning geometry;
4. predictions must span more than one realization family and beat IB/resource-rational/family-specific baselines;
5. ideally the candidate organizations are generated from a neutral basis rather than selected from a hand-authored portfolio.

A candidate question is therefore:

\[
\text{ecology dependency + developmental update structure + lifetime resources}
\to
\text{morphology/factorization frontier}
\]

rather than simply

\[
\text{compression price}\to\text{representation dimension}.
\]

## Important possibility

It may turn out that the best Track-B theory is merely a synthesis of:

```text
rate-distortion / IB for representation
+ algorithm selection for method choice
+ drift/SSP for acquisition dynamics
+ factored models/modularity for structure
+ meta-learning for changing update laws
```

If this parent product predicts the registered morphology phases as well as any proposed GMI law, the correct terminal is:

