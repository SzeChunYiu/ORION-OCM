# General Machine-Intelligence Theory Parent Atlas v1

Status: **mandatory subtraction layer** for Track B. This is not an endorsement of any one parent theory.

A project seeking a “general machine-intelligence theory” must subtract theories of general intelligence, adaptive regulation and predictive state—not only architectures such as neural networks.

---

## T0 — Universal Artificial Intelligence / AIXI

Core object: ideal Bayesian sequential decision agent over an algorithmic mixture of computable environments, maximizing expected future reward.

Owns / strongly threatens:

```text
claim to be the first formal general-agent theory
universal induction + sequential decision as a general intelligence ideal
architecture-independent optimal-agent framing under its assumptions
```

Limits relevant to Track B:

```text
ideal AIXI is incomputable
resource-bounded practical morphology is not resolved by the ideal alone
architecture/development/maintenance frontiers are not supplied automatically
```

Track-B residual can only be a **computable/resource-bounded morphogenesis theory**, not “a formal general agent exists.”

---

## T1 — Legg-Hutter Universal Intelligence

Core object: expected performance of an agent over an environment distribution weighted by algorithmic complexity.

Owns:

```text
a broad formal measure applicable across machine agents
architecture-independent intelligence evaluation at an idealized level
```

Threat:

Track B must not claim novelty merely for defining intelligence by performance across environments.

Residual:

```text
mechanism / morphology / developmental law
```

rather than another score.

---

## T2 — NARS / intelligence under insufficient knowledge and resources

NARS explicitly frames intelligence as adaptation while operating with insufficient knowledge and computational resources, and provides a formal reasoning system and implementations.

Owns / threatens:

```text
resource-bounded adaptation as a core principle of intelligence
non-axiomatic reasoning under incomplete evidence
open-ended task handling as a design goal
```

Track-B implication:

“Intelligence is adaptation under resource constraints” is not enough as our field-level novelty. Track B must provide a stronger predictive generative result about morphology/development.

---

## T3 — Soar / unified cognitive architecture

Soar's architectural hypothesis casts deliberate goal-directed behavior as selecting and applying operators to states, with working memory, production memory, preferences, learning and additional memory systems.

Historically, Soar's architecture also demonstrates an important negative lesson: an initially minimal set of mechanisms was extended with semantic/episodic memory and reinforcement learning as complex tasks exposed insufficiencies.

Owns / threatens:

```text
state + operator cycle
working/production memory
operator selection / impasses
chunk learning
minimal-cognitive-architecture ambitions
```

Track-B implication:

A general basis must not merely rediscover the production-system/operator cycle. Soar is also an empirical precedent that “minimal architecture” may expand under domain pressure.

---

## T4 — OpenCog / Hyperon / cognitive synergy

Hyperon explicitly aims to integrate multiple AI paradigms and cognitive algorithms in a common metagraph/MeTTa framework, including logic, probabilistic reasoning, evolutionary learning, functional/process-calculus ideas and external neural/SAT donors.

Owns / threatens:

```text
one shared substrate hosting heterogeneous cognitive algorithms
metagraph-based general cognition
cognitive synergy / multi-paradigm integration as a high-level AGI strategy
reflective/self-modifying cognitive programming
```

Track-B residual cannot be “multiple intelligence forms can live inside one system.”

A stronger residual is:

```text
predicting why/when forms arise and which frontier they occupy
```

from a parent-subtracted low-level basis.

---

## T5 — Cybernetics: requisite variety and the Good Regulator theorem

Ashby's cybernetics gives formal constraints on regulation under disturbances. Conant & Ashby show, under their stated conditions, that a maximally successful/simple regulator must embody a model isomorphic to the regulated system.

Owns / threatens:

```text
intelligent/adaptive regulation requires sufficient variety
successful efficient regulation may require an internal model under theorem assumptions
agent structure must be related to environmental/control structure
```

Track-B implication:

The ecology-dependent morphology idea has deep cybernetic parents. We should use these results to derive **constraints on morphology capacity/model structure**, not claim “good intelligence builds world models” as new.

Potential Track-B theorem direction:

```text
registered ecology distinguishability / disturbance variety
-> lower bound on morphology state/model variety
-> representation insufficiency boundary
```

with assumptions stated exactly.

---

## T6 — Free-Energy Principle / active-inference family

Friston's free-energy framework attempts to unify perception, learning and action through optimization of variational free energy / surprise bounds using generative models.

Owns / threatens:

```text
broad unification of perception + learning + action via one optimization principle
agent behavior as inference/generative-model adaptation
links among Bayesian inference, predictive coding, control and learning
```

Track-B implication:

A morphology theory based solely on “agents minimize prediction error/free energy” would collide with a major existing unification programme.

Residual question:

Does a resource-bounded morphogenesis law predict **structural forms and phase transitions** not implied by the free-energy objective alone?

---

## T7 — Computational Mechanics / epsilon-machines

Computational mechanics constructs causal states—equivalence classes of histories with the same predictive distribution—and proves minimality/optimality properties for the epsilon-machine predictive representation under its framework.

Owns / threatens:

```text
minimal predictive state representation
uniqueness/optimality of predictive causal-state models under stated conditions
information-theoretic structural complexity of a process
```

Track-B implication:

If we ask “what is the minimal internal state needed for prediction?”, there is already mature theory.

Residual:

```text
adaptive/action-conditioned morphology generation + developmental/resource laws
```

rather than static predictive-state minimality.

---

## T8 — Predictive State Representations (PSRs)

PSRs represent controlled dynamical-system state using action-conditional predictions of future observations rather than latent hidden-state identities. Linear PSR results show representational relationships to POMDPs; nonlinear PSRs can be more compact in some systems.

Owns / threatens:

```text
predictive observables as state
representation alternatives to hidden-state models
controlled dynamical systems represented through future tests
state compactness questions
```

Track-B implication:

The choice `latent world state vs predictive state` is already a morphology/representation parent axis. A “cognitive state is predictions about the future” claim is not new.

Potential phase-law question:

Under which ecology/resource regimes does a predictive-state morphology dominate latent-generative or explicit-symbolic state?

---

## T9 — Information-theoretic intrinsic objectives / empowerment

Empowerment defines an information-theoretic measure of an agent's potential influence/control via channel capacity from actions to later sensory states.

Owns / threatens:

```text
architecture-independent intrinsic control objective based on information
```

Track-B implication:

An “intelligence maximizes future controllability/optionality” objective is not new by itself. Morphogenesis would still need to explain structural realization and resource tradeoffs.

# Cross-parent lesson

Existing theories already cover many possible “one principle” stories:

```text
universal Bayesian decision          — AIXI
cross-environment intelligence score — Universal Intelligence
resource-bounded adaptation          — NARS / bounded rationality
state/operator cognition             — Soar
multi-paradigm cognitive synergy     — OpenCog/Hyperon
regulation/model variety constraints — cybernetics
unified inference/action objective   — free energy / active inference
minimal predictive state            — computational mechanics / PSR
information-theoretic control        — empowerment
```

Therefore Track B should **not** compete by proposing another verbally universal principle.

The stronger research target is a theory with all of:

1. a restricted, parent-subtracted generating basis;
2. bounded compilation into several known intelligence morphologies;
3. explicit developmental/update semantics;
4. complete resource/verification regime;
5. prospective prediction of morphology frontier transitions;
6. blind recovery and parent reduction;
7. ideally, a correctly predicted missing morphology.

That is substantially harder than a general-agent objective or architecture diagram.

- `ECOLOGY_IS_NOT_OPTIONAL`: NFL + cybernetics + learning theory make unrestricted architecture superiority incoherent/uninformative.

- `MODEL_OF_WORLD_IS_PARENT_OWNED`: world-model necessity claims require exact assumptions and parent subtraction.
- `MULTI_PARADIGM_INTEGRATION_IS_PARENT_OWNED`: integration alone is engineering.

- `GENERAL_OBJECTIVE_IS_NOT_GENERAL_MORPHOGENESIS`: an objective can be architecture-neutral while saying little about which architecture should emerge.
