# Meta-Morphogenesis v1

Status: **conceptual upper rung; execution not authorized**.

Track B eventually asks not only how a morphology develops, but whether the process that generates and develops morphologies can itself become better.

---

## 1. Separate three levels

### Level A — within-morphology learning

\[
M_{t+1}=U(M_t,e_t)
\]

Examples: SGD, Bayesian updating, rule induction, library learning.

### Level B — morphology change

\[
M' = \Gamma(B,E,R,V,H)
\]

Examples: topology change, new memory organization, new update law, hybridization.

### Level C — generator/meta-development

\[
(\Gamma_{g+1},U_{g+1})
=
\mathcal G(\Gamma_g,U_g,\mathcal H_g,\mathcal E_g).
\]

This is the strongest Track-B object: experience changes how future intelligence forms are generated and developed.

Do not conflate Level C with an ordinary self-edit of one implementation.

---

## 2. Parent subtraction

Major lower-level ownership already exists:

```text
MAML / learned optimizers            — learned update behavior
OOPS / PowerPlay                     — incremental solver/task and bias improvement
Gödel Machine                        — self-rewrite under proof condition
AutoML / NAS / AutoML-Zero           — learned/searched algorithms and architectures
DGM / AI4AI / OpenRSI-class systems  — empirical AI improving AI
POET / open-ended search             — coupled environment/solution evolution
```

Therefore Track B may not claim novelty for “the system improves its learner” or “the system searches architectures.”

The possible residual is narrower:

> Does verified developmental history improve the **morphology-generating process itself**, such that useful non-equivalent forms are proposed/discovered with lower complete cost or better predictive calibration on fresh ecologies?

---

## 3. Candidate measurement

For morphology generator `Gamma_g` and fresh registered ecology family `E` define

\[
B_{morph,g}(E)
=
E[\text{complete resources until first admissible frontier morphology}].
\]

A genuine meta-developmental signal requires, on matched fresh ecologies,

\[
B_{morph,g+1}(E) < B_{morph,g}(E)
\]

or higher verified frontier quality at matched resources.

Also track predictive calibration of the generator's phase model:

\[
Cal_g = \text{error of predicted morphology/frontier on fresh ecologies}.
\]

Candidate higher-order progress:

\[
Cal_{g+1} < Cal_g
\]

while search cost and complexity do not simply explode.

---

## 4. Required causal controls

A meta-morphogenesis claim needs at minimum:

```text
CONTINUED_GENERATOR
RESET_GENERATOR
STRONG_AUTOML / NAS / EVOLUTIONARY PARENT
FIXED_SEARCH_POLICY
SHUFFLED / CROSS-HISTORY CONTROL
```

and fresh ecology families not used to select the generator update.

The relevant history must change proposal/search/update behavior **before** the protected winning morphology is known.

---

## 5. Governance

The external verifier/evaluator/claim constitution does not self-modify inside the experiment.

The generator may propose changes to:

```text
basis selection
composition grammar
mutation/search distribution
update-law family
resource allocation
phase model
curriculum / experiment selection
```

but not protected evaluator identities, success thresholds, resource accounting, hidden test sets or adoption authority.

---

## 6. Gating conditions

Meta-morphogenesis remains blocked until all are true:

- [ ] at least two materially different known morphologies have been derived at D2 or better;
- [ ] a prospective phase prediction has been tested;
- [ ] morphology equivalence/reduction machinery is working;
- [ ] search grammar has a neutral, parent-tested implementation;
- [ ] complete morphology-search costs are measurable;
- [ ] ordinary AutoML/NAS/evolutionary parents are reconstructable;
- [ ] #149 governance can bind generator changes.

Until then:

```text
META_MORPHOGENESIS_NOT_YET_AUTHORIZED
```

---

## 7. Long-run claim ladder

```text
MG0 generator executes
MG1 generator finds useful morphologies
MG2 history changes future morphology proposals
MG3 changed proposals lower fresh morphology-acquisition burden
MG4 effect survives reset/shuffle/strong AutoML parents
MG5 generator-improvement slope replicates across ecology families
MG6 externally governed recursive morphology development
```

Even MG6 remains a scoped empirical claim, not proof of open-ended unbounded intelligence.
