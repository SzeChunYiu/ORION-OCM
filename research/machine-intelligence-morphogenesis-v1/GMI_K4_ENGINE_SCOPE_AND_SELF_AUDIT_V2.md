## 3. The remaining grammar defect — stated explicitly

The original K4 freeze describes three grammars as having disjoint primitive sets and disjoint state models:

```text
G1 typed tensor/dataflow
G2 symbolic/register program
G3 finite-state/message passing
```

The current V1/V2 search implementation does **not yet implement three independent interpreters/search spaces**.
It uses one common neutral mechanism/phenotype palette and three grammar-specific primitive price maps.

Therefore:

> A `K4_RECOVERY_GREEN` emitted by this engine means that a frozen property vector wins in the registered finite
> mechanism-palette phase test under that price model. It is **not** by itself the full `K4_LOFO` recovery demanded by
> the original freeze's disjoint-grammar clause.

The correct higher-level label is:

`K4_PALETTE_PHASE_EVIDENCE__FULL_GRAMMAR_NATIVE_RECOVERY_PENDING`.

This is not a cosmetic limitation. Shared palette design can encode conceptual bias even when the three price maps are
different. That is part of IG-5 and also an implementation-level predecessor to the external-authorship residue.

## 4. What a full successor must add

For each G1/G2/G3, implement and freeze **before protected execution**:

1. a disjoint primitive inventory;
2. its own program/genotype representation;
3. its own mutation/enumeration/search operator;
4. an interpreter or semantic evaluator;
5. property-vector measurement from execution traces, not from a target-family label;
6. known-control recovery inside that grammar;
7. coverage/exhaustion or a registered stochastic discoverability bound;
8. compiler/search cost charged under that grammar.

The same frozen obligation may then be searched by all three implementations. Agreement across those engines is much
stronger than price-map agreement over one palette.

- protected future-seeded phase-law stress tests;

- end-to-end validation of freeze → beacon → LUNARC → receipt → aggregate discipline.

A RED from this phase test is scientifically useful. A GREEN is calibration evidence, not the terminal K4 claim.
