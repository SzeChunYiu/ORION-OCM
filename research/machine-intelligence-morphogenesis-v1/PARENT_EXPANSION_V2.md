## A. General stateful/dynamical-system formalisms — direct threat to the proposed primitive

### Rutten — Universal Coalgebra (2000)

Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/S0304-3975(00)00056-6

**Owns:** a very general theory of state-transition/dynamical systems via coalgebras, homomorphisms and bisimulation/coinduction.

**Threat:** a primitive of “state + typed input + transition + output” can collapse to an ordinary coalgebra/state-machine parent.

**Track-B residual:** adaptation/update of the transition structure, resource-bounded developmental equivalence, ecology-conditioned morphology emergence.

Disposition: `ADOPT_AS_FOUNDATIONAL_PARENT`.

Depth: `SOURCE_INSPECTED`.

### Beer — agent/environment dynamical systems (1995; later minimal-cognition analyses)

Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(94)00005-L

**Owns:** autonomous agent and environment as coupled dynamical systems; behavior arises jointly from their interaction and adaptive fit.

**Threat:** Track B must not treat intelligence morphology as independent of ecology or assume behavior is an intrinsic property of an isolated machine.

**Track-B residual:** developmental/resource phase law across morphology families.

Disposition: `ADOPT_ECOLOGY_COUPLING`.

Depth: `SOURCE_INSPECTED`.

### Niu & Spivak — Polynomial Functors / dynamical systems as dependent lenses (2025)

Source: https://doi.org/10.1017/9781009576734.006

**Owns:** structured composition of Moore machines/dynamical systems through typed interfaces and wiring diagrams.

**Threat:** typed stateful composition is not Track-B novelty.

**Residual:** adaptive basis minimality, learning/update semantics, resource/developmental equivalence and morphology-selection laws.

Disposition: `STRONG_PARENT_FOR_B0_LOCAL_ADAPTIVE_TRANSDUCERS`.

Depth: `SOURCE_INSPECTED`.

**Threat:** `B3_STOCHASTIC_GENERATIVE_KERNEL` may be mostly parent-owned if it merely rephrases generic stochastic composition.

**Residual:** machine-learning/development morphology rather than equilibrium semantics.

## C. Learned inductive bias / representation — parent for morphology-dependent development

### Baxter — A Model of Inductive Bias Learning (2000)

Source: https://doi.org/10.1613/jair.731

**Owns:** formal learning of a hypothesis-space bias from an environment of related tasks, with generalization to new tasks under assumptions/bounds.

**Threat:** “history changes the space in which future learning occurs” is strongly parent-owned.

**Residual:** whole morphology generation/selection and heterogeneous validation/resource regimes.

Disposition: `ADOPT_META_LEARNING_FOUNDATION`.

Depth: `SOURCE_INSPECTED`.

### Maurer, Pontil & Romera-Paredes — Benefit of Multitask Representation Learning (2016)

Source: https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v17/15-242.html

**Owns:** conditions where learned shared representations over multiple tasks improve future/multitask learning; explicit regimes depend on samples, number of tasks and intrinsic dimension in the analyzed setting.

**Track-B use:** a concrete precedent for **phase/regime laws** rather than universal architecture claims.

Disposition: `ADOPT_AS_PHASE_LAW_TEMPLATE`.

Depth: `SOURCE_INSPECTED`.

## D. Information/resource theories — strong parents for developmental-frontier mathematics

### Tishby, Pereira & Bialek — Information Bottleneck

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0004057

**Owns:** constrained compression preserving information relevant to another variable; a rate-distortion-like variational problem.

**Threat:** Track B must not claim the generic idea that useful intelligence trades representation complexity against relevant predictive information.

**Residual:** morphology-level development and lifecycle/resource vectors, not only representation coding.

Disposition: `ADOPT_AS_REPRESENTATION_FRONTIER_PARENT`.

Depth: `SOURCE_INSPECTED`.

### Sims — Rate-distortion theory and human perception (2016)

Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2016.03.020

**Owns:** optimal perceptual coding under capacity constraints as a rate-distortion problem.

**Track-B use:** formal precedent for phase/frontier reasoning under explicit information capacity.

Disposition: `ADOPT_RESOURCE_CONSTRAINT_PARENT`.

Depth: `SOURCE_INSPECTED`.

### Lieder & Griffiths — Resource-rational analysis (2019/2020)

Source: https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X1900061X

**Owns:** methodology for explaining cognitive mechanisms as effective use of limited computational resources; emphasizes costs/constraints in reverse engineering cognition.

**Threat:** “architecture should be explained by resource tradeoffs” is parent-owned.

**Residual:** prospective machine-morphology phase law with explicit developmental dynamics and blind recovery.

Disposition: `ADOPT_AS_PHASE_LAW_METHOD_PARENT`.

Depth: `SOURCE_INSPECTED`.

## E. Neural morphology — stronger parents for why/where neural systems dominate

### Miconi, Stanley & Clune — Differentiable Plasticity (2018)

Source: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/miconi18a.html

**Owns:** plasticity parameters can themselves be optimized by gradient descent; plastic recurrent networks can exhibit fast/lifelong adaptation and meta-learning behavior.

**Threat:** “neural networks are static while OCM develops” is false.

Disposition: `REJECT_STATIC_NEURAL_STRAWMAN`.

Depth: `SOURCE_INSPECTED`.

### Kaplan et al. — Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models (2020)

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.08361

**Owns:** empirical power-law-like loss scaling with model size, data and training compute over broad ranges in the studied LM regime.

**Track-B use:** evidence that morphology families can have measurable internal scaling laws; phase theory must compare frontiers, not architecture names.

Disposition: `ADOPT_EMPIRICAL_SCALING_PARENT`.

Depth: `SOURCE_INSPECTED`.

### Hoffmann et al. — Training Compute-Optimal LLMs / Chinchilla (2022)

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.15556

**Owns:** compute-optimal model/data allocation within Transformer LM training; larger parameter count alone is not the relevant resource-optimal variable.

**Track-B use:** even within one morphology, optimal realization changes with resource regime.

Disposition: `ADOPT_INTERNAL_PHASE_PARENT`.

Depth: `SOURCE_INSPECTED`.

### Jouppi et al. — TPU analysis (2017)

Source: https://research.google/pubs/in-datacenter-performance-analysis-of-a-tensor-processing-unit/

**Owns:** substantial inference performance/energy advantage for a matrix-multiply-focused neural ASIC on the evaluated deployed workloads.

**Track-B consequence:** hardware-resource regime `R` can materially move morphology frontiers.

Disposition: `HARDWARE_CO_DESIGN_REQUIRED`.

Depth: `SOURCE_INSPECTED`.

### Dohare et al. — Loss of plasticity in deep continual learning (Nature 2024)

Source: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07711-7

**Owns:** broad empirical demonstration that standard deep-learning procedures can lose plasticity across long task sequences; diversity-injecting continual backprop mitigates it in the studied settings.

**Track-B consequence:** development ceiling must distinguish representational reach from update-law plasticity; neural phase can shift over lifetime horizon.

Disposition: `ADOPT_AS_MORPHOLOGY_LIMIT_PARENT`.

Depth: `SOURCE_INSPECTED`.
