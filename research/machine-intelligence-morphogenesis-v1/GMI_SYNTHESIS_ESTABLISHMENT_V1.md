## 2. Canonical theory statement

A deployed developmental system is evaluated relative to a declared obligation/development contract.

The least implementation-dependent state object is the **semantic developmental sufficient state**: an equivalence class of current developmental situations that cannot be distinguished by any registered future semantic consequence under the admissible intervention/development protocol.

A machine-intelligence morphology

\[
M=(F,\Theta,K,U,\Gamma,\kappa,\rho_M)
\]

is a computational/resource realization of those semantic distinctions:

```text
F       factorization/topology/program organization
Theta   mutable realization state
K       execution/cognition/action kernel
U       within-form learning/development
Gamma   structural/morphogenetic change
kappa   semantic realization/compiler map
rho_M   raw resource semantics
```

External verification/authority and the legal development protocol remain outside the morphology's unilateral authority.

The primary intelligence object is not one scalar but a verified **capability-resource developmental frontier** over a declared ecology family.

Generality is therefore a preorder on deployed systems and a partial order only after quotienting mutually profile-equivalent systems. Scalar generality requires a declared ecology measure and utility/cost scalarization.

### E6 — missing morphology

Only after a parent-frontier hole is prospectively established.
