- unrestricted exact lookup needs `N log2(q)` target bits;
- finite target family of size `M` needs `log2(M)` identity bits;
- finite realizable family has sample burden scaling with `log M`;

  `sum_{j<=r} C(N,j)(q-1)^j`;
- base-law + residual beats arbitrary table exactly when its code/burden is lower;
- unrestricted independent updates recover the full `q^N` family.

**Impossibility boundary**

Finite uncovered observations cannot distribution-free identify global compressibility.

**OPEN-BLOCKING**

- assumption-indexed real compressibility/rate-distortion estimator;
- noisy/approximate semantic law complexity;
- protected coefficient/basis/memory crossover.

Current: `K2/K3 synthetic`, not K4.
