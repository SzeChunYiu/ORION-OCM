# 4. Most promising unresolved direction

The highest-priority unresolved classical candidate is **constructive closure under physical/material lifecycle constraints**, not because closure itself is new—it is not—but because native construction may change the future resource substrate in ways that a purely symbolic simulator reproduces semantically only by paying materially different wall-clock, communication, materialization, sensing or actuation cost.

Required proof target:

Find a registered obligation family and overhead class H0-H2 such that

\[
R_{compiled}(n) / R_{native}(n)
\]

provably escapes the accepted overhead bound after all physical inputs/outputs and construction costs are charged.

If this fails, demote constructive closure fully to D8/D4 mechanism status.
