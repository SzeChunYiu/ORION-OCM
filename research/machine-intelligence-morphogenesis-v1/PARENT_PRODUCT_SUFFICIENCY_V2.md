# Does the parent product already equal Track B?

At the level of **conceptual objects**, almost yes.

One can compose mature parent theory into:

\[
\boxed{
\text{representation/search space}
+\text{development/update dynamics}
+\text{resource model}
+\text{task ecology}
+\text{verification/evaluation}
\to
\text{conditional best machine organization}
}
\]

This already captures much of the broad GMI story.

Calling this composition “a new general machine intelligence theory” would currently overclaim.

## What the parent product does *not* automatically give

Three residuals remain scientifically nontrivial:

### R1 — cross-paradigm structure -> developmental-geometry prediction

A frozen measurable transformation from actual structure/update/history to a compact predictive signature that works across materially different morphology families, without complete simulation or protected-outcome fitting.

### R2 — endogenous prospective phase recovery

A theory-derived frontier transition frozen before search, with the morphology candidates generated from a shared low-level space rather than selected from a human portfolio.

### R3 — predicted missing form

A theory identifies a region where known parents are all dominated/structurally mismatched, freezes the required properties, and neutral search discovers a non-parent-equivalent form there.

None is established.
