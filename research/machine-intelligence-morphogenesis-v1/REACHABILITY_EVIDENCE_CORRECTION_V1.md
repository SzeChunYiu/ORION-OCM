# Reachability evidence: scope correction

Date: 2026-09-13. Reviewed [PR #540 at 9e47d410](https://github.com/SzeChunYiu/ORION-OCM/blob/9e47d4109aa786fc39fe4cd9bc642c14c167c211/research/machine-intelligence-morphogenesis-v1/GMI_REACHABILITY_SCOPE_OF_FAMILY_VERDICTS_V1.md).
This note qualifies that unmerged source; it changes no frozen experiment.

## Recovered examples and complete reachable sets

A scan of recovered machines, followed by the registered intervention checks,
describes the surviving scanned machines. A missing family in that scan does
not establish its absence from every machine reachable under the development
law, or from the admitted realization universe. A recovered witness establishes
membership only when its identity, adequacy, provenance and execution scope
are certified. A logged in-run success is evidence of a reported event; the
strength of independent re-verification depends on retained objects and code.

Different finite-run outputs can occur under the same stochastic law. Conversely,
different initial measures need not change the support of every reachable set.
Observed output differences alone therefore do not identify distinct complete
reachable frontiers or measure the analytic DU countermodel. A distributional
comparison needs its registered sampling and inference contract; a support
claim needs an actual reachability or impossibility argument.

## What the repaired DU result permits

[DU-1](../gmi-grand-unification-v1/DEVELOPMENTAL_UNDERDETERMINATION_THEOREM_V1.md)
proves general non-identification when development is left unconstrained by
the static summary. It does not establish that no useful theorem about trained
outcomes can exist. Stronger development assumptions, invariants, constructive
algorithms and independently verified reachability certificates can supply
such theorems. Further scientific work can discharge those concrete premises.

DU-2 distinguishes a selected schedule from the union over all permitted
schedules. A complete transition/admission relation and initial set already
define that existential reachability graph; specifying a chosen schedule is
not necessary to define the union. An operator-name list without its semantics
is a different, incomplete input.

DU-3 proves that restricting a feasible set cannot decrease its own scalar
infimum. This alone does not preserve a competitive family exclusion. With
family A costs {2,9} and B cost {5}, removing A's cost-2 machine changes the
winner from A to B. Excluding B by comparison with A's cost-2 construction
survives only if the relevant comparator remains admitted and reachable at
the same scope. The corrected theorem gives that positive sufficient condition.

## Candidate-specific evidence and a recovery experiment

A frontier over hand-registered candidates remains a statement about that
register. Developmental evidence about a family does not automatically bind
the actual winning candidate. The packet's candidate-specific reachability
qualification is therefore substantive, independently of the incorrect claim
that all exclusions survive restriction.

If a successful genotype was discarded, a source-bound replay with additional
object capture is a reasonable repair experiment. Its success is not implied
by a historical evaluation count alone. Preserve the original run, record the
exact source, inputs, seed and instrumentation difference, and independently
verify the captured object. Until that succeeds, a proposed rerun is a method
for obtaining a witness, rather than an already established witness.

These distinctions preserve the useful scanned-occupant and candidate-scope
observations in PR #540 while removing its unsupported universal implications.
