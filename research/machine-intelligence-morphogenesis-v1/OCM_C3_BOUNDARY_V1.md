## Scientific significance of the negative

This is not a retreat in Track B. It separates two notions that a loose “developmental intelligence” theory could otherwise conflate:

```text

  experience changes how future target solutions are searched for

```

The first does not imply the second.

Any general theory must preserve that separation.
