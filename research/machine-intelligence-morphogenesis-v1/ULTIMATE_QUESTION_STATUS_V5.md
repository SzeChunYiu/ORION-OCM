# Why Cognitive Polyphenism follows from GMI rather than architecture brainstorming

GMI already separates:

```text
semantic developmental state
from
resource realization / morphology
```

and establishes that morphology preference is conditional on realization response, resources and bounded discovery.

A persistent system operating across recurrent heterogeneous regimes therefore faces two opposing costs:

```text
one fixed realization:
    low synchronization burden
    high mismatch burden outside its favored regime

many eagerly maintained realizations:
    low regime mismatch
    synchronization/update burden across all maintained forms
```

If semantic/developmental state is substantially shared, a third organization is implied by the cost geometry:

```text
one authoritative shared developmental state
+ only materialize/update the currently demanded realization phenotypes
```

This is the GMI-CP-V1 prediction.

The exact toy phase model is intentionally simple:

\[
\sigma(k,q)=\frac{q}{1/k+q-q/k},
\]

\[
C_{CP}=s_P+qu_0+c\sigma(k,q),
\]

\[
C_{eager}=s_H+qke.
\]

For fixed `q>0`, `e>0` and finite `c`, eager synchronization is linear in registered regime count `k`, whereas the lazy-recompilation term saturates. Registered negative twins reverse the winner under low diversity or high compilation cost.

The toy result is only a phase-pressure calibration. The scientific claim depends on E1–E7.
