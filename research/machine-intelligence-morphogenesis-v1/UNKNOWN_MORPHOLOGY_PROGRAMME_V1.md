# Unknown Morphology Programme v1

Status: **future gated programme**. Search is not yet authorized.

Question:

> Can Track B predict and then recover a form of machine intelligence that is not developmentally/resource-equivalent to registered known families?

The programme is deliberately theory-first. “Interesting graph found by evolution” is not evidence of a new form of intelligence.

---

## 1. Morphology identity

A candidate new morphology `M?` must differ from known families under bounded developmental equivalence, not merely implementation syntax.

Required comparison set at minimum:

```text
neural / differentiable
symbolic / production / rewrite
probabilistic / generative-program
programmatic / synthesis / library
registered hybrids / parent products
current OCM-like explicit epistemic morphology
```

A candidate fails novelty if there exists a bounded compilation into a registered parent family that preserves:

```text
capability
development/update behavior
retention/plasticity
revision response
transfer profile
resource scaling
```

within the registered tolerances.

---

## 2. Phase-diagram-hole criterion

Do not search for unknown forms until known morphologies have been mapped on at least a small controlled ecology family.

A **morphology hole** is a registered region `E*` satisfying all of:

1. every registered known morphology misses at least one protected requirement or is resource-dominated;
2. the failure is not merely bad tuning or an omitted strong parent;
3. a lower bound / constructive argument / known component combination indicates that the observed frontier is not obviously fundamental;
4. the missing properties can be stated without naming the architecture that should provide them;
5. the ecology and requirements are frozen before architecture search.

Example requirement vector:

```text
statistical generalization        high
exact compositional verification  required
local revision                    frequent
continual plasticity              required
very large reuse horizon          medium
persistent provenance             required
```

This does not imply a new morphology exists; it defines where one would be scientifically meaningful.

---

## 3. Property-first prediction

Before search, produce `MissingMorphologyPredictionV1`:

```text
prediction_id
ecology_identity
known_parent_frontier
unmet requirement vector
predicted structural properties
predicted update-law properties
predicted resource signature
predicted failure mode of known families
negative twin ecology
search grammar identity
success equivalence test
strongest parent product
claim ceiling
```

The predicted properties must be abstract enough not to encode the answer, but strong enough that the prediction can fail.

---

## 4. Neutral search requirements

Only after the basis/grammar survives GMI-D2 exact tests:

- no architecture labels in the primitive vocabulary;
- no `NEURON`, `ATTENTION`, `PRODUCTION_RULE`, `BAYES_UPDATE`, `PROGRAM_INTERPRETER` or OCM-specific unit as a primitive;
- search encoding tournament before interpreting found species;
- random search / ordinary multi-objective / QD parents receive first refusal;
- all rejected/failed candidates count in the denominator;
- protected ecology is never used to tune descriptors or mutation operators;
- descriptor choice cannot define morphology identity;
- search-side neural surrogate is external compute, not candidate cognition.

#221/#220 may later provide search infrastructure after their current non-neural restriction is removed or a neutral successor configuration is defined.

---

## 5. Reduction attack after discovery

For every candidate survivor `M?`:

1. simplify dead/redundant structure;
2. compile to strongest known neural parent;
3. compile to strongest symbolic/programmatic parent;
4. compile to probabilistic-program parent;
5. compile to strongest obvious hybrid/product;
6. test whether learning law rather than forward structure is the true novelty;
7. test whether hardware/accounting artifacts created the frontier point;
8. test on reminted/disjoint ecology;
9. ablate the supposedly distinctive component;
10. rerun with a different search encoding.

A new morphology must survive all applicable reductions.

## 7. Strong positive ladder

Do not jump directly to `NEW_FORM_OF_INTELLIGENCE`.

```text
U0 STRUCTURALLY_DISTINCT_CANDIDATE
U1 BEHAVIORALLY_DISTINCT_AT_SCOPE
U2 DEVELOPMENTALLY_DISTINCT_AT_SCOPE
U3 RESOURCE_FRONTIER_DISTINCT_AT_SCOPE
U4 THEORY_PREDICTED_BEFORE_SEARCH
U5 DISJOINT_ECOLOGY_REPLICATION
U6 INDEPENDENT_SEARCH/IMPLEMENTATION_REPLICATION
```

Only U4+ begins to support a serious morphogenesis-theory claim.

Even U6 does not prove a metaphysically new universal form of intelligence.

---

## 8. Why this is an upward claim

AutoML-Zero/NEAT/NAS show that search can discover neural architectures/algorithms.

Novelty search/QD/POET show that divergent/open-ended search can preserve stepping stones and diverse solutions.

The Track-B residual is therefore not “search can find unusual architectures.” It is:

```text
theory identifies a missing morphology region
→ predicts properties before search
→ neutral search recovers a non-parent-equivalent form
→ the same theory predicts where it wins and loses
```

That is the standard required before Track B claims it has discovered a genuinely new machine-intelligence morphology.
