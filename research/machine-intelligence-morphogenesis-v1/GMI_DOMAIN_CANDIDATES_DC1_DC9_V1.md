## 0. What a domain is, derived from the GMI normal form (the theory-side closure the programme needs)

A realization is `M = (Z, K, U, Γ, κ, ρ)`. The neutral primitive alphabet of the morphology IR (`morph.KINDS`) factors every
realization into **state primitives** (the carrier of `Z`: dense coefficient vector, table, key-value store, program term,
distribution, versioned table, …), **execution primitives** (`K`: fixed edge, lookup, nearest-neighbour, score-and-select,
linear map, program execution, search expansion, …) and **update primitives** (`U`: gradient, closed form, insertion, program
mutation, population update, …). Define the **reduction preorder** on carrier/law pairs: `(Z_a, U_a) ≼ (Z_b, U_b)` iff there is a
compiler from realizations of the first into realizations of the second that preserves the developmental response on every
registered ecology and intervention (R2 exact equivalence) with lifecycle burden overhead bounded by a declared polynomial in
the state size (the *bounded semantics-preserving reduction* of §2/§14 of the kingdoms document). A **structural domain** is an
equivalence class of the symmetric part of `≼` restricted to the registered family; a **new domain** is a class not reachable
by bounded reduction from D1–D9 that additionally satisfies criteria 4–6 (frontier change in a predicted niche; neutral recovery;
recurrence). Under this definition the existing domains are generated by the alphabet as follows (executed instances in brackets):

| domain | carrier primitive | law primitive | executed instances |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 coefficient / function-field | DENSE | LINEAR/AFFINE + GRAD | S4 (RV-009…035), zoo `gradient_net` |
| D2 exemplar / memory-indexed | TABLE, KVSTORE | LOOKUP/NEAREST/SCORESELECT + INSERT/CLOSEDFORM | S5, S5h, S5a (RV-025, 034), MONO_C/MOD_U (RV-032…038) |
| D3 probabilistic belief | distribution over hypotheses | Bayesian conditioning (SCORE/NORMALIZE) | S7 (RV-029/031) |
| D4 symbolic rule / program | PROGRAM | PROGEXEC + SEARCH/VERIFY | S2/S2a/S6 (RV-017…025), R1 (RV-030) |
| D5 search / deliberative frontier | PROGRAM + evidence | SEARCH expansion, VERIFY | S2a, VGSC (RV-042) |
| D6 dynamical-state / controller | recurrent state | iterated local map | credit rows R2/R3 (RV-030); not yet an IR kind (gap DG-1) |
| D7 collective / population | multiset of candidates | PMUTATE (population update) | S3 particles (RV-040/041b), RUN7–9 evolution (RV-023/028) |
| D8 morphogenetic / self-rewriting | MORPH_RULE over the genotype | mutation grammar (R5) | blind recovery runs; LMHM mesh flips (RV-043) |
| D9 hybrid / symbiotic | any composite | any composite | VLC, CP, VGSC, LMHM (RV-032/033/042/043) |

The programme below asks, for each candidate, the *same executable question*: implement the candidate's carrier and law
**natively** (a declared basis column whose native ops are the candidate's operators at declared unit prices) and **reduced**
(the same law compiled into the existing alphabet); if the reduced realization reproduces the developmental response exactly with
polynomially bounded overhead, the candidate is a **compiled specialization** (claim level `REDUCED_TO_PARENT`) — unless a
registered ecology exists in which the native carrier changes the frontier *qualitatively* (criterion 4), which is what the niche
microscope tests. Every candidate therefore ends in one of: `REDUCED_TO_PARENT(Dk)`, `SUBSTRATE_VARIANT(Dk, price vector)`,
`NEW_DOMAIN_CANDIDATE_SURVIVES_REDUCTION_AT_SCOPE` (then B1/B4 recovery and remint recurrence are required), or
`REGISTERED_FOR_EXPERIMENT`.

## 2. Per-candidate hostile programme

Each candidate has: (a) the native basis (declared op set and unit prices), (b) the reduction (its compilation into the
existing alphabet; the overhead is the reduction burden vector), (c) the niche ecology where the candidate's law is supposed to
matter, (d) the negative twin (same carrier size, law disabled or replaced by its parent), (e) the frozen prediction and kill
condition, (f) the claim level after execution. Exact microscopes live in `gmi_microscope/domains/`.

### DC1 hyperdimensional / VSA — microscope `vsa.py`, record RV-377-044

- Native basis `VSA_NATIVE(D)`: ops BIND, BUNDLE(k), PERMUTE, HAMMING, CLEANUP at unit price each (one op per hypervector,
  regardless of D — the "in-memory / associative hardware" price vector that VSA hardware papers assume). Reduced basis: the same
  ops compiled into B0's Boolean universe: BIND = D XORs, BUNDLE(k) = D majorities (each ~k ADD + 1 THRESH), HAMMING = D XOR + D ADD,
  CLEANUP = |codebook| × HAMMING.
- Reduction claim (theorem-level, checked exactly): the reduced realization reproduces the native developmental response bit
  for bit on every registered ecology and intervention (R2 exact equality), with burden overhead exactly linear in D per op
  (recorded as the reduction burden vector). Hence DC1 ≼ D2 × Boolean-D1 with polynomial (linear) overhead: criterion 3 fails.
- Niche ecology `E_rolefill(k, D)`: records are sets of k (role, filler) pairs over 4 roles and 8 fillers; development shows each
  record once as a bundle of bound pairs; queries ask (record, role) → filler including *novel* records composed of seen pairs and
  *noisy* cues (a corrupted record hypervector). Rows: VSA (record = BUNDLE_k(BIND(role_i, filler_i)); query = CLEANUP(BIND(record,
  role))), TABLE (D2: (record, role) → filler), KNN (D2: nearest stored record by Hamming on the raw pair list), and the negative twin
  VSA_NOBIND (bundle without binding: roles lost).
- Frozen prediction (calibrated before the run, see ledger): (1) VSA capability follows the bundle-capacity law: exact retrieval
  iff k < k*(D) with k*(64) and k*(128) from the calibration; (2) TABLE is exact at every k with description k·(2+3) bits per
  record, which is below D for k ≤ 12 at D = 64 — so TABLE wins the description term at every registered k; (3) VSA wins only the
  noisy-cue cells (graceful degradation), where KNN ties it; (4) VSA_NOBIND is inadmissible at every k ≥ 2; (5) under the native
  price vector VSA's exec per query is 3 ops vs TABLE's 1 lookup + KNN's |records| Hammings — VSA wins exec against KNN, never
  against TABLE. Kill condition for `REDUCED_TO_PARENT`: any registered cell where native VSA beats both TABLE and KNN by more than
  the price-vector factor D (a qualitative, not constant-factor, frontier change).
- Expected claim level: `REDUCED_TO_PARENT(D2 × D1-Boolean)` with `SUBSTRATE_VARIANT` under the native price vector.

### DC3 energy-landscape — microscope `hopfield.py`, record RV-377-045

- Native basis `ENERGY_NATIVE(N)`: op DESCEND (one asynchronous sweep to a fixed point) at unit price; HEBB (coupling update) at
  unit price. Reduced: DESCEND = N² MUL + N² ADD + N THRESH per sweep in B0; HEBB = N² MUL/ADD.
- Reduction claim: the reduced realization reproduces the native response exactly; overhead N² per sweep: DC3 ≼ D1 × D6.
- Niche ecology `E_complete(N, P, noise)`: P stored binary patterns of length N; queries are corrupted patterns (noise bits);
  the obligation is to return the stored pattern (pattern completion). Rows: HOPFIELD (Hebbian W, descent), KNN (D2: nearest stored
  pattern by Hamming — the parent that owns "completion"), TABLE (exact keys only: inadmissible under noise), negative twin
  HOPFIELD_RANDOMW (random couplings).
- Frozen prediction: (1) capacity law: HOPFIELD is admissible iff P/N < c* (c* from the calibration, expected ≈ 0.14 at N = 32 by
  the classical Amit–Gutfreund–Sompolinsky bound); (2) KNN is admissible at every P (exact nearest neighbour) with exec
  P·N per query vs HOPFIELD's sweeps·N²; (3) frontier: KNN at every P under the registered price vector; HOPFIELD wins exec only
  under the native price where DESCEND is one op and P > N·sweeps; (4) the negative twin is inadmissible. Kill: HOPFIELD admissible
  beyond c* or winning a cell in the reduced basis.
- Expected: `REDUCED_TO_PARENT(D2 by semantics; D1 × D6 by mechanism)`.

### DC2 self-organizing field — microscope `field.py`, record RV-377-046

- Native basis `FIELD_NATIVE(L)`: op STEP (one synchronous lattice update) at unit price; reduced: L × (rule evaluation) ops.
- Niche `E_spatial(L)`: 1-D ring of L cells; obligation: map an initial configuration to the configuration after T steps of an
  unknown local rule (learn the rule from examples), evaluated on unseen initial configurations. Rows: FIELD (search over the 256
  elementary rules with the field as the served state), TABLE (global map memorization: inadmissible on unseen configurations),
  GRADNET (D1 on the flattened configuration), negative twin FIELD_NONLOCAL (a global rule of the same description size).
- Frozen prediction: the field wins by sample efficiency exactly as the B1-A symmetry mechanism predicts (shared local rule =
  weight sharing): a compiled D6 + weight-sharing specialization, not a new carrier; reduction overhead linear in L.
- Expected: `REDUCED_TO_PARENT(D6 + weight sharing)`.

### DC7 quantum cognition — microscope `quantum.py`, record RV-377-047

- Native basis `QUANTUM_NATIVE(n)`: ops UNITARY (apply a declared unitary on n qubits), PROJECT (projective measurement with
  Born-rule probabilities) at unit price; reduced: exact state-vector simulation in fixed point, 2^n × 2^n MUL/ADD per unitary.
- Niche `E_order(n)`: the obligation is to reproduce a registered table of question-order response frequencies P(A then B),
  P(B then A) exhibiting order effects (QQ-equality holds for a quantum projective model); rows: QPROJ (unit state + non-commuting
  projectors; description = the state and two projector angles), BAYES_ORDERED (D3 with an explicit order variable), TABLE (D2:
  memorize the table), negative twin QPROJ_COMMUTING (commuting projectors: no order effect).
- Frozen prediction: (1) QPROJ and BAYES_ORDERED are both admissible; QPROJ has the smaller description (angles vs a full
  conditional table) — the parsimony that the quantum-cognition literature claims — by a constant factor; (2) the QQ-equality
  holds for QPROJ and fails for BAYES_ORDERED generically (an executed distinguishing witness); (3) the exact classical reduction
  reproduces QPROJ's response with 2^n overhead, so criterion 3 fails at every finite n; (4) the negative twin is inadmissible on
  order-effect cells.
- Expected: `REDUCED_TO_PARENT(D3 with amplitude state)` — a compiled specialization whose value is description parsimony plus a
  testable structural constraint (QQ), not a new carrier/law pair at finite scope.

## 3. Hostile perspectives applied (H1–H17 summary per candidate)

- H1 types: every candidate's carrier and law are typed against the IR alphabet; DC1/DC3/DC7 need NEW kinds (HYPERVECTOR/BIND/
  BUNDLE; COUPLING/DESCEND; AMPLITUDE/UNITARY/PROJECT) which are added to a *candidate* alphabet, never to the neutral grammar
  used for B1/B4 recovery (recovery must find them from lower primitives or not at all).
- H3 identifiability / H12 implementation equivalence: the reduction microscope IS the equivalence test: native and reduced
  realizations must be exactly equal in developmental response; any difference is a bug in one of them, not a domain.
- H8 resource completeness: the native price vector is a declared assumption (unit-cost associative ops); the frontier claims of
  the VSA/Hopfield/quantum hardware literatures rest on it and are reported as `SUBSTRATE_VARIANT` conclusions.
- H13 parent reduction: D2 (exemplar memory) is the strongest parent for DC1 and DC3; D3 for DC7; D6 for DC2/DC5/DC6/DC9;
  D2 + D6 for DC8; D7 × D5 for DC4.
- H14 adaptive search: niche ecologies are declared before the runs with calibrations disclosed in the ledger; kill conditions
  are frozen; failed clauses are preserved.

## 4. Prediction of the programme as a whole (frozen)

No candidate among DC1–DC9 survives criterion 3 at the exact layer: each is a compiled specialization or a substrate variant of
D1–D9, and their executed value is (i) a price-vector niche (DC1, DC3, DC5, DC6), (ii) a structural witness (DC7's QQ equality),
(iii) a symmetry mechanism inside D6 (DC2), or (iv) already-executed stochastic-search laws (DC4). The falsifier of this
programme-level prediction is a single candidate whose native realization beats every D1–D9 row on a registered ecology by more
than its reduction overhead factor. If that happens the candidate proceeds to B1 recovery and remint recurrence (criteria 5–6);
until then the honest terminal is `NO_NEW_DOMAIN_SURVIVES_BOUNDED_REDUCTION_AT_EXACT_SCOPE__CANDIDATES_TYPED_AND_PLACED`.
The theory-side consequence (§0) is that the GMI normal form *derives* the domain taxonomy as the reduction classes of its
primitive alphabet, and that new domains can only enter through carriers not expressible as bounded compositions of the
alphabet — which is what the unexplored region of §15 of the kingdoms document (developmental state distributed across
representation domains, morphology as cognition) would require: an IR whose *genotype* is part of the served state (D8 with
D9 composition), the object of the biosphere's B4 stage.
