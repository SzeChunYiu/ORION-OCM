# 1. Why an additive gate is needed

The original E1-E3 programme correctly requires:

```text
strong parents
surface reminting
family-held-out prediction
neutral morphology search
search-encoding tournaments
complete lifecycle accounting
negative twins
```

The theory hardening since the first VLC falsification exposes five additional risks:

```text
R1  composite-mechanism success mistaken for atomic causal understanding;
R2  obligation demand mixed with morphology response/resource context;
R3  adaptive search repeatedly fitting the protected evaluation ecology;
R4  query locality, update locality, speculative isolation and history persistence collapsed together;
R5  noisy Pareto/frontier decisions treated as deterministic labels.
```

E1-E3 should therefore pass the following gates before a strong predictive-morphogenesis terminal is allowed.

# 6. G4 — adaptive-search firewall

E3 is itself an adaptive optimizer over morphologies.

If search repeatedly observes the same protected worlds used for final confirmation, the search can overfit those worlds even without explicit architecture labels.

Adaptive-data-analysis literature makes this a generic statistical validity risk.

Therefore use three layers.

## Layer D — development/search worlds

May be queried adaptively by the morphology search.

Used for:

```text
proposal fitness
mutation/selection
search debugging
candidate pruning
threshold calibration allowed by protocol
```

## Layer V — validation worlds

Used sparingly for model/search-procedure selection before final freeze.

Every access is logged.

No claim may treat V as untouched confirmation after adaptive use.

## Layer P — protected one-shot confirmation worlds

Generated from frozen ecology generators/seeds/remint rules after the search procedure, witness thresholds and statistical analysis are frozen.

No candidate search, grammar retuning, threshold retuning or parent tuning may use P outcomes.

If P is reopened adaptively after failure, it becomes development data and a new protected split is required.

This is the simplest conservative protection. A formally valid reusable-holdout mechanism could replace it only if explicitly specified and justified.

Gate terminal:

```text
G4_ADAPTIVE_SEARCH_FIREWALL_GREEN
```

# 7. G5 — search-procedure first refusal and encoding robustness

A grammar can strongly prefer the predicted species through:

Therefore freeze at least two materially different low-level encodings/search implementations before protected E3.

For each search implementation meter:

```text

```

Required strong result:

```text
R* enrichment of the predicted mechanism vector survives alternate encodings,
AND no single encoding explains the majority of protected recovery evidence,
AND search cost remains charged.
```

If only one encoding recovers the target:

```text
SEARCH_ENCODING_DEPENDENT_RECOVERY
```

is the correct terminal.

Use hierarchical/bootstrap/permutation procedures appropriate to the registered generator and dependence structure.

# 9. G7 — multiobjective/frontier uncertainty

The primary target is a Pareto/frontier relation, not one post-hoc scalar.

With noisy estimated burden/capability vectors, naive empirical dominance can flip because of measurement error.

Therefore protected reporting must distinguish:

```text
CERTAINLY_DOMINATED under registered confidence region
CERTAINLY_NONDOMINATED / robust frontier member
FRONTIER_UNCERTAIN
```

A mechanism-core claim should propagate this uncertainty.

If a candidate's frontier membership is uncertain, do not use it as a hard universal quantifier in the empirical estimate of

\[
K^*(c)=\bigcap_{M\in F_\epsilon(c)} A(M).
\]

Use a conservative lower/upper core interval:

```text
K_core_lower = mechanisms present in every realization that could robustly be required under the confidence set;
K_core_upper = mechanisms present in at least all plausible frontier-core resolutions;
```

or another prospectively frozen uncertainty construction.

Do not silently convert noisy vectors into deterministic labels.

# 19. Parent-method ownership

This gate intentionally adopts established methodological warnings rather than claiming them as GMI novelty.

- Adaptive holdout/search validity: Dwork et al. (2015) show why adaptively reusing evaluation data can invalidate ordinary holdout reasoning.
- Invariant prediction: Peters, Bühlmann & Meinshausen (2016) motivate testing stability across interventions/environments instead of relying only on pooled association.
- Multiobjective/Pareto analysis: standard multiobjective optimization owns the frontier concepts.
- Identifiability: nonlinear ICA/disentanglement results motivate explicit assumptions/auxiliary variables rather than assuming latent coordinates are uniquely discoverable.
- Finite-size scaling: critical-phenomena literature owns the stronger asymptotic meaning of phase-transition evidence.

GMI's burden is to integrate these constraints into one cross-paradigm developmental/morphology protocol.
