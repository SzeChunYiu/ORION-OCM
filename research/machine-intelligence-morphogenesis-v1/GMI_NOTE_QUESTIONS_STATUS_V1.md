form(Ω, ρ, Γ) = the admissible realization that minimizes ρ-cost on Ω, IF Γ can reach it.

## 3. Question ③ — what limits a form; cost or other factors; d(development)/d(cost); why neural networks dominate

**Answer at current evidence: cost decides only among forms that pass three prior gates. The four limits, in the order they bind:**

1. **Existence in the generator's grammar** (Codex RP15). The dense gradient form did not exist in the frozen depth-2 update grammar (learning-rate 1 diverges for ≥ 2 active inputs); one more depth level makes it exist (0.9258). RV-377-015. Every earlier "no dense winner" was void, not negative.
2. **Admissibility under the ecology's criterion.** Under an all-inputs criterion the non-generalizing memory row was admissible (0.854); under the unseen-input criterion it is not (0.708) and the winner map changes. Parity admits no local form at all. RV-377-014/017/020. The generalization criterion is an axis of Ω, not a detail.
3. **Discoverability by the search/development process** (Codex RP5). Regularized evolution at 10^5 evaluations × 3 seeds never leaves the inert plateau (~0.79) on a single fixed smooth target although the learner exists and scores 0.93: **a fixed program fits one target better than any partial learner, so selection has no gradient toward learning.** RV-377-018. Predicted lever (frozen, then run): task diversity (Chan 2022 / Mikulik 2020 parents). Outcome RV-377-023: under four sign-flipped targets the search leaves the inert plateau in 3/3 seeds and converges on a context-conditioned memory form (one cell overwritten with the last label, multiplied into a fixed input feature: 0.73 in every seed) — the in-context-type form the parents predict under diversity — but still does not assemble the four-write gradient learner (0.92) at 10^5 evaluations. The ecology gate is confirmed; the remaining gap is search budget/family (10^6–10^7, functional-equivalence caching), i.e. #221-class.
4. **Cost** (only now). At equal admissibility the occupant is the cheapest under ρ; the r-axis slope is set by per-event update WORK, not by update locality (GMI-T10-A refuted, RV-377-009): search pays O(|grammar|) per revision (~200× the gradient row), the gradient row O(parameters), memory O(1) update but O(n) query and no generalization.

**d(development)/d(cost).** At the exact layer the only law of this kind is GMI-T10-B: the growth order of each charged phase (build / query / update) in the size ladder is fixed by two coordinates — the basis's store discipline (index → O(log n), scan → O(n)) and the update law (local rewrite → O(1) cells, dense gradient → O(p)) — and compilation preserves it. That is a compilation certificate, not a developmental-productivity law. The productivity question proper (does experience lower the burden of the *next* acquisition — Codex K1/K2 capital, establishment rows E1/E2) is **unestablished**: K1 has real evidence only in #323 (authored-program search), K2 is not established at its tested grammar. "→ ∞" is not supported by anything executed; the amortization inequality H(C_scratch − C_infer) > C_train + C_maint + C_update (N3) bounds it by the reuse horizon.

**Why neural networks dominate — two layers, keep them apart.**

- *Exact layer (executed, tiny scope):* the gradient form wins every revision-rate cell r ≥ 1 at every horizon in every basis on smooth-generalization ecologies (RV-377-017), because it is the only registered form that is simultaneously admissible on unseen inputs and has per-event update work bounded by its parameter count. It loses only where it is inadmissible (parity, exact discrete binding), at r = 0 (search is cheaper when nothing is ever revised), in the gate-level basis B2 where its arithmetic is emulated (~100× dearer), and — predicted, running — at short horizons against a generalizing memory (RV-377-021 H*(r)). **NN dominance at this layer is a region of the (ecology × basis × horizon × revision) diagram, not a universal fact.**
- *Real-system layer (parent hypotheses, not proven):* N1 differentiable credit assignment (update law, not architecture), N2 shared representation across related tasks, N3 amortization over huge reuse horizons, N4 empirical scaling laws, N5 hardware co-design (tensor accelerators), N6 learned plasticity; limits L1–L5 (plasticity loss, forgetting, no exact revision/provenance, sparse feedback, retraining economics). `NEURAL_MORPHOLOGY_PARENT_ANALYSIS_V1` ceiling: `NEURAL_MORPHOLOGY_PHASE_HYPOTHESES_REGISTERED`, not `WHY_NEURAL_NETWORKS_WIN_PROVEN`. Predictions P-N1…P-N5 are frozen there and untested outside the toy layer.

Claim ceiling: `LIMITS_ARE_FOUR_GATES__EXISTENCE_ADMISSIBILITY_DISCOVERABILITY_THEN_COST__EXHIBITED_AT_SCOPE__NEURAL_DOMINANCE_IS_A_REGION_NOT_A_LAW__REAL_LAYER_HYPOTHESES_ONLY`.

## 5. The diagram on the note (NN / OCM / Symbolic)

- **"NN: a lot of development, very powerful."** Supported as a region statement (§3), not as a law. The theory's own prediction is that neural dominance shrinks where exact revision, sparse feedback, or short reuse horizons dominate (P-N2, P-N5, RV-377-021).
- **"OCM: developmental learning → language, math → AGI?"** OCM is the explicit governed developmental morphology (persistent epistemic field, external verification, provenance, lifecycle). Its K1 (search-geometry) evidence is real at registered scope (#323); K2 (developmental capital on fresh families) is **not established** at the tested grammar; the math/code E3 arms are frozen protocols waiting on real episodes (#46, #208). The arrow to AGI is a hypothesis with no executed rung above K1.
- **"Symbolic: ⊗ (dead end)."** Not supported by the theory. The search/program form owns the r = 0 axis of every smooth ecology, owns exact discrete binding ecologies in every basis, and is the predicted occupant of the parity hole. Symbolic forms are region-limited, like every other form; they are not eliminated. What the theory does reject is symbolic (or any) form as the *unique* fundamental unit (§1).
