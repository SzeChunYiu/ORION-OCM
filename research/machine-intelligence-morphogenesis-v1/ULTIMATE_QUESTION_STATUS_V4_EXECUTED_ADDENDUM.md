## 3. Q2/Q3 — what generates and selects a form

**V4 said:** forms are resource-bounded factorizations; selection is `F(Ω, M_avail, ρ, π)` and what is found is `Γ(F, H_dev, B_discover, …)` (RP14, RP5); no demand-only selector.

**Executed evidence instantiates three RP rows and corrects one of our own theorems.**

- **Revision axis (GMI-T10-A) — mechanism refuted, parent instantiated.** Write locality does not set the r-axis slope; charged per-event update WORK does (RV-377-009). On E_smooth the exact-search row holds r = 0 and the gradient row overtakes at r ∈ [1, 2] (B0/B3), [0, 1] (B1/U/P3), never in B2 where its emulated arithmetic makes the update ~100× dearer. This is Codex GMI-RP3 (horizon crossover) with A = desc + H·exec_q and c = upd + ver + rev/4, and it is Levin/OOPS accounting. On E_smooth2 the test is `NOT_TESTABLE_WITH_REGISTERED_ROWS` (dense row 0.841 < 0.85 at 48 events; RV-377-014). **B4 stays NOT_EARNED.**
- **Type axis — as predicted, parent-null consistent.** Discrete exact binding ecologies are solved by memory forms in every column at every (H, r) (Stage E) and a label-free search recovers them (7/9 winners after canonicalization, one seed; RV-377-008/011). This is the Gold/exact-identification null, not a discovery.
- **Existence ≠ discoverability ≠ frontier occupancy (RP15/RP5) — exhibited on one instrument.** The frozen depth-2 update grammar could not express a stable multi-input gradient step (learning rate 1 diverges for ≥ 2 active inputs; one more depth level gives 0.926): the dense form *did not exist* in Stage F's search space (RV-377-015). Once it exists, random + hill-climb at 12 000 evaluations never finds it (plateau 0.7676 of inert programs, two seeds, two depths; RV-377-016). Every earlier "no dense winner" was void, not negative.
- **Same semantic state, different frontier (RP7) — finite instance.** M5 (indexed exemplar memory) and M5L (linear-scan list) are Dev-table-equal at n = 2…256 in nine columns and exec-separated from n = 4 (ratio 1.8 → 7.7). Gavranović 2022 is the categorical parent (lens composition = gradient checkpointing; resource content lives in 2-cells).

**Law status.** What survives as a *law* at scope is the per-phase growth-order certificate: the growth order of each charged phase (build/query/update) is set by two coordinates only — the store discipline the basis supplies (index → O(log n); scan → O(n)) and the update law (local rewrite → O(1) cells; dense gradient → O(p)) — and every compilation preserves it. It is a compilation certificate, data-structure-parent-owned; it makes `store_discipline` measurable label-free, which repairs one classifier defect. It is **not** a predictor of developmental burden on held-out tasks (Codex E1/E2 untested).
