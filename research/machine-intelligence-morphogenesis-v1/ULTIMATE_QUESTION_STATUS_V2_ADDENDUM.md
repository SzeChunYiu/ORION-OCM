# Ultimate-question status — V2 addendum (what the exact-checkable layer changes)

Additive to `ULTIMATE_QUESTION_STATUS_V1.md` (Codex, 2026-09-11). Same date. No terminal in V1 is
weakened; three are sharpened.

## Q1 — unique minimal atom?

V1: `NO_REPRESENTATION_INDEPENDENT_UNIQUE_COGNITIVE_ATOM_ESTABLISHED`; live target = resource-bounded
equivalence class of bases.
V2 adds the **criterion for where such a class could have content at all**: only at resolutions
`K ≺ K_sim(B)` or on the `upd / ver / rev` coordinates (GMI-T0, `proofs/GMI_PROOFS_V1.md` §T0). Above
the band every basis is one class (this is the quantitative twin of the finite flattening theorem).
The four candidates now each carry a frozen sub-band test with its own kill terminal
(`BASIS_CANDIDATES_V2_UNIVERSALITY_TESTS.json`); the expected outcome, if all four pass, is
`MULTIPLE_EQUIVALENT_MINIMAL_BASES` at poly-`K` with coordinate-specialized constants — no atom.

## Q2 — what generates forms?

V1: decomposition `M_B → F_B(E) ← Γ`, `M_{t+1} = U(M_t, e_t)`.
V2 adds a **label-free identity for forms** — five decidable observables
(`MORPHOLOGY_SIGNATURES_V2.json`) — and the **band lemma** (GMI-T3): classes and frontiers transfer
between `K`-equivalent bases up to a computed band, so "which form the basis generates" is
encoding-robust only when the frontier gap exceeds the band. The encoding hostile is now a number.

## Q3 — why does one form dominate a regime?

V1: frontier over a resource vector; one exact toy boundary (parent-owned economics).
V2 adds the first **direction-of-boundary prediction from observables** (GMI-T10-A): at equal
capability, a dense-update class loses to a local-update class as revision frequency rises, with the
sign fixed by `update_locality` before any cost is measured. Parent-anchored axis registry
(`ECOLOGY_AXES_V2.json`): the verification-contract axis (Gold/Pitt–Warmuth vs Angluin), the
feedback-type axis (SQ/parity; differentiable-vs-PAC), data-volume×compute, noise, distribution
structure, task diversity — each with a theorem-grade flip already in the literature (verification
of the primary sources in `PARENT_LEDGER_V2.json`, family P9B). The negative control `PH-5` (parity under
scalar loss only: nothing develops) is mandatory in every future phase diagram.

## Q4 — can the theory predict an undesigned form?

V1: `UNKNOWN_MORPHOLOGY_NOT_YET_PREDICTED_OR_DISCOVERED`. Unchanged as a terminal.
V2 adds the first instrument aimed at it (`SIGNATURE_HOLE_CENSUS_V1`): 2 987 coherent signature cells,
41 occupied by 43 designed forms after a completeness pass. Findings: (i) the first-pass "holes" were
registration gaps in the parent atlas (nine designed systems added), (ii) most surviving structural
holes are degenerate or compiled twins of occupied cycle-forms, (iii) three non-degenerate regions are
**pre-registered** as the only places a positive answer can be *reported* at this resolution
(HOLE-A discrete sample/score rewriting; HOLE-B O(1)-update trace distributions; HOLE-C
likelihood-scored applicability-matched method libraries), and (iv) otherwise an undesigned form
must differ from known forms at a resolution finer than the five observables — i.e. in Stage D's
charged constants or in a sixth observable. This converts Q4 from "not yet" into a falsifiable
pre-registration without authorizing any search.
