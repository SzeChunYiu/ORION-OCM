## Frozen audit conclusions to test

### MC-1 — metacognition and value of computation

Metacognition is registered **externally**: a system has it when its allocation
of charged computation depends on a registered internal estimate of expected
gain, and the only admissible evidence is the external allocation trace. The
exact single-step value-of-computation threshold is that one further
deliberation step is worth its charge **iff** the exact expected improvement in
the value of the finally chosen action strictly exceeds the step's exact
resource price. The allocation rule this induces is optimal at the registered
finite deliberation scope (instrumental backward-induction lemma).

**Hostile twin (required).** A system carrying no internal estimate whatsoever,
running a fixed schedule, that reproduces the metacognitive system's allocation
trace exactly. The registered reading of "reproduces" is pinned here as
**trace-for-trace equality on every registered instance**, not equality of
induced trace distributions. The conclusion to test is an exact **iff**: a
fixed-schedule twin exists exactly when the metacognitive allocation is a
deterministic function of the registered observable instance.

### MC-2 — social cognition / theory of mind

Registered externally: the focal system's behaviour depends on a registered
model of another agent's **unobserved** state, not merely on that agent's
observed action history. The conclusion to test is that this is an
**ecology-relative** property with an exact characterization: the best policy
measurable with respect to the other agent's observed-action channel is
strictly worse than the best policy measurable with respect to the other's
hidden state **iff** the observed-action channel is not sufficient for the
payoff-relevant partition of hidden states, with the gap an exact rational
number. Equality conditions are stated only on the positive-probability
support.

**Hostile twin (required).** An ecology in which a pure behaviour-reader
attains exactly the mentalizer's score (gap `0`), proving the capability is a
property of the ecology-plus-channel and not of the machine alone.

### MC-3 — communication

Registered externally as a charged channel whose use changes the jointly
achievable score. The conclusion to test is an exact threshold: a channel
partition strictly pays **iff** the exact refinement value it delivers strictly
exceeds its exact charged price. The apparent second conjunct "and the receiver
must be able to act differentially on it" is to be **proved equivalent** to the
refinement value being strictly positive, not carried as an independent
premise. Both boundary directions must be exhibited: a free but useless
channel, and a valuable but unaffordable channel.

**Hostile twin (required).** Coordinated behaviour arising from a shared
observation and no channel use at all, attaining the same joint score at zero
channel charge — proving coordination does not identify communication.

### MC-4 — imitation and teaching

Registered externally and separately: **imitation** is a strict fall in the
learner's charged acquisition cost caused by an observed demonstration;
**teaching** is the demonstrator paying a strictly positive charge that causes
that fall. The conclusion to test is that the two are logically independent
(both off-diagonal cells are realizable), that a demonstrator charge which does
not reduce learner burden is `FAILED_TEACHING` and not teaching, that teaching
is jointly worth its cost **iff** the aggregate learner saving strictly exceeds
the demonstrator charge, and that omitting the demonstrator charge from the
lifecycle resource vector strictly enlarges the set of instances declared
worthwhile — i.e. free labour manufactures the result.

**Hostile twin (required).** A learner whose acquisition cost falls for a
registered unrelated concurrent reason while a demonstration is present;
imitation must not be declared without a no-demonstration control arm.

### MC-5 — cultural accumulation

Registered externally: the population's achievable capability at generation
`n+1` strictly exceeds generation `n`'s **starting** capability through a
transmission channel rather than through re-derivation. The conclusion to test
is a complete exact classification of the registered recursion
`a_{n+1} = phi * a_n + g` over exact rationals — the ratchet condition, the
exact ceiling, and the exact return-to-baseline condition — together with the
finding that the **capability trajectory alone does not identify accumulation**.

**Hostile twin (required, and the scientific core of this row).** A
zero-transmission population that re-derives independently every generation and
produces an identical capability trajectory. The depth of the row is not the
twin itself but what follows it: the exact condition under which the **charged
lifecycle resource trajectory** does identify transmission, and the exact
boundary case in which even the resource trajectory fails to separate.

## Recursive-positivity commitment

A conditional finding is not a terminal. Where a derivation needs an extra
premise, the premise is to be named exactly and an unconditional lemma removing
it attempted. Where the obstruction is proved structural, the row closes as a
boundary with the counterexample shipped and the adjacent scoped positive
stated, labelled `EARNED_BY_COUNTEREXAMPLE`. A finding that a capability is
ecology-relative, delivered with the exact relativity condition, is registered
here in advance as a **positive** theorem, not a negative.
