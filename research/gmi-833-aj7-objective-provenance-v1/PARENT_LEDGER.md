# AJ7 strongest-parent / ownership ledger

AJ7 does not claim reward ambiguity, utility theory or inverse reinforcement learning as new mathematics.

Strongest parents include:

- **Ng, Harada & Russell (1999), Policy invariance under reward transformations**: policy-preserving reward transformations / reward shaping ambiguity.
- **Inverse reinforcement learning** beginning with Ng & Russell and subsequent reward-learning literature: behaviour-to-reward inference requires modelling assumptions and generally does not identify a unique reward.
- **Skalse et al. (ICML 2023), Invariance in Policy Optimisation and Partial Identifiability in Reward Learning**: formal characterization of reward ambiguities/invariances for multiple reward-learning data sources.
- **Skalse & Abate (Artificial Intelligence 2026), Partial identifiability and misspecification in inverse reinforcement learning**: broader mathematical analysis of reward ambiguity and misspecification.
- classical **decision/utility theory**: optimal decisions are conditional on a supplied preference/utility/requirement relation.

AJ7 residual: explicit placement of objective provenance as an independent input branch in the AJ operational-bottom GMI spine, plus the simple no-go that identical world dynamics admit incompatible registered requirement functions and therefore cannot alone determine a unique normative ordering.
