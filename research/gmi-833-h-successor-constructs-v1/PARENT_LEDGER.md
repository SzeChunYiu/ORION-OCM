Assimilation-first. Everything below is a parent whose function this package
does not re-invent. The residual is stated last, and it is small.

## 2. External parents

None of the five constructs is new as an object. Each has a strong parent that
owns it, and this package's job is to reach it from a generic grammar, not to
claim it.

**Program identification from a generic grammar.** Solomonoff, *A formal
theory of inductive inference*, Information and Control 7(1):1–22 and
7(2):224–254, 1964, doi:10.1016/S0019-9958(64)90223-2 and
doi:10.1016/S0019-9958(64)90131-7. Gold, *Language identification in the
limit*, Information and Control 10(5):447–474, 1967,
doi:10.1016/S0019-9958(67)91165-5. Koza, *Genetic Programming*, MIT Press,
1992. Schmidt and Lipson, *Distilling free-form natural laws from experimental
data*, Science 324(5923):81–85, 2009, doi:10.1126/science.1165893. Udrescu and
Tegmark, *AI Feynman*, Science Advances 6(16):eaay2631, 2020,
doi:10.1126/sciadv.aay2631.

**The strongest parent of the whole package.** Real, Liang, So and Le,
*AutoML-Zero: evolving machine learning algorithms from scratch*, ICML 2020,
arXiv:2003.03384. It searches a low-level generic instruction set and recovers
recognisable learning machinery without naming it. Absorbed: the ambition, the
family-blindness, the reading of a recovered program as a structural claim.
What this package adds to that parent is not scale and not search quality: it
is **exhaustion** — a registered node budget under which every strictly cheaper
program is enumerated and rejected, so a recovery carries a lower bound rather
than a search outcome.

**Per construct.**

- `COFOLD` / normalisation as a ratio of two co-indexed sums: Pearl,
  *Probabilistic Reasoning in Intelligent Systems*, Morgan Kaufmann, 1988. The
  normalising constant as a second sum over the same index set is his, not
  this package's.
- `STAGE` / depth: Minsky and Papert, *Perceptrons*, MIT Press, 1969; Hornik,
  Stinchcombe and White, *Multilayer feedforward networks are universal
  approximators*, Neural Networks 2(5):359–366, 1989,
  doi:10.1016/0893-6080(89)90020-8. That an affine stage composed with an
  affine stage collapses is textbook; `SC-2` re-derives it exactly on the
  registered rows only so that the twin is a one-property twin.
- `TIE` / weight sharing and equivariance: Fukushima, *Neocognitron*,
  Biological Cybernetics 36:193–202, 1980, doi:10.1007/BF00344251; LeCun et
  al., *Backpropagation applied to handwritten zip code recognition*, Neural
  Computation 1(4):541–551, 1989, doi:10.1162/neco.1989.1.4.541; Cohen and
  Welling, *Group equivariant convolutional networks*, ICML 2016,
  arXiv:1602.07576. The group-action formulation of weight sharing is theirs.
- `COMBINER` / invertible composition with a tractable volume term: Rezende and
  Mohamed, *Variational inference with normalizing flows*, ICML 2015,
  arXiv:1505.05770; Dinh, Sohl-Dickstein and Bengio, *Density estimation using
  Real NVP*, ICLR 2017, arXiv:1605.08803. The triangular-map construction whose
  Jacobian is a product of scale factors is theirs. The only move here is to
  keep it **exact and rational** by accumulating the product instead of a
  logarithm.
- `REDUCE` / inference as search over the response space: LeCun, Chopra,
  Hadsell, Ranzato and Huang, *A tutorial on energy-based learning*, in
  *Predicting Structured Data*, MIT Press, 2006. The argmin-over-responses
  formulation is theirs.

## 3. What is explicitly NOT claimed novel

The five families. The five constructs as mathematical objects. Symbolic
regression, program induction, minimum-description-length model selection,
equivariance theory, normalizing flows, energy-based inference, universal
approximation. Any statement about real systems at real scale.

## 4. The residual contribution of this tranche

One generic grammar extension — *a fold is a triple (combining operation,
body, index set); several folds may share one index set; folds may be composed
to depth two; a tying map may route index positions to parameter slots; a
reduction may range over a registered finite response set* — under which, at
five registered derivational scopes:

1. each family's defining functional is **outside** the image of `G_S` at the
   registered leaves and budgets, by exhaustion over the enumerated stratum,
   and
2. it is **inside** `G_H` at an exactly stated minimal charged cost, recovered
   by a family-blind ascending-cost search whose every strictly cheaper
   competitor was enumerated and rejected,
3. with a one-property negative twin per scope whose predicate is required to
   flip, and a base-rate null over the grammar rather than the vacuous
   response-permutation null that an exact-agreement criterion would make
   content-free.

That is the whole residual. It is a derivational result at derivational
scopes. It closes no Section-H row, and `R11` is refused, not deferred.
