## What is explicitly NOT claimed novel

- The boundary law, its derivation at `A = 2`, or its prospective validation.
- The candidate universe, the scoring interface, or the `20` frozen worlds.
- The concepts *order parameter*, *first-order transition*, *latent heat*,
  *finite-size scaling*, or the chance rate `1 - 1/A`.
- Any physical or thermodynamic content. The transition here is a property of an
  exact finite optimisation, not of a many-body system; there is no
  thermodynamic limit and no universality class is claimed.

## Residual contribution of this tranche

1. The **uniformity** result `CP-2`: every stateless candidate — not merely the
   optimal one — sits at exactly `N/2` delay errors, for every `L >= 2`. This
   turns the threshold derivation into a counting argument and is what makes
   `CP-4` and `CP-5` provable rather than merely enumerable.
2. The **exact latent-quantity relation** `CP-3`: the jump in `dJ*/dlambda`
   equals the jump in the order parameter, exactly, in `Q`, at every registered
   world — together with the exact-tie and zero-width statements that only
   survive under exact arithmetic.
3. **Zero finite-size drift** `CP-4`, proved `L`-uniformly and verified
   exhaustively at four rungs.
4. The **refutation of the registered law's own naive extrapolation** `CP-5`:
   `lambda* = eta*p/2` is an `A = 2` special case of `eta*p*(1-1/A)`. This is a
   correction to this programme's own claim ceiling, published as a failed
   prediction rather than absorbed silently.
5. The **blindness measurement** on the null: an `A = 2`-only adjudicator is
   blind to a measured `14/200` of randomized scaling laws, and the blind set is
   verified to consist exactly of the laws that agree at `A = 2`. The number is
   published rather than the blindness being asserted away.
