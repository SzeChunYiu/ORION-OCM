# Parents, applicability and complete declared costs

## Assimilation before mechanism
[DC-1](../gmi-developmental-capital-v1/DEVELOPMENTAL_CAPITAL_V1.md) distinguishes
new capital from a solved task. Its registered retry requires a new K1 mediator
and strongest-parent subtraction. The archived originals are the binding authority.
The original #323 population criterion and its 2/9 negative remain unchanged.

[M2 guided-first](../m2-traversal-capital-v1/m2p1/CORE.md) already separates a useful
mined library from a controller whose apparent gain disappears against a fixed
same-library parent. We inherit library-priority search, expose an independently
generated same-policy parent, and claim no residual advantage.
This is an adaptation to a tiny word grammar, not a rerun of M2's protected assay.

[DreamCoder](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.08381), pp.2–6, separately learns reusable
program abstractions and a search guide; shortening expressions and reducing
search breadth are distinct mechanisms. The current construction uses explicit
stored programs and an authored deterministic order. It does not implement the
paper's neural guide or reproduce its experiments. Library learning is inherited.

## Meter
A unit is an explicitly declared abstract operation, not a CPython opcode or
hardware operation. Each event has integer quantity and unit price one:
task-coordinate read, candidate construction (one control plus each token),
instruction dispatch, primitive arithmetic, macro lookup, output comparison,
body flatten/copy, admission check, name/store update, state-copy entry/token,
gate update, and retained-token holding per phase boundary.
Search evaluates all four inputs even on an early mismatch. Macro lookup never
replaces execution of the primitive body. Independent verification is paid too.
The abstract register treats bounded candidate construction/control as its stated
per-token operation. Host allocation, Python loop overhead, timing, energy and
bit complexity require another validated meter and are not silently identified
with this one. The finite run's integers and every output are retained.

Conditional K2 acquisition cost starts after a common prior-history checkpoint.
Full history construction is additionally charged in a separate lifetime total.
A cheaper conditional acquisition therefore need not repay history creation.
Matched same-state lesion/sham/restoration costs are assay costs, not forgotten
developmental costs. If deployment retains unbounded time, this phase-based
holding contract must be replaced; no continuous-time storage price is inferred.
