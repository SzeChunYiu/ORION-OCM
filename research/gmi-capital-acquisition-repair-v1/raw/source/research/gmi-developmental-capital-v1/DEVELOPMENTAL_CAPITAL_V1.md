# Developmental capital: first order supported, second order not

Status: **TWO-CLAUSE RESULT; CLAUSE 1 MEASURED, CLAUSE 2 NOT_ESTABLISHED**
Date: 2026-09-13

Ledger item 10 asks for two things that are usually run together and must not be:

1. experience yields **more stored knowledge**, and
2. experience yields a **better way of acquiring future intelligence**.

The repository separates them as K1 and K2, and they have different verdicts.

## 1. DC-1 — the criteria are distinct, and the second is strictly stronger

`GMI-T07` (K1) holds when inherited state *causally changes future cognition
generation before target success* and reduces verified burden against matched
controls, with pre-solution mediator `rank_H(m*) < rank_RESET(m*)`.

`GMI-T08` (K2) requires cheaper acquisition of **new K1**:

    E[B_H^acq(K1_new)] < E[B_RESET^acq(K1_new)].

K2 is second order. Cheaper solving of future *targets* does not establish it;
only cheaper acquisition of future *capital* does. A unit that shows K1 and
reports "experience improves learning" has not shown K2.

## 2. DC-2 — clause 1 is supported at its registered scope

The behavioural receipt records, over 29 worlds and 2,741 protected targets:

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| `rank_H` earlier than `rank_0` | 2,536 (92.5% of 2,741) |
| `rank_H` later | 202 |
| neither earlier nor later | 3 |
| median bits of search saved | 1.91 |
| worlds with every target earlier | 18 / 29 |

Two guards make this a statement about cognition generation rather than recall:
`m* ∉ history` is enforced by gate G2 with a `LEAKAGE_ALARM`, and `rank_0`
equals the ecology's recorded `baseline_first_index` on all 2,741 targets.

Scope: one authored program-search grammar. Not a claim about neural training,
open-ended ecologies, or any system outside that assay.

## 3. DC-3 — clause 2 is not established, and the negative is the finding

The prospectively registered attempt to show retained capital made acquisition
of new K1 cheaper terminated `NOT_ESTABLISHED`: the registered recombination
bar held on **2 of 9** fresh seeds. Two independent documents record this.

This is a registered negative at a named grammar, not an impossibility result.
Attribution is single-stage and explicit: the failure is at *retained-capital
recombination in the #323 grammar*, not at retention, not at measurement, and
not at the K2 definition.

## 4. DC-4 — what would close clause 2

The lever implied by DC-3's attribution is a different recombination operator
over retained capital, holding the assay, the bar and the seed protocol fixed.
A closure requires the bar to hold on a preregistered majority of fresh seeds
under an unchanged gate, with the 2/9 result retained as the prior attempt.

Not claimed here: that such an operator exists, or that 2/9 is near a boundary.
A second failure at a new grammar would make a structural obstruction the more
economical reading, and that possibility is left open rather than pre-judged.

## 5. Scope and falsifiers

Not claimed: K1 at any scope beyond the authored grammar; K2 at any scope; that
`rank_H < rank_0` implies improved final capability; that bits saved convert to
wall-clock or energy.

Falsified if the receipt's counts differ from those recorded here, if the
earlier/later/tied counts fail to sum to the target total, if K1 and K2 are
shown to be the same criterion, or if any document reports K2 as established.
