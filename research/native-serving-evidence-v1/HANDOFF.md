# Native OCM serving adoption

The local research module now routes a native proof task through the actual OCM
PROGRAMMATIC dispatcher, checks the issued proof independently, and commits a durable
use record. The learned I method is causally present in the enabled successful proof.

This is serving adoption of externally acquired methods. It is not endogenous OCM
acquisition, a new algorithm, a breakthrough result, open language competence or a
merged/installed product. The strongest conventional parent reproduces the computation.

The false tasks exhausted the registered bound and returned CANNOT_CHECK. Their issued

observations, not an established speedup. Cold means fresh process/compiler, not
evicted OS caches.
