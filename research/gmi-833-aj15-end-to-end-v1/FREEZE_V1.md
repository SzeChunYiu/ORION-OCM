6. The integrated theory must also preserve AJ10's `UNKNOWN_MORPHOLOGY` output channel; UNKNOWN need not imply novelty.

- either blind large-scale search uses a forbidden family/architecture token or registry data;
- the two blind procedures disagree behaviorally without an explicit failure/sensitivity terminal;
- `CURRENT_B` requires or selects >1 state under the frozen exact-behavior-then-positive-state-price rule;
