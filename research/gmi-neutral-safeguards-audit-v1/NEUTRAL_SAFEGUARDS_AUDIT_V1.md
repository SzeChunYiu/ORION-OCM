# Neutral-emergence safeguards audit v1

## Audited suite

The target suite is the four-family, two-grammar exact recovery in
`gmi-cross-grammar-four-family-v1`, joined to the five generic search methods
in `gmi-section-e-searcher-comparison-v2`.  This is a closed registered scope;
adding a future neutral experiment requires a new audit.

The recovery grammars exhaust direct finite structures and independently
structured expression/branch programs.  There are no mutation operators in
either recovery grammar, hence no target family receives a privileged one.
Both use scalar Boolean operations only, with no tensor, accelerator or hidden
hardware price.  Within each ecology every candidate sees the same complete
input/output relation and exact verifier.  The scorer uses only exactness,
declared description cost and a stable neutral tie-break; family labels are
introduced only after raw winners are selected.

## Search accounting and algorithms

Across the eight positive and eight twin searches, all 104,240 candidate
attempts are counted: 104 exact candidates and 104,136 failures.  The E2
receipt separately charges proposal/mutation attempts, truth-example touches,
unique candidates, and arithmetic update operations for uniform random,
strict evolution, neutral GP/CGP-style drift, NAS-like ablation and
DARTS-like gradient search.  Its cost ceiling and over-budget NAS result remain
visible rather than being normalized away.

## Enrichment and failure

The target post-run phenotype is recovered in both grammars for each of four
positive ecologies: 8/8 grammar-family cells.  It is recovered in 0/8 matched
twins; every twin selects a cheaper differently classified raw winner.  This
is exact enrichment at the bounded suite, not four anecdotes.

E2 preserves two negative terminals: strict evolution fails on its plateau,
and the misspecified singleton grammar states
`REPRESENTATION_MISSPECIFIED_NO_EXACT_AFFINE_TARGET`.  Neither is renamed as a
different kind of success.

## Claim ceiling

All ten safeguards are green for this closed finite suite only.  The audit does
not certify future grammars, real hardware equality, or universal searcher
fairness.
