# Parent / non-novelty ledger

AJ0 claims no new mathematical theorem. It is scientific-governance work that prevents category errors between a formal presentation, a finite axiomatisation, operational modelling choices, and ontological commitments.

Parent ideas that motivate the distinction include ordinary model theory (theory versus model/presentation), universal algebra (presentations versus abstract algebraic structure), operational/process theories (state/process roles), and the general philosophy-of-science distinction between modelling assumptions and ontological claims.

Novelty claim: none at the parent-mathematics level. The repository contribution is the explicit cross-package foundation-layer registry, executable anti-promotion gate, and AJ closure discipline.
