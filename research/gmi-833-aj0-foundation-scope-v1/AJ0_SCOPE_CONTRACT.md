# AJ0 Foundation-layer and claim-demotion contract

AJ0 is a **claim-scope repair**, not a new ontology theorem.

## Binding demotions

1. `G0-reg-v1` remains a registered architecture-uncommitted machine-generation grammar with explicit bias. Its existing instruction-class minimality is relative to a frozen operational requirement suite; it is **not** the ultimate, unique, or ontologically minimal substrate of machine intelligence.
2. The six-axiom `gmi-833-axiom-core-v1` remains a compact finite architecture-neutral theory core at its implemented semantics. Finite satisfiability is **not** absolute consistency and the axiom set is **not** an ultimate ontological basis.
3. `state carrier` is an operationally convenient object at current higher layers. AJ2 must test whether state/effect roles can be reconstructed from lower process/context structure. No current result establishes state as logically or ontologically primitive.
4. Every registered flagship result has a `FOUNDATION_LAYER` entry in `FOUNDATION_LAYER_REGISTRY.json`. Layer tags locate claims; they do not assert metaphysical priority.
5. `OPERATIONAL_PRIMITIVE -> ONTOLOGICALLY_FUNDAMENTAL` is forbidden unless a separately registered derivation theorem **and** invariance theorem support the promotion.

## Claim ceiling

`AJ0_FOUNDATION_SCOPE_AND_ANTI_PROMOTION_CONTRACT_AT_REGISTERED_FLAGSHIP_SCOPE`

AJ0 does not establish the operational bottom, process-theory uniqueness, foundation independence, physical completeness, or a full GMI theory.
