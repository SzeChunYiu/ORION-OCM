# Actual-source development trajectory v1

This is a new development pilot, not a replay promoted into protected evidence.
The complete machine, finite candidate catalogue, three attempted cycles,
semantic IDs, quality/resource gate and budgets are in
`DEVELOPMENT_REGISTRATION_V1.json`, frozen with executable sources before
outcomes. Seeds are unnecessary: all component algorithms and task choices are
deterministic. Source algorithms and exact worlds are the pinned E1 relevance
and E3 dependency components from the actual failed cognitive-ladder studies.
Original sweep scripts and outcomes are not modified or rerun as confirmatory
studies. New outputs have a new directory.

The proposer first receives the six sanitized historic summaries. Incomplete
original traces stay UNKNOWN. Actual baseline and counterfactual executions
then generate new measured evidence. External audit mapping and raw descriptive
trace labels are withheld from the finite proposal controller; that controller
sees opaque candidates, declared write classes and numeric measured outcomes.
The engineering team has seen the human diagnoses. This is interface blinding,
not an operating-system isolation claim or a blinded human experiment.

## Executed treatment

One persistent M11 machine begins with the actual discovering relevance arm and
learned dependency arm. Its replaceable configuration routes to existing
algorithm implementations. Every candidate changes one dispatcher coordinate;
all candidates are available from the beginning and no generation-dependent
repair list exists. Choice is generic ordered finite search: pay for candidates
at the lowest change class, retain measured Pareto improvements, select by
quality then registered lexicographic resource ordering. This is a conventional
search policy, not learned meta-cognition or invented mechanism structure.

Two resource coordinates are gated: donor counted work including construction,
maintenance, query/revision and (for relevance) checker expansions; persistent
store/index bytes. Exact task-quality noninferiority and zero stale/false
commitment violations are mandatory. Gains must recur on the disjoint
development shadow target without quality/resource regression on preservation
samples. Predicted per-task resource deltas and calibration errors are retained.
Source counters are inherited instrumentation, not a new independent bit-cost
audit. New probe, shadow, restart and host custody cost are reported separately.

The external host supplies the adopter and fixed policy. The machine cannot
change it. M11 registers prediction before shadow results, binds the proposed
plain-data config, admits only after external checks, persists and cold-restarts.
Every cycle then evaluates fresh tasks against the previous config with full
recomputation allowed. A rollback audit occurs on a copied host store so its
outcome cannot silently replace the evolved trajectory.

Fresh monitoring passes measured quality and resource ratios into M11, and an
external zero-regression resource gate invokes rollback for an adopted change
that regresses after restart. Fresh numerical prediction deltas are reported
for calibration, without claiming a calibrated bound that was never registered.
The persisted evolving object is the dispatch configuration and M11 history.
Each challenger task constructs a new donor field/library; this is not yet
persistence/migration of the complete acquired cognitive field.

## Experiences and boundaries

Relevance tasks use the same acquired game-rule objects, observations and store
growth machinery as E1, at newly registered exact query positions. Dependency
tasks use original E3 catalogues and revision events; a second declared ordering
reverses three independent-family events while preserving the redundant-pair
order. Store scales and executable positions/orders distinguish semantic IDs.
Families overlap across tasks: this is NOT cross-family or cross-domain holdout.
An episode is a complete query/revision lifecycle, not an individual primitive
operation. Three calls do not establish three generations.

F1/F5/F6 have summary intake only; no missing causal trace or challenger is
invented. F2 and F3 derive from one source observation and are not independent
historical cases. The third attempted cycle is further pressure from the same
relevance mechanism at greater scale; no promise of another beneficial change.

## Parents, falsifiers and completion

The identical ordered finite search over matched candidates is a conventional
AutoML parent by construction. A gain here is not residual superiority over
that parent. Random-search order replay may be analyzed as a diagnostic on
observed probes; it cannot substitute for full equally adaptive trajectories.
Bayesian, evolutionary, repair, learned-selector, reflection and neural parents
remain NOT RUN. The concurrent human-authored polynomial repair is a separate
engineering comparator, not an internally generated solution to F1.

Falsifiers: no observed adequate repair; only unidentifiable responsibility;
shadow regression; proposal costs exceed useful future savings; failure after
restart; preservation/rollback violation; fewer than three earned generations;
or conventional parent sufficiency. Report every attempted cycle, including
failures, and stop after the frozen three-cycle budget. No expansion of that
budget to manufacture success. A successor investigation requires a new
development version and preserves this run.

After development the machine emits a freeze record and rejects further
development intake. This exercises the boundary only: its protected protocol
is E0/NOT_READY and protected execution remains NOT RUN. The complete success
criterion in PROGRAMME.md is not discharged by this pilot.
