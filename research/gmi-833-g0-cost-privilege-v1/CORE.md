# GMI #833 E7 — registered G0 cost-privilege boundary

This capsule answers one question only: whether the registered finite G0 cost object can justify the statement that the grammar does not privilege a known family.

The answer is deliberately split.

* **Positive at a narrow scope:** the cost depends only on raw `(description length, reachability distance)`, not on post-hoc family labels, and is invariant under certified cost/search isometries.
* **Negative at the stronger scope:** equal semantic coverage does not imply equal cost geometry. A same-semantics non-isometric grammar pair reverses the selected semantic class under the same positive weights.

Therefore the scientific disposition is a boundary theorem, not `G0_UNBIASED`.
