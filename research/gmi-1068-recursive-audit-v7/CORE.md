## Core objects and their dependencies

1. **Processes.** A declared typed process category supplies possible
   transformations and composition. Restricting arrows preserves that structure
   only when identities and composition remain admitted. Resource-dependent
   admission may require resource state in the objects.
2. **Contexts.** Declared observation/evaluation maps, task targets, orders and
   comparison rules say what counts as success. Process law alone does not
   select a unique objective.
3. **Attainability.** The evaluated image of admitted finite histories describes
   reachable contextual outcomes. Orders, resource families and intervention
   semantics must be supplied when those questions are asked.
4. **State and information.** Histories may be identified only relative to
   specified future tests. For deterministic decision signals, refinement
   transports attainable loss profiles and is characterized by all binary
   decision problems under the stated policy assumptions.
5. **Construction and transfer.** A representation theorem, actual discovery,
   compiler correctness, model choice and empirical usefulness are different
   claims. None can be substituted for the others.

The registered dependency graph and all original requirement IDs are in
[SCOPE_SNAPSHOT_V7.json](SCOPE_SNAPSHOT_V7.json). The graph does not assert that
every listed premise has been established.

## What prevents whole-programme closure

The [concrete repair queue](NEXT_REPAIRS_V7.md) gives the next changes and
falsifying checks for each unfinished region.

The exact source and task domains for broader families are not yet uniformly
compiled, discovered and independently validated. R7/R8's historical supplied
candidates do not establish blind recovery. R9–R12 still need their missing
mechanism/parent derivations, R13 needs new real-scale evidence, and R14 needs
full intended-statement coverage. Earlier R0–R5 integrated reconciliation also
remains. R15–R17 cannot certify the unfinished chain.

All 222 original requirements remain unresolved at integrated round scope.
Local theorems are recorded separately so that this conservative status does
not erase their evidence. Final audit closure, if later earned, will remain
relative to a declared scope and cannot certify absence of unknown gaps.
