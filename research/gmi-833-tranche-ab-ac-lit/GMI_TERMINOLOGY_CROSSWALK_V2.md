> Match key: `EXACT` = an established academic term accurately matches the object (adopt it in
> paper-facing text); `PARTIAL` = established term covers part of the object, the residue must be
> qualified; `NON` = no adequate established term, a new term is permitted only with parent-term
> comparison and an operational definition (AB: "new GMI terms are allowed only when existing
> terminology is demonstrably insufficient").
>
> Migration rule key: RENAME / QUALIFY / PAPER-RENAME (paper-facing text only; internal tooling may keep
> the legacy name) / DEFINE (define operationally, then use).
>
> Citation status: `VERIFIED` = the reference was checked against a live source during tranche build
> (2026-09-15); otherwise the reference is a canonical field anchor entered from field knowledge and
> MUST be re-verified by the AC re-run before any manuscript freeze (AB/AC box: "Re-run literature
> search before manuscript freeze").
