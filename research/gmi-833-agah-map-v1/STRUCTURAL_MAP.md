## Where the real science is

Four clusters carry the genuine remaining derivation, in dependency order:

1. **AG6 rows 34–36 — three radically different universal bases.** Only the register/counter basis exists on `main`. A combinatory/rewrite basis and a cellular/local basis must be built at bounded executable scope before compilation overhead, description bias, reachability geometry and search burden can be compared, and before the decisive cross-basis MI-recovery test can run. This is the largest gap in AG/AH and the one that must not be rushed.
2. **AG2 row 13 + AG5 rows 30–32 — the G0 extension lowering ledger.** AJ5's `primitive_status` covers exactly five opcodes. The registered stochastic, local-graph, channel/tool and governed-self-change families each *introduce* a named operator and none has an AG5 status. This is the most tractable of the four and is specified as the immediate next tranche.
3. **AH4 rows 63–66 — the L0–L8 organization ladder.** AJ4's 260 organizations and AJ11's atlas supply the carrier; per-transition invariants and level-indexed nearest negatives do not exist.
4. **AG7 row 41, AH3 row 62 — emergence rather than primitive naming.** Metareasoning and endogenous experiment/search selection have no coverage at all; closing on partial coverage would narrow the rows' stated meaning.

`AG8` row 48 (independent formal-logic/foundations review of the stopping point) is `EXTERNAL-GATE` and is left open deliberately: a hurried model proxy is exactly the `POST_HOC_SUSPECT` class this corpus has already had to correct once.
