# Parents, operational costs and remaining obligations

Tian and Pearl (2000), [§4.2.3, eq25](https://ftp.cs.ucla.edu/pub/stat_ser/r271-A.pdf),
own the sharp binary PN bounds. Their response-type formulation is adopted;
this unit supplies a direct endpoint constructor and independent exact controls.
Balke and Pearl's [counterfactual computation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1302.6784)
provides the mature computational parent. These mechanisms are inherited.

Pearl's [SCM overview](https://ftp.cs.ucla.edu/pub/stat_ser/r350.pdf) supplies
surgical semantics, the identification/estimation distinction and the need
for causal premises. The unchanged CAU1–4 parent supplies the explicit complete
decoder-view and compatible-model-class qualifications. The finite procedure
is exhaustive elimination over that declared class, not a new causal calculus.

The cost boundary is explicit. The finite solver reports the actual number of
candidate evaluations. It computes nine joint laws per candidate, visiting
eight response types per law, plus validation, comparison, target evaluation
and selection work. The census records actual model/fiber/law comparison and
attainment counts. These are algorithm-operation counts, not total CPU cycles,
bit complexity, physical joules or paid access costs. No speedup or whole-cost
advantage is claimed. Rational numerator/denominator bit growth, constructing
the class, exact-law acquisition and scientific premise acquisition remain
additional costs for any deployment comparison.

Finite enumeration is a reference procedure; the direct inherited bounds avoid
model enumeration for this binary PN query. Neither exact population access
nor a complete causal class is free evidence about a physical setting. A
downstream GMI claim must pay its actual data/model/premise acquisition,
verification, computation, storage and intervention operations under its own
resource contract. This artifact never imputes those unknown prices as zero.

The following demands remain open: effective general structure discovery,
learned causal-premise warrant, finite-sample inference, interventions whose
surgical validity is established physically, and transfer to arbitrary graph
classes. Closing an exact-query subproblem does not close those demands.
