# Operational (κ, Ω, χ)

Theory V0.2 (PR #150) and the source review qualifications in
`research/evolvability-source-review-v1/` are adopted as **definitions**.
They are not OCM measurements of self-change.

## κ — locality / effective coupling

**Definition (code):**

```text
κ_H(z) = |impact_cone(ks, {z})| / |V_t|
```

`impact_cone` is `ocm.kso.revocation.impact_cone` (KS-T09). `|V_t|` is the
registered component/atom set of the self-model or field under change.

**Self-change measurement:** `CANNOT_CHECK_NO_SELF_MODEL_COMPONENT_GRAPH_FOR_REAL_INCIDENTS`.
F1–F6 do not expose a KnowledgeSpace of cognitive components for the failing
machine.

**Receipt proxy (not self-change κ):** on F4’s 120 numeric `step_table` rows,
`expected_cone / N` has min 0.00125, median 0.005625, mean 0.009, max 0.025.
Arm names were not used as root-cause labels. This is dependency-revision
locality on a pinned ladder receipt, not κ of dispatcher self-evolution.

Query `k/N` in the F2/F3 subspace table is a sparsity statistic, not κ.

## Ω — self-identifiability per diagnostic cost

**Definition:**

```text
Ω(ε) = H(Z) / B_id(ε)
```

`B_id` needs an allowed probe policy and `P(Ẑ ≠ Z) ≤ ε`. The source review
requires a transcript chain-rule bound, not a marginal bit/probe slogan.

**Measurement:** `CANNOT_CHECK_NO_PER_PROBE_TRANSCRIPT`. F5 lacks per-probe
choices; F1/F6 lack instance traces; Z must not enter the proposer channel.

The PR150 Fano example (m=30, ε=0.05, ≥4.3776 bits) is synthetic theory
pressure, not an incident result.

## χ — repair searchability

**Definition:** `χ(e) = 2^H(R|e)` for a **calibrated** posterior over
successful repairs. Entropy of a wrong heuristic is not search cost
(source review).

**Measurement:** `CANNOT_CHECK_NO_CALIBRATED_REPAIR_POSTERIOR`.

**Fallback allowed by the theory when no posterior exists:** complete
candidate count and evaluation-cost distribution from the pinned cycles:

- `|R| = 10`
- all C1, origin `existing_alternative`
- 8 evaluations per cycle

That count is not χ.
