## Current residual candidate

After subtraction, the surviving hypothesis is not any single mechanism above.

It is the joint lifetime phenomenon:

> A persistent heterogeneous machine converts verified task experience and verified self-interventions into explicit, revisable causal structure about both the world and its own cognition, and that accumulated structure causally lowers the information and search required for later learning and later self-change while external checking/adoption authority remains fixed.

This must be compared against a strongest parent *product* that composes the relevant mechanisms above.
