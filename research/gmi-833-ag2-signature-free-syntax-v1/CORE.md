## Boundary you must not drop

The AJ5 terminal histogram **saturates at step budget 6** (budgets 6–13 all give `63/107/314`), and
`8 / 200` randomized opcode-role permutations reproduce it. The histogram therefore does **not**
identify the semantics by itself. The identification rests on the full behaviour map, on which the
two independent routes agree exactly and which `0 / 200` randomized semantics reproduce.
