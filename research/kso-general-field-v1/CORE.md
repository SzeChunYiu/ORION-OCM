## Question

On the production Knowledge Space Object, with extra language / mathematics /
procedural types registered on a *local* `TypeRegistry` (never on
`DEFAULT_REGISTRY`):

1. Do language, mathematics, and procedural/scientific types register without
   changing core warrant join / meet / liveness / compose?
2. Does speaker commitment stay distinct from world truth (authority product
   lattice; meet, not max)?
3. Does formal-proof authority stay distinct from empirical applicability?
4. Does retrieved similarity (non-dependency `SIMILAR_TO` activation) stay
   distinct from identity and warrant?
5. Does a shared identity require a correspondence witness (compose bridge),
   rather than equal `content_ref`?
6. Does revision propagate across domain views by dependency (compose /
   impact cone), leaving unrelated competence live?
7. Is the same executive (`compose`, `fixed_point`, `impact_cone`) used on
   every domain view?

If those hold, `CURRENT_KSO_ALREADY_GENERAL_ENOUGH` is the success terminal
named by the issue. Current KSO receives first right of refusal; this capsule
does not create a second truth store.

- Shared `fact` (ev:src) composed into language / math / procedural views.
