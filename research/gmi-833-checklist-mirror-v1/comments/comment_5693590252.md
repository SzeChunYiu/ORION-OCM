## AH — Machine-intelligence cosmology: elementary substrates, transformation laws, and emergent MI hierarchy

This addendum captures the intended physical-cosmology analogy explicitly. The target is not merely a minimal language that can *describe* known machine intelligences. The target is to identify the lowest-level substrate/process ingredients and lawful interactions from which higher computational organizations can emerge, combine, persist, adapt, reproduce, self-modify, and eventually instantiate known and as-yet-unseen machine-intelligence forms.

This is a research programme, not a claim that the elementary objects have already been identified.

### AH0 — Physics analogy, stated precisely

Use the analogy only structurally:

```text
physics: elementary entities/fields + interaction laws
      -> bound states/composites
      -> atoms
      -> molecules/materials
      -> self-maintaining/autocatalytic organizations
      -> living/adaptive systems

GMI: elementary computational substrates + admissible transformation laws
   -> stable/composable process motifs
   -> stateful computational organizations
   -> reusable modules/representations/operators
   -> adaptive/developmental organizations
   -> machine-intelligence morphologies/species
   -> meta-developmental/open-ended MI systems
```

Do not assume the mapping is one-to-one or that MI has literal particles.

- [ ] Define what would count as an elementary **entity/substrate**, an elementary **transformation/reaction**, and a higher **organization** in GMI.
- [ ] Require that elementary objects also generate abundant non-intelligent structures; otherwise intelligence has likely been smuggled into the substrate.
- [ ] Require higher MI properties to appear only after lawful composition/development, not by primitive naming.

### AH1 — Candidate pre-intelligence “reaction” object

A deliberately weak candidate is a typed transformation/relation over substrates/resources:

```text
r : X_in -> X_out
```

or more generally

```text
r ⊆ Multiset(X) × Multiset(X)
```

for concurrent/compositional resource use, and

```text
K(dx' | x,u)
```

for stochastic state transition.

The important object is not the notation but the invariant content:

```text
substrates/states
+ admissible transformations
+ composition/concurrency
+ interaction/boundary channels
+ resource accounting
```

- [ ] Compare deterministic functions, relations, stochastic kernels, labelled transitions, rewrite reactions, process morphisms and constructor-theoretic tasks as candidate elementary descriptions.
- [ ] Find exact finite translations where possible.
- [ ] Permit more than one equivalent elementary formalism if no unique one is justified.

### AH2 — Strong parent analogies to absorb, not rename

1. **Constructor theory of information/computation:** information is characterized through possible/impossible transformations on substrates rather than assumed as an implementation-specific entity.
2. **Artificial chemistry / reaction-network approaches:** resources/entities plus elementary reactions can generate self-maintaining and autocatalytic organizations; higher organization is not placed in the individual reaction symbol.
3. **Universal algebra / Lawvere theories:** presentations/generators can differ while the abstract theory remains equivalent.
4. **Coalgebra / transition-system theory:** many state-based dynamical computational systems admit a common process-level semantics.
5. **Cellular automata/combinatory/register universality:** tiny local bases can already be computationally universal, proving that universality alone is not the intelligence-emergence criterion.

Every claimed GMI novelty must state what these parents already explain.

### AH3 — “MI atoms” must satisfy stronger criteria than Turing universality

A proposed elementary GMI substrate is not accepted merely because it can simulate arbitrary programs.

Required criteria:

```text
GENERATIVE:     can compose into broad computational organizations;
NEUTRAL:        does not name or privilege known MI families;
NONTRIVIAL:     generates both intelligent and non-intelligent organizations;
CROSS-BASIS:    equivalent higher laws survive materially different presentations;
RESOURCE-AWARE: compilation/development costs are explicit;
DEVELOPMENTAL:  supports transitions between organizations;
OPEN:           can admit new effective units/macros without redefining the base ad hoc;
FALSIFIABLE:    finite microscopes can refute claimed emergence laws.
```

- [ ] Reject `UNIVERSAL_COMPUTATION == INTELLIGENCE_ATOM`.
- [ ] Test whether candidate atoms support emergence of memory, routing, search, probabilistic state, symbolic rewriting, learning laws and self-modification without those appearing as primitive macros.

### AH4 — Hierarchy of organization / “periodic table” of MI

Attempt to derive an architecture-name-free hierarchy:

```text
L0 elementary substrate/process events
L1 stable local motifs / reusable transition patterns
L2 state-bearing modules / bounded computational organizations
L3 interacting modules / composed machines
L4 adaptive organizations whose internal state/update changes from experience
L5 developmental organizations that alter representation/operators/search law
L6 self-modeling/self-modifying organizations under verification/governance
L7 populations/collectives/cultural inheritance / multi-instance development
L8 open-ended grammar/theory expansion and new effective units
```

This hierarchy is provisional and must be parent-subtracted and falsified.

- [ ] Define measurable invariants for each transition `L_i -> L_(i+1)`.
- [ ] Construct nearest negative systems that remain at `L_i`.
- [ ] Determine whether any levels collapse or require additional intermediate layers.
- [ ] Do not define intelligence by membership in a level until capability/development evidence warrants it.

### AH5 — Emergence criterion

For a higher organization `O` built from lower components `{x_i}` and interaction law `R`, require more than syntactic aggregation.

Candidate criterion:

```text
O counts as a higher computational unit at registered scope only if
(a) it has a stable/reusable interface or behavioral equivalence class,
(b) replacing the internal network by the higher unit preserves protected semantics,
(c) the higher unit changes resource/search/developmental geometry materially,
(d) the effect survives alternative low-level presentations.
```

This links to existing HSG major-transition/module-promotion work without claiming novelty for abstraction itself.

### AH6 — Evolution of all seen and unseen MI forms

The scientifically defensible target is not an effective semantic enumeration of every unbounded possible intelligence. Instead:

```text
finite registered substrate + finite resource bound
    -> exact complete census/atlas where tractable;

unbounded substrate/process theory
    -> generative expressibility + structural theorems + impossibility boundaries
       + sampling/search over larger regions + UNKNOWN residual classes.
```

- [ ] Prove exact completeness of bounded `MI-space` slices where finite.
- [ ] Evolve/search from elementary substrate without family labels and recover multiple known MI classes.
- [ ] Hold out known families from phenotype classification until after generation/evaluation.
- [ ] Preserve `UNKNOWN` and search for unexplained stable organizations/capability frontiers.
- [ ] For any candidate unseen form, require fresh predictions from its inferred organization law before novelty wording.

### AH7 — Data does not create the MI cosmos; it changes selection/development

Once the generative MI space exists, information/data affects which organizations are favored and which developmental paths are accessible.

Schematic separation:

```text
ELEMENTARY SUBSTRATE + LAWS
        -> possible MI organization space M

M + developmental law + resources
        -> reachable region Reach

information/data/task evidence
        -> posterior/uncertainty over operating regimes and requirements

Reach + capability/resource response
        -> Pareto/preference frontier of MI species
```

- [ ] Keep `GENERATION`, `DEVELOPMENT`, `CAPABILITY`, and `SELECTION` as separate maps.
- [ ] Demonstrate same possible MI space with different data producing different selected morphologies.
- [ ] Demonstrate same data with different requirements/resources producing different preferred MI species.

### AH8 — Elementary-substrate hostile programme

Any claimed MI elementary object must survive:

1. **too-powerful atom hostile:** primitive directly encodes memory/search/attention/Bayes/etc.;
2. **universal-interpreter hostile:** all structure hidden inside an arbitrary program argument;
3. **presentation hostile:** another equivalent basis changes all claimed laws;
4. **resource laundering hostile:** one basis receives free primitives/precision/parallelism;
5. **non-emergence hostile:** candidate can represent MI but no neutral developmental law can recover it;
6. **intelligence-smuggling hostile:** primitive itself contains task/evaluator knowledge;
7. **non-intelligent universality control:** universal computation present but no developmental/capability signature;
8. **physical-substrate hostile:** a claimed abstract invariant fails under stochastic/analog/distributed realization.

### AH9 — Candidate deepest research question

> **Is there a small foundation-relative class of elementary computational substrates and transformation laws whose composition/development generates the bounded space of possible machine organizations, such that known machine-intelligence families emerge as higher organizational regimes and their capability/resource/developmental preference can be predicted from information and operating conditions?**

Possible outcomes are all admissible:

```text
ELEMENTARY_GMI_SUBSTRATE_CLASS_SUPPORTED_AT_SCOPE
MULTIPLE_EQUIVALENT_ELEMENTARY_BASES
NO_SMALL_ELEMENTARY_GMI_BASIS
UNIVERSAL_COMPUTATION_ONLY
INTELLIGENCE_EMERGES_ONLY_AT_ORGANIZATIONAL_LAYER
PRESENTATION_DEPENDENT_ONLY
RESOURCE_IRREDUCIBLE_SUBSTRATE_CLASS
CANNOT_IDENTIFY_ELEMENTARY_LAYER
```

### AH10 — Claim ceiling

Until this lane is actually executed, do not claim that GMI has found “elementary particles of intelligence.” The present scientific claim is only that #833 now treats this as a first-class derivation problem and requires the current grammar to be reduced below syntax before any fundamental-basis wording is allowed.

The desired end state is analogous to a **machine-intelligence standard model plus chemistry**: a minimal/relative substrate and transformation theory, laws for composition and emergence, a taxonomy of stable higher organizations, developmental dynamics between them, and capability/resource selection laws — all with explicit foundations, parent ownership, counterexamples and bounded exact microscopes.
