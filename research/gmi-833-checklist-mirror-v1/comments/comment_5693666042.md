### AI1 — Define three distinct meanings of “derive a neural network”

Never count mere Turing-universal expressibility as architecture-prior-free derivation.

```text
NN-D1  COMPILE:
       a supplied neural network is compiled to lower primitives.
       -> proves expressibility only.

NN-D2  RECOVER:
       a search over architecture-unnamed lower processes independently
       constructs a neural-network-like organization.
       -> candidate architecture-uncommitted recovery.

NN-D3  PREDICT/SELECT:
       a frozen GMI law predicts in advance the ecology/resource/data region
       where a neural-like organization should appear or dominate a frontier.
       -> strongest target.
```

- [ ] Make all future neural claims carry exactly one of `NN-D1`, `NN-D2`, `NN-D3` (or a conjunction explicitly earned).
- [ ] Forbid `NEURAL_NETWORK_DERIVED` from NN-D1 alone.

### AI2 — Derive a fixed feed-forward neural computation from sub-neural operations

Do not include `NEURON`, `LAYER`, `MLP`, `DENSE`, `BACKPROP`, `ATTENTION`, `CONV`, or equivalent semantic macros in the lower basis.

Target finite neuron-like computation only after construction:

```text
z_i = b_i + sum_j w_ij h_j
h_i = sigma(z_i)
```

Required lower ingredients should be stated without neural terminology, e.g. finite value carriers, read/write/routing, elementary arithmetic/transformations, comparison/branching or another construct capable of nonlinearity, and serial/parallel composition.

- [ ] Give an exact finite compilation from lower process primitives to weighted aggregation.
- [ ] Derive multiplication from still-lower arithmetic/iteration where the registered basis does not supply it; charge time/space overhead.
- [ ] Derive finite sum/dot-product composition from repeated elementary transformations.
- [ ] Derive a threshold unit from aggregation + comparison/branching.
- [ ] Derive a ReLU-like unit as `max(0,z)` or an exactly equivalent lower construction at the registered numeric scope.
- [ ] Treat sigmoid/tanh as either explicitly available mathematical functions or finite-precision approximations; never silently import transcendental-function hardware as free.
- [ ] Encode signed/fixed-point values explicitly if the lowest executable substrate carries only naturals/bits.
- [ ] Compose the resulting units into an acyclic process graph and prove semantic equivalence to a supplied finite feed-forward network.
- [ ] Produce a compiler from at least one frozen finite feed-forward network family into current `G0` (or its lower AG replacement) with explicit code-size, memory and runtime overhead.
- [ ] Produce an alternate compilation through a materially different low-level basis to test presentation independence.
- [ ] Preserve the result as `NN_D1_EXPRESSIBLE_AT_SCOPE`, not architecture discovery.

Strong parents receiving first refusal: McCulloch–Pitts threshold networks, circuit theory, register-machine compilation, universal computation.

### AI4 — Derive learning/backprop as a process transformation, not a neural primitive

For a parameterized compositional numeric program

```text
y = f_theta(x)
L = ell(y, target)
```

if registered elementary operations are differentiable (or have declared subgradient rules where appropriate), the computational graph plus chain rule induces derivative propagation. Reverse-mode automatic differentiation computes gradients of a scalar objective with respect to many parameters and specializes to neural backpropagation.

- [ ] Formalize the compositional graph independently of neural terminology.
- [ ] Derive local Jacobian composition by the chain rule.
- [ ] Derive reverse accumulation/reverse-mode AD on a finite acyclic graph.
- [ ] Instantiate it on the recovered weighted-aggregation/nonlinearity graph and show equality with standard backpropagation updates at the registered scope.
- [ ] Separate `gradient computation` from `parameter update`; add optimizer/update law only as a `DEV` transformation.
- [ ] Compare gradient descent against finite differences, evolutionary mutation/search and exact small-domain optimization under matched cost vectors.
- [ ] Charge forward activations, stored intermediates, backward computation, precision and optimizer state.
- [ ] Parent-subtract automatic differentiation/reverse mode: GMI novelty cannot be “backprop is chain rule.”
- [ ] Test whether a neural-like morphology remains selected when the evaluator forbids gradients; this distinguishes architecture preference from learning-law preference.

### AI7 — Strong neural derivation success criterion

Do not call the neural family genuinely derived until all of the following are simultaneously demonstrated at a declared scope:

```text
LOWER BASIS:      no neural-specific macro is supplied
COMPILATION:      a known network can be reconstructed exactly/approximately with charged overhead
RECOVERY:         independent search produces the motif without architecture labels
INVARIANCE:       recovery survives materially different low-level presentations/searches
PARENT SUBTRACTION: known circuit/NN/AD results are explicitly absorbed
SELECTION:        a frozen law predicts where the motif is preferred and where it is not
DEVELOPMENT:      training/update behavior is generated by the general DEV machinery
HELD-OUT TEST:    morphology transition prediction succeeds prospectively
UNKNOWN CHANNEL:  search remains capable of returning non-neural/unclassified organizations
```

Until then use scoped terminals (`EXPRESSIBLE`, `RECOVERED`, `SELECTED`) rather than the blanket phrase `NEURAL_NETWORK_DERIVED`.

### Literature anchors for this microscope

- McCulloch & Pitts (1943): threshold-like networks as logical computational structures — parent for the fixed-unit/network construction idea.
- Rumelhart, Hinton & Williams (1986): back-propagation for adjusting network weights — parent for the classical neural learning procedure.
- Cybenko (1989): single-hidden-layer sigmoidal networks have strong approximation capacity — capacity/representability parent, not a selection theorem.
- Baydin, Pearlmutter, Radul & Siskind (2018): reverse-mode automatic differentiation is more general than neural backpropagation; parent for the derivative machinery.
- Real et al., AutoML-Zero (ICML 2020): generic low-level mathematical operations plus evolutionary search can rediscover two-layer neural networks trained by backpropagation — empirical parent/precedent for architecture-unnamed recovery.

The purpose of this block is to make a neural network the first end-to-end test of the converged GMI spine:

```text
lower process laws --GEN--> candidate organization
                     --DEV--> parameterized/trainable organization
requirements/data --REL/CAP/SEL--> neural-like region preferred at scope
                     --EVID--> claim ceiling and replication
```

If we cannot derive/recover/predict a neural family without smuggling it in, the broader “all seen and unseen MI” programme is not yet credible.
