## Morphology Transformation Geometry & Recursive Closure Programme

This section extends #833 with an explicit transformation/topology programme. It is scoped to architecture-name-free mechanism classes, disclosed priors, resource-bounded development, and machine-checkable finite witnesses. It does **not** treat universal simulation as morphology equivalence and it does not allow a positive terminal to outrun hostile/held-out evidence.

### MTG-0 — Mathematical objects

Let `Omega` be the registered scientific scope and let `[M]_Omega` denote a computational-mechanism equivalence class under the #848 morphology relation. Define a **verified morphology transform** `T : M -> N` as a registered transformation carrying: (i) source/target classes, (ii) semantic distortion bound, (iii) intervention/development preservation contract, (iv) raw lifecycle resource vector, (v) explicit applicability assumptions, and (vi) verifier evidence.

A **static transform** preserves only the declared protected behavior/capability contract. A **developmental transform** must additionally satisfy a registered commuting/naturality condition of the form `T(Phi_M(D,e)) ~= Phi_N(T(D),e)` at the declared exact/approximate scope. Static compilation must never be promoted to developmental equivalence.

Define `Hom_Omega(M,N)` as the set of verified transforms. Identities and valid composition are required. Resource composition remains vector-valued; scalarization is secondary and must preserve the existing Pareto rules.

Define directed transform burden by the Pareto set of attainable resource/distortion vectors and, only under a frozen scalarization, `d_w(M,N)=inf_T w·cost(T)`. The scalar object is expected to be a Lawvere-style directed pseudometric when identities and subadditive composition hold. Asymmetry is allowed and scientifically meaningful.

### MTG-1 — Gauge/remint invariance

- [ ] Formalize semantics-preserving grammar/encoding remints as a groupoid/action on derivation records.
- [ ] Prove an equivariance/invariance theorem: mechanism-level scientific conclusions commute with registered remints.
- [ ] Add hostile twins where lexical renaming passes but semantic structure changes.
- [ ] Add alternate low-level encodings with identical semantics and verify equal quotient conclusions.
- [ ] Separate invariance of representation from invariance of search reachability/cost.

### MTG-2 — Transformation category

- [ ] Define `Hom_Omega(M,N)` without architecture names.
- [ ] Prove/execute identity laws.
- [ ] Prove/execute associativity of valid composition.
- [ ] Specify exact composition laws for semantic error, uncertainty, lifecycle resources, intervention contracts, and developmental reachability.
- [ ] Fail closed when transform assumptions are incompatible or composition loses required evidence.
- [ ] Prove morphology-equivalent source/target remints induce isomorphic transform sets at the registered finite scope.

### MTG-3 — Developmental naturality

- [ ] Define exact and approximate developmental commuting squares.
- [ ] Distinguish static compiler, learning-compatible transform, and full developmental-equivalence transform.
- [ ] Prove exact naturality closure under composition.
- [ ] Give a hostile counterexample: two statically behavior-equivalent systems whose learning/update trajectories do not commute.
- [ ] Track path dependence/hysteresis rather than erasing it through final-output equivalence.
- [ ] Extend to stochastic update kernels with calibrated uncertainty rather than deterministic overclaim.

### MTG-4 — Directed geometry / topology

- [ ] Define vector-valued directed transformation burden on mechanism classes.
- [ ] Prove identity-zero and triangle/subadditivity laws for frozen scalarizations.
- [ ] Preserve `+infinity`/unreachable transforms rather than fabricating finite distance.
- [ ] Prove symmetry is not required; add an asymmetric compile-vs-decompile hostile.
- [ ] Define reachable balls/order intervals from directed burden.
- [ ] Define topology/preorder only from earned structure; do not assume a Euclidean manifold.
- [ ] Test metric/topology stability under grammar remints and resource-coordinate perturbations.
- [ ] Investigate quantale/ordered-monoid enrichment so the primary geometry remains resource-vector valued.

### MTG-5 — Known-form closure as reachability, not simulation

For frozen known-family registry `K_known`, target theorem shape:

`forall K in K_known, exists M in Reach_Delta(G0,B_K) such that [M]_Omega ~= [K]_Omega`

with registered resource/semantic bounds and post-hoc family naming only.

- [ ] Prove bounded constructive realizations from `G0` for registered mechanism/property vectors.
- [ ] Require P3 or P4 no-smuggling status for flagship recovery.
- [ ] Require neutral initialization and actual developmental/search reachability.
- [ ] Require matched negative grammars removing the predicted mechanism while preserving irrelevant capacity.
- [ ] Require at least three materially different low-level search encodings.
- [ ] Hold out whole historical families, not source-code instances.
- [ ] Score property/mechanism recovery, not syntactic resemblance.
- [ ] Keep `UNKNOWN` as a valid result.
