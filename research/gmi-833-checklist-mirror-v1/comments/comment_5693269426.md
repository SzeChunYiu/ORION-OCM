## AF — Developmental capability space, information provenance, and barrier-displacement programme

This addendum follows the #833 AE information/structure programme and the #233 HSG foundation. It is **not** a claim that the classical barriers have been disproved. It turns the barrier question into a first-class GMI research object: identify the exact assumptions under which a boundary holds, determine the smallest principled changes in substrate/information/task/output/resource conditions that move the boundary, charge those changes explicitly, and preserve the residual boundary.

No checkbox below is scientifically closed by this comment.

### AF1 — Developmental capability response object

Data must not define the existence of the machine-intelligence possibility space. Let a registered computational/physical substrate be `S`, initial organization `M0`, developmental law `Delta`, information-provenance channels `Pi`, task/specification family `Q`, resource vector `R`, interaction horizon/protocol `H`, and verifier/output contract `V`.

Define a set-valued developmental capability object schematically as

```text
Gamma_{M0,Delta}^S(Pi,R,H,Q,V)
  = reachable capability/resource profiles under the registered conditions.
```

Raw capability/resource vectors and Pareto frontiers remain primary; no universal scalar intelligence score is presumed.

- [ ] Formalize `Gamma` architecture-neutrally and prove its relation to existing #833 capability, reachability, morphology and resource objects.
- [ ] Define current realized capability as a zero-development/registered-current-state slice rather than equating it with developmental potential.
- [ ] Define information/data-independent *possibility space* `M(S)` separately from history-conditioned reachable machines.
- [ ] Define provenance-tagged development channels at least for: initial/inherited organization; external observations; rewards/evaluator feedback; endogenous computation; stochastic variation; oracle/advice/tool channels; other-agent/social/cultural transfer; physical/environmental signals.
- [ ] Define capability acquisition provenance so an apparent gain cannot be called `emergent from nothing` when information was imported through initialization, arbitrary real constants, an oracle, evaluator feedback, another agent, or a physical measurement channel.
- [ ] Construct exact finite `G0` microscopes where two machines have the same current capability but different `Gamma`, and conversely.
- [ ] Replace/upgrade #833-L's single row `developmental potential separately from current capability` with this response-object view after theorem closure, not before.

### AF2 — Null-experience developmental envelope

Define the null-external slice by setting registered external observation/reward/task-feedback channels to null while keeping the substrate, initial organization and explicitly allowed endogenous processes visible:

```text
Gamma_null(M0,Delta;R,H,Q,V)
 = Gamma(Pi_external = 0, ...).
```

`NO_EXTERNAL_DATA` is therefore not synonymous with `NOTHING`.

- [ ] Define a hierarchy of null conditions: no sensory observations; no reward/evaluator feedback; no task-specific data; no external interaction; no endogenous randomness; no task-specific initialization; absolute registered null except substrate/dynamics.
- [ ] Prove a **Null Target-Information Acquisition** specialization of the data-processing inequality: if target `Theta` is independent of `(M0,W)` and no `Theta`-dependent channel enters development, then for `Mt=f(M0,W)` the machine cannot acquire target information, e.g. `I(Theta;Mt)=0` at the registered probabilistic scope.
- [ ] Parent-subtract HSG's algorithmic-information no-ex-nihilo result rather than re-claim it; distinguish Shannon target-information from Kolmogorov-description statements.
- [ ] Construct exact examples where accessible capability increases with zero external data through deduction/search/compilation/caching/self-simulation, while no independent target information is acquired.
- [ ] Separate `information acquisition` from `computational unfolding/accessibility` and from `random novelty generation`.
- [ ] Build hostile: random bits independent of target can generate new candidates/complexity but cannot create target alignment without a correlated selection/evaluation channel.
- [ ] Build hostile: a purported zero-data super-capability encoded in an oracle/advice string/arbitrary real parameter must be classified `IMPORTED_INFORMATION_OR_ADVICE`, not spontaneous intelligence.
- [ ] Introduce scoped terminals such as `NO_TARGET_INFORMATION_ACQUIRED`, `COMPUTATIONAL_UNFOLDING_WITHOUT_EXTERNAL_DATA`, `RANDOM_NOVELTY_WITHOUT_TARGET_ALIGNMENT`, `IMPORTED_ORACLE_OR_ADVICE_POWER`, and `UNEXERCISED_CAPABILITY` distinct from `INCAPABLE`.

### AF3 — Barrier context and barrier surfaces

A barrier statement must be relative to an explicit context rather than treated as one undifferentiated wall. Register a barrier context such as

```text
chi = (S, Pi, Q, R, H, eps, delta, V)
```

where `S` is substrate semantics, `Pi` information provenance, `Q` problem/query class, `R` resources, `H` horizon/interaction protocol, `(eps,delta)` approximation/confidence parameters, and `V` output/verifier contract.

Define typed status, not a single solvable/unsolvable bit, e.g.

```text
DECIDABLE
SEMI_DECIDABLE
CERTIFIABLE
SOUND_INCOMPLETE_APPROXIMATION
PROBABILISTIC_APPROXIMATION
IDENTIFIABLE_IN_LIMIT
NOT_IDENTIFIABLE_AT_SCOPE
UNDECIDABLE_RELATIVE_TO_S
RESOURCE_INFEASIBLE_AT_SCOPE
PHYSICAL_STATUS_UNKNOWN
```

A **barrier surface** is a region/boundary in `chi` where this status or attainable capability changes.

- [ ] Formalize barrier context, typed status and exact transition/displacement semantics.
- [ ] Require every claimed barrier displacement to state which premise/axis changed and the exact information/resource/substrate power added or requirement weakened.
- [ ] Distinguish theorem contradiction, domain restriction, promise problem, approximation, semidecision, abstention, list output, certificate/witness, interaction/query access, oracle/advice, randomness, resource relaxation and substrate expansion.
- [ ] Prove monotonicity only where added channels/resources are optional and the old strategy remains admissible; add mandatory-overhead/interference counterexamples.
- [ ] Require nearest residual barrier after every successful displacement; no `SOLVED_FOREVER` terminal.

### AF4 — Relative computability and the non-terminal frontier

The computability lane should test barrier displacement rather than merely recite halting/Rice/Gödel ceilings.

For an oracle/substrate `A`, the relativized halting set/Turing jump `A'` can decide questions not computable in `A`, but classical computability theory gives

```text
A <_T A'.
```

Thus a stronger oracle can genuinely displace a weaker system's halting boundary while generating a new relative boundary above it.

- [ ] Import/prove-at-parent-scope the exact relative-computability/Turing-jump statements needed by GMI; do not rename them.
- [ ] Formalize `Comp(S)` / computational power of substrate `S` as a registered object, with oracle/advice channels explicit.
- [ ] Derive the scoped GMI consequence `NO_TERMINAL_EFFECTIVE_FRONTIER_AT_REGISTERED_ORACLE_MODEL_SCOPE`: for the standard Turing-jump hierarchy, every fixed oracle degree has a strictly stronger jump.
- [ ] Distinguish that result from a universal metaphysical claim about all possible physics.
- [ ] Show how the machine-intelligence capability envelope changes under `S -> S+oracle`, charging oracle/advice information and access cost.
- [ ] Build hostile against `HALTING_PROBLEM_SOLVED_UNIVERSALLY`: solving the ordinary halting problem using an oracle does not decide the oracle-relative halting problem.
- [ ] Re-audit Gödel-machine/self-referential claims: provability-relative self-improvement is not escape from incompleteness; the proof system/axioms remain part of `S/Pi`.

- [ ] Test whether architecture/morphology changes when verification cost becomes a protected resource coordinate.

### AF7 — Physical substrate / hypercomputation audit

The original mathematical Church-Turing thesis concerns effective computation; stronger claims about all physically realizable computation are separate empirical theses. GMI must leave a substrate slot open rather than assuming physical Turing maximality by definition.

- [ ] Define an empirical substrate contract `S_phys` including precision, state preparation, noise, readout, repeatability, time, energy, spatial resources and error probability.
- [ ] Audit major hypercomputation/analog/oracle proposals only at their exact mathematical/physical assumptions.
- [ ] Treat arbitrary-real-weight analog super-Turing models carefully: known results connect them to nonuniform/advice-like computational power; measure/charge the information content, precision and readout assumptions in the real parameters rather than calling it free emergence.
- [ ] Construct precision/noise hostiles showing when apparent super-Turing power disappears under a finite operational contract.
- [ ] Keep `PHYSICAL_HYPERCOMPUTATION_OPEN` unless reproducible physical evidence under a registered contract justifies promotion.
- [ ] If future physics supplies a repeatable substrate exceeding the current `Comp(S)`, GMI should update `S` and recompute the frontier rather than treating the theory as broken.
- [ ] Forbid `PHYSICAL_CHURCH_TURING_FALSIFIED` from simulation/theoretical oracle models alone.

### Scientific interpretation

The target is not to worship impossibility theorems and not to hand-wave them away. The target is to turn them into **mapped boundary conditions**. A valid GMI result should be able to say both:

```text
under premises A: capability/property X is impossible or undecidable;
under the minimal registered change B: X becomes decidable/learnable/certifiable/approximately usable,
at explicit information/resource/substrate cost C;
and residual boundary D remains.
```

That makes barrier displacement itself part of the theory of developing machine intelligence.
