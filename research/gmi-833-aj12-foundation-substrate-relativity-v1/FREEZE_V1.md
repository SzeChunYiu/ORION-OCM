# AJ12 freeze — foundation-style and substrate relativity

AJ12 does not attempt to prove one absolute foundation of mathematics. It freezes a compact finite theorem core and requires two materially different formalization styles to reproduce the same scientific conclusions.

## Theorem core

Carrier `B={0,1}` with the four unary Boolean transformations. Registered claims to reproduce independently:
1. identity and composition closure;
2. exact observation of each process on both inputs;
3. translation preserves composition and observations;
4. operational equality under the full point-test family is identical across representations.

## Style A — set/relational

A process is represented extensionally by its graph relation `{(x,f(x))}`. Composition is relational composition; observations query membership of the graph.

## Style B — typed algebraic table

A process is a typed record `Bool -> Bool` represented by the output tuple `(f(0),f(1))`. Composition is tuple/function substitution; observations are indexed evaluation.

The theorem target is semantic invariance under the explicit bijection between these styles, not an assertion that set theory and type theory are globally equivalent foundations.

## Assumption classes

Every lower assumption must be tagged as one of:
`MATHEMATICAL_FOUNDATION`, `LOGIC/METATHEORY`, `PHYSICAL_SUBSTRATE_LAW`, `RESOURCE_MODEL`, `VALUE/REQUIREMENT_INPUT`.

## Alternative substrate/accounting boundary

AJ12 registers three mathematical computation regimes only:
- ordinary finite/discrete effective computation;
- an oracle/advice extension, whose external answers are charged as imported oracle information;
- exact real-parameter advice, whose usable digits/precision/nonuniform information must be charged explicitly.

No mathematical oracle/real-parameter model is promoted to physically realizable hypercomputation. `PHYSICAL_HYPERCOMPUTATION_OPEN` is mandatory absent reproducible physical evidence.

Gödel incompleteness and Tarski-style object-language/metalanguage limits remain parent boundaries: no sufficiently strong effective theory is treated as an all-truth, self-justifying final foundation.

Claim ceiling: `AJ12_FOUNDATION_STYLE_INVARIANCE_AND_SUBSTRATE_RELATIVITY_AT_REGISTERED_FINITE_SCOPE`.

Forbidden: `ABSOLUTE_FOUNDATION_PROVEN`, `PHYSICAL_HYPERCOMPUTATION_PROVEN`, `SELF_JUSTIFYING_FINAL_GMI_FOUNDATION`, `COMPLETE_GMI`.
