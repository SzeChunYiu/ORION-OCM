# AJ12 literature / ownership ledger

- Set theory and type theory are established alternative foundational programmes for formalizing mathematics; category/structural foundations are an additional programme. AJ12 does not select a uniquely true foundation.
- Church–Turing/computability theory owns the standard effective-computation boundary; physical Church–Turing variants are distinct physical theses.
- Relative computability/oracle theory owns explicit oracle-power hierarchies.
- Siegelmann/Sontag analog real-weight computation and Cabessa/Siegelmann interactive recurrent-network results connect exact real-weight models with nonuniform/advice-like power. AJ12 treats the embedded real/advice content as provenance that must be charged.
- Gödel incompleteness and Tarski truth/metalanguage results own the non-final/self-reference boundaries.
