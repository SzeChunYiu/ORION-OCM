# Literature / ownership ledger

AJ1 is a synthesis/registration result, not a claim to have invented process composition.

- **Process theories / monoidal categories** own the abstract systems-as-objects, processes-as-morphisms, sequential-composition and parallel-tensor backbone (e.g. Coecke & Kissinger's process-theoretic programme).
- **Operational probabilistic theories** own a broad operational treatment of systems, tests, transformations, sequential/parallel composition, probabilities, and state/effect roles (Chiribella, D'Ariano & Perinotti).
- **Relational semantics** supplies a direct finite nondeterministic instantiation.
- **Markov kernels / Markov categories** supply stochastic compositional instantiations (e.g. Fritz's categorical probability programme).
- **LTS / structural operational semantics** and **universal coalgebra** are strongest state/dynamics-first parents (Rutten for universal coalgebra). They are compared rather than forcibly renamed as process theories.
- **Constructor theory** is a strongest parent for substrate-relative possible/impossible transformations/tasks (Deutsch/Marletto).

AJ1 contribution: a deliberately weak, substrate-indexed operational interface joining these parent views to the #833 derivation spine, plus an executable finite counterexample suite showing why deterministic functions cannot be the only allowed semantics.

No literature item is claimed as GMI novelty.
