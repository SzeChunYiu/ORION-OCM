## 1. CD-1 — strongest_parents (PINNED_AT_SCOPE)

**Claim** (gmi-grand-unification-v1/CLAIM_LEDGER_V1.md:L30, authority
COMMON_DECODER_CORRECTION_V1.md:L20-L29): "Joint risk set is the convex hull of
deterministic common-decoder profiles. THEOREM + EXACT CHECKS. Scope: full
randomized decoder class; finite sets."

**Strongest parent (external, at CD-1's exact scope):** the
randomized-decision-function convexification of classical statistical decision
theory — for a finite state set and finite action set, the risk vectors attainable
by randomized decision rules are exactly the convex hull of the risk vectors of the
deterministic rules. Canonical locus: Wald (1950), *Statistical Decision
Functions*; Blackwell & Girshick (1954), *Theory of Games and Statistical
Decisions* — randomized decision functions and risk sets (Sections 7.2 and 8.3
cited for this material by G. Schwarz, "Ways of Randomizing and the Problem of
Their Equivalence", 1974, Springer DOI 10.1007/BF02756820).

**Delta:** zero at CD-1's own statement level — the row instantiates the parent
exactly (ecology set = state set; decoder tables = deterministic rules; mixture =
randomization). The correction tranche's novel content is CD-2's
simultaneous-attainment condition and CD-4's counterexample. The falsified
predecessor use (SEMANTIC_CUT_THEOREM_V1.md §§6–8; separately-optimized ecology
risks read as one attainable profile, refuted at COMMON_DECODER_CORRECTION_V1.md:
L7–L16) is the misuse the parent frame rules out.

**Verification:** statement verified via Warwick APTS *Statistical Inference*
notes ("the risk set is the convex hull of the rows of the risk matrix R"),
Wikipedia "Randomised decision rule", and Schwarz (1974)'s citation of B&G
§§7.2/8.3. The primary books were not read (paywalled); section numbers are
secondhand via Schwarz — recorded, not concealed. The source package's own
primary-source boundary (COMMON_DECODER_CORRECTION_V1.md:L72–L91) already names
Blackwell (1953) for CD-3's emulation result and Brooks-Frankel-Kamenica (2024) +
Rosenthal (arXiv:2510.08709) as side-information/scope contrasts — CD-1's pin is
the missing convexification parent for its own row.
